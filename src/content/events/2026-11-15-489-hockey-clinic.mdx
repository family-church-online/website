Train under national players and coaches who invest in the player, not just the game of hockey. Based on Philippians 4:8-9, 489 aims to equip young athletes to navigate, and to change sporting culture  in South Africa. 

Our identities are shaped by our thoughts and actions, not by our possessions or achievements. Holding on to our God given identity requires a firm grip on His principles and trust in His character. The wonderful people of the 489 team combine elite hockey training with teaching and encouragement to guide and support Godly character development in high school hockey players.

Please support this wonderful initiative in prayer and ask us how you can be involved.
