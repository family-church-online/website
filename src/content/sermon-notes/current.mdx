### 

Through the Scripture

This is the description of the throne room that starts the judgment. It ends the judgement after 7 seals, 7 trumpets, 7 bowls of wrath, the judgment of Babylon and that of the prostitute. In chapter 19 we have the same scene. Here it awaits Jesus‘ revelation as Judge over the earth - there as Saviour Of His people and Victor over evil.

#### The Elements Declare

Like the scene when the law was given on mount Sinai. Thunder declares a coming storm and power beyond our comprehension.

#### The Holy Spirit 

The presence of the Holy Spirit represented by 7 burning torches before the Fathers throne invites the revelation of the Son. 

Revelation 4:5B ESV and before the throne were burning seven torches of fire, which are the seven spirits of God,

#### The Sea of Glass

The vastness and perfection of this sea may echo that of God’s magnificent creation.

Revelation 4:6 ESV and before the throne there was as it were a sea of glass, like crystal.  

#### The Living Creatures Declare

The Living creatures suggest something like the Cherubim that Ezekiel saw, but differ substantially. They seem to be angelic and are probably symbolic representation thereof, and perhaps intended to represent all God‘s creation.

Revelation 4:6b-8 ESV And around the throne, on each side of the throne, are four living creatures, full of eyes in front and behind:  [7] the first living creature like a lion, the second living creature like an ox, the third living creature with the face of a man, and the fourth living creature like an eagle in flight. [8] And the four living creatures, each of them with six wings, are full of eyes all around and within, and day and night they never cease to say,  “Holy, holy, holy, is the Lord God Almighty, who was and is and is to come!”

#### The Elders Declare

The elders seem to represent the ultimate in human authority apart from Jesus Christ. Yet together with all of creation physical and spiritual, living and elemental, they fall down in awe and give glory to God. All the time. For all time. 

Revelation 4:9-11 ESV And whenever the living creatures give glory and honor and thanks to him who is seated on the throne, who lives forever and ever, [10] the twenty-four elders fall down before him who is seated on the throne and worship him who lives forever and ever. They cast their crowns before the throne, saying,  [11] “Worthy are you, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power, for you created all things, and by your will they existed and were created.”
