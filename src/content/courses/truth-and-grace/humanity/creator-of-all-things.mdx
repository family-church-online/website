statement: >-
  We believe (1) that in the beginning God sovereignly created all things
  visible and invisible and took delight in it all; (2) that He created
  humankind male and female as His image bearers, endowing each with a dignity
  of equal worth and calling to govern and care for His creation; (3) that
  human life is sacred to God and begins at conception; (4) that God created
  marriage to be an exclusive and lifelong union between a natural man and a
  natural woman, giving the man the responsibility of self-sacrificial and
  loving leadership and the woman the responsibility of compatible support;
  and (5) that the first man, Adam, sinned and thereby incurred the penalty of
  death, physical and spiritual, for all people, and as a consequence of his
  sin, all humans inherit a sinful nature, which results in actual
  transgression and personal guilt.

    heading: "All things were created through Christ and for Him, by the sovereign will of God"
    scriptures:
      - ref: "Colossians 1:16"
        text: >-
          For by him all things were created, in heaven and on earth, visible
          and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or
          authorities—all things were created through him and for him.
      - ref: "Revelation 4:11"
        text: >-
          "Worthy are you, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and
          power, for you created all things, and by your will they existed and
          were created."
    commentary: >-
      Together these passages establish that creation in its entirety —
      visible and invisible, earthly and heavenly — is the personal and
      purposeful work of Christ, accomplished by the sovereign will of God.
      Paul's deliberate repetition of "all things" in Colossians 1:16 is
      emphatic: no category of existence lies outside Christ's creative act.
      Crucially, the verse reveals not only the agent of creation but its goal
      — all things were made for Him, meaning the entire created order exists
      to serve His purposes and display His glory. Revelation 4:11 grounds
      this further in the character of God's will: creation was not compelled
      by need, constrained by circumstance, or produced by any force outside
      God Himself. The heavenly elders' declaration that God is worthy because
      He created confirms that Creator-worship is the fitting and eternal
      response of all who rightly understand who God is. To acknowledge God as
      Creator is therefore not merely a doctrinal statement but an act of
      worship.

  - id: B
    heading: "God alone initiated creation, and He took genuine delight in all He made"
    scriptures:
      - ref: "Genesis 1:1"
        text: >-
          In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.
      - ref: "Genesis 1:31"
        text: >-
          And God saw everything that he had made, and behold, it was very
          good. And there was evening and there was morning, the sixth day.
    commentary: >-
      These ten words of Genesis 1:1 stand as the foundational declaration of
      all Scripture, establishing God's absolute priority and sovereignty
      before anything else is said. The phrase "the heavens and the earth" is
      a Hebrew merism encompassing the totality of all that exists — nothing
      in the created order is excluded from God's authorship. There is no
      pre-existing matter, no co-creator, no rival: God alone initiates, and
      creation exists because He wills it to be. Genesis 1:31 then reveals the
      character of that creative act: God surveys the whole of His completed
      work and declares it not merely good but very good — a superlative
      signalling delight, completeness, and approval. This is not a casual
      remark but God Himself passing judgment on creation as a whole,
      affirming that everything He made perfectly served His intentions. The
      material world, human existence included, is not something God tolerates
      but something He lovingly fashioned and joyfully pronounced excellent.

takeaways:
  - >-
    God's creation is total and without exception. Both the visible world we
    inhabit and the invisible spiritual realm — including powers and
    authorities — owe their existence entirely to God. Nothing created itself;
    nothing exists outside His sovereign authorship.
  - >-
    Creation flows from God's sovereign will and pleasure alone. There was no
    external force, need, or necessity that prompted God to create. Heaven's
    own worship acknowledges this: He is worthy precisely because He created
    all things freely and by His will.
  - >-
    Scripture's opening words establish God's absolute priority. Before
    matter, time, or any created being, God was. Genesis 1:1 is not merely a
    statement about origins — it is a declaration of God's supremacy over all
    things that follow in the biblical story and in human history.
  - >-
    God takes genuine delight in what He has made. The divine verdict of "very
    good" in Genesis 1:31 reveals that creation is not incidental to God's
    purposes but a purposeful work in which He finds joy. This grounds a right
    appreciation for the goodness of the material world and human life within
    it.
  - >-
    *Creation is made for Christ and to display His glory.* Colossians 1:16
    teaches that creation's ultimate goal is not human flourishing in itself,
    but the honour and praise of the One through whom and for whom all things
    were made. This gives the created order its deepest meaning and the
    believer a framework for living within it worshipfully.
