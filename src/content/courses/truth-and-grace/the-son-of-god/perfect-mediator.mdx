  That He is, therefore, the perfect and only mediator between God and people.
statement: >-
  We believe (1) that when the time was right, the eternal Son of God took on
  human nature, and was made like us in every way, yet without sin; (2) that,
  after being conceived by the Holy Spirit, He was born of the virgin Mary,
  was named Jesus (meaning 'God saves'), and from conception was and always
  will be fully God and fully man in one person; and (3) that He is,
  therefore, the perfect and only mediator between God and people.

      - ref: "1 Timothy 2:5"
        text: >-

    commentary: >-

      identified specifically as "the man Christ Jesus." His genuine humanity,
      secured through the Incarnation, qualifies Him to represent humanity
      before God, while His deity qualifies Him to represent God to humanity.
      No other person, tradition, or system can occupy this unique mediating
      role.

  - id: C
    heading: "Christ's mediation established the new covenant through His own sacrificial death"
    scriptures:
      - ref: "Hebrews 9:15"
        text: >-
          Therefore he is the mediator of a new covenant, so that those who
          are called may receive the promised eternal inheritance, since a
          death has occurred that redeems them from the transgressions
          committed under the first covenant.
      - ref: "Hebrews 12:24"
        text: >-
          and to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant, and to the sprinkled
          blood that speaks a better word than the blood of Abel.
    commentary: >-
      Christ's mediating work is tied directly to the establishment of a new
      covenant, secured through His own death. As mediator, He does not merely
      negotiate between two parties but actively redeems those under the old
      covenant's condemnation, opening the way for them to receive the
      promised eternal inheritance. The writer of Hebrews places Jesus
      alongside the imagery of sprinkled blood that speaks a better word than
      the blood of Abel — where Abel's blood cried out for vengeance, Christ's
      blood speaks a message of mercy, forgiveness, and peace. His mediation
      is therefore not symbolic but effective, accomplishing what no earthly
      priest or covenant representative ever could.

  - id: D
    heading: "Christ continues as our righteous advocate before the Father"
    scriptures:
      - ref: "1 John 2:1"
        text: >-
          My little children, I am writing these things to you so that you may
          not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have an advocate with the
          Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.
    commentary: >-
      John presents Christ not only as mediator in the once-for-all sense of
      securing the new covenant, but also as an ongoing advocate for believers

      advocacy is perfectly effective, and because He shares our humanity, He
      is able to represent us with full understanding and sympathy. This verse
      offers great comfort, assuring believers that their standing before God
      rests not on their own performance but on the continual mediating and
      advocating work of Christ.
