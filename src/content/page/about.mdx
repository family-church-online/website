      We're a warm community for the whole family — through every stage, from toddlers to teens to young adults finding their feet. Wherever you are, and wherever you are in life, we want you to have a place to belong and grow in faith together, not do it alone.

      We're non-denominational — we are not a building, we are the people of God, under the authority of Jesus Christ. Church isn't somewhere you drive to on a Sunday and leave behind; it's a family you belong to every day, whether gathered in a home, online, or together in person. According to the Bible, the Church is built on the foundation of the Apostles and Prophets, with Jesus Christ as its Cornerstone. The Church exists to love and worship God, and to love others so that they can know, love and serve God too. Family Church is grounded on two foundational Scriptures — Matthew 22:37–40 and Matthew 28:18–20 — summed up simply as: Love, Worship, Honour & Obey.

      ## How does the Church do this?

      The early Church in Acts shows us four essentials, practised daily — not just on a Sunday — in public and at home:

      > And they devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers... And day by day, attending the temple together and breaking bread in their homes, they received their food with glad and generous hearts, praising God and having favor with all the people. — Acts 2:42–47 ESV

      These four essentials — Scripture, Prayer, Worship, and Serving — are the walls of Family Church, and they look a little different at every age. A young child learns Scripture through a bedtime story; a teenager wrestles with it through honest questions; a young adult builds the discipline of studying it for themselves. A family prays together over dinner; a young adult learns to pray honestly through exam stress and first jobs. Worship welcomes every generation in the room, not just the loudest or the youngest. And serving is something we do together — parents and children giving, teens and young adults given real responsibility, not just a seat to watch from.

      Our mission is simple: to weave these four walls into every part of family life, at every stage — whether you're joining us online or in person, in Dainfern or anywhere else.
