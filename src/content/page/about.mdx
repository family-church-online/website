    body: |
      We are a real, warm community for the whole family — through every stage, from toddlers to teens to young adults finding their feet. Wherever you are in Dainfern, and wherever you are in life, we want you to have a place to belong and grow in faith together, not do it alone.

      We're non-denominational — we are not a building, we are the people of God, under the authority of Jesus Christ. Church isn't somewhere you drive to on a Sunday and leave behind; it's a family you belong to every day, whether gathered in a home, online, or together in person. According to the Bible, the Church is built on the foundation of the Apostles and Prophets, with Jesus Christ as its Cornerstone. The Church exists to love and worship God, and to love others so that they can know, love and serve God too. 

      We're grounded on two foundational Scriptures — Matthew 22:37–40 and Matthew 28:18–20 — summed up simply as: Love, Worship, Honour & Obey.
