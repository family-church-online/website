      ## Information Officer


      Under POPIA, Keith Haines our secretary is our appointed Information Officer and can be contacted at secretary@familychurch.online for any privacy questions or requests.


      ## What we collect


      **Sign-in.** If you access course or study material, we ask for your email address to send you a one-time sign-in link. We check that email against our congregation membership records to confirm access — we don't store a password, and the link itself expires shortly after it's sent.


      **Analytics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, general location, device/browser type, time on site). Cloudflare Web Analytics doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. Google Analytics does use cookies — see \"Cookies\" below.


      **WhatsApp groups.** Event and ministry group sign-ups happen inside WhatsApp itself, not on our website. When you join a group, WhatsApp (Meta) — not us — collects and stores your phone number and profile info under its own privacy policy.


      We don't run advertising, and we don't sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.


      ## Cookies


      Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time; the site will still work, though analytics data will be less accurate. We don't use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.


      ## How we use your information


      Solely to: send you sign-in links, confirm access to member-only content, and understand overall site usage so we can improve it. We don't use your email for marketing without separate, explicit consent.


      ## Storage and security


      Sign-in requests are processed via Resend (email delivery) and checked against our Planning Center database. Data is stored and processed using reputable third-party infrastructure providers (e.g. Cloudflare) with reasonable technical safeguards. No online system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse, or unauthorised access.


      ## Your rights under POPIA


      You have the right to:


      - ask what personal information we hold about you;

      - ask us to correct or delete it;

      - object to how it's processed; and

      - lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) if you believe your information has been mishandled.


      To exercise any of these, contact our Information Officer at secretary@familychurch.online.


      ## Children


      This site isn't directed at collecting information from children. Kids Church and youth ministry information is provided for parents/guardians, who remain responsible for any sign-ups made on a child's behalf.


      ## Third-party sites


      Links to WhatsApp, YouTube, or other external platforms are governed by those platforms' own privacy policies, not ours.


      ## Changes to this policy


      We may update this policy as our practices change; the current version on this page always applies.


      ## Contact


      Privacy questions or requests: secretary@familychurch.online"
