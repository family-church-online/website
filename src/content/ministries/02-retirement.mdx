---
title: Retirement
---

Retirement is a season for deeper fellowship, not less of it. Our retirement group gathers regularly for Bible study, prayer, and the kind of unhurried conversation that is so easy to lose in a busy week.

This ministry exists to make sure that those in later stages of life are not on the margins of the church but right at the centre — carrying wisdom, praying for the congregation, and remaining fully part of the family.

If you are in this season of life, or know someone who might benefit from this community, we would love to hear from you.
