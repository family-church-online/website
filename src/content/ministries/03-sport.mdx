Sport is one of the most natural places to build friendship across ages and backgrounds, and we want to use that. Our sport ministry organises regular games and activities as a way to connect people in the church and create easy on-ramps for those who are not yet part of a faith community.

Whether you play at a competitive level or just want to have a run around, you are welcome. The goal is not performance — it is people. Bring a friend, show up, and enjoy the game.

Follow our announcements for upcoming fixtures and activities, or ask at the welcome desk on a Sunday.
