---
title: Retirement
---

There are two different outreaches to retirement estates in our area. At Lonehill Manor, residents meet on Sundays to watch the live stream of the service, and also meet during the week for Bible Study. On the first Sunday of the month our team visits to break bread and catch up. At Merrow Down our teams visit for a Lord's Table service once a month and facilitate a Bible Study twice a month.
