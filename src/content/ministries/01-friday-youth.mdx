---
title: Friday Youth
---

Our Friday night youth group meets weekly and is open to teens from Grade 8 through to Matric. It is a space to belong, ask honest questions, and grow in faith together with others your age.

Each Friday we work through Scripture, share a meal, and have time to connect. We believe teenagers need a community of their own — not just a seat beside their parents on Sunday — and Friday Youth exists to be exactly that.

If you would like to know more or bring a friend along, get in touch with our youth leader or speak to anyone on the team on a Sunday morning.
