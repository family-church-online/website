Sport offers a great platform to develop and support communities. 489 is an annual hockey clinic that brings together national and provincial players and coaches who are passionate about ministry to high school athletes. 489 afternoon sessions is an offshoot of this initiative that gathers on Sunday afternoons to teach, encourage and disciple young sports people and their families.
