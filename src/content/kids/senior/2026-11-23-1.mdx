  - heading: "ACTIVITY - TRUTH or LIE quiz"
    content: "1.\ 

      • Statement: “Peter asked Jesus, ‘Lord, we don’t know where you are going, so how can we know the way?’”

      • Answer: LIE

      • Explanation: It was Thomas who asked this question in John 14:5.


      2.\ 

      • Statement: “Jesus said there are lots of different roads to get to God, and you can pick whichever one you like best.”

      • Answer: LIE

      • Explanation: Jesus said, “I am the way... No one comes to the Father except through me” (John 14:6). He is the one true path to God.


      3.

      • Statement: “Jesus never lies, and everything He says about God is completely real.”

      • Answer: TRUTH

      • Explanation: Jesus is the Truth. Unlike people who sometimes make mistakes or tell stories, we can always trust Jesus’ words.


      4.\ 

      • Statement: “Right before John 14:6, Jesus told His friends He was going to His Father’s house to prepare a place for them.”

      • Answer: TRUTH

      • Explanation: In John 14:2–3, Jesus promised to get a place ready for us in heaven and come back for us.


      5.\ 

      • Statement: “Jesus only helps us while we are alive on Earth, and that’s the end of it.”

      • Answer: LIE

      • Explanation: Jesus is the Life—He gives us abundant life right now and eternal life forever with God."
