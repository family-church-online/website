reflection: |
  There is a question that can empty a room of words. Not what do you do, or how have you been — we keep answers ready for those. This one is quieter and it travels deeper: do you like yourself? Sit down someone who looks entirely capable from the outside, a person who runs a business and raises a family and carries it all, ask them that, and notice how often the honest answer is a long silence.

  Where does the silence come from? It is built slowly, one voice at a time. A parent, tired and trying their best, whose corrections somehow land in a small child's ears as I am always in trouble. A teacher's you could do better. A first love who leaves without explaining. A workplace that keeps overlooking you. Layer settles on layer, until somewhere along the way you quietly accepted a verdict — not clever enough, not strong enough, not quite lovable — and you have been carrying it ever since as though it were simply the truth about you.

  But it was never the only voice, and it is not the truest one. There is Another Who was speaking before the parents and the teachers and the heartbreaks ever arrived — Who formed you in your mother's womb, Who saw every mistake you would make and still chose you. He does not read out your failures. He calls you loved, chosen, worthy, a work of His own hands, His child.

  Two voices are speaking over your life. One has been talking for years and knows exactly where you are tender. The Other has been speaking since before you were born and has never once changed His mind about you. Everything you become will grow from the one you decide to believe.

lifeApplication: |
  The old verdict will speak up today — in the mirror, in a moment of comparison, in a small failure that somehow feels far bigger than it is. When it does, do not argue with it inside your own head, where it always wins. Answer it out loud with what God has already said: I am fearfully and wonderfully made; I am called by name; I belong to Him.

  Write one of those truths somewhere you will see it more than once today, and let it begin to wear a groove deeper than the old one.
prayer: |
  Father, You know the voices I have believed about myself for far too long.

  Thank You that You formed me on purpose and have never once regretted making me.

  Where the world has told me I am not enough, let me hear You call me loved, chosen, and Yours.

  Quiet the old accusations, and teach me to see myself the way You see me.

  In Jesus' name, Amen.
