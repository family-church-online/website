reflection: |
  Down in the blue, a diver learns a strange and wonderful skill. Weigh yourself just right, and you neither sink toward the dark below nor drift up toward the light above. You simply hang there, weightless, held. It is the nearest thing to flying — like lying in a hammock that sways with the sea, tons of water all around you and yet nothing pressing on you at all. The whole ocean is carrying you, and you do not have to do a single thing.

  It is not always so. Get the balance wrong and you are working every moment — kicking hard so you don't sink, pushing down so you don't rise to the surface, arms and legs never still. And all the while your air is running out, spent on the sheer labour of staying level. Many of us live like that, exhausted, certain that if we ever stopped striving we would drop like a stone.

  Paul found a different way to float. "My grace is sufficient for you," he heard, "for My power is made perfect in weakness" — and so he learned to boast in the very frailty he once feared. Here is the balance our tired hearts are aching for: to know honestly how weak we are, and to know just as honestly how strong God is, and to let those two truths hold us between them. When you stop insisting you must keep yourself afloat, you discover you were never the one doing it. You are not sinking. You are not floating. You are held — and the moment you feel it, the striving falls away and something like peace settles over you, quiet and deep as the sea.

lifeApplication: |
  Think of the one situation where you are kicking hardest right now — the problem you keep churning over at three in the morning, sure that if you relax your grip for a moment it will all go under. What would it be to go still there, just for today?

  Not to give up, but to stop thrashing; to say, "I cannot hold this level on my own, and I am going to trust that I am already being held." You may be astonished to find that you do not sink. The everlasting arms were underneath you the whole time, and rest was always closer than you thought.
prayer: |
  Lord, I am so tired from trying to keep myself afloat. My strength is nearly gone.

  Thank You that Your power is made perfect in my weakness, not in my pretending.

  Underneath me are Your everlasting arms; teach me to rest my whole weight there.

  Where I have been thrashing to stay in control, help me to go still and trust You.

  Hold me in that place of peace, neither sinking nor striving, simply carried by You.

  In Jesus' name, Amen.
