  ref: "John 11:9"
  text: "Jesus answered, 'Are there not twelve hours in the day? If anyone walks in the day, he does not stumble, because he sees the light of this world."
reflection: |
  Think of someone working out in a field as the sun climbs and falls — a laborer who has only the daylight to get the work done. There are exactly twelve hours of light. Not fourteen, not ten. No one comes along to cut the day short at the eleventh hour; while the light lasts, the work goes on, and when the light is gone, the work is simply finished for the day.
  
  Jesus said this when His friends were begging Him not to go to Jerusalem. They were afraid He would be killed before His time, and He answered them almost gently: "Are there not twelve hours in the day?" As if to say — no one shortens My day. I have exactly the time I was given, and I will use all of it.
  
  There is deep comfort folded into that, and a quiet challenge too. The comfort is that our days are measured — appointed before we were born, written in His book when there was not yet one of them. No one is cut off at the eleventh hour. No illness, no accident, no enemy takes us a single moment before our work is done. But the challenge is just as real: the light does not last forever. There is work to do while it is still day.
  
  Your life is not a frantic race against a clock that might stop early, and it is not an endless afternoon you can afford to waste. It is twelve hours of daylight, measured out by a faithful God — long enough for everything He made you to do, and not one hour longer. The work was handed to you before you were ever born. So walk in the light while you have it: without fear, because no one can shorten your day, and without delay, because the light will not last.

lifeApplication: |
  Hold both halves of this today. If you have been living afraid — bracing for the day to be cut short, anxious about how much time you or the people you love have left — let the measured day settle you. No one is taken before their twelve hours are complete.
  
  And if you have been living as though the light will never fade — putting off the thing He has asked of you, assuming there will always be more time — let the falling sun wake you up. Who is the one person you could tell about Jesus while it is still day? What has He been asking of you that you keep postponing? Live this day as what it is: a measured, numbered gift, long enough for its purpose. Then do the work, and leave the length of the day to Him.
prayer: |
  Father, thank You that my days are measured by You and written in Your book before one of them came to be.
  
  Thank You that no one can cut my day short — I have exactly the time You appointed.
  
  Forgive me for living either in fear of running out of time or as though time would never run out.
  
  Show me the work You have given me for today, and the person You want me to tell about You.
  
  Help me to walk in the light while I have it, without fear and without delay.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
