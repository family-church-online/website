reflection: |
  There is a scroll resting in a hand — held out, deliberately, in the right hand of the One on the throne. And there is a detail easy to rush past: it is covered in writing on both sides. Not the neat single face you would expect of an official document, but text spilling over onto the back, as though whoever wrote it had more to say than the page could hold. In an old prophet's vision a scroll like this was filled with words of lamentation and mourning and woe — and even that could not contain it all.
  
  A scroll written front and back is a scroll straining under its own contents. It is God's plan, His verdict on all that has gone wrong, His faithfulness to every promise He has ever sworn — too full to fit on one side. And He does not hide it away. He holds it out in His right hand, in the open, under His own authority, waiting.
  
  It is a strange comfort to know that history is not a blank page waiting to see what evil will write on it. It is already written, on both sides, held in a hand strong enough to keep it. Nothing that grieves you today is off the edge of the page. Every tear and every injustice and every unkept-looking promise is accounted for in the writing God is holding — and He has not let go of it.

lifeApplication: "When your life feels like a story running out of control, remember there is a fuller account than the one you can see, held in a steady hand. Bring the part that feels most senseless — the loss, the delay, the thing that seems written off — and place it on the page God is already holding. You are not writing this story alone into the dark."
prayer: |
  Father, thank You that the whole of history rests in Your right hand and not in the hands of evil.
  
  When my days feel unaccounted for, remind me they are written in Your book.
  
  Help me trust the fullness of a plan I can only see one side of.
  
  Nothing that grieves me is off the edge of Your page — thank You for holding it all.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
