  ref: "Revelation 6:3–4"
  text: "When he opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, 'Come!' And out came another horse, bright red. Its rider was permitted to take peace from the earth, so that people should slay one another, and he was given a great sword."
reflection: |
  Tonight, in all likelihood, you will lock your door and go to sleep. The house will be quiet. There will be no sirens, no scramble for shelter, no sound of anything breaking in the dark. You will probably not even notice the peace of it — we rarely do. Ordinary, unremarkable safety is the kind of gift we only see once it is gone.
  
  But that peace is more fragile than we let ourselves believe. It is on loan. Across history and across the world, countless people have gone to sleep in a peaceful house and woken to find the peace lifted off the earth overnight. The red horse rides out and takes it, and suddenly there is no corner of the world left untouched by conflict.
  
  We build so much of our calm on circumstances staying exactly as they are — the safe home, the steady job, the body that still works, the people still here. And when one of those is taken, our peace goes with it, because it was resting on the wrong thing all along.
  
  There is another kind of peace. Jesus was careful to say it was not like the world's. The world's peace is the absence of trouble, and it can be removed in a single night. His peace is His own presence, and no horseman can lift it off the earth, because it was never the world's to give or to take. It holds in the quiet house, and it holds when the sirens sound.

lifeApplication: |
  Tonight, when you lock your door, pause for just a moment and notice the peace you usually walk straight past. Let it make you grateful — and let it make you honest. Ask yourself where your sense of calm is actually resting. On your circumstances staying exactly as they are? On the people and props that could be gone by next year?
  
  That kind of peace will not survive a hard season, and hard seasons come to everyone. So begin, today, to move the weight of your peace off your circumstances and onto Christ Himself. Bring Him the one thing you are most afraid of losing, and let Him be the ground you stand on — the only ground that cannot be taken from you.
prayer: |
  Thank You for the ordinary, unnoticed peace of this day, and for every quiet night I have taken for granted.
  
  Forgive me for building my calm on things that can be taken, instead of on You.
  
  When my circumstances shake, hold me in the peace that only You can give.
  
  Teach me to hand You the thing I am most afraid of losing.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
