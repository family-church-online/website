reflection: >-
  They had worked the lake their whole lives. All night they had let the nets
  down and drawn them up empty, again and again, until the sky greyed and their
  arms ached and there was nothing left to do but wash the nets and go home with
  nothing. And then a carpenter — a man of sawdust and workbenches, not water —
  told them to push out again and let the nets down one more time. You can
  almost feel the tiredness in the reply: but at Your word, we will try.


  The catch nearly sank the boats. Nets strained and tore, fish spilled over the
  sides, men shouted for help. And there, in the middle of that ridiculous,
  glittering, flapping abundance, a seasoned fisherman fell to his knees on the
  slippery deck and said the strangest thing: "Depart from me. I am a sinful
  man." It sounds like humility. It even sounds noble. But listen again to what
  a man is really saying when he tells God to keep His distance because he is
  too broken to be near.


  He is saying: You are not strong enough to handle what I am. He is dressing
  his pride up as unworthiness. True humility does not push Jesus away in the
  name of our brokenness; that only insults His power to save. True humility
  kneels down in the mess and says the opposite: "I am a sinful man — and yet,
  because You are who You are, You can do something even with this. So here I
  am. Take me."

lifeApplication: >-
  There is a way of feeling unworthy that quietly keeps God at arm's length. "I'm
  too much of a mess. He couldn't want this part of me." It can even feel like
  reverence. But underneath, it is a refusal to believe He is strong enough for
  the worst of you.


  Bring Him the very thing you are most ashamed of — the habit, the failure, the
  corner of your heart you assume is beyond help. Do not tell Him to depart from
  it. Kneel down in the middle of it and hand it over. He is not searching for
  people who have their houses in order. He is searching for people who will let
  Him near enough to do what only He can do.
prayer: >-
  Lord, I confess the false humility that tells You to keep Your distance because
  I am too broken.

  Forgive the pride hidden inside it — the doubt that You are strong enough for
  the worst of me.

  I kneel down here in the middle of my mess and hand it to You.

  Do what only You can do with a sinful man who belongs to You.

  Thank You that You came into the world for sinners, and that I am never too
  much for You.

  In Jesus' name, Amen.
