reflection: |
  Sit still beside a fire in the bush long enough and you slowly realise you are not the audience but a latecomer to a performance already well underway. The frogs start it, low and insistent. Then the insects layer themselves in, then the night birds, until the darkness is thick and busy with sound on every side.
  
  And when the thunder begins to roll in from far off, the creatures do not fall silent. They answer. They lift their voices louder, not quieter, because they knew before you did that rain was coming, and they long for it even more than you do — having no tap to open, no other hope but the sky. The whole living darkness leans toward the storm and sings.
  
  Around the throne of God there are living creatures who never cease, day or night, to say, "Holy, holy, holy." Not a song they switch on for an audience, but an unbroken answer to who He is. The prophets heard it too — the whole earth is full of His glory. And here is the thing we so often miss: we come to worship imagining we are beginning it, as though the music only starts when we decide to sing. But the frogs knew. The heavens have been declaring His glory since before we drew our first breath. To worship is not to start a sound. It is to finally join one that has never once stopped.

lifeApplication: "You do not have to work up worship today as if from nothing. It is already happening — in the birds outside your window, in the vast wheeling sky, around a throne you cannot yet see. Your part is simply to add your voice to a chorus already in full song. So hum it over the dishes. Whisper it on the commute. Let a grateful heart answer God in the middle of an ordinary day, and know that you have joined something ancient and unceasing and far larger than yourself."
prayer: |
  Holy, holy, holy are You, Lord God Almighty — worthy of every voice in heaven and on earth.
  
  Forgive me for the days I have stayed silent while all creation sang around me.
  
  Teach me to notice Your glory in ordinary places and to answer it with a grateful heart.
  
  Let my life today add its small voice to the song that never ceases before Your throne.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
