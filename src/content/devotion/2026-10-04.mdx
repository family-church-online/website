  text: Steadfast love and faithfulness meet; righteousness and peace kiss each other.
reflection: |
  Anyone who has watched enough rugby remembers a tackle so heavy it silenced the whole ground — the kind of collision where two forces meet with such impact that everyone feels it in their chest, even from the stands. Some truths land like that. They are not gentle facts you file away; they hit you full in the body and leave you sitting with the force of them.
  
  Here is one built to land with exactly that weight. We sometimes squirm at the claim that Jesus is the only way — it can sound narrow, even harsh. But stand inside the search of heaven for one moment: a call goes out for anyone worthy to put the world right, and in all creation not a single other name answers. Now ask the harder pair of questions. How could God be just if He simply shrugged at sin and let it slide? And how could God be merciful unless that sin was punished somewhere other than in us?
  
  The only place those two demands could ever meet is a cross. There, God's justice fell in full — and His mercy held us safe — in the same single act. Righteousness and peace did not merely shake hands; they kissed. That is why there is no other way: not because God is stingy with the doors He opens, but because only One door lets justice and mercy embrace at the same time. Let that hit you like a ton of bricks. He put all of this in place because He adores you.

lifeApplication: "If the phrase \"the only way\" has ever felt like a hard, narrow rule to you, let today reframe it as the astonishing lengths God went to so that justice and mercy could both be satisfied over you. Let that truth land with real weight — enough to stir awe, not just agreement. Then live this week like someone who has been adored at that cost: quicker to forgive, slower to condemn, and unashamed to point others to the one door where mercy waits."
prayer: |
  Gracious Father, I am in awe of what You have done.
  
  Thank You that at the cross Your justice and Your mercy embraced in one act.
  
  Forgive me for the times I have found the cross narrow instead of glorious.
  
  Let this truth hit me with full weight and set me on fire for You.
  
  You are worthy, Lord Jesus — I want to be ready for the day You come. Amen.
