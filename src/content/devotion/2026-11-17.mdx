reflection: |
  On a clear night, far from the streetlights, the stars come out in their thousands. The same constellations hang there that your grandparents looked up at, and their grandparents before them — fixed, dependable, turning slowly through the seasons exactly as they always have. There is a quiet comfort in that constancy. Whatever has gone wrong in the day, the sky at least keeps its appointments. The sun will rise tomorrow. The moon will do what it has always done.
  
  And then the prophet speaks of a day when even that fails — the stars withholding their light, the sun dark at its rising, the moon gone quiet, the very floor of the universe shaken out of its place. These words were set down nearly nine hundred years before the last book of the Bible was ever written, and yet they describe the same day, with the same weight. Across the long centuries, through prophet after prophet, and from the lips of Jesus Himself, one truth keeps surfacing: there is a day coming that all of history is moving toward.
  
  It is tempting to treat that day as a distant rumour, something meant for other people in other times. But the dependable sky is only a borrowed constancy; it was never meant to be our final security. The day of the Lord is not a vague threat hanging over the world — it is a fixed appointment, woven through the whole of Scripture. The only question that finally matters is not whether it will come, but which side of it we will be found on.

lifeApplication: "We plan as though time were endless — next year, when things settle; one day, when I get round to it. But no one is promised the sunrise, and the things we keep postponing with God are usually the ones that matter most. You do not need to live in fear of that coming day; you need only to be ready for it. Today, settle the one question that outranks all your other plans: are you hidden in Christ? If you are, let that steady you more deeply than any dependable sky. If you are not sure, do not leave it for a tomorrow that may not be yours to spend."
prayer: |
  Lord, You hold all of history in Your hands, and a day is coming that nothing can delay.
  
  Thank You that I need not fear that day if I am found in You.
  
  Forgive me for living as though time were mine to waste.
  
  Help me to settle what matters most today, and not to presume upon tomorrow.
  
  Make me ready, and make me grateful.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
