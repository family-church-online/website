reflection: |
  There is a world of difference between something torn from your hands and something you kneel down and set carefully in place. When a thing is snatched from you, your fingers are still grasping as it goes; you are the victim of a force stronger than you. But when you deliberately open your hands and lay something down, the whole meaning changes. It was never taken. It was given.

  We are tempted to picture the cross as the first kind of moment — as though Jesus were overpowered, a good man swept up in cruel events, reluctantly allowing the worst to happen to Him. But that is not the picture He gives of Himself. No one takes it from Me, He said. I lay it down of My own accord. He was not cornered. He was not a leaf caught in a current He could not resist. At any moment He had the authority to walk away, and He did not. He knelt down, in full freedom, and laid His life in place — like a shepherd stepping between the flock and the wolf, not because He was trapped, but because He would not leave us to the dark.

  Let that reshape how loved you understand yourself to be. You were not saved by an accident. You were not rescued by a reluctant God who got caught up in events beyond His control. Every step toward that hill was chosen, with you in view. The hands that could have held on to everything opened instead — for you.

lifeApplication: |
  Somewhere beneath the surface, many of us suspect we are barely tolerated by God — loved, perhaps, but grudgingly, the way you might do a favour you would rather avoid. The cross tells a different story. It was not wrung out of Him. He chose it, freely, knowing your name and your failures, and He would choose it again.

  Today, let that truth settle the restless question of whether you are truly wanted. Walk into your ordinary day — the commute, the chores, the difficult conversation — as a person for whom the Son of God deliberately opened His hands. When the old accusation whispers that you are a burden, answer it with the fact of a life laid down on purpose, for you.
prayer: |
  Lord Jesus, thank You that You were never a victim of the cross but its willing King.

  No one took Your life from You — You laid it down, of Your own accord, for me.

  Forgive me for imagining You as reluctant, when in truth You chose me with Your eyes wide open.

  Let the freedom of Your sacrifice heal the part of me that fears I am only barely loved.

  Help me to walk today as one for whom You gladly opened Your hands.

  In Jesus' name, Amen.
