reflection: |
  There is a particular thing a parent says when they watch someone they love begin to walk toward something that will wound them. I cannot stand here and watch you do this. I know how badly it is going to hurt you, and I will fight to keep you safe from it — even if you do not want me to. It is not said in anger. It is said because love cannot bear to stand by and watch.
  
  Most of us long for exactly that kind of love, whether or not we ever had it. We want someone who will step in, who will not shrug and let us destroy ourselves, who will fight for us.
  
  We are often taught to picture God as endlessly gentle — and He is gentle. But a love that is only ever mild is not the strongest love there is. The strongest love has teeth. It gets angry at what is killing you. When Scripture speaks of God's wrath against evil, it can frighten us, until we realise what it is: the fierce side of the same love that bends down to carry us the way a parent carries a child.
  
  His anger burns against everything that is destroying the people He made. That is not the opposite of His love for you. It is the measure of it. The One who fights so fiercely against the evil in the world is fighting that fiercely for you.

lifeApplication: |
  Is there something in your life right now that you know, deep down, is leading you somewhere painful — and are you quietly resenting the people who love you enough to say so? Their refusal to stay silent may be the very mercy you need.
  
  And perhaps you have carried a picture of God as distant, as someone who looks at your struggles and shrugs. Lay that picture down. He is not standing at a safe distance watching you walk into harm. He is fighting for you — and the safest thing you can do is to stop running from the One whose anger burns on your behalf, and take refuge in Him instead.
prayer: |
  Thank You that Your love for me is not mild or indifferent, but fierce enough to fight for me.
  
  Forgive me for the times I have walked toward what wounds me and resented those who warned me.
  
  Where I have believed You were distant, show me that You have been fighting for me all along.
  
  Teach me to run to You as my refuge rather than away from You in fear.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
