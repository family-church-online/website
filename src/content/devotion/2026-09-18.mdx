  text: "And behold, an angel of the Lord stood next to him, and a light shone in the cell. He struck Peter on the side and woke him, saying, 'Get up quickly.' And the chains fell off his hands."
reflection: |
  He was asleep — which is remarkable, because he was to be executed in the morning. Chained by both wrists to a soldier on either side, deep inside a prison, on the last night anyone expected him to have. And still he slept. Then, without warning, light. An angel stood over him and struck him on the side — not a gentle nudge but a blow, a clap hard enough to wake a condemned man — and said, "Get up quickly." The chains simply fell from his hands. "Dress yourself. Put on your sandals." Half-certain he was dreaming, he obeyed, and they walked: past the first guard, past the second, up to the great iron gate that opened into the city — and the gate swung open by itself. He stepped out into the cold night air, a free man.
  
  And then the most human detail of all. He hurried to a house where his friends were praying for him, and knocked. The servant girl who came to the door was so overcome with joy at his voice that she ran back inside to tell everyone — and forgot to open the door. So there he stood, newly delivered from death, left knocking in the street while the household argued about whether it could possibly be true.
  
  God kept him that night. But we should be honest: later in his life, the keeping looked different, and he did finally give his life for his faith. So this was never a promise that we escape every hardship. It was something deeper — a promise of presence. No prison, no chain, no locked gate can finally hold the one God has chosen to keep. Sometimes He opens the gate and walks us out. Always He is there in the cell. His keeping is not the guarantee that trouble never comes; it is the certainty that nothing can hold us which He has not allowed, and nothing can claim us which He has already redeemed.

lifeApplication: "What is the cell you are in tonight — the fear you cannot shake, the situation locked tight, the waiting that has gone on too long? You may be kept through it rather than sprung from it. But you are kept. The God for whom iron gates swing open of their own accord is with you in the dark, and He has not lost His keys. Lie down tonight like someone who knows Whose hands hold them, and sleep."
prayer: |
  Father, thank You that no chain, no prison, and no locked gate can hold the one You have chosen to keep.
  
  Thank You that even when You do not remove my trouble, You are with me in the middle of it.
  
  Forgive me for the nights I have lain awake as though everything depended on me.
  
  Help me to rest in Your keeping, certain that You hold the keys to every gate I fear.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
