reflection: |
  Once a year the high priest put on garments heavy with meaning, and over his chest he wore a small square pouch stitched with twelve stones — one for each tribe, each set in gold. The first stone was a carnelian, red as blood. The last was a jasper, clear as a diamond lifted to the light. And so, every time he walked into the Holy Place, he carried the names of a whole people over his heart — not in a ledger, not on a list, but pressed against the beating centre of him, where a person carries what he loves.
  
  Here is something easy to miss. Those two stones — the first and the last, blood-red and diamond-clear — are the very colours the throne of God is wrapped in. The One seated on the throne wears the colours of the priest who carries His people. It is as if heaven itself is dressed in the reminder that we are held.
  
  And we are held not only at our beginning, when faith is fresh and everything feels close, nor only at our end, when the story is finished and complete. We are carried now, in the long, unresolved middle — the space between the first stone and the last, where we actually live. Our great High Priest bears us over His heart and has engraved us on the palms of His hands. He is Himself the First and the Last, and everything in between — the half-finished, the not-yet-healed, the still-waiting — is carried, right now, over the heart of the One who does not put us down.

lifeApplication: "Where are you in the middle today? Perhaps in a season that feels stalled — a prayer still unanswered, a healing still coming, a chapter that refuses to resolve. Hear this and let it steady you: you are not carried only at the start and the finish. You are held in the middle, over His heart, right now. Nothing about your in-between is forgotten by the One who bears your name into the very presence of God."
prayer: |
  Father, thank You that You carry my name over Your heart and have engraved me on the palms of Your hands.
  
  Thank You that I am held not only at my beginning and my end, but in every unresolved middle.
  
  Forgive me for feeling forgotten when the waiting is long.
  
  Help me to rest today in the knowledge that You do not put me down.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
