reflection: |
  The breakfast is finished. The fish is gone, the fire is burning low, and into the quiet comes a question — gentle, and yet somehow the hardest question Peter has ever been asked. "Do you love Me?" Once, this same man had stood in a lamplit room and boasted that even if every other disciple abandoned Jesus, he never would. He had loved louder than all of them. Now, on the far side of a rooster's crow, the big words will not come.

  There are two loves in the language they are speaking. There is the towering, self-giving love that lays down its whole life — and there is the humbler love of a friend, a brother, warm but honest about its limits. Jesus asks for the first. And Peter, so thoroughly found out, will not pretend anymore. "Lord, You know that I love You" — but he uses the smaller word. I cannot claim the greater one. Not after what I did. You know exactly how much, and how little, I have.

  And here is the wonder of it. Jesus does not turn away the smaller, honest love. The third time, He Himself reaches down and uses Peter's humbler word, meeting him precisely where he truly is — and still, every single time, He answers, "Feed My sheep." He would far rather have our honest, ordinary love than our boasted, inflated one. He meets us at the love we actually have, and then, in His own strength, grows it into the love we could never have manufactured on our own.

lifeApplication: |
  It is tempting to tell God a bigger love than we actually feel — to sing the loud words, to promise towering devotion, and to hope quietly that He does not check. But He always knows the true measure of our hearts, and He is not looking for a performance.

  So tell Him the truth today. If your love is small and tired and honest, say so: "Lord, You know that I love You — this much, and no more than this." That honest confession is not the end of your love; it is the very place where He takes hold of it and begins to grow it into something you could never produce yourself.
prayer: |
  Lord, You know everything; You know the true size of my love, and I will stop pretending it is bigger.

  Forgive me for the loud promises I have made and failed to keep.

  Thank You that You do not reject my small and honest love but reach down and take hold of it.

  Meet me where I truly am, and grow in me the self-giving love I cannot manufacture on my own.

  And as You do, let me hear Your call to go and feed Your sheep.

  In Jesus' name, Amen.
