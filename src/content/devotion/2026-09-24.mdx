  text: "They cast their crowns before the throne, saying, 'Worthy are You, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power, for You created all things, and by Your will they existed and were created."
reflection: |
  Picture the ones seated on the thrones that ring the great throne — real people, given an honour almost too large to take in, crowns resting on their heads, an authority most of us could never imagine holding. You might expect them to sit very still and savour it, to guard the dignity of the place.
  
  Instead, again and again, they do a strange and beautiful thing. Every time honour rises toward the One seated at the centre, they come down from their seats, lift the crowns from their own heads, and lay them on the ground at His feet. The gesture needs no words, but if it had them they would be these: thank You for the honour You gave me — but it was always Yours. I am not worthy of it. You take the glory.
  
  Whatever crown you wear was placed on your head by a hand that was not your own. The gift you are proud of, the position you have worked for, the quiet satisfaction of a thing done well — every bit of it was given. And the ones around the throne have discovered the freedom hidden inside that truth. There is a lightness that comes only when you stop guarding your crown and lay it down: suddenly there is nothing left to defend, nothing to lose, nothing to prove — only the deep joy of handing it back to the One it belonged to all along.

lifeApplication: "Somewhere in your life is a crown you clutch a little too tightly. A reputation you defend. Credit you are quietly desperate to receive. A gift you have started to think of as your own achievement. Today, try laying it down on purpose. Say thank You for it, and then hand it back — give God the glory out loud, hold the gift open-handed, let someone else be praised. You will be surprised how light your head feels without a crown you were never meant to guard."
prayer: |
  Lord, everything I have and everything I am came from Your hand, and to You it belongs.
  
  Forgive me for wearing my gifts like achievements and clutching at glory that is Yours alone.
  
  Teach me the freedom of the lowered crown, the joy of giving the honour back to You.
  
  Let my whole life be an offering laid gladly before Your throne.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
