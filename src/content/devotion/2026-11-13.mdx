reflection: |
  You have felt it, even if you would never have called it worship. A hundred thousand voices in a stadium chanting a single name until the sound becomes a physical thing you can feel in your chest. An anthem sung by sixty thousand strangers, and without deciding to, the hair on your arms stands up and your throat tightens. Something in you is switched on — lifted out of yourself, swept up into something far bigger than you. For a few seconds you are not a spectator; you are part of one enormous voice.
  
  We were built for that. Not for the team or the anthem — those are only the echoes — but for the thing the echo points to. Listen to the song of that final crowd and count the words they pour out: blessing, glory, wisdom, thanksgiving, honour, power, might. Seven of them, heaped one upon another, the number of fullness — worship that is complete, holding nothing back. Angels, elders, strange living creatures, and a multitude beyond counting, all caught up in one voice before the throne. This is creation doing at last the very thing it was made to do.
  
  It can feel strange, even uncomfortable, that God would make us for His own glory — because when a man demands to be worshipped, everything in us recoils and says, no, that is not right. And we are correct to recoil: he is not worthy of it. But that very instinct is the proof. The reason it is wrong for us to be worshipped is that there is One for whom it is entirely right — One who is perfect, who deserves it, for whom receiving worship is simply the truth being told. And when we finally give it to Him, something deep in us awakens with joy. The surge you felt in that crowd was a rumour of home. You were made to worship, and there is nothing better a human being can do.

lifeApplication: "The next time your hair stands up — at a song, a scene, a swell of sound in a crowd — don't just enjoy the feeling and let it pass. Follow it home. Let it remind you what that longing is actually for, and turn it, even quietly, toward the One who deserves it. You do not have to wait for a perfect mood or a grand occasion to worship. Breathe, and let the breath praise Him. The joy you keep chasing in lesser crowds is waiting, full and complete, in the presence of the only One truly worthy of the song."
prayer: |
  Lord, You are worthy — of blessing and glory and wisdom and thanksgiving and honour and power and might.
  
  Thank You that You made me to worship, and that worshipping You is the truest thing I can do.
  
  Forgive me for pouring out my awe on lesser things and leaving You unsung.
  
  Awaken joy in me as I turn my heart toward You today.
  
  Let every breath You give me become praise.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
