  ref: "2 Corinthians 12:9"
  text: "But He said to me, 'My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is made perfect in weakness."
reflection: |
  Do you remember the long walk down the corridor to the headmaster's office? That ice-cold feeling spreading down your back, the knowledge that you are caught and there is no excusing it, no lying your way out, no hiding. They know who you are and they know what you did, and you are going. Every step toward that door feels longer than the last.
  
  And do you remember the one small mercy on that walk — when a friend had been caught alongside you, and you made the journey together? Nothing about the punishment changed. You would both face exactly the same thing. But somewhere along that corridor you caught each other's eye and very nearly laughed, because it is a different thing entirely to walk in with someone beside you than to walk in alone.
  
  There was a man who carried a thorn he could not shake, a private suffering he never names for us. On his good days he could bear it; on his bad days he broke and begged God to take it away. Three times, from the depths, he pleaded for it to be removed. And God's answer was not what he asked for. The thorn stayed. But the answer came: "My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is made perfect in weakness."
  
  That is the heart of it. God's reply to our weakness is rarely the removal of the hard thing we keep praying about. It is His nearness in the middle of it. You do not have to be strong. You do not have to overpower what you are facing. You only have to let Him walk in beside you — and the weaker you are, the more plainly His strength will show through you.

lifeApplication: |
  Name the thorn you keep begging God to remove — the chronic pain, the difficult person, the weakness you are ashamed of, the fear you cannot seem to outrun. It is right to ask Him to take it away, and sometimes He will. But hear the other answer too. Sometimes He does not remove the thing; He walks the corridor with you.
  
  Stop waiting for the thorn to vanish before you are willing to live. Do not put your obedience, your joy, your service on hold until the hard thing is gone. Let His grace be enough for today — not for the whole imagined future at once, just for today — and take the next step knowing you are not walking in alone.
prayer: |
  Lord Jesus, thank You that You do not send me down the hard corridor by myself — You walk in beside me.
  
  Here is my thorn; You know exactly what it is, and I lay it before You again.
  
  If You choose not to remove it, then be my strength inside it.
  
  Thank You that my weakness is the very place where Your power is seen most clearly.
  
  Let me never believe the lie that I am facing this alone.
  
  Your grace is enough for me today.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
