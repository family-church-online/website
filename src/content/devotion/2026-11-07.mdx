  ref: "Mark 4:38"
  text: "But He was in the stern, asleep on the cushion. And they woke Him and said to Him, 'Teacher, do You not care that we are perishing?"
reflection: |
  A squall drops onto the lake without warning. One moment the water is calm; the next, waves are breaking over the sides of the boat and water is rising around the men's feet. These are seasoned fishermen — they have known rough water before — and even they are bailing and shouting and certain this is the end.
  
  And in the stern of the boat, on a cushion, Jesus is asleep. Not pretending. Genuinely, peacefully asleep, while grown men panic around Him. They shake Him awake with the question that fear always asks: "Teacher, do You not care that we are perishing?"
  
  Here is what their terror could not let them see. Not one man in that boat was going to drown that night. The gospel had not yet gone out through their mouths. The church had not yet been built on their witness. Every one of them still had work to do that the world could not yet do without them — and so a storm on a small lake was never going to have the final word.
  
  If you belong to Jesus, you are guarded until you have finished what He sent you to do. That does not mean the absence of storms — these men were soaked and shaking and sure they would die. It means the storm does not get to write your ending. He does. The waves felt like the end, but the One asleep in the stern had already decided they were not.

lifeApplication: |
  You may be in a storm of your own right now — an illness, a crisis, a fear that keeps you awake and bailing water in the dark. The temptation is to believe the storm is in charge, that it will take whatever it wants from you, that God has gone quiet or fallen asleep on you.
  
  He has not. The same Jesus who was at peace in the stern is in your boat, and your life is not at the mercy of the wind. He will not lose you before your work is done. That truth will not calm every wave this week, but it can calm you in the middle of them. Let the fear of being overwhelmed give way, little by little, to the quiet confidence that the One who began a good work in you intends to finish it.
prayer: |
  Lord Jesus, thank You that no storm gets to decide my ending — You do.
  
  Thank You that I am guarded until I have finished the work You gave me to do.
  
  Forgive me for the times I have believed You were asleep or did not care.
  
  When the waves rise and fear takes hold, remind me that You are in the boat.
  
  Give me Your quiet confidence in the middle of what frightens me.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
