reflection: |
  You have made the promise before. Standing on the touchline while your child plays, you feel the old heat climb into your chest — the referee has missed it again, plainly missed it — and everything in you wants to shout. And you remember last week, when you swore you would not be that parent again. You truly meant it. Yet here you are, hearing your own voice carry across the field, and afterward comes the quiet, familiar sting: I did it again.

  There is a picture in Scripture for exactly this. A man looks at his own face in a mirror, sees it plainly, and then walks away and immediately forgets what he looks like. That is what it is to hear the truth and not live it — to nod along on Sunday and be unchanged by Wednesday, to read the words and leave them lying on the page.

  But notice what we are actually asked to do. Not be perfect. Practice. He does not say get every reaction right and then you may belong to Me. He says practice these things — the language of something done daily, clumsily at first, over and over, the way anyone learns something hard and worth the learning.

  That is a mercy hiding in plain sight. It means the failure on the touchline is not the end of the story; it is part of the practising. It means you are allowed to begin again tomorrow, and the day after that. He was never asking for a flawless performance. He was after a life quietly, stubbornly reshaped by actually doing what you have heard.

lifeApplication: |
  Think of one thing you have heard many times and still have not done — the apology you keep rehearsing but never make, the patience you mean to show and don't, the small habit you keep meaning to start. Do not try to master it. Simply practice it once today, on purpose, and expect that you will need to practice it again tomorrow.

  Growth is not a single triumphant moment; it is the willingness to begin again the morning after you failed.
prayer: |
  Lord, too often I look into Your word, see myself clearly, and then walk away and forget.

  Thank You that You never asked me to be perfect, only to keep practicing.

  Give me the humility to begin again after every failure without giving up on myself, because You have not given up on me.

  Make me a doer of Your word today, in one real and ordinary way.

  In Jesus' name, Amen.
