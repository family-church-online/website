reflection: |
  There is a particular sound a trumpet makes at a great occasion — bright, clean, deliberate, cutting straight through the murmur of a crowd. The moment it sounds, everyone knows what to do. Conversations stop. People rise to their feet and turn toward the door. A trumpet is never an accident or a stray noise; it is a summons, an announcement, the sign that someone of importance is about to arrive and that everything from this point will unfold in order.
  
  John sees seven angels standing before God, and into the hands of each one a trumpet is placed — one by one, in sequence, nothing rushed, nothing random. We tend to imagine the end of all things as pandemonium, a world tipping into pure chaos. But that is not what John sees. He sees structure. He sees seven angels, seven trumpets, handed out with deliberate care, each waiting its turn. Even here, at the very edge of final judgment, there is unmistakable order.
  
  That ought to change how we read the trembling world around us, and our own trembling lives within it. What looks from the ground like everything spiralling out of control is, from heaven's vantage, meticulously held. Nothing has slipped God's grip. The same hand that gives out the trumpets one by one is the hand that holds our days — and when life feels like noise and confusion, the trumpet reminds us there is a King arriving, and He is not improvising.

lifeApplication: "When your own life feels like chaos — a diagnosis you did not expect, a relationship coming apart, a week where nothing goes to plan — it is easy to believe that everything is unravelling and no one is holding the thread. But the God who hands out the trumpets in careful order is the same God ordering your days, even the ones that feel random and cruel. Today, name the situation that feels most out of control to you, and hand it deliberately back to Him. You are not watching a world that has slipped its moorings; you are living inside a story whose Author has never once lost the plot."
prayer: |
  Father, when my life feels like noise and confusion, remind me that You are never improvising.
  
  Thank You that nothing has slipped out of Your control, not even the things that frighten me.
  
  I hand You now the one situation that feels most chaotic in my life.
  
  Help me to trust the order of Your hand when I cannot yet see it.
  
  Let the sound of Your coming steady me today.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
