reflection: |
  There is an old way of welcoming a king. The crowd lines the road, people reach up and strip long palm branches from the trees, and they wave them overhead — a green, rustling sea of them — as the One they have been waiting for passes by. Some even lay the branches down in the dust for Him to ride over. On one such day, on a road into Jerusalem, people did exactly this, shouting a word that holds both praise and rescue in a single breath: Hosanna — save us now. For a moment they saw who He was, and they welcomed Him as King.
  
  Now lift your eyes to that final crowd before the throne, and there are the palm branches again — but no longer in a few hands on a single road. They are in the hands of a multitude from every nation, every tribe, every people, every language, all waving, all crying the same cry: salvation belongs to our God. What began with a handful on a dusty road, and then faltered within a week, has become at the last a harvest gathered from the whole earth.
  
  And here is the part that searches us. If that crowd is drawn from every people on earth, then it holds the ones we find hard to love. The group that hurt us. The kind of people we have quietly hardened our hearts against, the faces we switch past on the screen with a sigh. Some of them are there in white, palm branches in hand, singing beside us. The grace that reached us reached further than our grudges, and it gathered in the very people we had written off. If He has made room for them, then we are being asked to make room for them too — starting now, on this side of the throne.

lifeApplication: "Bring to mind, honestly, the group or the person you have hardened against — the ones you would be surprised, even reluctant, to meet in that crowd. Then picture a palm branch in their hand and a white robe on their shoulders. Pray for them today, not through gritted teeth but asking God to do for them the very thing He has done for you. We cannot wave our branch beside theirs in eternity while refusing to share the same room with them now. Love is the rehearsal, and heaven is coming to complete it."
prayer: |
  Lord, salvation belongs to You, and You alone are worthy of every branch raised and every voice lifted.
  
  Thank You that Your welcome runs wider than any road, gathering people from every nation on earth.
  
  Bring to mind the ones I find hard to love, and soften my heart toward them.
  
  Help me to pray for those who have hurt me, as You prayed for those who hurt You.
  
  Make room in me now for the people You have already made room for.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
