reflection: |
  On a certain evening each year, a school holds its farewell dance, and something surprising happens on the pavement outside before the music has even started. People gather at the roadside — parents, neighbours, strangers who simply heard it was happening. Cars ease up to the curb, doors open, and out steps a young woman in a gown she has imagined for months, a young man standing a little taller in his suit. And the crowd, who came for nothing but this, lets out a soft sound of delight. We are moved, and we hardly know why.
  
  It is not only an evening like that. A mountain does it to us. The sea at dusk. The face of a newborn. A sky thick with stars. Something in us leans toward beauty and falls quiet before it, as if we were made to. And we were. There is a reason a lovely thing can stop us where we stand.
  
  Every beautiful thing is a rumour of its Source. The One seated on the throne is described as having the appearance of jasper, clear as a held-up diamond, and carnelian, red as blood, with an emerald rainbow arched around Him — a glory so great that the ones who glimpsed it could not find words, and were left weak on their feet. Every earthly beauty that has ever taken your breath is a small spark thrown off from that fire. The ache that beauty stirs in us is not sentimental; it is homesickness. The loveliness we keep reaching for is a hint, pointing us home to the God who is Himself the beauty behind it all.

lifeApplication: "The next time something beautiful stops you today — a slant of light, a piece of music, a face you love — do not simply consume it and move on. Let it become a doorway. Follow the spark back to its Source and let your delight turn, even for a moment, into worship. Beauty was always meant to lead you somewhere: to the One who made it, and made you to be moved by it."
prayer: |
  Father, thank You that You are the source of every beautiful thing, and that every lovely moment is a whisper of Your glory.
  
  Forgive me for the times I have chased Your gifts and forgotten You, the Giver.
  
  Open my eyes today to the beauty around me, and let it lead me back to You.
  
  Fill me with awe for who You are, and let my wonder become worship.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
