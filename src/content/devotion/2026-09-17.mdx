  text: "Around the throne were twenty-four thrones, and seated on the thrones were twenty-four elders, clothed in white garments, with golden crowns on their heads."
reflection: |
  Picture a man who smells of fish. It is worked into the weave of his clothes and settled under his nails, and it does not scrub out. He is crouched by the water in the dry heat, scaling the day's catch — his supper, and his family's — his hands rough and split with salt. No one in his town would look at him twice. He is not learned. He is not wealthy. He is a working man, doing the only thing he has ever known how to do.
  
  And to a man like that comes the most staggering promise imaginable: a throne. A white robe. A crown of gold. A seat in the throne room of God Himself, and a share in His reign. Around the throne are elders robed in white with crowns upon their heads — and they are not distant, shining strangers. They are human beings. People who once smelled of fish and dust and ordinary labour, lifted into glory.
  
  This is what grace does. Heaven is not filled with the impressive and the qualified; it is filled with ordinary people raised up by a love that saw them when no one else did. The crown was never a wage paid to the deserving. It is a gift pressed into the hands of the beloved. If you have ever felt overlooked — too plain, too unimportant, your work too small to matter — then hear it: the God of heaven has a robe and a crown kept ready, and your name is on them.

lifeApplication: "Whatever ordinary, unnoticed life you are living — the work no one praises, the faithfulness no one sees, the quiet keeping-on that feels too small to count — live today as someone already honoured. You are not defined by how the world ranks you. You are a son or daughter destined for a crown you did not earn and cannot lose. Let that dignity change how you carry yourself through an ordinary Thursday."
prayer: |
  Father, thank You that You see the overlooked and lift up the lowly, and that grace, not merit, prepares a crown.
  
  Thank You that my ordinary life and unnoticed faithfulness are precious in Your sight.
  
  Forgive me for measuring my worth by how others rank me.
  
  Help me to walk today as one already honoured, robed and crowned by Your love.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
