reflection: |
  Hold a newborn and you are holding pure helplessness. The head must be cradled or it falls; the hands grasp at nothing; the whole of that small life depends utterly on someone else for warmth, for food, for every single thing. And it is beautiful. A room full of grown adults will go quiet and tender over a creature that can do absolutely nothing for itself.

  But no one leans over the cradle and hopes the child will still be lying there, helpless, in twenty years. We treasure the birth precisely because it is a beginning. The first breath is a starting line, not a finish. A child who never learned to lift her head, never rolled over, never took a wobbling step, would turn all that joy into heartache — not because we stopped loving her, but because we love her far too much to want her to stay where she began.

  To be born again is a wonder as real as any first breath. But it was never meant to be the whole of the Christian life — only its opening moment. The peace of knowing you are forgiven is not a place to lie down and stay; it is the ground you stand up from. His love for you is too great to leave you lying in the cradle. He means for you to grow.

lifeApplication: |
  It is possible to mistake the comfort of being saved for the whole journey — to settle into the cradle of "I'm forgiven" and never learn to walk. Ask yourself honestly: am I growing, or have I simply been lying still, content that I am no longer condemned?

  Then take one small step today. Learn one thing you did not know yesterday. Master one reaction that has always mastered you. You do not have to sprint; a newborn never does. But move. Growth, not comfort, is the sign that life is really there.
prayer: |
  Father, thank You for the new birth You gave me, for the wonder of being made Your child.

  Forgive me for the times I have treated Your grace as a place to lie down rather than a place to rise from.

  Stir in me a hunger to grow, the way a healthy child hungers to move and reach and stand.

  Show me the next small step, and give me the strength to take it.

  Thank You that You love me too much to leave me where I began.

  In Jesus' name, Amen.
