reflection: |
  Watch a shepherd in the hill country of the Middle East and it overturns what you thought you knew. He does not drive the flock from behind, shouting and chasing. He walks out in front, and the sheep simply follow — because they know his voice. He has raised them from the time they were lambs. He has stood between them and the wolf, found them water when the heat was killing them, led them to grass when they were starving, gone searching for the one that strayed, and spoken low to them when they were afraid in the dark. They are his, bound to him by a hundred small rescues, and they would follow that voice anywhere.
  
  That is the oldest and most comforting picture we have of God. The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. Any shepherd in Israel's long history would have understood it instantly — they knew themselves to be sheep, led for forty years through a wilderness by a God who had first taught Moses the trade. It is an image we can rest inside. He is the Shepherd; we are the sheep. All is as it should be.
  
  And then this last vision tilts the whole thing on its head. The multitude looks up to find their Shepherd — and He is a Lamb. Not merely a lamb, but a Lamb who still carries the wounds of having been slaughtered for them. The One who leads the flock went first through the knife Himself. Here is what undoes me: the God of all the universe chose to become a sheep, so that He could be our Shepherd. He became one of us, suffered everything we suffer, let us see our sin and even work our hands in it, and then washed it clean with His own blood. And at the very end He is still bending near — guiding the countless flock to springs of living water, and wiping, one by one, every tear from their eyes. That is the Shepherd whose voice is worth following anywhere.

lifeApplication: "Whatever you feel your place is or isn't, whatever role you have or have not been given, the Shepherd leading you is the Lamb who was slain for you. His is the voice worth learning to recognise above all the others clamouring for your attention today. When fear rises, when the way grows dark, when the tears come, remember who walks out ahead of you — not a distant master driving you on, but the One who became a sheep to find you, bearing the scars to prove it. Follow that voice. And let the promise rest gently on you that a day is coming when His own hand will wipe the last tear from your eyes."
prayer: |
  Lord, You are the Shepherd who became a Lamb, who laid down Your life to find me.
  
  Thank You that You still bear the scars of what it cost to make me Your own.
  
  Teach me to know Your voice above all the others, and to follow You wherever You lead.
  
  Lead me to springs of living water, and carry me through the valleys I cannot see the end of.
  
  I rest in the promise that one day You will wipe away every tear from my eyes.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
