keyScripture:
  ref: "Revelation 5:2"
  text: "And I saw a mighty angel proclaiming with a loud voice, 'Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?"
reflection: |
  Picture a wide, dry pan under an enormous sky. A great male lion moves across it, marking his territory, magnificent and unbothered. And then, just at the edge of the frame, comes the matriarch of the hyena clan. She follows him deliberately, and everywhere he marks, she scratches it up and marks over it. Behind her, the whole clan is waiting — dozens of them, pressing closer, threatening. And you realise, watching, that as majestic and powerful as this one lion is, he cannot hold the line. The numbers are against him. The territory is slipping.
  
  That feeling is not confined to a wildlife scene. Look honestly at the world and something in you recognises the same losing battle — evil pressing in from every side, injustice marking over what is good, and a quiet dread that no one is strong enough to turn it back. Into that dread a voice rings out across heaven itself: Who is worthy? Who will step into the clearing and put things right?
  
  The ache we carry is not weakness or faithlessness. It is the honest recognition that the battle is real and beyond us. But the very fact that the question is being asked in heaven means Someone is listening for an answer. The line will not stay broken forever. The One who is worthy is about to step into the frame.

lifeApplication: "When the news, or your own street, or your own family feels overrun by what is wrong, notice the temptation to conclude that no one is coming. Today, when you feel outnumbered, let the ache turn into a listening posture rather than despair. The battle being bigger than you is not the last word — it is the reason to lift your eyes and ask who is worthy, and to wait for the One who answers."
prayer: |
  Father, I confess how often I look at the world and feel the line breaking.
  
  Where evil presses in and I feel outnumbered, remind me that You are still on the throne.
  
  Teach me to bring my dread to You instead of letting it settle into hopelessness.
  
  Thank You that heaven itself is asking who is worthy — and that You already know the answer.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
