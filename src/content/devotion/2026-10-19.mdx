  text: "When He opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been slain for the word of God and for the witness they had borne."
reflection: |
  A man stands on a street corner with an open Bible in his hands. He is not shouting. He is reading aloud — words about turning from a life of sin, words about a God who forgives — and slowly a ring of uniforms closes in around him. Stop that, they tell him. You are antagonising people. He answers that he is only reading the Scriptures, but the hands are already on his arm.
  
  Not far from such a scene, a woman stands near a fence with her head bowed. She holds no sign. She says nothing at all. Someone approaches and asks what she is doing. I am praying, she answers. And for that — for the silent movement of her heart toward God — she too is led away.
  
  These are small pictures of a very old thing. John saw a whole company of them, gathered under the altar, people who had been slain for the word of God and for the witness they had borne. The word of God has always been offensive, not because it is cruel but because it is true, and the truth has a way of exposing us. To stand on it has never once made a person popular.
  
  But notice what John says they were slain for: the witness they had borne. Not a witness that failed, not words that fell to the ground and vanished. The man on the corner may be bundled into a van and the woman led quietly away, and the watching world may decide their words came to nothing. Heaven decides otherwise. Every faithful word, even the costly ones, even the ones that seemed to change no one — God gathers them up and keeps them. Nothing spoken for Him is ever truly lost.

lifeApplication: |
  Most of us will never be arrested for our faith. But we know the smaller version of the same cost. There is the conversation where you could speak the truth gently and lose a little standing, or stay silent and keep your comfort. There is the raised eyebrow at work, the family table where faith is unwelcome, the moment where being honest about what you believe will make you, as the world measures things, not cool.
  
  This week, when one of those moments comes, do not despise it as too small to matter. The quiet, costly word is a witness, and it is seen. You are not speaking into a void. You are speaking into the hearing of the One who gathers every word under His altar.
prayer: |
  Father, thank You for all who have stood on Your word when it cost them everything, and whose witness You have not forgotten.
  
  Forgive me for the times I have stayed silent simply to stay comfortable.
  
  Give me courage this week for the small, costly words that honour You.
  
  Help me to trust that nothing spoken for You falls to the ground and is lost.
  
  Remember those imprisoned and surrounded today for bearing witness to Your name.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
