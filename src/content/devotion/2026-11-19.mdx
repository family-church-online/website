reflection: |
  Have you ever watched smoke rise in perfectly still air — a candle just blown out, a single grey thread climbing straight up, unhurried, until it thins and disappears into the ceiling? Or woodsmoke on a cold, windless morning, rising dead straight from a chimney into a pale sky? There is something almost prayerful in the way it climbs, as though it were carrying something upward, as though the air itself were drawing it home.
  
  In John's vision an angel stands at the altar with a golden censer, and into it is poured much incense, mingled with the prayers of all the saints — and together the smoke and the prayers rise before God. Every one of them. Not a single petition lost on the way. Among that rising smoke are the long prayers, the ones prayed for years with no visible answer, the aching cry of hearts that asked "how long, O Lord?" and wondered if anyone was listening. All of it kept. All of it gathered into a golden bowl. All of it rising to the very throne of God.
  
  We pray, and so often it feels like words spoken into an empty room, dissolving as soon as they leave us. But that is not what becomes of them. Heaven treasures our prayers the way a censer treasures incense; they are collected, remembered, and carried up before God — and in His time they come down again with power. Nothing offered to God in faith ever simply evaporates. It rises.

lifeApplication: "Is there a prayer you have almost given up on — a wandering child, a marriage grown cold, a healing that has not come, a door that will not open? It is tempting to conclude that God did not hear, or did not care. But the prayer you prayed in tears last year is not gone; it is held in a golden bowl before the throne, rising still. Today, pray it again. Add it to the incense. Perseverance in prayer is not nagging a reluctant God; it is trusting a faithful One who loses nothing we bring Him. Keep praying. The smoke is still rising."
prayer: |
  Father, thank You that not one of my prayers is ever lost to You.
  
  Forgive me for the times I have stopped praying because the answer was slow.
  
  I lift again the prayer I had almost given up on, and I lay it on Your altar.
  
  Thank You that You treasure my words like incense and carry them to Your throne.
  
  Teach me to pray and not lose heart.
  
  In Jesus' name, Amen.
