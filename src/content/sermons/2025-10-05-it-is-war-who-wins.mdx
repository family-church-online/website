# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You are in a spiritual war the moment you follow Jesus — but God never loses, and He uses every enemy attack to advance His Kingdom.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 16:25-26 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "About midnight Paul and Silas were praying and singing hymns to God, and the prisoners were listening to them, and suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken, and immediately all the doors were opened, and everyone's bonds were unfastened."

mainPoints:
  - title: Spiritual warfare is constant, not conditional.
    body: The day you become a Christian and begin to tell others about Jesus is the day you enter a war — prayer, worship, obedience are all acts of combat.
  - title: "The enemy's attacks start in the spiritual realm and manifest in the physical."
    body: "A demon-possessed girl follows Paul shouting truth to sabotage God's quiet strategy; it escalates to mob violence, false accusation, and brutal punishment."
  - title: "God has given you weapons: prayer, praise, and obedience."
    body: At midnight, beaten and in stocks, Paul and Silas pray and sing — and God responds with an earthquake that opens every door and breaks every chain.
  - title: God redeems every attack for His purposes.
    body: The jailer and his household are saved, a church is planted in Philippi, and the gospel spreads — all because the enemy thought he could stop it with suffering.

keyIllustration: "A soldier on a battlefield doesn't get offended when someone tries to shoot him — he expects it. He signed up for it. Yet many Christians are surprised and offended when they face opposition for following Jesus. The issue isn't the attack; it's that we forget we're in a war."

application:
  - "Wake up every day and ask God to order your steps — don't charge into the day trusting your own wisdom or strength."
  - "When suffering comes, remember: you're not fighting human beings, you're fighting spiritual forces. Pray for the people involved; fight the real enemy with the weapons God has given you."
  - Worship and praise are not just for celebration — they are weapons that do damage to the enemy in the spiritual realm.

toRemember: God uses all things together for good — even the slander, the suffering, and the sabotage — because when He says He will do something, it happens.
