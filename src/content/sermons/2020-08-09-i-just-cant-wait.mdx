# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God never wastes your waiting — He uses it to prepare you for the purpose He's already promised, so you can't just wait, you must actively obey Him through the suffering."
keyScriptureRef: "Psalm 2:1-4 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Why do the nations rage and the peoples plot in vain? The kings of the earth set themselves, and the rulers take counsel together against the Lord and against His anointed, saying, \"Let us burst their bonds apart and cast away their cords from us.\" He who sits in the heavens laughs; the Lord holds them in derision."

mainPoints:
  - title: "David's life was a thirty-year lesson in waiting through betrayal."
    body: "Anointed king at fifteen, he faced Saul's jealous murder attempts, betrayal by the cities he saved, kidnapping of his family, and constant persecution — yet he refused to seize the throne by force, trusting God's timing completely."
  - title: "David was God's instrument of judgment; we are God's instruments of grace."
    body: "David's purpose was to execute judgment on Canaan's rebellion; the apostles' purpose was to preach the gospel and heal. We share the apostles' calling — to be with Jesus, preach His Word, cast out demons, and bring healing."
  - title: Psalm 2 is ultimately about Jesus, the true Anointed King.
    body: The nations raged against David, but they raged even more against Jesus — crucifying Him. Yet God laughed, because three days later Jesus rose, and now sits enthroned until every enemy becomes His footstool.
  - title: "You can't just wait — you must obey while you wait."
    body: "David didn't passively endure; he rescued cities, protected shepherds, worshiped God, and did the next right thing even when people betrayed him. Whatever you're waiting for, God is calling you to active obedience in the meantime."

keyIllustration: "David spared Saul's life twice — once in a cave when Saul came in to relieve himself, and once when he crept into Saul's camp and took his spear and water jar. His men urged him to kill Saul, insisting God had delivered the enemy into his hands. But David refused, saying, \"This is God's anointed. It's not my job to take him out. God made me king. God will remove Saul when God decides to. I will wait patiently for God to do what God does.\" David knew the difference between God's work and his own."

application:
  - "Trust that God is preparing you through this season, not punishing you — whatever pain you're in, whoever betrayed you, whatever you've lost, God will not waste it."
  - Do the next right thing God has called you to do — rescue where rescue is needed, feed where feeding is needed, encourage where encouragement is needed, preach the gospel, stand against spiritual powers.
  - Pray for those who persecute you instead of seeking revenge — our war is not against flesh and blood, and God alone judges the wicked.

toRemember: "When you feel like saying \"I just can't wait,\" remember instead to say, \"I can't just wait\" — because God will empower you to wait, but He's also calling you to obey Him in the waiting."
