# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Christ's single sacrifice accomplishes what no repeated ritual ever could: it removes your sin permanently, rewrites your heart to desire obedience, and positions Jesus at God's right hand to intercede for you."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 10:12-14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But when Christ had offered for all time a single sacrifice for sins, he sat down at the right hand of God, waiting from that time until his enemies should be made a footstool for his feet. For by a single offering he has perfected for all time those who are being sanctified.

mainPoints:
  - title: Old rituals could never remove sin.
    body: Priests stood daily, repeating sacrifices that covered sin but never took it away or cleansed the conscience.
  - title: "Christ's single offering perfects forever."
    body: His sacrifice is effective, ethical (God-pleasing), and safe—he sat down because the work is finished.
  - title: Jesus sits at the right hand of God.
    body: "This shows the Father's approval, Christ's authority over all powers, and His ongoing intercession for you."
  - title: "The Holy Spirit writes God's law on your heart."
    body: "Like a sea turtle hard-wired to navigate home, you are given both knowledge of God's law and the desire to obey it."
  - title: God remembers your sins no more.
    body: Even the worst sin, genuinely repented, is erased forever—leaving only gratitude and the capacity to worship.

keyIllustration: "A sea turtle hatches on a beach, swims for 48 hours to escape predators, then migrates 1,600 kilometers to feeding grounds. Fifteen to twenty years later, it navigates back to the exact beach where it was born, using magnetic particles in its brain to plot GPS coordinates and a sense of smell to identify the precise sand and conditions. God hard-wired not only the ability but the desire to return. In the same way, the Holy Spirit writes God's law in your mind and heart—giving you both understanding and the longing to obey."

application:
  - "Stop trying to earn forgiveness through repeated rituals, guilt, or effort. Christ's work is finished."
  - Trust that the Holy Spirit is remaking you—your growing desire to obey is His work, not yours.
  - "Know that Jesus is interceding for you right now at the Father's right hand. You are not alone in your struggle."

toRemember: Where there is forgiveness of sins, there is no longer any offering for sin—God remembers your sin no more, and you are remade to love what He loves.
