# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Communion is an invitation from Jesus to meet with Him — remembering His sacrificial death, encountering Him in the present, and anticipating the wedding feast to come.
keyScriptureRef: "1 Corinthians 11:23-25 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "For I received from the Lord what I also delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus on the night when He was betrayed took bread, and when He had given thanks, He broke it, and said, \"This is my body, which is for you. Do this in remembrance of me.\" In the same way also He took the cup, after supper, saying, \"This cup is the new covenant in my blood. Do this, as often as you drink it, in remembrance of me.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "The Lord's Supper fulfils a pattern woven through Scripture."
    body: "From Melchizedek's bread and wine to the daily offerings in the tabernacle to the Passover meal, God has been pointing toward Jesus — the Lamb of God whose body and blood bring forgiveness."
  - title: Communion is a three-directional encounter.
    body: We look back at what Jesus did on the cross; we look up and meet with Him now; we look forward to the wedding feast of the Lamb when we will eat and drink with Him in glory.
  - title: Self-examination is required, but fear is not.
    body: "If you recognise that Jesus' body was broken and His blood was shed for you, and if you trust Him for forgiveness, you are welcome at His table. Perfect love casts out fear."

keyIllustration: "A journalist photographed the last meal requests of death row inmates. Many chose simple comfort foods — fried chicken, ice cream, meals that reminded them of childhood. These hardened criminals, about to die, chose food that connected them to family and memory. Jesus, too, chose His last meal — and He invited His disciples to share it. Communion is not a ritual; it's intimacy, memory, and love in the face of death."

application:
  - Before you take communion, examine your heart — not to disqualify yourself, but to make sure you recognise what Jesus has done and who He is.
  - "Communion is not only about remembering; it's about meeting with Jesus now and anticipating eternity with Him."
  - You are part of a family that has been gathering at this table for thousands of years — take your place with joy and gratitude.

toRemember: Jesus has invited you to His table — to meet with Him now, to remember what He did, and to look forward to the day when you will feast with Him forever.
