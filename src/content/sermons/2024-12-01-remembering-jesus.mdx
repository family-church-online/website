mainPoints:
  - title: Remembering equips us to move forward.
    body: "Joshua 1:9 tells us not to fear what's coming or be dismayed by what's past. When we're stuck between fear and regret, God tells us to remember — to trace what He's already done so we can plot a course forward with assurance."
  - title: Jesus is present throughout all of Scripture.
    body: "He's not the bomb squad arriving at the end. He is there from Genesis 3:15 (the first gospel promise), through the Passover lamb, in the angel who stopped Abraham's sacrifice, marching toward incarnation — and He will come again to defeat Satan and restore all things."
  - title: Remembering Jesus transforms our everyday lives.
    body: After the crucifixion, the disciples hid in fear and dismay because they forgot Jesus said He would rise. When Mary reminded them, they ran with joy to the empty tomb. Remembering fills us with hope, courage, and strength in our daily struggles.
  - title: "Jesus' love is most tangible when we feel most ashamed."
    body: "Jesus describes Himself as \"lowly in heart\" (Matthew 11:28-30) — the God of the universe is most accessible when we want nothing to do with ourselves. When we remember who He is, shame loses its power."

keyIllustration: "A man arrived at a school ceremony underdressed — the second time that year — and felt crushing shame. In that moment, he remembered Matthew 11:28-30, where Jesus says He is \"lowly in heart.\" Jesus, the God of the universe, is most accessible when we are most ashamed. That memory gave him the strength to walk onto the stage. This illustrates how remembering who Jesus is equips us to face situations where we feel paralysed by shame or inadequacy."

application:
  - When fear or regret paralyses you, pause and trace what God has already done in your life — it will plot a course forward.
  - "Take communion seriously: it is Jesus' dying wish that we remember His love and the access we have to Him because of His sacrifice."
  - When shame tells you to hide, remember that Jesus was publicly stripped, beaten, and shamed on the cross — and His love for you is most tangible in that moment when you feel most unworthy.

toRemember: "Jesus earnestly desires to share the Lord's table with you because He loves you — and one day, you will partake in that meal with Him in heaven."
