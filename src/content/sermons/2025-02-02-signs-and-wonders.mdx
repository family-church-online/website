When our awe of Him eclipses every substitute fear—news cycles, social anxiety, existential dread—we finally become people who run toward Him instead of away. What would happen if your fear of God outweighed everything else?
subtitle: The early church feared God so deeply that multitudes ran toward Him for healing—while others were too terrified to come near.
hook: Why did the apostles see regular miracles and mass healings when we barely see either today? What created an environment where God moved so powerfully—and how did fear play a role no one expected?

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The early church saw many signs and wonders because they feared God rightly—an awe that drew them to Him rather than drove them away—and when we replace substitute fears with holy fear, we position ourselves for God to move powerfully.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 5:11-12, 16 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And great fear came upon the whole church and upon all who heard of these things. Now many signs and wonders were regularly done among the people by the hands of the apostles… The people also gathered from the towns around Jerusalem, bringing the sick and those afflicted with unclean spirits, and they were all healed.

mainPoints:
  - title: Two Responses to the Same Fear
    body: After Ananias and Sapphira died, the whole church and all who heard were gripped with fear—but some ran toward God in repentance while others stayed away in terror.
  - title: We Are Designed to Fear God
    body: Humans pay to be scared on roller coasters and watch horror films, yet resist fearing the One who holds eternal authority. Scripture repeatedly commands us to fear God—not as slaves, but as children in awe of His holiness and power.
  - title: Substitute Fears Sabotage Us
    body: When we fail to fear God, we fill the void with anxiety over news, social media, health, politics, and reputation. These substitutes distract us from worship and paralyse us with dread instead of freeing us with awe.
  - title: The Gospel Makes Holy Fear Possible
    body: "Because Jesus bore God's wrath on the cross, we can approach God with reverent fear without terror of condemnation. Perfect love casts out fear of punishment—but not fear of His majesty."

keyIllustration: A single human cell detects breakage, sends a message to the nucleus, unzips DNA, locates blueprints, creates a copy in RNA, manufactures the replacement part, packages it with an address, and sends it via a protein molecule walking 100 steps per second to the exact location for installation. Researchers encountering this complexity either run toward God in awe or run away declaring it happened by chance.

application:
  - "Pray daily: \"Unite my heart to fear Your name\" (Psalm 86:11)—ask God to collapse all scattered fears into one holy fear of Him."
  - "Cut out news and social media addiction: these platforms profit by making you afraid; replace them with meditation on God's promises and character."
  - "When substitute fears grip you—health, finances, politics—immediately declare: \"Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God\" (Isaiah 41:10)."

toRemember: When you fear God rightly, you will fear nothing else—and that is the environment where signs, wonders, and healing break out regularly.
