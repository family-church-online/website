# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: When you fear God more than man, obedience becomes possible even when persecution is certain.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 5:29-32 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But Peter and the apostles answered, \"We must obey God rather than men. The God of our fathers raised Jesus, whom you killed by hanging him on a tree. God exalted him at his right hand as Leader and Savior, to give repentance to Israel and forgiveness of sins. And we are witnesses to these things, and so is the Holy Spirit, whom God has given to those who obey him.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: The Sadducees feared losing control, not losing the truth.
    body: "They didn't believe in angels, the resurrection, or life after death — yet an angel freed the apostles from their locked prison. When Scripture contradicts your assumptions, you either humble yourself or harden your heart."
  - title: "The apostles feared God, so they didn't fear shame."
    body: "After being beaten and publicly humiliated, they left rejoicing that they were counted worthy to suffer dishonor for Jesus' name. The honor of God outweighed the shame imposed by man."
  - title: You cannot know God if you do not read His Word for yourself.
    body: Relying on sermons, podcasts, or teachers alone leaves you vulnerable to deception. The Holy Spirit reveals God through Scripture, but only if you open it and read it cover to cover.

keyIllustration: A student could not study under Gamaliel until he could sit before him as a child and recite the first five books of the Bible word for word from memory. Gamaliel would listen, assess accuracy, and decide whether to accept him. Knowing Scripture that deeply did not guarantee righteousness — but ignorance of it guaranteed blindness.

application:
  - Stop waiting for permission to obey God. When His command is clear, the cost is irrelevant.
  - "Read the Bible cover to cover. Not once — repeatedly. Understanding comes from the Holy Spirit, but only if you're reading."
  - "Name the fear you're living under. Anxiety, people-pleasing, control — all point to fearing man more than God."

toRemember: "The fear of God casts out all other fears — not because it's smaller, but because it's bigger."
