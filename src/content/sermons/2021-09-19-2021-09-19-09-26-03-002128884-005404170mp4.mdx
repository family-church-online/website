mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus makes time for children.
    body: He was busy changing the world, confronting religious authorities, heading toward His death — but He stopped everything to welcome children into His arms and bless them.
  - title: The only permanent blessing is a relationship with God.
    body: Education, health, and success are all temporary. The one thing your child can take with them past the grave is knowing the God who created them.
  - title: Childlike faith means receiving grace, not earning it.
    body: "Children don't approach Jesus listing their achievements. They simply receive His love with gratitude. That's the only way into the kingdom of God."
  - title: You can trust God with your children for four reasons.
    body: "He prioritises them. He gave His own Son first. He doesn't waste suffering. He has power over death."

keyIllustration: "A family's premature twins were born at 29 weeks during COVID lockdown — one weighing just 740 grams. After 131 days in neonatal ICU, chronic lung disease, surgeries, and separation, both children survived. A year later to the day, a friend gave birth to twins at the same hospital, same gestational age, same complications — and benefited from the maternity leave policy changes the first mother had fought for. Every tear had purpose. Every miracle encouraged a praying community. God wastes nothing."

application:
  - Bring your children to Jesus — in prayer, in worship, in dedication — and trust Him with the blessing only He can give.
  - "Come to the Father like a child: not listing what you've earned, but simply receiving His love with gratitude."
  - "When life is hard, remember: God gave His Son first, and He will not waste your suffering."
