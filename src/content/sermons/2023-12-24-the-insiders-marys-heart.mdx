# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God chose Mary — a nobody from nowhere — because He saw a heart fully surrendered to Him, and He will equip every heart that says yes.
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 1:38 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And Mary said, \"Behold, I am the servant of the Lord; let it be to me according to your word.\" And the angel departed from her."

mainPoints:
  - title: Mary was courageous.
    body: "Unlike Moses, Gideon, and Saul, who hesitated or hid when God called, Mary's response was immediate: \"I am the Lord's servant.\" She said yes without knowing the cost."
  - title: Mary was resilient.
    body: "She gave birth in a stable, fled to Egypt with a newborn, lost Jesus in Jerusalem — yet she trusted God's plan through every upheaval, knowing His power is made great in weakness."
  - title: Mary was wise.
    body: She stored up treasures in her heart — every angelic visitation, every act of worship, every miracle — so that when calamity came, her heart could not be emptied.
  - title: Mary was faithful.
    body: "She had complete faith that Jesus was the Son of God, trusting Him even when she didn't understand, even when He was lost, even when He hung on the cross."

keyIllustration: "A prankster dressed as a scrawny cleaner walks into a gym where massive bodybuilders are lifting enormous weights. They laugh at him — until he casually picks up their heaviest barbell with one hand while dusting. That's God's power made great through weakness: it's impressive when the strong succeed, but mind-blowing when the unlikely do the impossible."

application:
  - "God doesn't choose the equipped; He equips the chosen. If He's called you, He will prepare your heart."
  - "Store up treasures in your heart — moments of God's faithfulness — so you have reserves when life empties you."
  - "No matter how broken your heart is, Jesus came to make it right. That's the promise of Christmas."

toRemember: God chose Mary not because she was somebody, but because her heart was wholly His — and He offers the same favour to every heart that surrenders.
