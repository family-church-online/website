# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The return of Christ is not a debate to win — it's a truth to live by, calling you to readiness, holiness, and hope-fuelled work until He comes."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Thessalonians 4:13-14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But we do not want you to be uninformed, brothers, about those who are asleep, that you may not grieve as others do who have no hope. For since we believe that Jesus died and rose again, even so, through Jesus, God will bring with Him those who have fallen asleep.

mainPoints:
  - title: Be ready — not just informed.
    body: "Paul's priority isn't eschatological precision; it's holy living. Work in faith, labour in love, persevere in steadfast hope."
  - title: Two interpretations, one foundation.
    body: "Pre-tribulation, mid-tribulation, post-tribulation — Christians disagree. The thousand-year reign is either literal or symbolic. But the gospel stands: Jesus is coming back."
  - title: "We suffer evil, but never face God's wrath."
    body: Noah, Lot, Rahab, the Israelites in Egypt — all suffered in evil contexts, but were removed before judgment fell. Jesus took your wrath; you will never experience it.
  - title: The Jewish wedding unlocks Revelation 19.
    body: Jesus has gone to prepare a place. He will return to take His bride to the marriage supper. Eleven verses later, He descends in judgment.

keyIllustration: "A beloved family cat climbed a dangerous yucca tree to escape a German Shepherd and stayed paralysed at the top for eight hours, refusing to come down. When finally rescued, the cat immediately ate and drank — he'd been too terrified to move. Christians can do the same: hide in fear, paralysed by the headlines, waiting for Jesus to fetch them. But that's not why He told us He's coming back. He told us so we'd work harder, more joyfully, at what He's given us to do until He arrives."

application:
  - "Turn off the fear-driven news cycle. Don't let anxiety about the world paralyse the work God has given you."
  - Hold your eschatology with conviction and humility. You can disagree with other believers on the timing and still love them deeply.
  - "Anchor your hope in this: if you are in Christ, you will never face the wrath of God. That changes everything."

toRemember: Jesus has prepared a place for you — and He is coming to take you there.
