# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Paul prays for the Holy Spirit to strengthen believers' inner beings so Christ can dwell permanently in their hearts — only then can they comprehend His infinite love and be filled with all the fullness of God."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 3:14-19"
keyScriptureText: For this reason I bow my knees before the Father, from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named, that according to the riches of His glory He may grant you to be strengthened with power through His Spirit in your inner being, so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith — that you, being rooted and grounded in love, may have strength to comprehend with all the saints what is the breadth and length and height and depth, and to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.

mainPoints:
  - title: Justification vs. Sanctification
    body: Justification is the point you are saved — confessing Jesus as Lord and believing God raised Him from the dead. Sanctification is the lifelong process of letting Jesus take more control each day, growing in maturity in Christ.
  - title: The Indwelling Problem
    body: "The Greek word for \"dwell\" means to take up permanent residence. Many believers are justified but Christ remains only a visitor — our inner beings are still wrecked by sin (our own or sins done to us), making us unsuitable for Him to live there permanently."
  - title: "The Holy Spirit's Work"
    body: Only supernatural power from the Holy Spirit can prepare our broken inner beings so Christ can move from guest to permanent resident — fixing whatever is stopping us from growing in maturity.
  - title: The Fullness of God
    body: When Christ dwells in your heart permanently, you comprehend His infinite love (breadth, length, height, depth) not just intellectually but emotionally — and this transforms you into His image, the fullness of God at work in you.

keyIllustration: "A fisherman fished all night and caught nothing. A stranger told him to cast his net on the other side. The nets filled to breaking point, the boat almost sank — and the fisherman fell flat on his face, saying \"Get away from me, Lord. I am an evil man.\" This is the moment Peter realized who Jesus truly was — and immediately felt the weight of his own unworthiness."

application:
  - "Ask yourself: Does Jesus live in your heart on Thursdays, Fridays, Saturdays — or only on Sundays? Is He comfortable there, or is He a visitor who doesn't feel welcome?"
  - If you struggle with the head-heart gap (knowing Christ loves you intellectually but not believing it emotionally), ask the Holy Spirit to strengthen your inner being so Christ can dwell there permanently.
  - "When you believe Christ's love in your heart — not just your head — everything changes: you know you're worthy of love, you believe God has a purpose for you, and you pursue it with gratitude and action."

toRemember: We cannot have Christ living permanently in our hearts in our current state — we need the supernatural power of the Holy Spirit to make it His permanent dwelling.
