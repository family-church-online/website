# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God did not reveal His mystery to satisfy curiosity, but to equip His church—Jew and Gentile united—to stand firm in truth and discern all things by Scripture.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 3:6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: This mystery is that the Gentiles are fellow heirs, members of the same body, and partakers of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel.

mainPoints:
  - title: "The mystery is not about knowing facts—it's about becoming the person who can handle the answer."
    body: Paul was trained in Scripture, passionate for God, and pursued intimacy with Christ. He became capable of receiving revelation not through cleverness, but through devotion.
  - title: The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom.
    body: Our posture toward God determines what we can understand. If we revere Him, we trust His Word above competing explanations—whether about the moon, the world, or salvation itself.
  - title: "God's mystery—the inclusion of the Gentiles in Christ—cost Paul his freedom."
    body: Yet he kept preaching, because he knew the Word of God is not bound. The church exists because he refused to be silent about what matters most.
  - title: Every claim must be tested by Scripture.
    body: If a revelation, experience, or teaching does not accord with the Bible, we reject it—no matter how compelling or culturally acceptable it may be. Jesus created all things, visible and invisible, and every knee will bow to Him.

keyIllustration: "A teacher earns five hundred US dollars an hour training young adults how to speak on the phone without anxiety, because our generation is so isolated that in-person conversation has become paralyzing. Yet God's answer to loneliness is not a workshop—it is the church, a family designed from the beginning to connect us, equip us, and hold us accountable to truth."

application:
  - "Read Scripture daily—even if you don't understand everything at first. The more you devour God's Word, the more you become the kind of person who can receive His wisdom."
  - "Test everything by the Bible. If a news story, experience, or teaching contradicts Scripture, reject it—even if it's sensational or widely accepted."
  - Lean into the church community. We need each other to discern truth, to strengthen one another, and to carry the mystery forward together.

toRemember: Whatever mysteries you chase, whatever answers you long for, live close enough to God that you can hear what you need to know—for His glory and your joy.
