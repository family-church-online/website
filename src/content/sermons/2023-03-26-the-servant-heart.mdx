# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You were created to serve God—not because He needs you, but because serving Him is where you become fully human and fully alive.
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 25:23 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "His master said to him, 'Well done, good and faithful servant. You have been faithful over a little; I will set you over much. Enter into the joy of your master.'"

mainPoints:
  - title: Embrace your identity as a servant.
    body: The New Testament writers—Peter, Paul, James, Jude, and John—all identified first as bond servants of Christ. Your identity is not self-made; you were bought with the blood of Jesus.
  - title: Embrace a servant attitude like Jesus.
    body: "Jesus emptied Himself and took on the form of a servant, though He was God. He teaches us to count others more significant than ourselves and to put God's glory above our comfort."
  - title: Embrace service like Jesus by acting on it.
    body: "Use the gifts God has given you to serve one another. This isn't about earning approval—it's about stewarding grace so that God is glorified in everything."

keyIllustration: "A young female leopard killed a mature baboon and discovered a day-old baby clinging to its dead mother. Instead of eating the infant, the leopard carried it to safety, protected it from hyenas, and stayed with it through the night—sacrificing her own meal. Researchers couldn't explain it. Selfless service doesn't fit the \"survival of the fittest\" narrative—and that's exactly the point. When believers serve sacrificially, the world sees something that doesn't make sense apart from God."

application:
  - "Wake up each morning and ask: \"Lord, I belong to You—what do You want me to do today?\""
  - "When bitterness rises because someone mistreats you, remember: you're not the master—you're the servant. And Jesus served even those who executed Him."
  - "Filter the teaching you consume. If it's all about you—your breakthrough, your blessing, your prosperity—it's missing the heart of the gospel."

toRemember: "At final judgment, Jesus will say, \"As you did it for one of the least of these my brothers, you did it for me.\" Every act of selfless service is an act of worship to the Master who served us first."
