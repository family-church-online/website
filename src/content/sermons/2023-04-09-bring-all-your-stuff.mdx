# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

mainPoints:
  - title: The resurrection is the cornerstone of Christian faith.
    body: "Without it, there is no Christianity. Jesus didn't just die — He conquered death, turning the world right-side up again."
  - title: Thomas loved Jesus deeply and doubted honestly.
    body: "He wasn't a sceptic; he was a man whose heart was shattered. He needed more than testimony — he needed to encounter the risen Christ himself."
  - title: "Jesus didn't condemn Thomas's doubt — He answered it."
    body: "He invited Thomas to touch His wounds, and Thomas responded with one of the greatest declarations of faith: \"My Lord and my God.\""

keyIllustration: A diver separated from his group in murky water experiences the terror of isolation and disorientation. In the same way, Thomas — separated from the other disciples on resurrection Sunday — was alone in his grief and doubt, unable to see clearly. Only when Jesus appeared and invited him to touch His wounds did clarity come. The resurrected Christ is the solid ground beneath the chaos.

application:
  - Doubt is not the opposite of faith. Honest struggle with God is part of the journey toward deeper trust.
  - "Bring everything to the empty tomb — your pain, your questions, your shame. Jesus doesn't turn away those who come honestly."
  - "If you're wrestling with whether the resurrection is real, ask Jesus to open your mind to the Scriptures and reveal Himself to you."

toRemember: The resurrected Jesus sets right what you bring to Him — doubt becomes faith, fear becomes courage, shame becomes freedom.
