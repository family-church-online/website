mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus has been there.
    body: "His opening credential to Smyrna is not a title but a testimony: He is \"the first and the last who died and came to life.\" Before He commands faithfulness, He establishes that He has personally passed through death — which means He speaks as one who knows, not one who merely sympathises."
  - title: "The real adversary is Satan, not suffering's visible causes."
    body: Jesus names the devil — not Rome, not hostile neighbours — as the one casting believers into prison. Every severe trial is ultimately a test of whether saving faith can be destroyed; the story of Job demonstrates it cannot.
  - title: Those who conquer the first death escape the second.
    body: The crown of life belongs to those who hold fast. The second death — eternal separation from God — has no power over those born twice. Those born once die twice; those born twice die once.

keyIllustration: "Satan asks God's permission to strip a righteous man — Job — of everything: wealth, family, and health. Job loses it all and grows confused, angry, and demoralized, yet his faith in God survives. God then restores him doubly. The story shows that Satan operates only within limits God sets, that saving faith withstands total devastation, and that God's purposes extend beyond any suffering He permits."

application:
  - When trials come, anchor yourself in who Jesus is — the One who died and returned — rather than in the scale of the suffering. Ask honestly whether your faith is real enough to survive cost, not just comfort.
