# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You cannot control whether the seed grows in others, but you can cultivate your own heart into good soil that receives God's word and bears lasting fruit."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 13:23"
keyScriptureText: As for what was sown on good soil, this is the one who hears the word and understands it. He indeed bears fruit and yields, in one case a hundredfold, in another sixty, and in another thirty.

mainPoints:
  - title: The sower and the seed never change.
    body: The farmer keeps sowing and the seed is always the same; our task is to spread the word, not to guarantee its growth.
  - title: The four soils are four kinds of hearers.
    body: Hardened, shallow, distracted and fruitful — the same word meets very different ground.
  - title: Good soil has to be cultivated.
    body: Surrender, humility, forgiveness and grace are the nutrients that turn poor ground into a fruitful harvest.
  - title: Keep feeding the soil daily.
    body: Renew your mind, be a doer of the word, and grow the fruit of the Spirit one week at a time.

keyIllustration: Across Johannesburg the winter trees stand completely bare for months without rain, yet weeks before the first spring rains arrive they suddenly push out leaves, flowers and fruit. As the soil warms, it releases stored water and nutrients — a picture of how God sustains His people and produces fruit in them even through dry, difficult seasons.

application:
  - "Sow God's word freely and trust the Holy Spirit for the results, instead of anxiously measuring who accepts it."
  - Examine what weeds — money, work, screens, small compromises — are quietly crowding out your growth, and pull them.
  - "Take the weekly challenge: choose one fruit of the Spirit, carry a verse about it with you all day, and let it reshape how you respond throughout the week."

toRemember: "Every day you choose your soil: will you bear fruit today, or hide out with the weeds?"
