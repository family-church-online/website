# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: No matter how corrupt or chaotic earthly authority becomes, Jesus Christ holds all authority in heaven and earth—and He will never waste your faithfulness.
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 28:18-20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And Jesus came and said to them, \"All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Luke names six rulers to show us the world into which John and Jesus came.
    body: Tiberius Caesar, Pontius Pilate, Herod, and corrupt high priests—these were cruel, cowardly, immoral men. Their rule mirrors the uncertainty and dysfunction we feel today.
  - title: "God's plan unfolds perfectly even under broken human authority."
    body: "You can trust that every earthly ruler only holds power because Christ permits it. Jesus told Pilate directly: \"You would have no authority over me at all unless it had been given you from above.\""
  - title: Repentance prepares the way for hope.
    body: John the Baptist called people to take ownership of their own sin—the one thing no corrupt leader can control. Confession and turning back to God give you a fresh start and clear purpose.
  - title: The Great Commission gives you unshakable purpose.
    body: No law, no pandemic, no political chaos can stop you from making disciples, loving God, and loving others. Your obedience this year has eternal weight.

keyIllustration: "Joni Eareckson Tada, paralyzed for over fifty years, watched old films of herself walking and riding horses. Grieving her losses, she realized the time since her injury was already longer than the time remaining before she'll walk again in glory. Our suffering may be long, but our salvation draws near—and Christ promises resurrection to all who trust Him."

application:
  - Stop looking to human leaders as saviors. Place your confidence in the One who conquered death and holds all authority.
  - Take stock of your own life before God. Confess where you fell short in 2020 and trust His forgiveness to give you a fresh start.
  - Make 2021 count by obeying the Great Commission—make disciples, love radically, and live knowing Christ is with you always.

toRemember: "Whatever 2021 brings, not one act of obedience or love will be wasted—every day of your life is written in God's book, and Christ is with you to the end of the age."
