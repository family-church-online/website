# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: We cannot be ready for the return of Jesus if we are not helping others to be ready for His return.
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 2:36-38 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And there was a prophetess, Anna, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. She was advanced in years, having lived with her husband seven years from when she was a virgin, and then as a widow until she was eighty-four. She did not depart from the temple, worshiping with fasting and prayer night and day. And coming up at that very hour she began to give thanks to God and to speak of him to all who were waiting for the redemption of Jerusalem.

mainPoints:
  - title: God chooses the overlooked to be insiders.
    body: "Anna was from the tribe of Asher—part of the despised north, from Galilee—yet God chose her to be a prophetess and witness to the Messiah. Her name means \"grace\" and her father's name means \"the face of God.\" By grace, she saw the face of God in the infant Jesus."
  - title: Devotion to God is not dependent on circumstances or emotions.
    body: After losing her husband, Anna could have lived in bitterness. Instead, she devoted herself to worship, fasting, and prayer—day and night—regardless of how she felt. She made the most of every opportunity to serve God.
  - title: Waiting for Jesus means preparing others to meet Him.
    body: "Anna didn't keep her experience to herself. She immediately spoke of the Messiah \"to all who were waiting for the redemption of Jerusalem.\" Our readiness for Christ's return is incomplete if we are not sharing the good news with those around us."

keyIllustration: "A tiny, pale turquoise eggshell with speckles—broken and lying on the ground. You can look at it and see only destruction: a broken egg, something beautiful now ruined. Or you can see it for what it really is: the evidence that something took its first breath, that new life broke free and is now flying, giving God glory. Much of 2024 will be like that eggshell—it depends which way you choose to look at it."

application:
  - "Offer worship, prayer, and fasting regardless of your emotions or circumstances. Don't wait until you \"feel like it\" to meet with God."
  - Be in the right place spiritually by staying connected to the body of Christ—meeting together, forming Bible study groups, and hanging out with people who are also waiting for Jesus.
  - "Share your testimony. Your experience of God's faithfulness is a powerful weapon against the enemy and a lifeline for those who don't yet know Christ."

toRemember: You cannot stop God from fulfilling His purpose for you—but you must be waiting for Him to do so.
