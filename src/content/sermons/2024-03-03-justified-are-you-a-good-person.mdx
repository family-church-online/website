# ── ABOUT LISTS ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
takeaways:
  - Justification means God declares you innocent — righteous before Him — not because you are good, but because Jesus is.
  - No religious act, moral effort, or cultural alignment can make you right with God; only faith in Christ does.
  - "If you add anything to faith in Jesus to earn God's approval, you are calling Christ a liar and rejecting the gospel."
  - The law exists to show us we cannot keep it, driving us to Christ for mercy.
  - "Once justified by faith, you live for God's will not to earn His love, but because He has already given you everything in Christ."

audience:
  - "You feel pressure from the media, your community, or even the church to prove you're good enough by aligning with certain causes or keeping certain rules."
  - "You're exhausted trying to earn God's approval and wondering if you'll ever measure up."
  - You want clarity on what the gospel actually says about how you are made right with God.
  - You need to be reminded that your identity and acceptance before God rest entirely on Christ, not on your performance.

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You are justified — declared righteous before God — by faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by anything you can do, and that changes everything about how you live.
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 2:16"
keyScriptureText: Yet we know that a person is not justified by works of the law but through faith in Jesus Christ. So we also have believed in Christ Jesus in order to be justified by faith in Christ and not by works of the law, because by works of the law, no one will be justified.

mainPoints:
  - title: Justification means God declares you righteous.
    body: It is not about who you are; it is about how God sees you because of Jesus.
  - title: Paul repeats the gospel three times in one verse.
    body: You are justified by faith in Christ, not by works — a general truth, a personal truth, and a universal negative.
  - title: If justification required the law, Christ would be a servant of sin.
    body: To add anything to faith in Jesus is to call Him a liar and reject the gospel.
  - title: Jesus taught and demonstrated justification by faith.
    body: The Pharisee trusted in himself and was not justified; the tax collector confessed his sin and was. The thief on the cross was saved by faith alone in his final moments.

keyIllustration: "The structure of the New Testament itself is a three-part witness to the gospel. The books of the apostles who walked with Jesus, the writings of Paul who received the gospel directly from the risen Christ, and the letters of James, Peter, John, and Jude — three independent sources with the same message: you are justified by faith in Christ alone."

application:
  - Stop trying to prove yourself to God, the media, or anyone else. You are already accepted by faith in Christ.
  - Do not add works, causes, or cultural alignment to the gospel. Christ alone is enough.
  - "Live for God's will not to earn His love, but as a response of gratitude for the righteousness He has already given you."

toRemember: "God looks at you and says, \"There is a good person\" — not because of anything you have done, but because you believe in His Son, Jesus Christ."
