mainPoints:
  - title: "The early church had a rhythm: gather, go, gather again."
    body: They came together to be empowered by the Holy Spirit and by each other, then went out into the world to preach, heal, and testify. When they faced persecution, they came back to encourage one another and were strengthened to go out again — a pattern like a heartbeat.
  - title: They were one heart and soul because they loved Jesus above everything else.
    body: Being a believer meant loving Christ so fully that worldly wisdom, personal possessions, and selfish ambition became secondary. That love levelled the ground and created deep unity.
  - title: "This wasn't communism — it was Christian charity at its finest."
    body: "Giving was voluntary, not compulsory. Believers didn't see their possessions as their own but as resources for the whole community. Distribution was made according to need, not enforced equality."
  - title: The same power at work in the early church is at work today.
    body: You may not have the apostles, but you have the Holy Spirit. The power to preach, to serve sacrificially, to love radically is still available — you just have to be willing to be used.

keyIllustration: "Imagine liquidating everything you own — your house, your car, your savings — and bringing it all to church next week in cash. Picture everyone doing the same: some arriving with trucks full of money, others with wheelbarrows, some with just an envelope. Then imagine pooling it all together to meet every need in the community. What would that do to your heart? What would it do to the church? There would be no needy person among you. You'd be free from the system of always wanting more."

application:
  - "Read your Bible from beginning to end — not just for inspiration, but to know the full story of God's work in the world."
  - "If you're not tithing, start. It's biblical, and there's power in giving that changes both you and the community around you."
  - "Join a home group. That's where you can share your doubts, fears, and struggles with others on the same journey."
  - "Go out into the world and live what you've learned — then come back and share what God did, what you learned, and what hurt. Build that rhythm of going and gathering."
