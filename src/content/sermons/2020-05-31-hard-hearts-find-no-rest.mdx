# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Hard hearts develop when we focus on our circumstances instead of God's faithfulness, and the consequence is forfeiting the rest He offers us both now and eternally."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 3:7-11 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Therefore, as the Holy Spirit says, \"Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion, on the day of testing in the wilderness, where your fathers put me to the test and saw my works for forty years. Therefore I was provoked with that generation, and said, 'They always go astray in their heart; they have not known my ways.' As I swore in my wrath, 'They shall not enter my rest.'\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Rebellion leads to a hard heart.
    body: When we turn our backs on God and blame Him for our circumstances instead of trusting Him, we harden our hearts against Him.
  - title: Testing God leads to a hard heart.
    body: "Israel experienced God's deliverance through the plagues and the Red Sea, yet still demanded proof He would provide—this lack of trust hardens us."
  - title: Letting your heart go astray leads to hardness.
    body: When we focus on circumstances instead of on God, we become overwhelmed and turn to other sources of security.
  - title: "Not knowing God's ways leads to a hard heart."
    body: "Despite seeing God's faithfulness daily in the wilderness, Israel still didn't trust Him when it mattered—knowing about God is not the same as trusting Him."

keyIllustration: "The twelve spies returned from Canaan carrying an enormous bunch of grapes on a pole, describing a land flowing with milk and honey. The people's excitement turned to terror when the spies reported walled cities and giants. Despite everything God had done—delivering them from Egypt, parting the Red Sea, providing manna and water—they believed the giants instead of God. Their hard hearts cost them the promised land."

application:
  - "When fear or despair creeps in, repent immediately—turn away from focusing on the crisis and turn back to God's character and promises."
  - "Replace fear with Scripture by reading God's word daily, especially the Psalms and Gospels, allowing His promises to refocus your faith."
  - Remember the worst that can happen is stepping through death into eternity with Jesus—no more pain, suffering, or tears.

toRemember: "You don't know what tomorrow holds, but you know who holds tomorrow—and He has never failed you."
