# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Isaiah 53 describes Jesus' death with such precision that it proves both His identity as the Servant and the full weight of what His death accomplished—paying the debt for our sin, transgression, and iniquity."
keyScriptureRef: "Isaiah 53:4-5 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Surely he has borne our griefs and carried our sorrows; yet we esteemed him stricken, smitten by God, and afflicted. But he was pierced for our transgressions; he was crushed for our iniquities; upon him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with his wounds we are healed.

mainPoints:
  - title: The prophecy is unmistakably about Jesus.
    body: Isaiah switches from future to past tense, describing a beaten, unrecognizable figure who will be mourned one day—precisely what happened at the cross and what will happen when Israel finally recognises Him.
  - title: "Jesus' death paid for three distinct failures."
    body: "Sin (missing the mark), transgression (willful rebellion), and iniquity (our broken, unequal nature)—Scripture names all three, and Jesus' death covers all three."
  - title: "\"It is finished\" means the debt is paid in full."
    body: "The moment Jesus died—pure, sinless, and obedient—the price was complete. You don't carry guilt or shame anymore because He already removed it."

keyIllustration: A jumping spider mimics an ant so perfectly that other ants walk past it without a second glance. Familiarity breeds blindness. After decades of Easter celebrations, we can glance at the cross and think we know what it means without ever really seeing the full weight of what Jesus carried.

application:
  - Confess openly, like David did in Psalm 51—naming your sin, your rebellion, your broken nature—knowing the debt is already paid.
  - "Stop carrying guilt and shame. Jesus said \"It is finished.\" That means you walk free."
  - Let the cross produce grief over what your sin cost, but let it end in joy over what His death accomplished.

toRemember: "It was your sin that put Him on the cross—but His last words were not \"Look what you did to Me.\" They were \"It is finished.\""
