  - title: "What we must do:"
    body: "Ask \"What does this mean?\" and make Jesus president of our hearts, not just resident — fanning His flame to life in everything we do."

  - "Don't explain away God's power with worldly wisdom. When you encounter Him, ask \"What does this mean?\" and let the answer change you."

  - "Earnestly desire the gifts of the Spirit. Don't choose which ones — ask God to empower you as He sees fit, and watch your work become your ministry."
