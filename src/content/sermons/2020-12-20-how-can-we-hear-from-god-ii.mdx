# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You cannot hear God if you centre your life on yourself — even if that self-centredness wears religious clothing.
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 2:36-38 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And there was a prophetess, Anna, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. She was advanced in years, having lived with her husband seven years from when she was a virgin, and then as a widow until she was eighty-four. She did not depart from the temple, worshiping with fasting and prayer night and day. And coming up at that very hour, she began to give thanks to God and to speak of him to all who were waiting for the redemption of Jerusalem.

mainPoints:
  - title: The Pharisees did religious things for selfish reasons.
    body: They prayed to be seen, fasted to be admired, and sought honour rather than God — so their ears were closed when the Messiah arrived.
  - title: Anna devoted herself to God, not good deeds.
    body: She worshiped with fasting and prayer night and day, not for applause but to know God and hear His voice.
  - title: Hearing God requires knowing His Word.
    body: "Anna and Simeon recognised Jesus because they knew Isaiah's prophecies about the comfort and redemption God promised — they were waiting because they'd been listening."
  - title: "True devotion isn't altruism — it's \"theorism.\""
    body: Living for God (not for others or yourself) is what opens your ears. When you centre your life on pleasing Him, obedience and love for others flow naturally.

keyIllustration: "A skydiving instructor and his student's parachute failed. As they plummeted, the instructor positioned himself underneath to take the impact, crushing every bone in his spine while saving her life. Philosophers debate whether true altruism exists — whether anyone ever acts without selfish motive. But Scripture reveals a different category: theorism. Jesus didn't die for us out of altruism; He died in obedience to the Father, for God's glory. When we live for God rather than ourselves or even others, we hear Him — and love flows from that."

application:
  - "Examine your motives: Are you doing good things to be noticed, or because you love God and want to obey Him?"
  - Devote time to prayer, fasting, and Scripture — not as religious performance, but to actually know God and hear His voice.
  - "Wait for God's promises by studying them. Anna and Simeon recognised Jesus because they knew Isaiah. Do you know what God has promised?"

toRemember: "The world may not notice your devotion to God, but He does — and when you centre your life on Him, you'll recognise His work when everyone else misses it."
