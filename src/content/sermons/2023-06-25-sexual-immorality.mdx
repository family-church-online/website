# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Sexual immorality is so detestable to God that it must not even be named among believers — and the only real defense is a heart filled with gratitude for what Christ has done.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 5:3-4 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But sexual immorality and all impurity or covetousness must not even be named among you, as is proper among saints. Let there be no filthiness, nor foolish talk, nor crude joking, which are out of place, but instead let there be thanksgiving.

mainPoints:
  - title: Sexual immorality is anything that causes sexual arousal outside of marriage between a man and a woman.
    body: This includes sex before marriage, adultery, pornography, LGBTQ lifestyles, and even the lust of the heart Jesus condemns in Matthew 5. Media, entertainment, and social platforms are saturated with temptation.
  - title: The world has moved sexual sin from shameful to celebrated.
    body: What was once hidden is now normalized and celebrated. Biblical truth is rejected, corrected, and commiserated. This process has accelerated with movements like LGBTQ+, and Christians are being made to feel ashamed of Jesus instead of sin.
  - title: The real danger is covetousness — greed for sexual sin.
    body: "When lust becomes a pursuit, it becomes idolatry. Broken marriages, broken children, bitterness, and even murder (through abortion) flow from sexual immorality. It's a cycle that damages people and perpetuates itself."
  - title: Gratitude is the antidote to greed and lust.
    body: "When you live with a spirit of thankfulness for what God has given you — especially Jesus — you won't be driven by lust to pursue what you don't have. A life of gratitude naturally pulls you away from sexual immorality."

keyIllustration: "Two poor, freezing children are invited inside by an old woman on a stormy night. She warms them by the fire and gives them hot cocoa. The little girl stares at the cup and saucer, then tells the woman, \"Your cups match your saucers.\" After they leave, the woman realizes how rich she truly is — not in wealth, but in blessings. She leaves the muddy footprints on her floor as a reminder never to forget how blessed she is. Gratitude transforms how we see our lives."

application:
  - Examine your life — your media, entertainment, social feeds, fantasies — for patterns of sexual immorality. Flee from them.
  - Start practicing gratitude daily. Remember what Christ has done. Let thankfulness drown out the voice of greed.
  - Stop being ashamed of Jesus. The world will reject biblical truth, but holiness is still your calling as a saint.

toRemember: When you live a life of gratitude, sexual immorality will flee from you.
