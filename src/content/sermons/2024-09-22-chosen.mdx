# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God chose you before the world began—not when you got noticed, not when you were given responsibility, but before anything existed. Your value is rooted in His sovereign choice to love you and call you to believe, not in the recognition or position others give you.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 1:3-4 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places, even as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and blameless before Him.

mainPoints:
  - title: Faithfulness matters more than fame.
    body: Matthias followed Jesus from His baptism to His ascension without title or recognition, yet when the moment came, God revealed he had been chosen all along.
  - title: God values belief, not position.
    body: Judas was one of the Twelve yet never believed; Matthias was overlooked yet clung to faith. Where you stand with God depends on whether you believe, not what responsibilities you carry.
  - title: The Holy Spirit sustains you while you wait.
    body: "The eleven weren't alone between Ascension and Pentecost—Jesus had already breathed the Spirit on them. His presence was with them even before His power was poured out on Pentecost."
  - title: Scripture governs everything, even painful things.
    body: "Peter reminded the believers that Judas' betrayal wasn't an accident—it was prophesied. God's sovereign plan doesn't lose control, even when everything looks like it's falling apart."

keyIllustration: "A diver separated from his group underwater can feel utterly alone, yet his air supply never stops flowing. The presence of oxygen doesn't depend on his ability to see the surface—it's already there, keeping him alive. The disciples waited for ten days between Ascension and Pentecost, but they weren't alone. Jesus had already breathed the Holy Spirit on them. They had His presence; they were waiting for His power."

application:
  - Stop measuring your worth by who notices you or what title you carry. God chose you before the world began—not when you finally got recognized.
  - Hold on to belief, not achievement. Judas had position and responsibility but no faith. Matthias had faithfulness and belief, even without recognition.
  - "Gather together in obedience, worship, and prayer while you wait. That's not passive—it's exactly what God calls you to do until He reveals what's next."

toRemember: "Who cares who knows your name? What matters is whether you've believed, because God chose you—and that choice was never in question."
