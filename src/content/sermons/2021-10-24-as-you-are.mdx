# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God isn't waiting for you to be holier or stronger. Through Christ, you are already pleasing in His sight—and He is working in you right now to make that experientially true."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 13:20-21 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Now may the God of peace who brought again from the dead our Lord Jesus, the great shepherd of the sheep, by the blood of the eternal covenant, equip you with everything good that you may do his will, working in us that which is pleasing in his sight, through Jesus Christ, to whom be glory forever and ever. Amen.

mainPoints:
  - title: God is working in you now—not later.
    body: "Not when you pray more, sin less, or feel ready. Now. Whatever you've brought into this place, God is already at work through your weakness."
  - title: The God of peace equips you through Christ.
    body: "He doesn't just give you tools and tell you to get to work. He furnishes you completely—the tools, the talent, and the desire to obey."
  - title: Faith in Christ is what pleases God.
    body: "Without faith, it is impossible to please Him. Your best efforts without Christ are like dirty rags, but your faith in Him is good and pleasing in God's sight."
  - title: What is true in Christ will become experientially true in you.
    body: "You've been purified by faith. God is working in you until you know it, until you are transformed into the image of Christ."

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "Stop waiting to be \"ready enough\" for God to use you. He meets you as you are."
  - "When you confront your weakness in obedience, remember: God places the desire in you, gives you the power to obey, and then rewards you for it."
  - Hold fast to your confession of Jesus as the Son of God. That faith—not your performance—is what is pleasing in His sight.

toRemember: "Your belief in Jesus Christ is enough. Hold on to that. Whatever's waiting for you beyond these doors, God is working in you right now—as you are."
