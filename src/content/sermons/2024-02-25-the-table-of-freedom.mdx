# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The lie that we can earn God's approval through our works is so powerful that even the apostle Peter fell for it — but salvation comes only through faith in what Christ has already done."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 2:14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But when I saw that their conduct was not in step with the truth of the gospel, I said to Cephas before them all, \"If you, though a Jew, live like a Gentile and not like a Jew, how can you force the Gentiles to live like Jews?\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "Peter's hypocrisy threatened the gospel."
    body: When he withdrew from the Gentiles out of fear, he enacted the lie that faith in Christ alone is not enough — that works and ethnic identity still matter for salvation.
  - title: The lie of self-salvation is seductive because it feels easier than following Jesus.
    body: Working for approval lets us hide our sin; following Christ means acknowledging it, battling it, and cutting it out.
  - title: We must fight for truth in love, not with hard hearts.
    body: "Paul's rebuke was public because the whole church was at risk, but it was done out of love — evidenced by Peter's later writings affirming Paul's apostleship."

keyIllustration: A North Korean father was taken away after authorities found a Bible buried in his garden. Before his arrest, he had taught his daughter that Christians are the salt of the earth — but if we lose our love for others, even our enemies, we lose our saltiness and our humanity. In the face of constant oppression and the temptation toward bitterness, he showed that love is what preserves our witness.

application:
  - When you or others slip into performance-based faith, call it out — but stick to the facts, ask questions, and correct in love rather than making it personal.
  - Be gentle with yourself when you fail; the fruit of the Spirit includes gentleness toward your own heart, not just toward others.
  - Ask God daily to equip you with what you need to fight for truth without losing compassion — these are supernatural capacities we cannot manufacture on our own.

toRemember: "We don't work for our salvation, but when we are saved, we are saved for works — to labour for His kingdom while resting in His finished work on the cross."
