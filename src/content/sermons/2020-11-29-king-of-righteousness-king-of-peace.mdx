# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Peace comes not from chasing comfort or seeking relief from anxiety, but from humbly trusting the eternal King of Righteousness—Jesus Christ—who gives you His righteousness as a gift and overwhelms every anxious thought with His presence.
keyScriptureRef: "Philippians 4:6-7 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.

mainPoints:
  - title: Righteousness must precede peace.
    body: "Melchizedek's name means King of Righteousness, then King of Peace—peace only comes when we trust the One who makes us righteous, not through our own merit but through Christ's finished work."
  - title: "Abraham's humility models our response to Jesus."
    body: After his greatest military victory, Abraham gave a tenth of everything to Melchizedek, received his blessing, and acknowledged him as greater—this is how we are to approach Jesus, the eternal High Priest.
  - title: Pride robs us of peace.
    body: Whether after victory or during crisis, our temptation is to rely on ourselves or others rather than God. Only when we submit everything to Christ—giving Him the glory—do we experience lasting peace.
  - title: Gratitude and submission overwhelm anxiety.
    body: "When we focus on Christ's greatness, trust His righteousness, and practice thanksgiving, His peace overwhelms every anxious thought and guards our hearts."

keyIllustration: "A dive master worked for hours with a young girl who could never descend past five meters due to sinus pain and her father's constant criticism. Finally, holding her securely and monitoring depth carefully, he got her past five, then ten, then fifteen meters—and she didn't panic. When she realized what she'd done, the look in her eyes was unforgettable. But immediately after, another diver delivered a humbling rebuke about pride. The illustration shows that we need God's peace not just in crisis, but especially after victory when pride tempts us to trust ourselves instead of Him."

application:
  - Stop seeking peace for its own sake. Instead, focus on Jesus, the King of Righteousness. Submit to Him, give Him the glory, and trust His ability to make you righteous—His peace will follow.
  - When anxiety overwhelms you, respond with gratitude and thanksgiving. Acknowledge that Christ is greater, that He has no beginning or end, and that He deserves everything you have.
  - Be especially vigilant after spiritual victories. Pride can steal your peace faster than crisis can. Humble yourself before Christ and give Him all the glory.

toRemember: The peace of God which overwhelms every thought will guard your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus when you trust the King of Righteousness with everything.
