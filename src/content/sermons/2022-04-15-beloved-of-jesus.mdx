# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "John's three questions at the cross—why didn't Jesus save Himself, who should pay, and how can life have meaning now—reveal that Jesus died in our place, we put Him there, and His love defines us even in devastation."
keyScriptureRef: "1 John 1:1-3"
keyScriptureText: That which was from the beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen with our eyes, which we looked upon and have touched with our hands, concerning the word of life—the life was made manifest, and we have seen it, and testify to it and proclaim to you the eternal life, which was with the Father and was made manifest to us—that which we have seen and heard we proclaim also to you, so that you too may have fellowship with us; and indeed our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ.

mainPoints:
  - title: "He saved others—why didn't He save Himself?"
    body: "Jesus didn't save Himself because He came to die as the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring us to God through His atoning sacrifice."
  - title: "Who's to blame for this brutal injustice?"
    body: We put Him there—not just Judas or the Pharisees, but all of us, because the Lord laid on Him the iniquity of us all.
  - title: How can life have meaning now?
    body: The love of Jesus defines us and gives life meaning; because He lives in us through His Spirit, we can love one another and His love is made complete in us.

keyIllustration: "John's mind drifted to Lazarus' tomb, where Jesus wept and then commanded the stone to be rolled away despite the stench of death. That same grinding sound now sealed Jesus in His own tomb—but this time, there was no voice thundering for Him to come out."

application:
  - Your sin—no matter how great—is what Jesus came to pay for; you are the reason He refused to save Himself.
  - When life collapses and nothing makes sense, your identity as one beloved by Jesus remains the anchor that holds.
  - Because God so loved us, we are called to love one another, making His love complete and visible in the world.

toRemember: "If anybody does sin, we have one who speaks to the Father in our defence: Jesus Christ, the Righteous One."
