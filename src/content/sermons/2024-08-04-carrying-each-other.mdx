# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When you're saved by grace, you stop comparing yourself to others and start carrying their burdens—because that's exactly what Jesus did for you."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 6:2-5 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ. For if anyone thinks he is something, when he is nothing, he deceives himself. But let each one test his own work, and then his reason to boast will be in himself alone and not in his neighbor. For each will have to bear his own load."

mainPoints:
  - title: Restore gently, not publicly.
    body: When someone sins, go to them privately first to help them carry the burden—not to expose or shame them (Matthew 18).
  - title: Bearing burdens fulfills the law of Christ.
    body: "Love God with everything and love your neighbor as yourself; when you help carry someone's struggle, you're living out both commands."
  - title: "Don't compare—test your own work."
    body: "Your value isn't found by rating yourself better or worse than others; it's found in faithful stewardship of what God has given you."
  - title: Sow to the Spirit, not the flesh.
    body: "How you spend your time and resources today determines what you'll reap eternally—invest in the kingdom, not just earthly comfort."

keyIllustration: "A runner collapses 600 meters from the finish line after running 89 kilometers in the Comrades Marathon. A stranger stops, picks them up, and carries them across—saving them from wasting everything. That's the church: some are collapsing in sight of the finish line, and we're called to carry them, not to judge them."

application:
  - "Ask someone in your life right now: \"How can I help you carry what you're carrying?\""
  - "If you're the one collapsing, cry out—let your church family know you need help."
  - "Examine how you spend your time and money: are you investing in eternity or just earthly comfort?"

toRemember: "You will receive your reward in due time if you do not give up—so don't waste the race when the finish line might only be 600 meters away."
