# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: To love God is to obey Him. To obey Him, you must first honor His Word. Discipleship begins with daily devotion to Scripture, not weekly attendance.
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 28:19-20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.

mainPoints:
  - title: Honor and obey Scripture first.
    body: You cannot love God if you do not revere His Word. Obedience begins with reading Scripture regularly and treating it as God speaking directly to you.
  - title: Love God by trusting, obeying, and enjoying Him.
    body: Loving God is not merely emotional, nor is it simply following rules. It is both—trusting His Word, obeying His commands, and delighting in His presence.
  - title: Make disciples by teaching obedience.
    body: The Great Commission is not fulfilled by good deeds alone. We make disciples by teaching others to obey all that Jesus commanded, starting with ourselves as models.
  - title: The church is daily, not weekly.
    body: The early church devoted themselves to Scripture, prayer, worship, and service every day. The modern church cannot be alive if it only gathers once a week.

keyIllustration: "A teacher named Pamela Cantor faced lockdown with students who had no internet access. She asked friends to record children's books as WhatsApp voice notes. The idea went viral, reaching thousands of children across the country. The people who recorded one story and passed it on were not the church—they were participating in a good work. Pamela was the church: she saw the need, initiated the solution, and mobilized others. The church doesn't wait for instructions—it sees needs and makes disciples who reach people who reach people."

application:
  - Read Scripture daily. Even twenty minutes a day will take you through the entire Bible in less than two years. Download a Bible reading plan or use the YouVersion app.
  - "Ask yourself: Am I seeking God's approval or people's approval? God's standard does not change. When you pursue His approval through obedience, you are free from the exhausting task of pleasing others."
  - "Make disciples intentionally. Don't wait for a church program to do it for you. Start with one person. Teach them to obey what Jesus taught. Model daily devotion to Scripture, prayer, worship, and service."

toRemember: If we all devoted ourselves daily to Scripture, prayer, worship, and making disciples, the world would know the church is alive and powerful.
