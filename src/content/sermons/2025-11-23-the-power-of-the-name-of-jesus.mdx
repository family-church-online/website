bigIdea: Only those in relationship with Jesus can exercise His authority—religion, ritual, and substitutes are powerless against the enemy and will leave you exposed, ashamed, and wounded.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 19:15-16 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But the evil spirit answered them, \"Jesus I know, and Paul I recognize, but who are you?\" And the man in whom the evil spirit was leaped on them, mastered all of them, and overpowered them so that they fled out of that house naked and wounded."

mainPoints:
  - title: God does extraordinary miracles through ordinary believers.
    body: "Luke, a doctor who had seen countless miracles, calls these \"extraordinary\"—not because God's power had increased, but because He was working in culturally specific ways (through Paul's garments) to confront Ephesus' obsession with charms and magic."
  - title: Demons know the difference between pretence and authority.
    body: The evil spirit recognised Jesus and Paul, but exposed the seven sons of Sceva as frauds. They had no relationship with Christ, only a desire to profit from His name—and the demon overpowered them, leaving them naked, wounded, and ashamed.
  - title: "Burn the bridges to whatever you've trusted instead of Jesus."
    body: The Ephesian believers publicly burned fifty thousand pieces of silver worth of occult materials. When you come to Christ, you must destroy—not merely discard—anything that once held spiritual authority in your life, so you can never return to it.
  - title: The word of God prevails mightily over all evil.
    body: "The same Greek word used to describe the demon \"overpowering\" the exorcists is used to describe the word of the Lord \"prevailing\" over Ephesus. Evil overpowers those without Jesus; the word of God overpowers everything."

keyIllustration: "A diver separated from his group and camouflaged against a clay bank stood motionless as another person walked right up to him—then shot him with paint at point-blank range. The illustration demonstrates Satan's strategy: to operate undetected, wreaking havoc in believers' lives while remaining camouflaged, because if you don't recognise the problem, you will never solve it."

application:
  - Identify anything you have relied on more than Jesus—medication, substances, apps, rituals, charms, horoscopes, spiritism—and make the decision to physically remove it and spiritually renounce it. Burn the bridge.
  - Stop trying to fight spiritual battles in your own strength or through religious formulas. Only a living relationship with Jesus gives you authority over the enemy.
  - Recognise the difference between godly fear (which humbles you and draws you to God) and ungodly fear (which shames you and drives you away). The enemy uses fear to isolate; God uses fear to connect.

toRemember: Jesus always wins, and He wins forever—whatever loss you suffer is temporary and will be used for an eternal victory that only He can accomplish.
