# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Your words carry the power to give life or destroy it—and through the Holy Spirit, you are called to wield that power as God intended: to build up His people, not tear them down."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 4:29-30 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Let no corrupting talk come out of your mouths, but only such as is good for building up, as fits the occasion, that it may give grace to those who hear. And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.

mainPoints:
  - title: Corrupt speech is the perversion of what God created speech for.
    body: "Corrupt (Greek sapros : rotten, putrid) includes crude jokes, empty conversation, careless words, and jokes at others' expense. Jesus warned we will give account for every careless word. Corrupt speech reveals a corrupt heart and has the power to destroy lives—through bullying, cutting words, and irreversible damage."
  - title: Edifying speech is what we were created for.
    body: Building up and edifying speech strengthens confidence, self-esteem, and faith in those who hear it. It is appropriate to the occasion and imparts grace—a gift from God, seasoned with salt.
  - title: Corrupt speech grieves the Holy Spirit.
    body: "The Holy Spirit is not a force but a person who feels grief. He cannot tolerate uncleanliness and unholiness. The fact that He grieves means He loves us—and though we grieve Him, we are sealed for redemption, which speaks of God's grace and mercy."
  - title: Corrupt speech produces bitterness, wrath, anger, slander, and malice.
    body: "Bitterness is smoldering resentment from unwillingness to forgive. Wrath is violent outbursts. Anger cherished becomes identity. Slander injures another's good name. Malice is wishing evil on others. These are the natural outcomes of corrupt talk."
  - title: Kindness, tenderheartedness, and forgiveness are supernatural.
    body: "These are not our default—they require the Holy Spirit working in us. Kindness is affection for others like family. Tenderheartedness is compassionate willingness to bear others' burdens. Forgiveness flows from understanding what Christ did for us while we were still sinners."

keyIllustration: "Nick Vujicic, born without arms or legs, faced brutal bullying at school. One day, after deciding to end his life if anyone insulted him again, a girl called out to him—and instead of more abuse, she said, \"Nick, I just wanted you to know, you look great today.\" He didn't take his life that day. Today Nick travels the world as an evangelist, telling his story and pointing people to Jesus. That is the power of building up speech."

application:
  - "Ask God to search your heart for bitterness, anger, and patterns of corrupt speech—not just profanity, but careless words, jokes at others' expense, and cutting remarks."
  - Seek to understand the forgiveness Christ extended to you while you were still a sinner. The depth of that understanding unlocks your ability to forgive and speak life to others.
  - Rely on the Holy Spirit daily to use your words as God intended—to give life, build up the Church, and impart grace to those who hear.

toRemember: In your mouth exists a power from the dawn of time—God gave it to you to give life, not take it.
