# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Scripture is not just inspired—it is God-breathed, and when you honor and obey it, God's voice becomes louder than every wave of fear, deceit, or loss that threatens to overwhelm you."

mainPoints:
  - title: "God's word has creative power."
    body: When God speaks, things happen—light exists, seas part, storms calm. Scripture carries that same authority because it is His voice.
  - title: Scripture is God-breathed, not just inspired.
    body: "It doesn't merely contain God's word—it is God's word. Men spoke as they were \"carried along\" by the Holy Spirit (2 Peter 1:21)."
  - title: We must honor Scripture, not just obey it.
    body: You can obey out of fear or duty, but honoring it means recognizing its worth and submitting joyfully because it is from God.
  - title: Scripture equips us for righteousness and good works.
    body: "It leads us to salvation and then trains us for godly living. Righteousness brings peace—God's voice silences the waves."

keyIllustration: "A father watches his three-year-old daughter pulled into the ocean by a wave. He runs in but loses sight of her in the white foam. In desperation, he cries out to Jesus—and something tells him to look left. A small hand surfaces just long enough for him to dive and grab her. Even when you can't see clearly, God's voice can break through if your heart is soaked in His word."

application:
  - "Turn off the noise. Make God's word louder than the news, social media, or fear by committing to daily Scripture reading."
  - "Honor God's promises as if they are what they claim to be—His very breath, His living voice speaking directly to you."
  - "Don't wait for the storm to hit. Soak your heart in Scripture now so that when waves come, you know where to look."

toRemember: "When the storm rages and the waves overwhelm, remember this: God's voice is louder—and He speaks through His word."
