# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The belt of truth is not about your truthfulness—it's about knowing and holding fast to the unchanging truth of who God is, what He has done, and what He has promised, so you can stand firm when Satan lies about His character."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 6:14-15 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Stand therefore, having fastened on the belt of truth, and having put on the breastplate of righteousness, and, as shoes for your feet, having put on the readiness given by the gospel of peace.

mainPoints:
  - title: You are in a war, whether you acknowledge it or not.
    body: "Christians who fail to put on the armor aren't neutral—they're exposed. The belt of truth is the first piece of armor you must have on at all times to remain ready."
  - title: "Satan's primary tactic is accusing God's character."
    body: "From Eden to the wilderness, the enemy's goal has been to make you doubt whether God is good, loving, or trustworthy. Every lie starts there."
  - title: "The belt is God's truth, not yours."
    body: "You cannot defend yourself by claiming you are truthful. You defend yourself by knowing what Scripture says about God's Word, His character, and His power."
  - title: "You must memorise and meditate on God's truth daily."
    body: "Jesus defeated temptation by saying \"It is written.\" You must know Scripture well enough to answer the lie before you even recognise the attack."

keyIllustration: A Roman soldier at rest could remove his helmet, shield, and sword. But he never removed his belt, sandals, or breastplate—because he had to remain ready for an ambush. The belt of truth is not something you pick up when you feel attacked. You wear it constantly, or you will be caught unprepared.

application:
  - "When you wake up depressed or angry, first ask: \"Am I believing a lie about God's character?\" Remind yourself immediately of what Scripture says He is—merciful, gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love."
  - "Memorise Exodus 34:6-7. Learn to pray it when emotions overwhelm you. Declare God's character out loud when Satan whispers that He has forgotten you."
  - "Trust is built on three pillars: God's Word (He cannot lie), God's character (He is good), and God's power (He is able to fulfill His promises). Test your faith regularly against these three."

toRemember: "The most important thing about you is what you think about God—because if you don't know Him for who He truly is, you are already on the path of destruction."
