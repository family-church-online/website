# ── PRESENTATION COPY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
tagLine: If God can break apostles out of prison, He can break through what divides us.
shortDescription: "You've been chosen, redeemed, adopted—but now what? God hasn't called you into comfort; He's called you into a life empowered by the Holy Spirit to love people you wouldn't naturally choose, to forgive what feels unforgivable, and to build a church that looks nothing like the world around you."
subtitle: "The Christian life is not about earning God's favour—it's about responding to what He's already done with every ounce of power He's already given."
hook: "How do you live in a way that matches what God has already accomplished in you? Paul moves from three chapters of doctrine to three chapters of duty—but the hinge is power. Without the Holy Spirit, you'll be worn down by people and demons. With Him, you'll do more than you can imagine."

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God has done everything to save you, adopt you, and empower you—so now live in a way that reflects the magnitude of what He's already accomplished, trusting His Spirit to do what you cannot."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 4:1-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: I therefore, a prisoner for the Lord, urge you to walk in a manner worthy of the calling to which you have been called, with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love, eager to maintain the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Paul writes from prison—but it's not Rome's prison."
    body: "He's only there because Jesus allows it. The same God who broke apostles out of sealed cells has Paul exactly where He wants him. If God is for you, who can hold you?"
  - title: Christianity is backwards from every other religion.
    body: "We don't obey to earn God's favour—we obey because He's already given it. Grace comes first. Gratitude follows."
  - title: "You've been called into a body, not a club."
    body: "The word for church (ekklesia) means \"the called out ones.\" God initiated. You responded. Now walk worthy of that calling—with humility, patience, and sustained love for people who will frustrate you."
  - title: Unity requires Holy Spirit power, not compatibility.
    body: "Paul prayed that the church would be \"strengthened with power through His Spirit\" because loving each other long-term—especially when it's hard—is impossible in human strength alone."

keyIllustration: "William Wilberforce fought slavery in Britain's parliament for years until he was ready to quit. On his deathbed, John Wesley wrote to him: \"Unless God has raised you up for this very thing, you will be worn out by the opposition of men and devils. But if God be for you, who can be against you?\" Wesley died days later. Wilberforce kept that letter for forty-five more years of opposition—until three days before his own death, slavery was abolished."

application:
  - "Stop trying to manufacture Christian behaviour in your own strength. Ask God daily to fill you with the Holy Spirit's power to love, forgive, and persevere."
  - "Check your humility before you correct someone. Jesus says take the log out of your own eye first—not because you shouldn't help others, but because you can't do it rightly without acknowledging your own need."
  - "Fight for unity in your church. That means putting others' needs first, forgiving seventy times seven, and letting worship style or personal preference take a back seat to the bond of peace."

toRemember: The same power that breaks prison doors can bind believers together—if we ask for it, trust it, and stop grieving the Spirit with pride and division.
