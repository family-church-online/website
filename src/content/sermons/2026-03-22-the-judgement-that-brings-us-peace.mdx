# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When you grasp that God's judgment is certain and comprehensive—seeing every lie, every injustice, every motive—you discover the peace that outlasts any earthly courtroom, because the only verdict that matters has already been settled by Jesus."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 10:19-20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: When they deliver you over, do not be anxious how you are to speak or what you are to say, for what you are to say will be given you in that hour. It is not you who speak, but the Spirit of your Father speaking through you.

mainPoints:
  - title: Hatred can never rest until it destroys.
    body: "High priest Ananias walked 150 kilometers and hired a lawyer to prosecute one man—consumed by a need for vengeance that only God's wrath can satisfy. Christians are commanded to never avenge themselves, but to leave justice to God."
  - title: The Holy Spirit is your Advocate.
    body: "Paul refuted three capital charges clinically and calmly—not because he was trained, but because the Spirit gave him the words in that moment. Tertullus, the hired Roman lawyer, was flattened in one sentence by God's Advocate."
  - title: There is no injustice for a Christian, only delayed justice.
    body: "God sees every lie, every corrupt motive, every plot. When you trust that His judgment is coming and comprehensive, you can sit in the courtroom and know that the real trial is not yours—it's theirs."
  - title: Judgment is the source of peace.
    body: "Felix was alarmed when he heard about righteousness, self-control, and coming judgment—because he realized he stood condemned. But for those who trust in Jesus, judgment means we will never face God's wrath. That certainty is the only peace that outlasts injustice."

keyIllustration: "A near-miss in traffic shakes you—but you only fully appreciate what you've avoided if you've been in a wreck before. When you know the pain of the tow truck, the insurance, the body shop, a close call fills you with relief. In the same way, you cannot appreciate Jesus' sacrifice unless you grasp the judgment you've been saved from."

application:
  - "When you face false accusations, remember: the Holy Spirit will give you the words. You are not alone in that courtroom."
  - "If you're driven by a need for revenge, release it. God says, \"Vengeance is mine, I will repay.\" You will never have peace until you hand justice to Him."
  - "If you're watching injustice unfold, trust that God sees every detail. No lie is hidden from Him. His judgment is certain, comprehensive, and perfectly fair."

toRemember: For a Christian, there is no injustice—only delayed justice. And because Jesus took your judgment, you are free to speak life even to those who accuse you.
