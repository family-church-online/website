# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's law and God's grace are not contradictions — they are two stages of one rescue plan. The law convicts you of sin and restrains you until you come to faith, and grace saves you and brings you into sonship with God."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 3:21-22 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Is the law then contrary to the promises of God? Certainly not. For if a law had been given that could give life, then righteousness would indeed be by the law. But the scripture imprisoned everything under sin, so that the promise by faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe.

mainPoints:
  - title: The law cannot give eternal life.
    body: "God never promised that obedience to the law would save you. The law's job was to diagnose the disease of sin, not to cure it."
  - title: The law convicts and restrains us until faith comes.
    body: Like a strict guardian temporarily placed over a child, the law holds us accountable and keeps us from destroying ourselves — but it was never meant to be permanent.
  - title: Faith in Christ removes the guardian and makes us sons.
    body: "When you believe in Jesus, you are no longer under the law's condemnation. You are brought into a living, loving relationship with God as His beloved child."

keyIllustration: "Two wealthy men came to Jesus. The rich young ruler asked, \"What good deed must I do to have eternal life?\" Jesus said, \"Keep all the commandments perfectly.\" The man said he had — but when Jesus told him to sell everything and follow Him, he walked away. He couldn't do it. Zacchaeus the tax collector, despised and rejected, climbed a tree just to see Jesus. Jesus called him down and said, \"I'm coming to your house today.\" Zacchaeus didn't claim to be good — he confessed his sin and gave away half his wealth in repentance. Jesus said, \"Today salvation has come to this house.\" The difference? One tried to earn acceptance. The other received grace."

application:
  - "If you do not have peace that you are saved, it's likely because you are still trying to prove yourself to God rather than resting in what Christ has already done."
  - "Stop asking \"What must I do?\" and start believing \"It is finished.\" Eternal life is not earned by performance — it is received by faith."
  - You are loved like a child by a Father who adores you, not hired like an employee who must perform to stay employed.

toRemember: "The law shows you you're trapped. Grace says you're free. Stop trying to earn what's already been given."
