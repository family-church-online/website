keyScriptureText: "For Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, that He might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh but made alive in the spirit… when God's patience waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was being prepared, in which a few, that is eight persons, were brought safely through water."

mainPoints:
  - title: "Christ's patience in suffering models God's heart."
    body: Jesus endured unjust suffering—not because He deserved it, but to reconcile the unrighteous to God. His resurrection proves the sacrifice was effective.
  - title: "God's patience in Noah's day gave humanity 120 years to repent."
    body: Noah was a herald of righteousness, building the ark and preaching while the world ignored him. God delayed judgment to give every opportunity for repentance.
  - title: "Baptism pictures our participation in Christ's death and resurrection."
    body: "It is not a ritual that saves, but a visible appeal to God for a good conscience through faith in Jesus' finished work."
  - title: Patience is sustained by faith, not wishful thinking.
    body: "Noah believed God's word and acted on it for decades. His patience came from trusting that God rewards those who seek Him."

keyIllustration: "Three families—the Napiers, Dixons, and Smallridges—demonstrate the power of godly patience and forgiveness. After Eric Smallridge killed Megan Napier and Lisa Dixon while driving drunk, their parents endured years of pain and anger. Eventually, Eric's genuine repentance moved the families to forgive him. They then pleaded in court for his sentence to be reduced. Today, Eric travels with Renee Napier, teaching others about forgiveness and the dangers of drunk driving. Their witness shows that patience rooted in God's mercy transforms even the deepest injustice."

application:
  - "Trust that God's justice is coming—His patience is not indifference, and He will settle all accounts in His time."
  - "Get busy with the work God has given you while you wait. Noah's patience was sustained by purposeful obedience."
  - Pray for mercy for those who sin against you, knowing that you too have been shown undeserved patience.

toRemember: "If God could use the death of two young women to produce an extraordinary witness of forgiveness, imagine what He can do with your circumstances if you trust Him and stay faithful to the work He's called you to."
