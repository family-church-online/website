# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: A living church is a growing church, and growth happens day by day in homes devoted to Scripture, prayer, worship, and service.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 2:42-47 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And they devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers. And awe came upon every soul, and many wonders and signs were being done through the apostles. And all who believed were together and had all things in common, and they were selling their possessions and belongings and distributing the proceeds to all, as any had need. And day by day, attending the temple together and breaking bread in their homes, they received their food with glad and generous hearts, praising God and having favor with all the people. And the Lord added to their number day by day those who were being saved."

mainPoints:
  - title: The early church was devoted to four daily practices.
    body: Scripture, fellowship, breaking bread, and prayer — this was not a weekly rhythm but a daily devotion that shaped their entire lives.
  - title: Jesus builds His church through the Holy Spirit.
    body: Growth is not a human achievement. As living stones, we are being built together into a dwelling place for God by the Spirit.
  - title: Daily growth requires both public and private worship.
    body: The early church attended the temple and broke bread in homes. A resilient church meets together and disciples daily in households.
  - title: The Lord adds to the church as we obey the Great Commission and Great Commandment.
    body: When the church is alive — devoted, generous, praying, worshipping — God adds to its number day by day those who are being saved.

keyIllustration: "A virus replicates, consumes resources, and responds to stimuli — but biologists still debate whether it's truly alive. The same question applies to the church: How do we know a church is alive? A living church grows. It reproduces. It responds to the Holy Spirit. And just as a body renews itself cell by cell, the church grows day by day as the Lord adds those who are being saved."

application:
  - Gather your family around the table this week. Break bread together. Worship together. Read Scripture and pray together — not just on Sundays, but daily.
  - "Look for opportunities to be the church where you are: with neighbors, security guards, shop attendants, colleagues, even telemarketers."
  - Ask the Lord to show you where your home can become an outpost of the Kingdom — a place where the gospel advances day by day.

toRemember: "The church that survived two thousand years of persecution didn't do so because of buildings or budgets — but because homes became sanctuaries where the Word was taught, prayers were offered, and the Lord added daily to their number."
