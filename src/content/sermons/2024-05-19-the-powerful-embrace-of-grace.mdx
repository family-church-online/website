# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Grace does what law can never do: it not only restrains sin, but transforms us into Christ's image, unites us in His body, and empowers us to live beyond mere obedience out of gratitude for what He has done."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 3:26-29 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "For in Christ Jesus you are all sons of God, through faith. For as many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ. There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus. And if you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's offspring, heirs according to promise."

mainPoints:
  - title: We become sons of God through faith alone
    body: — Not through circumcision, not through keeping the law, but by believing what God has promised, just as Abraham did.
  - title: "Being \"in Christ\" has four dimensions"
    body: "— We are in His body (the church), in His death and resurrection, clothed in His righteousness, and positioned under God's unconditional promises to Abraham."
  - title: Grace erases every human barrier
    body: — Under law, Jew, Greek, slave, free, male, and female were treated differently; under grace, all are saved the same way—by faith—and united as one in Christ.
  - title: "Baptism follows belief, it doesn't cause it"
    body: — Paul assumes every baptised person has already been saved by faith; baptism is the public sign of what grace has already accomplished.

keyIllustration: "A father watches his son's sports match and becomes furious with the umpire. The law restrains him—he doesn't verbally attack the official. But then he watches another coach use the same unfair situation to build up his players and teach them character. The first father only avoided sin; the second went beyond restraint to do something good. That's the difference between law and grace."

application:
  - "When someone wrongs you, don't just restrain yourself—ask how you can reflect Christ's character in that moment."
  - "Remember that your first identity is Christian, not employee, parent, or citizen—wear Christ's name before any other badge."
  - "If you've believed in Jesus, baptism is your next step: the public declaration that you belong to Him and His church."

toRemember: "Grace doesn't just stop you from sinning—it lifts you to do great and wonderful things out of gratitude for what Jesus has done for you."
