# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God measures your life not by what you achieve, but by how you love the people no one else sees.
keyScriptureRef: "James 1:27 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Religion that is pure and undefiled before God the Father is this: to visit orphans and widows in their affliction, and to keep oneself unstained from the world."

mainPoints:
  - title: The Heart and Works of Tabitha
    body: She sewed clothes for widows—not a grand act, but deeply personal care that met essential needs and restored dignity. Her life was the gospel lived out in small, tangible ways.
  - title: The Faith Response of the Community
    body: "When Tabitha died, her community didn't prepare a burial—they prepared for a miracle. They acted with audacious faith, complete surrender, and dependence on God, sending for Peter expecting the impossible."
  - title: "God's Response and the Bigger Picture"
    body: God raised Tabitha, transformed Joppa from a place of rebellion into obedience, and prepared the way for the gospel to spread to the Gentiles. He wastes nothing—not even suffering—when we live in surrender.

keyIllustration: "A lifeguard cannot rescue someone who is still struggling to stay in control. Before entering the water, they must see complete surrender from the victim—either crying for help or already drowning. If the victim fights, both can drown. God works the same way: He does His best work when we stop trying to control the situation and fully surrender."

application:
  - Look around you—there are people with real needs right now. A kind word, a hug, a small act of service can restore dignity and point someone to Christ.
  - Stop chasing empires and titles that mean nothing. Use your gifts to build an empire of love through small, personal acts of kindness.
  - When life falls apart, invite God in with complete surrender—not as a consultant you control, but as the One who takes over entirely.

toRemember: Strive to live a life so full of Christ that when you die, people miss you so much they are drawn to the One you worshipped.
