# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God created you to work—not as punishment, but as an act of worship—and Christ's sacrifice redeems your daily labor so that every task, no matter how small, can honor Him and reflect your eternal inheritance."
keyScriptureRef: "2 Thessalonians 3:6-8 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you keep away from any brother who is walking in idleness and not in accord with the tradition that you received from us. For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we were not idle when we were with you, nor did we eat anyone's bread without paying for it, but with toil and labor we worked night and day, that we might not be a burden to any of you."

mainPoints:
  - title: "Work is part of God's original design."
    body: Before the fall, God worked—creating, sustaining, judging, redeeming—and He commanded Adam and Eve to have dominion over creation. Work became toilsome after sin, but it was never punishment. It is woven into the fabric of creation.
  - title: Paul modeled work as ministry.
    body: Though entitled to financial support, Paul worked with his hands (likely as a tentmaker) to set an example. He commanded the idle to stop being busybodies and earn their own living, warning that those who refuse to work should not eat.
  - title: The cross transforms work into worship.
    body: "Jesus took the world's dysfunction—cruelty, betrayal, injustice—and transformed it into love. Because you are adopted into God's family through the cross, you now work for an eternal inheritance, doing all things heartily as to the Lord, not to man."
  - title: You must choose your work ethic.
    body: "The world's ethic is self-focused, leading to corruption and entitlement. The Christian ethic is other-focused and God-glorifying, rooted in the hope of resurrection and the reality that Christ has redeemed even your Monday mornings."

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "Stop waiting for a \"spiritual\" job to honor God—He is glorified when you serve Him through any honest work, whether you're teaching children, building software, or stacking shelves."
  - "If you're able but idle, repent. Freeloading off others—or the state—dishonors the God who worked to save you and the community called to reflect His character."
  - "Reframe your workday: you are not ultimately answerable to your boss but to Christ, and your paycheck is a foretaste of the inheritance waiting for you in glory."

toRemember: Whatever you do, do it heartily as to the Lord—because the cross has redeemed not just your soul, but your workweek.
