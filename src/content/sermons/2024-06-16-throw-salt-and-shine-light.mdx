mainPoints:
  - title: Be Salt
    body: Salt was priceless in the ancient world — it preserved meat from decay, added flavor, and created thirst. Christians are to preserve truth, add zest to human society, and create a longing in others to know Jesus. But salt that loses its flavor is worthless and discarded. We lose our saltiness when we refuse to share our testimony and the good news of what God has done for us.
  - title: Be a City on a Hill
    body: Just as the cities around the Sea of Galilee — Capernaum, Tiberias, Magdala — could not be hidden, the gospel living inside believers cannot be concealed. Your faith is meant to be visible, not private.
  - title: Be Light
    body: "When you bring light into darkness, darkness cannot remain. Christ lives inside you, and His light must shine through you. It doesn't matter what job you hold — street sweeper or surgeon — what matters is that you do your work with all your heart and let God's glory shine through faithful obedience."

keyIllustration: A counselor from a drug rehab center visited a school and shared his brutal, difficult testimony about addiction and recovery. Afterward, students lined up to meet him — not because of eloquent theology, but because of the power of his story and his testimony that the only way he recovered was through God. The kids needed to hear that testimony, and it opened doors no prepared lesson could.

application:

  - Stop worrying about your career path and start focusing on how you do your work. Do it with all your heart, knowing Jesus has you exactly where He wants you.
  - "Don't hide your light. When you follow Jesus, let His light shine through every conversation, every task, every ordinary moment of your life."
