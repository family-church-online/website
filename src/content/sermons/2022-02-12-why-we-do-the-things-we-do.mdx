# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You recognize authentic gospel ministry by what it costs the messenger—and you become an authentic messenger by imitating those who paid the price.
keyScriptureRef: "1 Thessalonians 2:9-12"
keyScriptureText: For you remember, brothers, our labor and toil. We worked night and day that we might not be a burden to any of you, while we proclaimed to you the gospel of God. You are witnesses, and God also, how holy and righteous and blameless was our conduct toward you believers. For you know how, like a father with his children, we exhorted each one of you and encouraged you and charged you to walk in a manner worthy of God, who calls you into his own kingdom and glory.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Work: They labored at personal cost."
    body: Paul supported himself night and day so the gospel would be free. A message that costs the hearer more than the messenger is suspect.
  - title: "Walk: They lived transparently among believers."
    body: Their conduct was holy, righteous, and blameless—open to scrutiny. False teachers hide the details of their lives because those details contradict their message.
  - title: "Words: They used language that exhorted, encouraged, and charged."
    body: True ministry convicts the conscience, heals it with grace, then commissions the hearer to go and do likewise. Anything less is incomplete gospel.

keyIllustration: "The Piltdown Man fraud took 41 years to expose—a human skull fitted with an orangutan jaw, treated to look ancient. Scientists who questioned it early were silenced by majority opinion. The fraud persisted because there was no genuine \"missing link\" to compare it against. The lesson: you identify counterfeits by knowing the real thing intimately, not by studying fakes."

application:
  - "Test teaching by comparing it to the pattern Paul set: Does it cost the teacher? Is their life open to examination? Do their words convict, comfort, and commission?"
  - "See your everyday work as the platform that funds and authenticates your gospel witness—your job isn't a distraction from ministry; it is part of your ministry."
  - Walk in a way worthy of Christ in the mundane moments—traffic, difficult conversations, mundane tasks—because someone is always watching, and your behavior either confirms or contradicts the message.

toRemember: "Christ is returning, and when He does, He will say, \"Come into the kingdom prepared for you.\" Live today in a way that makes you ready—and helps someone else get ready too."
