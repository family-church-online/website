# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When everything is uncertain and you're terrified of tomorrow, the answer to \"will I be okay?\" is found entirely in who Jesus is—not what will happen, but who is walking with you through it."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 5:7-10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: In the days of his flesh, Jesus offered up prayers and supplications, with loud cries and tears, to him who was able to save him from death, and he was heard because of his reverence. Although he was a son, he learned obedience through what he suffered. And being made perfect, he became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey him, being designated by God a high priest after the order of Melchizedek.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus suffered and cried out to God—and was heard.
    body: He knows what it is to lie awake in agony, weeping, begging God for deliverance. His prayers were answered—not always immediately, but always perfectly.
  - title: Jesus learned obedience through suffering and became the perfect sacrifice.
    body: He was always perfect, but through His suffering He became the perfect sacrifice —sinless, tested, able to pay the full price for our sin.
  - title: Jesus is the source of eternal salvation to all who obey Him.
    body: Genuine faith always leads to obedience. If you know Him, you will trust Him. If you trust Him, you will obey Him.

keyIllustration: An elephant was discovered standing over the carcass of another elephant, gently lifting its feet, chasing away birds, blowing clouds of ash or mist over the body. It seemed to grieve—to fight death itself. Like us, creation knows something is deeply wrong about death. But unlike the elephant, we can know the One who defeated it.

application:
  - Stop asking what , when , how . Start asking who . Everything about your tomorrow depends on whether you truly know Jesus.
  - "Walk through the four steps to knowing Him: (1) Find out who He is by reading Scripture, (2) Accept Him for who He is, not who you want Him to be, (3) Trust His promises, (4) Obey Him out of love and gratitude."
  - If you know Jesus, it will be okay—not because nothing bad will happen, but because He will be with you, He will not waste your suffering, and death has lost its power over you.

toRemember: Knowing Jesus is the best way to live—and the only way to die.
