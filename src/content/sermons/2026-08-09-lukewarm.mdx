# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: A comfortable, going-through-the-motions faith is not acceptable to Christ — He calls the lukewarm to wake up and repent while there is still time.
keyScriptureRef: "Revelation 3:15-16 (ESV)"
keyScriptureText: "I know your works: you are neither cold nor hot. Would that you were either cold or hot! So, because you are lukewarm, and neither hot nor cold, I will spit you out of my mouth."

mainPoints:
  - title: Know who is speaking.
    body: Christ introduces Himself as the Amen, the faithful and true witness, and the maker of all things — so when He addresses us, we must listen.
  - title: Lukewarm faith is unacceptable.
    body: "Spiritual indifference and going through the motions earned Laodicea Christ's sharpest words and, uniquely among the seven churches, no commendation at all."
  - title: Comfort can blind you.
    body: The wealth they trusted bred pride and self-satisfaction, leaving them unable to see they were spiritually wretched, poor, and naked.
  - title: His remedy is zealous repentance.
    body: "Christ counsels them to \"buy\" true riches at the cost of their pride and to turn back quickly — precisely because He loves them."

keyIllustration: "A well-known nineteenth-century painting, \"The Light of the World,\" shows Christ standing outside a long-shut door, lantern in hand. The door has no handle on the outside — it can only be opened from within — picturing Christ patiently knocking and waiting for someone to let Him in."

application:
  - "If your faith has cooled into routine, don't wait — respond to Christ's call to repent today, while the door is still open."
  - "Take an honest look at whether comfort, success, or pride has quietly taken God's place in your life."
  - Treat Christian fellowship as essential, not optional — we are meant to build one another up, not coast alone.

toRemember: "Christ's rebuke to the lukewarm is not rejection but love — He disciplines those He loves and still stands at the door, knocking."
