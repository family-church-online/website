# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "No one's faith can save another person except Christ's, but a believer's faithful life and persistent prayer create the holiest environment for God to draw an unbeliever to salvation."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Corinthians 7:14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For the unbelieving husband is made holy because of his wife, and the unbelieving wife is made holy because of her husband. Otherwise your children would be unclean, but as it is, they are holy.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Paul's teaching carries divine authority."
    body: "When Paul writes \"I, not the Lord,\" he is not speaking opinion—he is distinguishing between quoting Christ's earthly teaching and delivering new revelation by the Holy Spirit. All Scripture is God-breathed."
  - title: "Only Christ's faith can save another."
    body: "The paralytic's friends brought him to Jesus, but his own faith was required for forgiveness. Christ alone is the mediator whose sacrifice covers sin. No believer's faith can substitute for another's repentance and belief."
  - title: Believers sanctify the home, not the reverse.
    body: "The early church feared contamination from unbelieving spouses. Paul reverses the logic: the believer is salt and light, creating a purified space where the unbeliever has the greatest opportunity to encounter Christ."
  - title: Faithful living and persistent prayer are your tools.
    body: You cannot force salvation, but you can live in obedience and pray without ceasing. These are powerful and effective, and God honours them in His timing.

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - If you love an unbeliever, do not give up praying or living faithfully before them—your life may be the clearest gospel they ever see.
  - If you are considering marriage, choose a believer. Being unequally yoked invites unnecessary pain, even though God can work in any situation.
  - Trust that God desires all people to be saved, and He will honour your faithfulness even when you see no change.

toRemember: How do you know, wife, whether you will save your husband? Or how do you know, husband, whether you will save your wife?
