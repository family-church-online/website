As I pray this morning, I'll invite you, just at the end, to submit your own prayers from the bottom of your hearts. Oh, great King, we thank you so much for your word. We thank you for who you are. We thank you for work. You gave work to us as a gift. It was corrupted, and you used the gift to bring us back to you. We pray for each of us gathered here today, as employees and employers, that you'll show us and convict us deep in our hearts how to live out our faith in the workplace. Help us to take Jesus with us to work consciously, an act of knowing that he is there. Help us, Almighty God, to develop an eternal perspective on our work, that we are not just working for the here and now, for our salaries at the end of the month. We are working for you. Help us to consider that. Help us to realize what that means. Help us each to make the changes in our own lives that we need to, so that we can live out your word. Convict us all. And above all, for each of us, Father, bring us closer to you. Show us the things that disappoint you and displease you and that separate us from you. And bring us into your loving care, so that we can understand your love for us with everything that we are. Because when we realize that, everything changes. I pray that for all of us gathered here today, Father, let us know your love for us that is beyond our comprehension. Let us understand enough that will convict us and equip us to live Christian lives in every aspect, including the workplace. I invite you now all to say your own prayers in the quiet of your hearts. Almighty God, we lift up these prayers to you from the bottom of our hearts, and we ask that you'll hear them in the faithful name of your Son, our Lord Jesus Christ. Amen.
