# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The name of Jesus doesn't just carry power to heal bodies — it has the authority to redefine your entire identity, because His name means God is with you, God saves you, and God has given you a purpose beyond survival."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 3:6-7 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Peter said, \"I have no silver and gold, but what I do have I give to you. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk.\" And he took him by the right hand and raised him up, and immediately his feet and ankles were made strong."

mainPoints:
  - title: "The name of Jesus reveals who He is: fully God, fully human."
    body: "Jesus means \"God saves.\" Christ means \"Anointed One.\" Nazareth means He chose humiliation and poverty. His name carries the authority of the God who said \"I AM\" — eternally self-existent, all-powerful, all-sufficient."
  - title: "The healing isn't about legs; it's about identity."
    body: "This man was known only as \"the one who was born lame.\" His disability defined him. Peter didn't give him money or mobility first — he gave him Jesus. The miracle was proof that his identity had been rewritten."
  - title: "Being a witness starts with something simple: making eye contact."
    body: "Peter and John didn't preach at him. They looked at him. They saw him. Witnessing begins when you stop avoiding people and start seeing them as God does."
  - title: Your identity must be rooted in what Jesus did for you, not what you do for Him.
    body: "The man didn't run off to show his friends. He ran into the temple to worship. That's the response of someone who knows they've been given Jesus, not just a miracle."

keyIllustration: "A Brazilian Olympic skateboarding medalist was forbidden from mentioning Jesus by name during her interview. As the camera focused on her, she pointed upward, then traced a path with her finger — \"Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life.\" The gesture went viral. People all over the world asked what she meant. Ordinary believers began explaining it online, testifying to what Jesus had done for them. One small act of witness sparked a wildfire of gospel conversations."

application:
  - "Stop letting your limitations, failures, or circumstances define you. Your identity is \"saved by Jesus\" — everything else is secondary."
  - "Don't underestimate the power of making eye contact, asking someone if they're okay, or offering to pray. Witnessing often starts with the smallest act of seeing."
  - "When you pray — especially in public — pray in Jesus' name. It's not magic; it's a declaration of who you belong to and what He's done for you."

toRemember: At the name of Jesus, every knee will bow. Let yours bow in gratitude now, because one day every knee will bow — either in worship or in terror.
