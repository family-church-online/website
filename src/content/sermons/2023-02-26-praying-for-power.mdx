# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's power is unlimited, but the church will only access it when we stop relying on our own strength, stop chasing power for pride, and start praying together for God's glory alone."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 3:20-21 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Now to Him who is able to do far more abundantly than all that we ask or think, according to the power at work within us, to Him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus throughout all generations forever and ever. Amen.

mainPoints:
  - title: "The seed of power is Christ's love for you."
    body: "Before you can pray for power, you must first be overwhelmed by the magnitude of Christ's love—His sacrifice, His knowledge of you by name, His gift of salvation. When that love moves from your head to your heart, power begins to flow."
  - title: "The gauge of God's power: immeasurably more than you can think."
    body: God can do more than the wildest thing you can imagine. Stop planning in your own wisdom and start trusting His.
  - title: "The grace of God's power: given in proportion to His infinite supply."
    body: "God doesn't give the church a little power from His reserves—He gives according to the infinite power He has. That's an unlimited resource."
  - title: "The goal of God's power: the glory of the Father, in the Son, through the Holy Spirit."
    body: Power is never for entertainment, ego, or credentials. It exists so that people see God and want to know Him.
  - title: "The ground of God's power: the church, united with Christ."
    body: "God's power flows through Christ and His bride, the church. You cannot operate alone. You need the body."

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "Take time this week to meditate on the breadth, length, height, and depth of Christ's love for you. Ask God to make it real to your heart, not just your mind."
  - "Stop trying to generate power through programs, personality, or performance. Pray for God's power instead."
  - "Commit to being part of the body. Show up. Serve. Share your gifts. God's power doesn't flow through isolated individuals."

toRemember: The foolishness of God is wiser than human wisdom, and the weakness of God is stronger than human strength—so stop relying on your own and start praying for His.
