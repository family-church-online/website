# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus is not a failed Messiah—He sits enthroned in heaven with all authority, orchestrating every detail of history and every moment of your life for His glory.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 2:33-36 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Being therefore exalted at the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, He has poured out this that you yourselves are seeing and hearing. For David did not ascend into the heavens, but he himself says, 'The Lord said to my Lord, \"Sit at my right hand, until I make your enemies your footstool.\"' Let all the house of Israel therefore know for certain that God has made Him both Lord and Christ, this Jesus whom you crucified."

mainPoints:
  - title: The death and resurrection of Jesus were prophesied.
    body: Peter quotes Joel and the Psalms to show that God planned the cross, the empty tomb, and Pentecost long before they happened—none of it was an accident.
  - title: "Jesus' body did not see decay because death had no right to hold Him."
    body: Unlike David, whose tomb remained, Jesus was raised physically because He never sinned—Scripture itself proves it.
  - title: The ascension places Jesus on the throne with all authority.
    body: "He didn't just defeat death and leave—He was exalted to the right hand of God, where He intercedes and governs every event in heaven and on earth."
  - title: When you realize you killed Jesus, the only response is repentance and baptism.
    body: Three thousand people heard the truth, were cut to the heart, and walked into eternal life that day—not because of eloquence, but because the Holy Spirit met them at the gospel.

keyIllustration: "A pastor woke in the middle of the night violently ill, certain he wouldn't make it to his son's inter-school athletics meet the next day. After an hour of prayer in the dark, the nausea lifted just enough. He drove separately, watched his son's one race, then received a call—a beloved church member had passed. Because he wasn't locked on a bus until evening, he could leave immediately and be with the grieving family. Not even a stomach bug escapes God's sovereign plan."

application:
  - Stop interpreting your circumstances as evidence that God has failed—He is on the throne, and nothing is outside His care or control.
  - "Own the weight of the cross: your sin put Jesus there, and His love kept Him there until every price was paid."
  - "Repent and be baptized—these aren't optional extras but the fruit of genuine belief and the obedience that follows salvation."

toRemember: "Whatever you're going through, don't for a moment think He's not on the throne, orchestrating every single event for His will and His glory."
