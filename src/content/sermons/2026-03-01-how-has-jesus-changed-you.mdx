# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When Jesus truly changes you, the evidence is unmistakable: law becomes grace, rebellion becomes obedience, silence becomes witness, and what was wasted becomes redeemed for His kingdom."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 22:1"
keyScriptureText: Brothers and fathers, hear the defense that I now make before you.

mainPoints:
  - title: From Legalism to Grace
    body: Paul once used the law as a weapon of punishment; now he testifies to the gospel of the grace of God. A changed heart sees people through the lens of grace, not judgment.
  - title: From Self-Authority to Obedience
    body: "Jesus knocked Paul off his horse and told him, \"You will be told all that is appointed for you to do.\" True conversion moves you from being your own authority to humble submission under Christ."
  - title: From Silence to Witness
    body: Paul was appointed to be a witness of what he had seen and heard. When Jesus changes you, you cannot keep Him to yourself—witness may start quiet, but it grows irrepressible.
  - title: Relationships Will Change
    body: "Jesus told Paul, \"They will not accept your testimony.\" When Christ changes you, some friendships will fall away—not because you rejected them, but because you are no longer the same person."
  - title: God Wastes Nothing
    body: "Paul's Roman citizenship—seemingly irrelevant to his Jewish calling—became critical to his mission. Every skill, privilege, and even trauma in your past can be redeemed and repurposed by God."

keyIllustration: "A young man went on a company ski trip in Austria, living recklessly and drunk most of the time. On his second trip, standing on a snow-covered mountain peak above the clouds, he was struck by the beauty of creation and decided he no longer wanted to live that way. Without saying a word, he quietly stopped drinking and partying. Within two days, his seat on the bus was no longer saved, conversations became superficial, and friendships evaporated. The illustration shows how even subtle change—when it's real—causes those around you to respond, either drawing near or pulling away."

application:
  - "Ask yourself honestly: has Jesus changed you, or have you just become a nicer version of yourself? Look for the evidence—grace replacing judgment, obedience replacing defiance, witness replacing silence."
  - "If relationships have shifted since you began following Jesus, don't be surprised or ashamed. It's a sign that real transformation is taking place."
  - "Plead with God for change if you don't see it yet. Real transformation is His work, not yours—but you can ask Him for it, trust Him for it, and watch for it with patience and faith."

toRemember: Jesus wastes nothing—not your gifts, not your failures, not even your darkest years. When He changes you, He redeems it all for His kingdom.
