mainPoints:
  - title: God determines your value, identity, and purpose—not you.
    body: "A label tells you what something is worth, what it is, and what it's for. When you are recreated in Christ Jesus, God assigns all three. Your value comes from being loved by the God who gave Himself for you. Your identity comes from being His workmanship—intricately woven for beauty and usefulness. Your purpose comes from good works He prepared beforehand for you to walk in."
  - title: King Saul was anointed, given the Spirit, and given a new heart—but he never accepted his God-given label.
    body: "He remained \"little in his own eyes\" and constantly tried to prove himself or take control. The further your self-perception is from God's design, the less peace and power you will have."
  - title: Satan attacks your identity by questioning it, demanding you prove it, or offering shortcuts to your purpose.
    body: "Jesus defended His identity by trusting His Father's word, refusing to test God's love, and refusing to shortcut His mission through compromise. When tempted to prove yourself, take control, or force God's hand—alarm bells should ring."
  - title: Peter failed, denied Christ, and returned to his old life—but Jesus rebooted him at a charcoal fire.
    body: Jesus rolled time back to where Peter first met Him, asked him three times about his love, and recommissioned him. If your label is wrong, Christ offers a fresh start—not condemnation, but restoration and a new commission.

keyIllustration: A plushie named Jack Pickles was pulled from the jaws of a doberman not because of its market value, but because of who loved it. A father risked inconvenience and discomfort to rescue what was precious to his daughter. Your value works the same way—you are precious not because of what you can do, but because the God of the universe loves you and gave His Son for you.

application:
  - Ask God to show you if your self-assigned label matches who He says you are—and be willing to let Him tear off the wrong one.
  - Stop trying to prove your value or force God to demonstrate it—trust that you are as loved as Jesus Christ by the Father.
  - "If you've failed or misidentified yourself, confess it honestly and let Christ reboot you—restoration is always available at the fire."

toRemember: You do not need to prove who you are to anybody, because you are loved by the living God—and that is where your value, identity, and purpose come from.
