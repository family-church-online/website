bigIdea: "A new song is a fresh revelation of God's grace — it can happen with old words, old melodies, or no music at all, whenever you recognise His rescue in a new and powerful way."
keyScriptureRef: "Isaiah 42:5-10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Thus says God the Lord, who created the heavens and stretched them out, who spread out the earth and what comes from it, who gives breath to the people on it and spirit to those who walk in it: \"I am the Lord; I have called you in righteousness; I will take you by the hand and keep you; I will give you as a covenant for the people, a light for the nations, to open the eyes that are blind, to bring out the prisoners from the dungeon, from the prison those who sit in darkness. I am the Lord; that is my name; my glory I give to no other, nor my praise to carved idols. Behold, the former things have come to pass, and new things I now declare; before they spring forth I tell you of them.\" Sing to the Lord a new song, his praise from the end of the earth!"

mainPoints:
  - title: A new song comes from God, for God, and about God.
    body: It is always a response to His grace — not our creativity. Nine times Scripture commands it, always connected to creation, rescue, or justice.
  - title: "We deserve God's wrath, but Christ took it."
    body: The new song Isaiah announces is sung because Jesus opened the dungeon. God does not punish His children. If you trust in Christ, you will never face His wrath.
  - title: You choose the soundtrack to your year.
    body: When hardship comes, you can remember suffering or you can remember the God who carried you. Your response becomes the song others hear.

keyIllustration: "A painted reed frog burns twenty times its normal energy to sing its mating call every summer night near Kruger National Park. The call is deafening, yet invisible — the frog is tiny, brilliantly coloured, and nearly impossible to see. But when you hear that sound, you're home. It's the soundtrack to every family holiday, every moment of rest. The frog doesn't sing because life is easy. It sings because that's what it was made for. Your life is meant to be that song for someone else — exhausting, invisible, but unmistakable."

application:
  - "When darkness surrounds you this year, stop and ask: What has God created? What has He rescued me from? What justice has He promised? Let those three realities anchor your gratitude."
  - "Your response to hardship will be the song someone else remembers God by. Sing loudly. Sing when it costs you. That's how rescue spreads."

toRemember: When you look back on this year, you will remember either what happened to you or who carried you through it — and that memory will be the song you sing for the rest of your life.
