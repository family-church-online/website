# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The gospel—that Jesus died to pay for your sin so you can have grace and peace with God—is so precious that it's worth protecting at all costs, and so powerful that it can change any life completely."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 1:1-5 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Paul, an apostle—not from men nor through man, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised him from the dead—and all the brothers who are with me, to the churches of Galatia: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ, who gave himself for our sins to deliver us from the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father, to whom be the glory forever and ever. Amen."

mainPoints:
  - title: "Paul's authority came directly from Christ."
    body: "He didn't learn the gospel from other apostles—Jesus Himself appeared to Paul and taught him. When you read Scripture, God is speaking to you."
  - title: "Grace and peace are what's at stake."
    body: "Grace means God giving you what you don't deserve—forgiveness instead of punishment. Peace means you are no longer God's enemy; you are right with Him through faith in Jesus."
  - title: Jesus satisfied both justice and mercy.
    body: "God demands justice for sin, but in His mercy, He let Jesus take the punishment you deserved. No other religion solves this problem. You can't earn your way out or compare yourself to others—only the cross sets you free."
  - title: The gospel must not be diluted.
    body: "People will try to add rules, philosophies, or conditions to the gospel. Paul's passion was to protect believers from losing the freedom Christ bought for them. Don't let anyone steal that from you."

keyIllustration: "A three-year-old girl was pulled from the ocean after being swept into the waves. For three hours, she clung to her father with arms locked around his neck, legs wrapped tight, refusing to let go—even in the shower as he cleaned seaweed from her ears. That's how we must cling to the gospel: desperately, unshakably, refusing to let go no matter what."

application:
  - "When guilt or shame attack, go back to Romans 3 and remind yourself: Jesus paid it all—your debt is settled."
  - Stop trying to prove yourself to God. Confess your sin, trust that Christ has covered it, and live in gratitude.
  - Hold fast to the gospel. If someone teaches you must add anything to faith in Jesus to be saved, reject it.

toRemember: Christ died for you so that you would have peace with God—nothing and no one can take that away from you as long as you hold on to the gospel.
