mainPoints:
  - title: "The enemy attacks your mind with one lie: it's over."
    body: "When suffering drags on and God seems absent, Satan whispers that you've lost, that Christ isn't coming, that it's time to quit. The helmet of salvation exists because that attack is relentless—and predicted in Scripture."
  - title: "Salvation is bigger than today's rescue—it's eternity secured."
    body: "God sometimes delivers us from the fire like Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. But Stephen shows us the fuller picture: eyes fixed on Jesus, spirit safe in His hands, unshakeable even in death. That's the helmet at work."
  - title: Biblical prophecy is clicking into place right now.
    body: "Joel, Ezekiel, and Zechariah—centuries apart—foretold nations surrounding Israel, attacks from Lebanon and Gaza, and Christ's return to the Mount of Olives. The Mount hasn't split yet. The itinerary is unfolding. He is coming back."
  - title: "Live as if He's arriving soon—because He is."
    body: "If someone you loved deeply told you they were coming home after years away, you'd prepare. You'd make room. You'd plan. Jesus is returning. The question is: are you getting ready, or just killing time?"

keyIllustration: "In a grueling cricket match under extreme heat, the youngest player on the team turned to the oldest—cramping and collapsing at 81 runs—and said, \"Stop complaining. I'm here for you. You will not leave this ground until you've scored your hundred.\" The senior batter's focus shifted from his pain to the prize, and he surged from 81 to 109 in minutes. When your mind is fixed on how it ends, you find strength you didn't know you had."

application:
  - Write down three things you want to accomplish for Jesus before He returns—and read them often. Let His imminent return reorder your priorities, your calendar, and your courage.
  - "When the voice whispers \"it's over,\" preach the itinerary to yourself: Christ wins, Satan loses, death dies, and you go home forever. That's not wishful thinking—it's revealed truth."
  - "Your perseverance under pressure isn't just for you—it's a testimony. Stephen's faith at his execution may have planted the seed that turned Paul from executioner to apostle. Who's watching you hold on?"

toRemember: We know Jesus has won before we see the victory—and that knowledge gives us hardcore strength to finish the race.
