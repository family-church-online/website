# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Christ's victory over Satan was declared in Eden before the first blow was struck — the match was won before it began, and your freedom was secured before you ever felt enslaved."
keyScriptureRef: "Genesis 3:15 ESV"
keyScriptureText: I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring; He shall bruise your head, and you shall bruise His heel.

mainPoints:
  - title: God dealt with sin first.
    body: Before cursing Adam and Eve, God cursed the serpent — transforming Satan from the most prudent (arom) to the most cursed (aror) creature, condemned to eat dust as a symbol of total defeat.
  - title: The protoevangelium breaks the unholy alliance.
    body: "Genesis 3:15 is the first prophecy and the first gospel — God declares war on Satan, ending the alliance between serpent and man, and promising that Eve's offspring will crush the serpent's head."
  - title: "Jesus crushed Satan's head at Calvary."
    body: "Satan bruised Christ's heel through suffering and death, but Jesus — sinless — crushed Satan's head, stripping him of the power of sin and opening the way to adoption as sons."
  - title: This enmity is a gift, not a curse.
    body: "Our capacity to hate evil and struggle against sin is God's blessing to us — without it, there would be no salvation, only eternal bondage to Satan."

keyIllustration: "South Africa entered the 2019 Rugby World Cup final as underdogs — they had lost their opening match, adopted a controversial kicking strategy, and narrowly scraped through the semifinal. England, meanwhile, dominated their semifinal against New Zealand 19–7. But when the final kicked off, something had shifted. The Springboks dismantled England 32–12, winning by a margin unheard of in World Cup finals. Later, the documentary Chasing the Sun revealed how every previous match had been preparing them for this moment — the final was won before it even began. So too with Christ: every struggle, every prophecy, was building toward Calvary, where Satan's defeat was sealed before the battle ever looked fair."

application:
  - Hold onto the truth that the outcome was never in doubt — no matter what pain, injustice, or disappointment you face, your champion has already won.
  - Recognize that your struggle against sin is not evidence of failure but proof that God broke the alliance between you and Satan — you are no longer enslaved.
  - Celebrate this Advent season with the assurance that Christ has come, has set you free, and is coming again to finish what He started.
