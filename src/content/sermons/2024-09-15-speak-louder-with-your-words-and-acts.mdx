# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God calls us to persevere patiently in His power as He fulfills His plan—a plan that includes the salvation of Israel and the return of Christ, triggered when the last Gentile believes.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 1:8"
keyScriptureText: But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus worked by the Holy Spirit—and so must we.
    body: Everything Jesus did was empowered by the Spirit. He promises that whoever believes in Him will do the same works—and even greater works—because the same Spirit now dwells in us.
  - title: "Waiting isn't passivity—it's faithful preparation."
    body: "Jesus told the disciples to wait in Jerusalem for the Spirit's power. Waiting means doing the basics faithfully until God does the impossible, not sitting idle until we feel ready."
  - title: "God's plan for Israel hasn't failed—it's unfolding."
    body: A partial hardening has come upon Israel until the fullness of the Gentiles comes in. When the last Gentile believes, all Israel will be saved and Jesus will return.
  - title: Your witness matters more than you know.
    body: "One day, someone will witness to the final Gentile. That moment triggers the completion of God's redemptive plan. What if it's you? What if it's tomorrow?"

keyIllustration: "A pastor lost his voice to an unexplained condition that built scar tissue on his vocal cords. For three years, doctors couldn't help him. He lost his church, his income, his hope. On the brink of suicide, he decided to fulfill one last commitment: teaching a small group using an amplified microphone. That morning's lesson—planned four years earlier—was Psalm 103. As he reached the words \"He redeems your life from the pit,\" his voice cracked back into existence. The next day, doctors confirmed the scar tissue had vanished without explanation. He kept doing the basics faithfully until God did the impossible."

application:
  - "Don't quit. Ask God for strength for one more day of faithful witness—then another, and another—until Christ returns."
  - "Speak to the people He puts in your path this week. You don't know which conversation might be the one that completes His plan."
  - Trust that the same Spirit who raised Jesus from the dead is at work in you to accomplish what you cannot do in your own strength.

toRemember: "God is asking us to persevere patiently in His power until all His plans are complete—because that's exactly what He does with us."
