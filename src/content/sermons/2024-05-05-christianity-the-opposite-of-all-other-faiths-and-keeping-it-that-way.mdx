# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The law was added to reveal our sin and drive us to grace; obedience is the proof that we've believed the gospel, not the means by which we earn it."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 3:19 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Why then the law? It was added because of transgressions, until the offspring should come to whom the promise had been made, and it was put in place through angels by an intermediary.

mainPoints:
  - title: The Preciousness of the Gospel
    body: "The law was added to expose our sin, remind us of God's holiness, and show us our desperate need for a Savior—making the promise of grace infinitely precious."
  - title: The Permanence of the Gospel
    body: The law was temporary in purpose—it pointed us to Christ. The promise of grace through faith is eternal and cannot be undone by later conditions.
  - title: The Personal Nature of the Gospel
    body: God gave the promise directly to Abraham; the law came through angels and Moses. Grace is personal—God Himself makes you right with Him through faith in Jesus.

keyIllustration: "A diagnosis from a doctor only offends us if we think we're healthy. The law diagnoses our sin, and until we accept that diagnosis, the cure means nothing. Once we know we're sick, the gospel becomes the most precious gift imaginable."

application:
  - Stand firm on the gospel even when the world calls your convictions offensive—there is no other way to be made right with God.
  - "Obey God not to earn His favour, but out of gratitude for the grace you've already received through Christ."
  - "Live in a way that demonstrates the gospel is true: your obedience is proof that you believe Jesus died and rose for you."

toRemember: "The things you get wrong all the time will not be held against you, because Jesus already took the punishment—now go live like that's true."
