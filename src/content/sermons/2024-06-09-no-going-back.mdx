# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You cannot supplement Jesus with anything—not technique, not ritual, not spiritual wisdom from other sources—without rejecting Him entirely.
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 4:8-9 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Formerly, when you did not know God, you were enslaved to those that by nature are not gods. But now that you have come to know God, or rather to be known by God, how can you turn back again to the weak and worthless elementary principles of the world, whose slaves you want to be once more?

mainPoints:
  - title: Before Christ, you did not know God.
    body: No other religion leads to the Father. Whether through law-keeping or pagan practice, all paths outside Jesus are slavery to deception and demonic forces.
  - title: "You've been bought out of the cage—why crawl back in?"
    body: Christ paid for your freedom with His blood. Returning to law or false religion is choosing slavery over sonship.
  - title: Even small steps backward are deadly.
    body: "Observing festivals or adding spiritual techniques seems harmless, but it signals misplaced faith. You're trusting something other than grace."
  - title: "The warning is severe: you may lose everything."
    body: Paul fears his labour was in vain. If you add to Jesus, you push Him away. The gospel saves only those who hold fast to it.

keyIllustration: "A slave market: imagine being locked in a filthy cage, property of a master who works you to exhaustion. Jesus walks through the market, sees you, and buys you with His own blood. He unlocks the cage, sets you free, and says, \"Now serve Me out of gratitude.\" Then imagine turning around and climbing back into the cage, locking the door behind you. That's what it means to return to law or false religion after knowing Christ."

application:
  - When bad news comes, drop to your knees before Jesus first—then phone someone else.
  - "Test everything: where does this teaching come from? Who taught this technique? If it's not rooted in Christ and Scripture, reject it—no matter how well it seems to work."
  - "Repent immediately if you've mixed Christ with other spiritual sources. Reach through the bars of the cage and cry out to Him. He is faithful to come and get you."

toRemember: Nothing that works is worth what it will ultimately cost you—except Jesus, who already paid the full price.
