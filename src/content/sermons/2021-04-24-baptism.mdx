# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Baptism doesn't save you—faith does. But baptism is still a critical act of obedience and worship, publicly declaring what Christ has already done for you."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 3:26-27 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For in Christ Jesus you are all sons of God through faith. For as many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ.

mainPoints:
  - title: The new covenant replaces the Mosaic covenant, not the Abrahamic one.
    body: Infant baptism assumes baptism replaces circumcision as the covenant sign—but circumcision marked the eternal Abrahamic covenant, not the Mosaic one that has passed away.
  - title: You enter the new covenant by faith, not by birth.
    body: Under the old covenant, Israelites were born into the covenant community. Under the new covenant, you enter only through personal faith in Jesus Christ.
  - title: Baptism is a sign for those already saved.
    body: Scripture so closely associates baptism with salvation that it assumes anyone baptized has already been saved. The sign follows faith, not the other way around.
  - title: Baptism symbolizes death, burial, and resurrection.
    body: Immersion vividly pictures being buried with Christ in His death and raised with Him to new life—a declaration that cannot be made for an infant who has not yet believed.

keyIllustration: "A wedding ring is not what makes you married—it's the covenant promise between two people. You wouldn't give a child a wedding ring and say \"wear this until you're married one day,\" because the sign belongs to those already in the covenant. In the same way, baptism is the sign of a covenant you've already entered by faith, not a placeholder for faith that might come later."

application:
  - "If you've never been baptized as a believer—if the decision was made for you as an infant—consider being baptized now as an act of obedience and worship."
  - "If you have children, trust God's faithfulness to draw them to Himself. Bless them, pray over them, raise them in the knowledge of His love—but let baptism come when they believe."
  - "If you disagree with believer's baptism, you are still welcome at the table. This is a conviction held in grace, not a line drawn in the sand."

toRemember: Baptism is not what saves you—but it declares to the world the glory of what Jesus has already done.
