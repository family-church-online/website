# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Knowing God's will requires three commitments: being persuaded you want it, being prepared to live it daily, and persevering when obedience costs you something."
keyScriptureRef: "Jeremiah 9:23-24 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Thus says the Lord: \"Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom, let not the mighty man boast in his might, let not the rich man boast in his riches, but let him who boasts boast in this, that he understands and knows me, that I am the Lord who practices steadfast love, justice, and righteousness in the earth. For in these things I delight, declares the Lord.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "Are you persuaded you want God's will?"
    body: "You can't ask for direction if you're not committed to obeying it—even when it costs you wisdom, power, or wealth."
  - title: "Are you prepared to live God's will daily?"
    body: "Knowing His will isn't about big decisions; it's about practicing repentance, faith, baptism, the Holy Spirit, and daily surrender through prayer and fasting."
  - title: "Will you persevere in doing God's will?"
    body: "Obedience doesn't guarantee instant reward, but those who seek His glory by patience in well-doing will find the right doors open."

keyIllustration: "A family facing financial pressure started a home business after much prayer. Before launching, they felt convicted to ask their employer's permission—even though their contract didn't require it. They risked losing their reduced income entirely. The employer not only approved but offered to help set up parts of the business they hadn't arranged yet. The client who had withdrawn an order gave it back after hearing about their integrity. Obedience opened doors preparation alone couldn't."

application:
  - "Stop compartmentalising God's will for big decisions while ignoring it in daily obedience—fasting, prayer, and repentance train you to seek His kingdom first."
  - "Ask, seek, knock—but test every door against what God delights in: love, justice, and righteousness, not just what feels safe or profitable."
  - "When the right choice costs you something, trust that God's plans are better than yours—He withholds nothing good from those who love Him."

toRemember: "You cannot know God's will sporadically—you must choose it daily, prepare for it through repentance and faith, and persevere in it even when obedience hurts."
