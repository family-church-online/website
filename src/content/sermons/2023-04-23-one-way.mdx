# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "True unity in the church is built on the seven unities Paul declares: one body, one Spirit, one hope, one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father of all. Unity without theological fidelity is not unity—it's rebellion. The church must discern carefully what teaching it allows in, because false teaching always comes dressed in sheep's clothing."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 4:4-6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: There is one body and one Spirit—just as you were called to the one hope that belongs to your call—one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all.

mainPoints:
  - title: "One body and one Spirit prove God's love."
    body: Jesus prayed that the church would be one as He and the Father are one, so the world would know God sent Him and loves His people. The Trinity is both the model and the power source for unity.
  - title: One Lord, one faith, one baptism guard the gospel.
    body: Jesus is the only mediator. The faith was delivered once for all to the Saints. Any teaching claiming new revelation or requiring you to appeal to ancestors or Saints violates Scripture. One baptism marks entry into the church by repentance and faith.
  - title: One God and Father demands discernment.
    body: "False teaching often sounds biblical but subtly elevates human authority, denies Christ's divinity, or tells you suffering means you lack faith. Test everything against Scripture. Don't follow personalities or popular teachers—follow Christ."

keyIllustration: A devotional booklet was distributed at the church with solid content—until you reached the back. There, the acknowledgments listed teachers who deny core doctrines. The material itself had been sanitized, but the references sent readers directly to false teaching. The booklet was discontinued, not because every page was wrong, but because it opened a door to deception. False teaching is always subtle—it bleats like a sheep before it attacks like a wolf.

application:
  - "Know Scripture deeply enough to test what you read, listen to, and share. If you can't explain why something contradicts the gospel, you're vulnerable to compromise."
  - Reject any teaching that elevates human personality, claims new revelation, or tells you that suffering is always evidence of weak faith.
  - "Love people who hold false beliefs, but do not accept their doctrine. Compassion and clarity are not opposites—they're both necessary for guarding the faith."

toRemember: "Unity in the church is built on unity with God—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—and it's worth defending, even when it costs you popularity."
