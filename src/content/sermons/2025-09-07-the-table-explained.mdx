# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The table declares that God solved the impossible tension between justice and mercy by having His Son become the substitute—fully human to represent us, fully God to be sinless, and fully willing to die so we could live.
keyScriptureRef: "Ezekiel 36:26-27 ESV"
keyScriptureText: I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit I will put within you. And I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh. And I will put my Spirit within you, and cause you to walk in my statutes and be careful to obey my rules.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Eden's broken trust brought death into the world."
    body: "Adam and Eve's sin wasn't just disobedience—it was choosing to trust the serpent's word over God's, and that fracture in faith unleashed suffering, disease, and death across all creation."
  - title: Justice and mercy meet at the cross.
    body: God cannot ignore sin, nor can He stop loving us. The only way to satisfy both was for Jesus—sinless, human, and divine—to take our punishment in our place.
  - title: "You're offered a heart transplant, not a self-improvement course."
    body: "Ezekiel promised a new heart of flesh to replace the heart of stone. That's what happens when you trust Jesus: not just forgiveness, but transformation from the inside out."
  - title: "Jesus chose the cross—He wasn't forced."
    body: "He could have called down twelve legions of angels to rescue Him. He didn't. He chose your life over His own."

keyIllustration: "A man received a heart transplant from a 23-year-old who died of a heroin overdose. Two years later, guilt-ridden and reluctant, the recipient finally met the donor's parents. A photograph captures the moment: two grieving parents, stethoscopes pressed to the chest of the man their son saved, listening to their boy's heart still beating. The young man died on Good Friday. The transplant happened on Easter Sunday. It's an imperfect picture, but it points to the real thing—a Father watching His Son die so your heart could beat forever."

application:
  - "Stop carrying guilt for what grace already covered. If you've trusted Jesus, your sin was dealt with at the cross—not minimised, not overlooked, but fully punished in Him."
  - Communion is your weekly reminder that your life cost Jesus His. Let that reality fuel gratitude, not shame, and let gratitude fuel obedience.
  - "The new heart you've been given isn't just positional—it's practical. You can now want what God wants, empowered by His Spirit to live differently than you did before."

toRemember: If your heart cost the Son of God His life, use it today to live for the One who gave it to you.
