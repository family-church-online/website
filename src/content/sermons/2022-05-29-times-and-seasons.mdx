# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Because God is faithful and will fulfill His promise to establish Christ's kingdom on earth, believers must live awake, armored, and active—not as passive spectators, but as soldiers ready for battle."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Thessalonians 5:4-6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But you are not in darkness, brothers, for that day to surprise you like a thief. For you are all children of light, children of the day. We are not of the night or of the darkness. So then let us not sleep as others do, but let us keep awake and be sober.

mainPoints:
  - title: God controls times and seasons.
    body: "Scripture repeatedly shows that God sets up and removes kingdoms according to His plan—Daniel's interpretation of Nebuchadnezzar's dream, the restoration promised to Israel, and Jesus' own words confirm this sovereignty."
  - title: The day of the Lord is distinct from the rapture.
    body: "While the rapture brings rescue and joy, the day of the Lord brings judgment and wrath on God's enemies—sudden destruction like labor pains, unavoidable and intensifying."
  - title: Christ will establish a physical, earthly kingdom.
    body: "God's unconditional promise to Israel must be fulfilled; if He does not restore the kingdom to Israel as promised, His faithfulness is called into question. We trust His future promises because He keeps His past ones."
  - title: Believers are not destined for wrath, but for readiness.
    body: We belong to the day, not the night. Our daily posture is to wake up, put on the armor of faith, love, and hope, and live as soldiers—not comfortable spectators—until Jesus returns.

keyIllustration: "A soldier wakes at 5 a.m., not to comfort but to duty—inspecting boots, ironing gear, ready for what the day demands. Believers are called to the same readiness: every morning, we dress in spiritual armor—the breastplate of faith and love, the helmet of hope—because we are in a battle, not an audience."

application:
  - "Stop living for comfort. You are a soldier, not a spectator. The certainty of Christ's return should produce urgency, not apathy."
  - Put on your armor daily. Faith, love, and hope are not abstract ideas—they are the breastplate and helmet that protect you in the fight.
  - Encourage one another in the battle. When you see someone suffering for doing right, your response in love and faith strengthens the whole body.

toRemember: The day is coming when the Lord will establish His kingdom, judge His enemies, and reward His faithful. Until then, we are awake, we are armored, and we are busy doing what He has called us to do.
