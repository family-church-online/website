# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The Lord's table is not about getting the location or the liturgy right—it's about single-minded devotion to the God who rescued you when you were already dead."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Corinthians 10:16-17 ESV"
keyScriptureText: The cup of blessing that we bless, is it not a participation in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break, is it not a participation in the body of Christ? Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for we all partake of the one bread.

mainPoints:
  - title: You can break bread anywhere.
    body: The early church broke bread daily in homes and in the temple—what makes the table sacred is not the place or the person leading, but the heart of devotion to Christ.
  - title: The table demands exclusive loyalty.
    body: You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons—coming to this table is a public commitment that you belong to Jesus and Him alone.
  - title: Four directions to look when you come to the table.
    body: Look back (remember Jesus), look forward (anticipate the feast in eternity), look in (examine your heart), look out (celebrate unity with the body).
  - title: "You don't come because you're worthy—you come because you're dead."
    body: "If you feel too sinful to take communion, you've misunderstood the hospital; we come to the table not to celebrate our strength, but to receive His mercy."

keyIllustration: "A marine biologist named Nan Hauser was filming humpback whales when a 14-meter whale began pushing her, lifting her, tucking her under its fin—terrifying her. Then she saw the tiger shark coming straight at her. The whale wasn't attacking—it was shielding her, lifting her above the water, swimming her back to the boat. A year later, she encountered the same whale and lay on its pectoral fin, weeping, saying \"thank you, I love you.\" That is altruism—selfless love with no benefit to the giver. But even that falls short: in sin, you are not facing danger; you are already dead, sinking. And Christ reaches down, breathes life, and raises you."

application:
  - "If you've avoided communion because you sinned this week, come anyway—this table is for the sick, not the strong."
  - "If you're at home or far from the physical gathering, break bread where you are—God meets you in your devotion, not your location."
  - "Examine your heart not to disqualify yourself, but to ask: Am I here because I love Him? Do I recognize what He did for me?"

toRemember: You were dead in the water, incapable of anything—and being rich in mercy, because of His great love, He made you alive with Christ.
