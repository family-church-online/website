# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Scripture's warnings against falling away are real and necessary — not to terrify believers into performing, but to guard our hearts from drifting so far from Christ that repentance becomes impossible."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 6:4-6"
keyScriptureText: For it is impossible, in the case of those who have once been enlightened, who have tasted the heavenly gift, and have shared in the Holy Spirit, and have tasted the goodness of the word of God and the powers of the age to come, and then have fallen away, to restore them again to repentance, since they are crucifying once again the Son of God to their own harm and holding him up to contempt.

mainPoints:
  - title: The five warnings in Hebrews form a progression.
    body: "Don't drift from the Word (Hebrews 2:1-3), don't doubt the Word (Hebrews 3:12), don't become dull to the Word (Hebrews 5:11-12), don't deliberately disobey the Word (Hebrews 10:26-27), and don't reject the Word entirely (Hebrews 12:25-27). Each stage leads closer to apostasy."
  - title: This passage describes someone who has genuinely experienced salvation.
    body: "Enlightenment, tasting the heavenly gift, sharing in the Holy Spirit, experiencing God's goodness and power — these descriptions apply to real believers. The warning is serious because the danger is real."
  - title: Falling away means total rejection of Christ, not stumbling into sin.
    body: "Peter denied Jesus and was restored. David committed adultery and murder and was forgiven. Falling away means saying \"I want nothing more to do with Jesus\" — crucifying Him again by joining those who rejected Him."
  - title: Both warnings and assurances are necessary for healthy faith.
    body: "Jesus promised no one can snatch us from His hand (John 10:28). The Holy Spirit seals us as a guarantee (Ephesians 1:13-14). But we must still work out our salvation with fear and trembling, trusting God works in us (Philippians 2:12-13)."

keyIllustration: "A bull elephant in musth — hormones raging, temporal glands streaming, aggressive and unpredictable — approached two observers who were parked between him and another elephant. The warning signs were clear: heightened aggression, rapid approach, the unmistakable scent on the wind. Some people would flee at the first sight of any elephant; others would ignore every warning until too late. Wisdom means respecting the signs, taking the warning seriously, and responding appropriately — not living in constant terror, but not taking foolish risks either."

application:
  - "Ask God daily to search your heart and reveal any \"grievous way\" in you (Psalm 139:23-24) — not out of paranoia, but out of love for Him."
  - When you sin, respond like David (immediate repentance) rather than Ahab (resentment toward correction).
  - "Stop asking \"Can I get away with this?\" and start asking \"Does this please God?\" — your goal is intimacy with Him, not just avoiding punishment."

toRemember: "The question isn't whether you can lose your salvation or whether you're safe no matter what — the question is whether you want to please the God who loves you enough to warn you when you drift."
