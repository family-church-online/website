# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Fasting is not optional or extreme—it's a biblical discipline Jesus expects His followers to practice. When we voluntarily set aside food or other legitimate appetites to pray and seek God, we align ourselves with His purposes and create space for Him to meet us with clarity, power, and intimacy."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 6:16-18"
keyScriptureText: And when you fast, do not look gloomy like the hypocrites, for they disfigure their faces that their fasting may be seen by others. Truly, I say to you, they have received their reward. But when you fast, anoint your head and wash your face, that your fasting may not be seen by others but by your Father who is in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will reward you.

mainPoints:
  - title: Motive matters more than performance.
    body: Jesus warns against fasting to impress people. The reward comes from the Father who sees in secret—not the applause of others.
  - title: Fasting is expected, not optional.
    body: "Jesus says \"when you fast,\" not \"if\"—placing it alongside prayer and giving as a core spiritual practice for every believer."
  - title: "Fasting aligns us with God's purposes."
    body: "It doesn't manipulate God into action. It clears space for Him to speak, sharpens our spiritual sensitivity, and tunes our hearts to His will."
  - title: "Six spiritual benefits of fasting:"
    body: "It initiates revival and spiritual growth (2 Chronicles 7:14), brings clear revelation and direction (Acts 13:2), frees people from spiritual bondage (Mark 9:28-29), pushes back demonic powers (Daniel 10), helps us navigate the journey ahead with confidence (Ezra 8:21-23), and deepens intimacy with Jesus (Matthew 9:14-15)."

keyIllustration: "A Pharisee stands in the temple and prays aloud, thanking God that he fasts twice a week and tithes—but his prayer is really about himself, not God. Nearby, a tax collector can't even lift his eyes, beating his breast and crying, \"God, be merciful to me, a sinner.\" Jesus declares the tax collector went home justified. The difference? Humility and motive. Fasting without humility is just self-righteousness with an empty stomach."

application:
  - "Set a specific time, a clear objective, and block it in your diary. Decide what you're fasting from (food, technology, entertainment) and what you're fasting for (revival, direction, deliverance)."
  - "Don't broadcast it. Keep it between you and God. The reward comes from the Father who sees in secret."
  - "If you can't fast from food for medical reasons, fast from something else—coffee, chocolate, social media, television. The principle is the same: setting aside something you enjoy to create space for God."
  - Involve your children. They can fast from sweets, screen time, or a favourite pastime. It teaches them early that God is worth more than comfort.
  - "Keep a journal. Record what you're praying for and how God answers. Years later, you'll see His faithfulness in your own handwriting."

toRemember: "Fasting is a powerful statement that says to Jesus: I want You more than my food, my entertainment, my comfort. You are my treasure, and I long for Your presence."
