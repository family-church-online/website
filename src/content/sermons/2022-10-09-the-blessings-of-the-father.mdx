  - title: God prioritises spiritual blessings over material ones.
    body: Material wealth is temporary—it rusts, breaks, and gets stolen. Spiritual blessings last forever. Where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.
  - title: "Six spiritual blessings God has already given us:"
    body: (1) We are chosen by the Father, not overlooked. (2) We were chosen before the foundation of the world, not as an afterthought. (3) We are saved for something (to be holy and blameless), not just from something. (4) We are adopted as sons, not abandoned. (5) We are given eternal purpose according to His will, not futility. (6) All of this is to the praise of His glorious grace.
  - title: "Knowing your freedom isn't the same as using it."
    body: "A wildebeest was rescued from a snare but lay on the ground refusing to move—it had been held captive so long it didn't know it was free. Many believers are the same: they know they're free but won't live like it."
  - title: Praise moves truth from your head to your heart.
    body: "Paul's entire passage is worship. When you declare God's blessings—not just meditate on them, but speak them out and share them with others—that's when they become real in your life."

keyIllustration: "A wildebeest caught in a neck snare was wrestled to the ground by a rescue team who cut the snare away. Once freed, the animal lay motionless on the ground, refusing to move—it had been held captive so long it didn't know it was free. Eventually someone had to chase it before it finally ran. The illustration shows how believers can know they're free but still live as though the snare is still there, stuck in patterns of anxiety, abandonment, or futility."

application:
  - Stop rehearsing what held you and start declaring what God has done. The noose of abandonment, rejection, or futility only has power if you keep focusing on it.
  - "Go tell someone this week about one of the six blessings. When you declare God's goodness to others, it moves from your mind to your heart."
  - "Live for God's glory in the everyday—your work, your dishes, your small obedience. Nothing done for God is futile, even if it has to be done again tomorrow."

toRemember: You were made to praise God—and when you do what you were made for, everything else clicks into place.
