# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's plan from Eden to eternity is marked by three feasts that show His faithfulness: He prepares, He invites, and He fulfills every promise — from the harvest to Pentecost to the wedding feast of the Lamb."
keyScriptureRef: "Revelation 19:6-7 ESV"

mainPoints:
  - title: The Feast of Weeks foreshadowed Pentecost.
    body: "Seven weeks after Passover, Israel celebrated the harvest with the Feast of Weeks (Deuteronomy 16). Seven weeks after Jesus' resurrection, the Holy Spirit was poured out at Pentecost, equipping the church to bear spiritual fruit (Acts 2). Both feasts called God's people to gather, remember, and celebrate His provision."
  - title: The wedding feast of the Lamb is coming.
    body: Jesus promised He would go away, prepare a place, and return to fetch His bride (the church). At the rapture, believers will be taken to be with Christ during the seven-year tribulation. Then Christ will return to earth, destroy His enemies, and reign for a thousand years before the final judgment and the recreation of heaven and earth (Revelation 19–21).
  - title: God always prepares, invites, and fulfills.
    body: "Every feast in Scripture follows the same pattern: God prepares the blessing, He initiates the invitation, and He remains faithful to complete what He promised. From the harvest to the Holy Spirit to the new creation, God keeps every promise."

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "Hold fast to your faith. You have been invited to the wedding feast of the Lamb — don't let anyone take that invitation from you."
  - Use your gifts in the body of Christ. Just as the harvest was shared in community, the Holy Spirit equips us to serve one another in love.
  - Live in hope. Every time we break bread together, we look forward to the final feast with Jesus in the new heaven and new earth.

toRemember: God is faithful — He promised to restore all things, and He will. From Eden to eternity, He has prepared a place for you.
