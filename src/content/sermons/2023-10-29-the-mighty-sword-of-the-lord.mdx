# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The sword of the Spirit — God's Word — is your only offensive weapon against the enemy, but it's useless if you don't know how to wield it through the power of the Holy Spirit."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 6:17 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Satan's strategy is always the same: attack the truth."
    body: "From Genesis 3 to today, the enemy obscures, twists, and replaces God's truth with lies — about who you are, what you deserve, and whether God loves you."
  - title: "God's Word is living, active, and more powerful than any enemy."
    body: "Scripture is not a relic or a moral guide — it is the sharpest weapon in existence, able to pierce soul and spirit, discerning thoughts and intentions (Hebrews 4:12)."
  - title: The sword is useless without the Holy Spirit.
    body: Memorizing verses without understanding is like swinging wildly in battle — dangerous to yourself and others. The Word and the Spirit must work together.
  - title: Counter every lie with Scripture, just as Jesus did.
    body: "When Satan tempted Him, Jesus responded, \"It is written.\" You must do the same: take every accusation captive and replace it with the truth."

keyIllustration: "A medieval soldier holds two swords. One is heavy, blunt, requiring two hands — used for judgment. The other is short, razor-sharp, lethal in close combat. That second sword is the one Paul describes: agile, precise, and devastating when wielded correctly. But if you swing it without training, you'll hurt your allies and yourself. Scripture in the hands of someone who doesn't understand the Spirit is just as dangerous."

application:
  - Read the Bible daily — start with an easy translation, join a group, use your phone — whatever removes the barrier.
  - Pray before and during your reading, asking the Holy Spirit to reveal truth before you Google commentaries.
  - "When the enemy whispers a lie, speak Scripture back: \"I am loved\" (John 3:16), \"I can do all things through Christ\" (Philippians 4:13), \"God has not given me a spirit of fear\" (2 Timothy 1:7)."

toRemember: The truth is under attack, but you have the weapon to repel it — take up the sword of the Spirit and use it until Jesus returns to finish the job.
