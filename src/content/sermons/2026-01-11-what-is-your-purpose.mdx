# ── ABOUT LISTS ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
takeaways:
  - Your value is not performance-based—God formed you with intention and wrote every day of your life before you lived one
  - Envy and comparison will always distort your calling; contentment and obedience unlock it
  - You are not meant to function alone—God designed you to be necessary to others in the body of Christ
  - Purpose is discovered not by chasing big opportunities but by faithfully doing the small, right things in front of you
  - Thinking rightly leads to doing rightly, and doing rightly invites the peace of God into your daily life

audience:
  - "You're starting the year wondering if you've wasted time or missed what you were supposed to do with your life"
  - "You catch yourself measuring your progress against someone else's success and feel the weight of inadequacy"
  - You struggle to see what unique contribution you could possibly offer to your church or community
  - "You're tired of striving and want to understand what it actually means to rest in God's plan for you"

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: When you know who you are in Christ and faithfully do the small, right things in front of you each day, God unfolds His purpose for your life in ways you could never orchestrate yourself.
keyScriptureRef: "Psalm 139:13-17 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "You formed my inward parts; You knitted me together in my mother's womb. I praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Wonderful are Your works; my soul knows it very well. My frame was not hidden from You, when I was being made in secret, intricately woven in the depths of the earth. Your eyes saw my unformed substance; in Your book were written, every one of them, the days that were formed for me, when as yet there was none of them. How precious to me are Your thoughts, O God! How vast is the sum of them!"

mainPoints:
  - title: Envy undermines purpose.
    body: When you work, strive, or spend out of comparison with others, you are building on breath—fleeting, meaningless. God calls you to contentment, not competition.
  - title: You are intricately designed for community.
    body: Just as every organ in the body serves a purpose, God has placed you in the body of Christ with gifts no one else has—you are necessary.
  - title: Right thinking leads to right living.
    body: "Fill your mind with what is true, honourable, just, pure, lovely, and commendable. Your actions follow your thoughts, and obedience in small things opens the door to God's bigger plan."

keyIllustration: "A shepherd sits alone in the desert with his sheep, overlooked by his own family. His father doesn't even call him in when a prophet arrives to anoint the next king. But every day, he does the small, faithful thing—he guards the sheep, writes songs, prays, fills his mind with God. When the moment comes, God chooses him. Not because of his appearance or résumé, but because of his heart. David's path from shepherd to king was never in his control—it was scripted by a God who sees what man cannot."

application:
  - "Stop measuring your progress against someone else's Instagram feed. Ask God to search your heart for envy and to replace it with gratitude for what He has already given you."
  - Get busy doing the next right thing—however small it seems—and trust God to weave those acts of obedience into His larger plan for your life.
  - Surround yourself with people who love the Lord so you can think rightly and live rightly together, and recognize that you were never meant to figure this out alone.

toRemember: When you decide to be faithful in the small things, the mighty God of the universe takes care of all the big details.
