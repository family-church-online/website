  - title: You are intricately designed for community.
    body: Just as every organ in the body serves a purpose, God has placed you in the body of Christ with gifts no one else has—you are necessary.
  - title: Right thinking leads to right living.
    body: "Fill your mind with what is true, honourable, just, pure, lovely, and commendable. Your actions follow your thoughts, and obedience in small things opens the door to God's bigger plan."

keyIllustration: "A shepherd sits alone in the desert with his sheep, overlooked by his own family. His father doesn't even call him in when a prophet arrives to anoint the next king. But every day, he does the small, faithful thing—he guards the sheep, writes songs, prays, fills his mind with God. When the moment comes, God chooses him. application:

  - Surround yourself with people who love the Lord so you can think rightly and live rightly together, and recognize that you were never meant to figure this out alone.
