# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's wrath is not something to avoid preaching—it's the believer's greatest source of freedom, because when you trust God to judge, you no longer have to carry bitterness or pursue revenge."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Thessalonians 2:13-16 ESV"
keyScriptureText: We also thank God constantly for this, that when you received the word of God, which you heard from us, you accepted it not as the word of men, but as what it really is, the word of God, which is at work in you believers. For you brothers became imitators of the churches of God in Christ Jesus that are in Judea. For you suffered the same things from your own countrymen as they did from the Jews, who killed both the Lord Jesus and the prophets and drove us out, and displease God and oppose all mankind by hindering us from speaking to the Gentiles that they might be saved—so as always to fill up the measure of their sins. But wrath has come upon them at last.

mainPoints:
  - title: "You must accept all of Scripture as God's word—not just the comfortable parts."
    body: "When you pick and choose what to believe, God's word loses its power to work in you. Churches that reject difficult teachings inevitably drift from truth entirely."
  - title: Persecution is proof the word of God is alive in you.
    body: "Paul tells the Thessalonians that their suffering is evidence God's word is working, not that something has gone wrong. All who desire to live godly lives will face opposition."
  - title: For the believer, there is no injustice—only delayed justice.
    body: "God will settle every account. You don't have to fear that wrongdoers will escape judgment, which means you are free to forgive and pray for mercy on their behalf."
  - title: "Jesus already absorbed all of God's wrath for you."
    body: "If you are in Christ, you will never face divine judgment—Jesus took it in your place. What remains is God's loving discipline, not condemnation."

keyIllustration: "A father struggles daily with aggressive taxi drivers mounting the pavement to skip traffic, putting pedestrians in danger. One morning, after praying and handing his anger over to God, he drives past multiple taxis parked empty on the pavement—and then sees metro police ahead. It's the first time he's ever witnessed enforcement. The lesson isn't that the problem is solved, but that God's justice is real, whether we see it immediately or not."

application:
  - "Stop holding grudges. You don't need to make people pay—God will handle it justly and mercifully in His time."
  - "Accept difficult teachings in Scripture. If you only believe what's comfortable, you cut yourself off from God's full work in your life."
  - "Embrace suffering as evidence of God's word at work. Persecution isn't proof something is wrong—it's proof you're living faithfully."

toRemember: "Vengeance is Mine; I will repay, says the Lord—so bless those who persecute you, trust God's timing, and walk free from bitterness."
