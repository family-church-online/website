# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You can identify false teaching by four signs—compromise, manipulation, hypocrisy, and pride—and recognise true ministry by four marks: Christ-centred speech, confidence in the power of the cross, willingness to suffer for the gospel, and the refusal to seek personal glory."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 6:14, 17 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Far be it from me to boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by which the world has been crucified to me and I to the world... From now on let no one cause me trouble, for I bear on my body the marks of Jesus.

mainPoints:
  - title: Four warning signs of false teachers.
    body: Watch for compromise to avoid persecution, manipulation through persuasive speech or emotional pressure, hypocrisy in life versus teaching, and pride that draws attention to themselves instead of Christ.
  - title: Four marks of faithful ministry.
    body: True teachers boast only in Christ and His cross, focus on the cross as the sole power for transformation, accept suffering for the sake of the gospel, and deflect all glory to God—never to themselves.
  - title: The test is in the scars, not the stage.
    body: "Paul's physical marks authenticated his message. False teachers demand you bear the cost so they can take the glory. True teachers bear the cost themselves so God receives the glory."

keyIllustration: "A diver conducting an experiment on emotional manipulation in film watches a tear-jerking scene, then presses mute. Without the background music, the emotional pull vanishes—revealing how much of the feeling was manufactured rather than organic to the message. The parallel: if a teacher relies on dimmed lights, swelling music, or emotive prompting to move you, ask yourself whether you're being taught or manipulated."

application:
  - "Bring teaching you're unsure of to trusted church leaders and learn how to discern truth from error on principle—not just case by case."
  - "Evaluate the content flooding your feeds: How often does the teacher mention Jesus? Do they compromise to avoid offence? Does their life match their message?"
  - Be willing to bear the marks of following Christ—socially, professionally, relationally—so that He, not you, receives the glory.

toRemember: "Jesus kept His scars in His glorified body. When we meet Him, we will meet Him scars and all. If that's what it takes to authenticate the message and bring others to faith, ask Him for the strength to bear them—that He alone would be glorified."
