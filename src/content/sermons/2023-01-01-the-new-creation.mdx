# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Your anxiety about food, money, and survival is rooted in the curse—but Jesus tells you to stop worrying and seek first the kingdom, because God will provide and one day fully undo what sin destroyed.
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 6:31-33 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Therefore do not be anxious, saying, 'What shall we eat?' or 'What shall we drink?' or 'What shall we wear?' For the Gentiles seek after all these things, and your heavenly Father knows that you need them all. But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you."

mainPoints:
  - title: The curse changed everything.
    body: Sin brought toil, thorns, fear, and death. What was freely given now costs sweat and pain. Adam and Eve believed Satan over God—and the ground itself was cursed because of it.
  - title: Creation is groaning, not evolving.
    body: "Lions once ate grass. There were no thorns, no predators, no disease. The brokenness we see—animals killing, plants defending themselves with toxins—is not God's original design. It's the result of sin, and it will be reversed."
  - title: "The world's solutions miss the point."
    body: Climate activism, vegetarianism as virtue, saving the planet—none of it addresses the real problem. The earth is dying because of sin, and only God can fix it.
  - title: Jesus offers a different food.
    body: Stop working for bread that perishes. Feed daily on the Word of God. Man does not live by bread alone, but by every word that comes from the mouth of God.

keyIllustration: "A conservationist named Kevin Richardson walks freely with lions—opens the door of his truck, they jump in like dogs, one rests her head on his shoulder. It looks impossible, but something in you says: That's how it was meant to be. That instinct is right. Fear and predation weren't part of God's original creation—they entered after the flood, after sin had ravaged everything."

application:
  - "Make 2023 the year you read the Bible cover to cover. Don't let another year pass where you haven't feasted on every word that comes from the mouth of God."
  - When anxiety rises—about money, food, work, the state of the world—pray for your daily bread and trust that God knows what you need.
  - Stop letting the world distract you from the real problem. Sin is the root. Only Jesus can undo the curse.

toRemember: Seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you.
