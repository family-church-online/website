# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You were purchased at the highest price ever paid — the precious blood of God's only Son — not with silver or gold, but with the only currency that could cover the cost of rescuing you from eternal separation from God."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 1:7-10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: In him we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of his grace, which he lavished upon us, in all wisdom and insight making known to us the mystery of his will, according to his purpose, which he set forth in Christ as a plan for the fullness of time, to unite all things in him, things in heaven and things on earth.

mainPoints:
  - title: You have been purchased by the precious blood of Christ.
    body: You no longer belong to yourself or to the slave master of sin. You belong to God, adopted as a child, not owned as a commodity. The currency used was not silver or gold, but the blood of the sinless, eternal Son of God — the only payment precious enough to cover the cost.
  - title: "Redemption brings forgiveness of sin and rescue from God's wrath."
    body: "Christ's blood purchases you from the control of the devil and — more importantly — saves you from the wrath of God. You were an enemy of God, and only the death of His Son could reconcile you to Him."
  - title: God lavished His grace on you for the purpose of reconciliation.
    body: "You've been given an inheritance that will not perish or fade. God's purpose is to unite all things in Christ — to reconcile you to Himself and to one another through the cross."
  - title: "God asks one question in response: where is your heart?"
    body: He gives the gift unconditionally, but then asks what you will do with it. Have you given Him your heart, or are you still living as if you belong to yourself?

keyIllustration: "A person preparing a marine tank discovers a lobster at a restaurant — not in a pet shop, but in a holding tank where diners select their meal. Instead of letting it be boiled and eaten, they pay the full restaurant price and take the lobster home to care for it permanently. The value of Christ's blood rests on where we were purchased from: the edge of hell with no other alternative but to plunge into it for eternity, until God said, \"I want that one. You're coming home with Me forever.\""

application:
  - Stop living as if you belong to yourself. You were bought with a price — conduct yourself accordingly.
  - Remember where you were rescued from, not just what you were rescued for. The cross is precious because there was no other way.
  - "Give God your heart, not just your attendance. Where your treasure is, that's where your heart will be."

toRemember: Nothing but the blood of Jesus could ever afford the cost of your soul — and He paid it willingly, knowing exactly where He was rescuing you from.
