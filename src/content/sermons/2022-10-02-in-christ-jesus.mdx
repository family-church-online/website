# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When you are \"in Christ Jesus,\" you are set apart as a saint, given grace, and offered peace—not because of what you've done, but because of who you belong to."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 1:1-2"
keyScriptureText: "Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, to the saints who are in Ephesus and are faithful in Christ Jesus: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ."

mainPoints:
  - title: Paul writes as an apostle commissioned by Christ Himself.
    body: His authority came directly from Jesus, he saw the resurrected Lord, he wrote Scripture, he worked miracles, and he planted churches—meeting every biblical requirement for apostleship.
  - title: "To be a \"saint\" means to be set apart for God's work."
    body: Like the holy vessels in the tabernacle, you are made holy not by appearance or achievement, but because you are separated unto God.
  - title: Grace always comes before peace.
    body: "God's grace—His unmerited favour—is the means by which He saves us. Peace with God is the result of that grace, made possible through Jesus Christ."
  - title: "Being \"in Christ\" is the believer's true identity."
    body: "The phrase \"in Christ\" appears 130 times in the New Testament. The believer is in Christ, and Christ is in the believer—inseparable, united, abiding."

keyIllustration: The bird is in the air, and the air is in the bird. The fish is in the water, and the water is in the fish. The iron is in the fire, and the fire is in the iron. The believer is in Christ, and Christ is in the believer.

application:
  - "You are a saint—not because you're perfect, but because God has set you apart for His purposes."
  - True peace cannot be found without grace. Stop seeking peace through worldly means; receive the peace that only comes through Christ.
  - Your identity is not based on what you do, but on your union with Jesus. Let this truth anchor you when circumstances shake you.

toRemember: There is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus—because when you are in Him, everything changes.
