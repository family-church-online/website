# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The same grace that freed Gentiles from the law also freed Jews to keep it—not to earn God's love, but to express gratitude for His forgiveness already given through Christ."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 21:24 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Thus, all will know that there is nothing in what they have been told about you, but that you yourself also live in observance of the law.

mainPoints:
  - title: The law was never meant to save us—it was meant to point us to Jesus.
    body: "Everything in the temple rituals, the Passover lamb, the tabernacle design—all of it foreshadowed Christ. The law's purpose was to show us we cannot be perfect and desperately need a Savior."
  - title: "Faith without obedience isn't real faith, but obedience without faith is self-righteousness."
    body: Abraham believed God and it was counted to him as righteousness—belief came first. Then obedience followed. The two must go hand in hand or neither is genuine.
  - title: "Grace doesn't give us permission to sin—it gives us power to obey."
    body: When we truly believe Christ died for our sin, how can we turn around and say we have to sin a bit? His Holy Spirit changes our desires so we want to be holy, not because we have to earn salvation, but because we already have it.

keyIllustration: "A taxi association spokesperson protested the high cost of traffic fines, asking government to lower them because drivers couldn't afford them. The logic sounds absurd—if you can't afford the fine, don't break the law. But if your understanding of law is that it exists only to extract money from you, the protest makes sense. Many of us view God's law the same way: a burden meant to limit us, not a gift meant to protect us."

application:
  - "Stop measuring yourself against other people. One sin makes you guilty of breaking the whole law—there's no \"not as bad as them\" category before God."
  - "You cannot make up for past sin with good behavior. Confess it, receive Christ's forgiveness, and let the Holy Spirit produce obedience in you."
  - "Obedience that honors God flows from gratitude, not guilt. Once you grasp that Christ already paid your debt, you're free to obey out of love."

toRemember: God loves you just the way He finds you—but He loves you too much to leave you that way.
