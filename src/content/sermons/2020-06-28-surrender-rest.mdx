# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God offers two kinds of rest: salvation rest—freedom from proving yourself righteous—and daily rest—the peace of surrendering control and trusting His direction even when the plow is heavy."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 11:28-30 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.

mainPoints:
  - title: Salvation rest ends the exhaustion of self-justification.
    body: "You cannot earn your way into God's presence by balancing good against bad. When you come to Christ confessing your inability to be righteous on your own, He gives you the rest of knowing you are already accepted—not because of your works, but because of His grace."
  - title: "Daily rest comes from taking Christ's yoke and surrendering control."
    body: "An ox doesn't question the farmer's direction—it pulls the plow and trusts the one holding the reins. When you stop being a backseat driver in your own life, asking \"Why, Lord?\" out of distrust instead of humility, you enter the rest Jesus promises for your soul."
  - title: "Peace isn't the absence of hardship—it's the presence of God while you work."
    body: Joy, love, and peace are fruit of the Spirit that endure regardless of circumstances. When you focus on doing the work God has placed in front of you today, trusting Him with tomorrow, the God of peace will be with you.

keyIllustration: "South African rugby player Os du Randt was told at 18 he'd never play again after destroying his knee. By 23, he'd won a World Cup. At 28, another injury forced retirement. Recalled at 32, he felt out of place, too old, crushed by doubt—until a teammate said, \"I'm proud to play with you.\" That moment of acceptance changed everything. Os stopped listening to critics, put his head down, and did the work in front of him. He became South Africa's number one prop again and won a second World Cup at 34. The illustration demonstrates what happens when you stop carrying the weight of others' opinions and simply trust the role you've been given."

application:
  - "Stop asking \"Why, Lord?\" out of distrust. Instead, pray with humility: \"If You want me to know why, show me—but I trust You either way.\""
  - "Each morning, pray \"Your will be done\"—not as a formality, but as genuine surrender of control over outcomes you were never meant to carry."
  - "When anxiety rises, follow Philippians 4: take it to God in prayer, leave it with Him, then redirect your thoughts to what is true, honorable, pure, and commendable. Do the work in front of you."

toRemember: "God appoints a day He calls \"today\"—and today, He invites you to stop driving from the backseat, take His yoke, and rest in the knowledge that He is in control."
