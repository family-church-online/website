# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Baptism is the beginning of a life of devoted, day-by-day discipleship—not the conclusion of it.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 2:42-43 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And they devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers. And awe came upon every soul, and many wonders and signs were being done through the apostles."

mainPoints:
  - title: Baptism begins with hearing and believing the gospel.
    body: "Three thousand people believed Peter's message, the Holy Spirit changed them, and baptism was their public response—not the cause of their salvation, but the declaration of it."
  - title: Being the church means being constantly, diligently devoted.
    body: The early church devoted themselves to Scripture, fellowship, worship, and prayer—not occasionally, but day by day. That same rhythm is what transforms us from church attenders into the church.
  - title: We worship, gather, and give not for ourselves, but for others.
    body: "The early believers didn't ask \"What do I need today?\" but \"Who will miss out if I'm not there?\" Their generosity, encouragement, and awe of God flowed outward."
  - title: "Jesus builds His church—but we do the \"and, and, and.\""
    body: The Lord adds to the church day by day, but that growth is connected to our day-by-day faithfulness in Scripture, fellowship, worship, and prayer.

keyIllustration: "A military veteran took other vets—many suffering with severe PTSD—on excursions to the North Pole. After weeks of teamwork and witnessing the awe of the Northern Lights, psychologists tested them and found dramatic reductions in stress hormones and improvements in sleep. The conclusion: regular awe of God's majesty is not religious decoration—it's how we're designed to cope, to heal, and to thrive."

application:
  - "Ask yourself: Am I devoted to Scripture, fellowship, worship, and prayer—or am I falling into a habit of neglecting them?"
  - "Don't wait for church to feel convenient. Others need your encouragement, and you need theirs—especially as the day of Christ's return draws near."
  - "Pray that Jesus would add to the church day by day—and commit to being part of the \"and, and, and\" that makes space for that growth."

toRemember: Baptism is the beginning—what comes after is the life of constant, joyful devotion that makes you the church.
