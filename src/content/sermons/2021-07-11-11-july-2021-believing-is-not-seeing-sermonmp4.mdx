# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "True faith is trusting in God's Word, character, and power—not in the outcomes we want to see."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 11:1-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Now faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen. For by it the people of old received their commendation. By faith we understand that the universe was created by the word of God, so that what is seen was not made out of things that are visible.

mainPoints:
  - title: Faith pleases God when we trust without seeing.
    body: "God commends those who believe even when they can't comprehend what He's doing—like Abram leaving home not knowing his destination, or believers trusting God created the universe by speaking it into existence."
  - title: Our faith must rest in God Himself, not the outcome we want.
    body: "We trust three things: His Word (the Scriptures), His character (He is good, faithful, just), and His power (He can fulfill every promise). This is the anchor that holds when outcomes disappoint."
  - title: "Prayer aligns us with God's will—it doesn't command Him."
    body: "Jesus prayed \"Thy will be done\" and taught us the same. Prayer transforms us before it changes circumstances. We ask humbly, never arrogantly declaring what God must do."
  - title: Declaring outcomes is not biblical faith.
    body: "Only God speaks things into existence. Moses was punished for claiming God's power as his own. We declare what God has said, not what we want Him to do."

keyIllustration: "A man needing multifocal lenses could not try them before purchasing—he had to trust the optometrist's word, the product's power, and the character of the professional first. He couldn't see through them until after he committed. Faith in God works the same: we trust His Word, His character, and His power even when we can't yet see the outcome."

application:
  - Discern daily what teachings you follow—subtle errors in understanding faith compound over time and can lead to spiritual shipwreck.
  - "Pray for God's will first, then trust His character and power to fulfill what He has promised—not what you've demanded."
  - "Hold fast when disappointment comes. Real faith says with Habakkuk, \"Yet I will rejoice in the Lord,\" even when nothing makes sense."

toRemember: Why limit God by telling Him what to do, when He can do far more abundantly than we could ever ask or imagine?
