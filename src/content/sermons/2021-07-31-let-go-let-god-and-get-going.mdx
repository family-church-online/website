# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "True faith doesn't mean withdrawing in fear or acting frantically in your own strength — it means letting go, letting God, and getting going through gratitude, reverent fear, trust, and obedience."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 11:17-19 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "By faith Abraham, when he was tested, offered up Isaac, and he who had received the promises was in the act of offering up his only son, of whom it was said, 'Through Isaac shall your offspring be named.' He considered that God was able even to raise him from the dead, from which, figuratively speaking, he did receive him back."

mainPoints:
  - title: Gratitude leads to giving your best.
    body: "Like Abel offering the best of his flock, Abraham was willing to give Isaac because of overwhelming gratitude for God's goodness — not out of duty but joy."
  - title: Fear and fascination must work together.
    body: Abraham walked closely with God daily (like Enoch) while maintaining reverent fear of Him — the greater fear of God drives out lesser fears from circumstances, news, or uncertainty.
  - title: Trust enables obedience even when the outcome is unclear.
    body: "Abraham told his servants \"we will come back\" because God promised Isaac would be his heir — he trusted God's word completely and obeyed fully, knowing God would handle what he couldn't understand."

keyIllustration: "Six days before breaking the world record, Olympic gold medalist Tatiana Schoenmaker posted a prayer asking only that God's will be done and that He be glorified. Yet she still trained for fifteen years — waking at 4 a.m. daily, living and breathing her goal. She let go and let God, then got up and got going. Faith doesn't withdraw; it acts boldly within God's will."

application:
  - Walk with God daily — wake each morning thanking Him, consulting Him in decisions, and saturating yourself in Scripture to build proper fear of Him.
  - "Trust God's promises in the big unknowns while obeying Him in the small knowns — ask if something honors God, obeys His Word, and serves others, then let Him handle outcomes you can't control."
  - "Recognize that when you truly let go of something for God, He often gives it back once it no longer controls you — but even if He doesn't, His promise of eternal life through Christ means nothing is ultimately lost."

toRemember: "It's a lifestyle of daily time with God, gratitude for every blessing, reverent fear grounded in Scripture, and obedience in the small things — this hardens you toward adversity and softens your heart in relationship with Him."
