# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: To respond rightly to Revelation, you must know Jesus as He fully is—both the Lamb who was pierced and the almighty God who is, who was, and who is to come.
keyScriptureRef: "Revelation 1:8 (ESV)"
keyScriptureText: "\"I am the Alpha and the Omega, says the Lord God, who is and who was and who is to come, the Almighty.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: He is.
    body: "The blessing comes from \"him who is,\" echoing God's own name \"I am\" in Exodus—so John leaves the grammar broken rather than bend the unchanging God to human rules."
  - title: The complete Spirit.
    body: "The \"seven spirits before the throne\" signify the one perfect and complete Holy Spirit, not seven angels, since seven is the number of fullness."
  - title: The Son of Man.
    body: "Calling Jesus the firstborn of the dead and the One coming on the clouds identifies Him with Daniel's divine Son of Man—so the pierced Messiah is God Himself."

keyIllustration: Encounters with vast wild creatures—swimming beside a whale shark, crouching within reach of a grazing buffalo, standing pinned as an elephant leaned in—picture awe as a mixture of fear, fascination, and adoration. That overwhelming nearness illustrates what it will be to stand before Jesus in the fullness of His glory.

application:
  - Refuse a one-sided picture of Jesus; hold His tenderness and His terrifying holiness together, or the rest of Revelation will be lost on you.
  - "Slow down over familiar phrases like \"who is and who was and who is to come\" until they stir genuine awe rather than routine."

toRemember: The same Jesus who was pierced for you is coming on the clouds as almighty God—know Him fully, and awe will meet your peace.
