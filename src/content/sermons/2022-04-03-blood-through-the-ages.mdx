# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The Lord's Supper is not merely a ritual—it is God's declaration that the blood of Christ has done what centuries of animal sacrifice could not: permanently cover our sin and restore our broken relationship with Him."
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 22:19-20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And he took bread, and when he had given thanks, he broke it and gave it to them, saying, \"This is my body which is given for you. Do this in remembrance of me.\" And likewise the cup after they had eaten, saying, \"This cup that is poured out for you is the new covenant in my blood.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Sin separated us from God and created a hole only He can fill.
    body: When Adam and Eve ate the forbidden fruit, evil entered the world and humanity suffered immediate spiritual death—our connection with God was broken. That missing connection is the human condition, the hole we try to fill with money, status, substances, anything but God.
  - title: God instituted blood sacrifice as a temporary bridge back to Him.
    body: At Passover, the blood on the doorframes covered the Israelites and spared them from death. The sacrificial system provided temporary atonement for sin, but it was laborious and incomplete—pointing forward to a better, permanent solution.
  - title: Jesus is the perfect, unblemished Lamb who takes away the sins of the world.
    body: "Every animal sacrifice foreshadowed Christ. John the Baptist declared, \"Behold, the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world.\" His blood accomplishes what animal blood never could: a permanent, unbreakable bridge back to God."
  - title: "The Lord's Supper replaces the old covenant and calls us to remember Christ."
    body: "Jesus instituted the Lord's Supper to replace the old sacrificial system because it is no longer needed. We are to remember who He is, what He has done, and the new covenant sealed in His blood."

keyIllustration: "A fisherman named Peter, after a miraculous catch, fell to his knees before Jesus and cried, \"Get away from me, Lord. I'm a wicked man.\" That moment captures the incomprehensible reality of the Lord's Supper: the holy God who should reject us has instead sacrificed everything to draw us near."

application:
  - Stop trying to fill the God-shaped hole in your soul with substitutes—only reconnection with Him will satisfy.
  - "When you take the Lord's Supper, remember that Christ's blood covers all your sins permanently. You don't have to keep earning your way back."
  - Confess your sins honestly before God. He already knows them and has already paid for them.

toRemember: If you love the Lord Jesus, remember Him—not just the cross, but who He is to you, and the fact that He chose to know you anyway.
