# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You are not a lone brick — you are being built together with believers across all time into a holy temple where God Himself dwells by His Spirit.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 2:19-22 ESV"
keyScriptureText: So then you are no longer strangers and aliens, but you are fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God, built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus himself being the cornerstone, in whom the whole structure, being joined together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord. In him you also are being built together into a dwelling place for God by the Spirit.

mainPoints:
  - title: The church is built on an immovable foundation.
    body: "Scripture — the witness of the apostles and prophets — is held together and aligned by Christ the cornerstone. This foundation does not shift because God's Word and God's character do not change."
  - title: The church is constantly growing.
    body: You are not just joining the people in this room or this generation. You are being added to the household of God that stretches back through centuries and forward into eternity.
  - title: The church is fitted closely together.
    body: "Like temple stones cut to fit precisely, believers are meant to be joined tightly by the Holy Spirit. Gaps allow in what doesn't belong. Close community is costly but essential."
  - title: "The church is God's dwelling place."
    body: "Just as God's glory filled Solomon's temple, the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit now dwell in the church — not a building, but a people. You are part of that living temple."

keyIllustration: "A single brick is useless. It cannot shelter you. It cannot house you. But when thousands of bricks are fitted together properly, you get a building like the one we're sitting in. God never designed you to be a lone brick — He designed you to be part of something vast, something lasting, something alive."

application:
  - The culture is pushing you toward isolation through technology, urbanisation, and self-protection. The church calls you into deep, costly, Spirit-filled community.
  - When you are hurt in the church, do not withdraw. Speak. Heal. Stay. Unresolved conflict will follow you wherever you go.
  - You cannot reach spiritual maturity alone. Jesus builds us in our own lives and then builds us together. Both are necessary.

toRemember: "You are a member of God's eternal household, being fitted together by Christ Himself into a temple where the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit dwell."
