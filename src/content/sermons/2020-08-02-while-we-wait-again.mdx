# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God doesn't waste waiting seasons—He uses them to heal, rebuild, and prepare you for what He's called you to do."
keyScriptureRef: "Exodus 2:15, 21-22"
keyScriptureText: "When Pharaoh heard of it, he sought to kill Moses. But Moses fled from Pharaoh and stayed in the land of Midian. And he sat down by a well... And Moses was content to dwell with the man, and he gave Moses his daughter Zipporah. She bore a son, and he called his name Gershom, for he said, \"I have been a sojourner in a foreign land.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Waiting is hard because of pain, powerlessness, and pointlessness.
    body: Moses fled Egypt a murderer—in pain, stripped of power, wondering what his life was for. He sat at a well in Midian with nothing left.
  - title: God gave Moses a family when he had none.
    body: "A man with no father met a family with no son. For forty years God healed Moses' wounds, restored his identity, and taught him humility through the love of a stable family."
  - title: "The wilderness wasn't wasted—it was preparation."
    body: Forty years tending sheep in the desert trained Moses to lead two million people through that same wilderness. God used every moment.
  - title: Moses became a blessing, not just a recipient.
    body: Through Moses, Jethro came to know that Yahweh is greater than all gods. What looked like wasted time brought an entire family into the knowledge of God.

keyIllustration: A man who once tried to save his people by murdering an Egyptian sat powerless at a well. Seven women drew water for their flocks, only to be chased away by bullies who stole their labour. Moses could have stayed seated—he had enough problems. But something rose in him. He defended them. He watered the flock. He was invited to eat bread with the family. That meal became forty years of healing, and those forty years became the foundation for leading a nation.

application:
  - Use this season to heal family relationships—be a father, mother, brother, or sister to someone who needs one.
  - Stop trying to fix everything yourself. Trust God to work while you wait.
  - "Ask God what He's preparing you for in this hidden season. He doesn't waste time—He redeems it."

toRemember: "Waiting isn't wasting—not in God's hands. He's using this time to rebuild you for what's next."
