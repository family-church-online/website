# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God builds kingdom momentum through your testimony, weaving together disconnected relationships and imperfect pasts into His perfect plan to scatter the gospel to the ends of the earth.
keyScriptureRef: "Revelation 12:11 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And they have conquered him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony, for they loved not their lives even unto death.

mainPoints:
  - title: Persecution plants the church in new soil.
    body: "Saul's attempt to ravage the church only scattered believers who preached Christ everywhere they went — to Phoenicia, Cyprus, Antioch — multiplying the very faith he tried to destroy."
  - title: God connects the right people at the right time.
    body: Barnabas saw life in Paul when everyone else saw a murderer; he later fetched Paul from obscurity in Tarsus to teach the Antioch church, proving that God orchestrates relationships across years and distance.
  - title: "The pattern for church growth: gospel, encouragement, teaching."
    body: "First, the unnamed believers shared the gospel and \"a great many believed.\" Then Barnabas was sent to encourage the new converts. Finally, Paul taught them for a year — each step essential."
  - title: Your testimony is a weapon that conquers hell.
    body: Satan is defeated not by eloquence or credentials, but by the blood of Jesus and the word of your testimony — what He has done for you, spoken plainly to those He has placed in your life.

keyIllustration: A longtime friendship stretched across continents and decades, marked by silence about Jesus despite shared history from childhood. Years later, one friend testified to how Christ had freed him from addiction and sin. That testimony — spoken at last — became the door through which the Spirit moved, reconnecting what distance and shame had severed. The very relationship that seemed wasted became the ground God had been preparing all along.

application:
  - "Pray earnestly: \"Lord, send laborers into the harvest — and send me.\" Ask Him to show you who you can bring Him to today."
  - Stop keeping your gifts, abilities, and testimony to yourself. Speak Jesus into the relationships He has given you — even if years have passed in silence.
  - "Trust that no distance, difficulty, or broken connection is beyond God's power to redeem and use for His kingdom purposes."

toRemember: "You are called into His kingdom to save other people's lives — Christianity is a selfless faith, and you are part of the army that crushes sin, death, and hell under your feet by His power and your testimony."
