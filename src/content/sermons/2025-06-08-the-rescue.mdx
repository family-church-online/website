# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Peter's physical rescue from Herod's prison is a precise picture of how Jesus rescues every believer spiritually—pulling us from chains we can't break, waking us from sleep we don't realize we're in, and sending us out to tell others what He's done."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 2:4-9"
keyScriptureText: But God, being rich in mercy, because of the great love with which He loved us, even when we were dead in our trespasses, made us alive together with Christ—by grace you have been saved—and raised us up with Him and seated us with Him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus, so that in the coming ages He might show the immeasurable riches of His grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus. For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Peter's condition mirrors ours before Christ:"
    body: "Chained between two guards, locked behind iron gates, sentenced to die at dawn, and fast asleep—utterly helpless. This is what Scripture calls being \"dead in our trespasses.\""
  - title: "The rescue is God's work alone:"
    body: "Peter didn't pick the lock, bribe the guards, or wake himself. The angel struck him, dressed him, led him past every barrier. Even halfway through, Peter thought it was a vision. Salvation is the gift of God, not a result of works."
  - title: "The first response is to share the story:"
    body: "The moment Peter realized he'd been rescued, he ran to the house where believers were praying and knocked until they let him in. When Jesus frees you, your first instinct should be to tell someone what He's done."
  - title: "Even faithful believers struggle to believe the miracle:"
    body: "The church had been praying for Peter's release constantly. When Rhoda announced he was at the door, they told her she was crazy. We pray for the impossible, then doubt it when God delivers."

keyIllustration: "A church gathered in John Mark's mother's house praying fervently for Peter's release. When a servant girl named Rhoda heard Peter's voice at the gate, she was so excited she forgot to open it and ran inside to tell everyone. They responded, \"You're out of your mind.\" Even when she insisted, they said it must be his guardian angel. Meanwhile, Peter kept knocking. The very people who'd been praying for the miracle couldn't believe it when it arrived."

application:
  - "If you haven't been rescued yet—if you're still chained, still asleep—respond to Jesus now. Don't wait for Him to wake you roughly. Say, \"Lord, I need You. I'm not getting out of this life without You.\""
  - If you have been rescued, share every detail. Tell people what Jesus has done. Find the ones who need to hear it most and knock until they open the door.
  - Keep praying for the impossible. God often acts at the very last minute because it leaves no doubt as to how much He loves us and how powerful He is.

toRemember: "Jesus gave His life to rescue you—tortured to death on the cross to set you free. His joy is to pull you out of the darkest place you're completely comfortable in, wake you up, and bring you to your senses."
