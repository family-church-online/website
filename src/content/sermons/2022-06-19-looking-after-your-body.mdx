# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Your body, spirit, and gifts exist not for your survival but to ensure all of us — together — are ready when Jesus returns.
keyScriptureRef: "1 Thessalonians 5:23-24 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Now may the God of peace himself sanctify you completely, and may your whole spirit and soul and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. He who calls you is faithful; he will surely do it.

mainPoints:
  - title: "The Holy Spirit's gifts are corporate, not private."
    body: "Prophecy is given for the upbuilding, encouragement, and consolation of the church — not for individual benefit. If you're absent, someone else misses what God intended to do through you."
  - title: Prophecy must be tested by Scripture.
    body: "True prophecy never contradicts, adds to, or takes away from God's Word. The body discerns together whether a word is from God — which means you need each other to operate safely in spiritual gifts."
  - title: "Paul's benediction addresses the whole church, not individuals."
    body: "When he prays for \"your spirit and soul and body,\" he uses singular nouns but plural pronouns — highlighting that we carry collective responsibility for one another's readiness for Christ's return."
  - title: Self-focus is survival mode; other-focus is how you survive.
    body: Anxiety intensifies when you obsess over your own pain. The cure is stepping outside yourself to serve others — which not only blesses them but frees you from your own burden.

keyIllustration: "A dog named Ruby was rejected by five families and scheduled for euthanasia. One shelter worker refused to give up, convinced Ruby needed to be a working dog. After eighteen months of training — three times longer than standard — Ruby graduated top of her class in the canine unit. Seven years later, she found a dying teenage boy in the woods. The boy's mother was Pat Inman — the woman who had fought to save Ruby's life years earlier. She had no idea she was saving her own son."

application:
  - "Ask yourself: Who in this congregation am I praying for? Whose burden am I carrying? Who needs me to show up?"
  - "Stop isolating. Trust and vulnerability aren't optional — they're how the body functions. If you can't trust the people in church, who will you trust?"
  - "If God has given you a prophetic word, share it humbly with leadership so it can be tested and, if confirmed, shared for the body's good."

toRemember: "It's not \"My Father\" — it's \"Our Father.\" You were never meant to make it alone."
