# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The slightest distortion of the gospel doesn't improve it — it destroys it, turning life-giving truth into spiritual death."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 1:6-8 ESV"
keyScriptureText: I am astonished that you are so quickly deserting him who called you in the grace of Christ and are turning to a different gospel — not that there is another one, but there are some who trouble you and want to distort the gospel of Christ. But even if we or an angel from heaven should preach to you a gospel contrary to the one we preached to you, let him be accursed.

mainPoints:
  - title: The gospel cannot be modified.
    body: "Paul uses two Greek words for \"another\" — one meaning \"another of the same kind\" and one meaning \"another of a different kind\" — to make clear there is only one true gospel, and any variation is not just different but deadly."
  - title: False teachers are spiritual murderers.
    body: Unlike those who commit physical murder, false teachers destroy not just the body but the soul, leading people into eternal death by teaching a counterfeit gospel that cannot save.
  - title: We must avoid and isolate false teachers.
    body: Scripture commands believers to watch out for, avoid, and refuse to welcome those who distort the gospel — not out of hatred, but to protect the church from spiritual poison.

keyIllustration: "Ozone-treated water appeared safe but became toxic when the chemical failed to dissipate. Like oxygen atoms arranged in threes instead of twos, the water looked identical but was deadly to breathe. The gospel works the same way: the smallest rearrangement — adding works to faith, diminishing Christ's deity, removing the reality of sin — transforms life-giving truth into poison."

application:
  - Be vigilant about what you listen to, read, and sing — gospel distortions appear in worship music, podcasts, and casual spiritual conversations.
  - "Test every teaching against the core gospel: salvation by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone, apart from works."
  - "Love people while refusing to tolerate false teaching — protecting the gospel is not unloving; it's the most loving thing you can do."

toRemember: "You are saved by what you believe, and what you believe — if it's real and empowered by the gospel — changes what you do. Never reverse that order."
