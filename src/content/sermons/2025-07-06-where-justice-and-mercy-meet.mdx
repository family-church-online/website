# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's sovereignty means that every rebellion, every jealousy, every failure — including yours — only tightens the alignment of His plan, never derails it."
keyScriptureRef: "Romans 8:28 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And we know that for those who love God all things work together for good, for those who are called according to His purpose.

mainPoints:
  - title: "God's faithfulness outlasts human rebellion."
    body: "Despite Israel's repeated rejection — from Egypt through the judges to the crucifixion — God continued to offer them the gospel first, keeping His promise."
  - title: Rejection advances the plan.
    body: "When the Jews rejected Paul's message out of jealousy, God used that very rejection to open the gospel to the Gentiles, furthering His purposes rather than hindering them."
  - title: "God's justice and mercy meet at the cross."
    body: He is gracious, slow to anger, and forgiving — yet He will not leave the guilty unpunished. Christ bore that punishment so we are freed from condemnation.

keyIllustration: "Throughout Israel's history, every attempt to thwart God's plan became the means of its fulfilment. Joseph's brothers sold him into slavery to destroy him; God used that to position him as a ruler who would save the nation. Israel demanded a king against God's will; God used that to bring David, the forerunner of the Messiah. The Jews crucified Jesus to silence Him; God used His death to provide salvation and proved it by raising Him from the dead."

application:
  - Stop carrying condemnation over sin Christ has already carried. Confess it, repent, and trust that God is using even your failures according to His purposes.
  - "When you face attacks, illness, betrayal, or financial collapse, remember: if God used the crucifixion itself to bring life, He can use anything in your life for His good plan."
  - "Do not trivialise sin just because God turns it to good. Repentance and gratitude go hand in hand — you don't want to sin against the One who gave His life for you."

toRemember: "The harder you push back against God's will, the more surely His will is done — and His will for you is freedom, peace, and the full assurance of His love."
