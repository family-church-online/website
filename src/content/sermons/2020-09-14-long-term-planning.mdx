# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God has appointed Jesus as both eternal High Priest and King — and every plan you make must be surrendered to His authority and aligned with His mission, not your comfort.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 5:5-6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "So also Christ did not exalt himself to be made a high priest, but was appointed by him who said to him, \"You are my Son, today I have begotten you\"; as he says also in another place, \"You are a priest forever, after the order of Melchizedek.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: The human high priest represented sinful people because he himself was weak and ignorant.
    body: He had to offer sacrifices for his own sin before he could offer them for others, and he could only serve temporarily because death ended his priesthood.
  - title: Jesus was appointed by God as both High Priest and King.
    body: Unlike human priests, He had no sin of His own and offered Himself as the perfect sacrifice. He is both the shepherd who cares for you and the sovereign you must obey.
  - title: "God's long-term plans span thousands of years and never fail."
    body: From Melchizedek to David to Christ, God has been preparing the perfect Priest-King. Your plans must be built around His will, not your own desires or comfort.
  - title: You must bring every decision — no matter how big or small — to Christ first.
    body: "Ask whether your plans further His mission, not just whether they benefit you. Pray \"Your kingdom come, Your will be done\" before you ask for anything else."

keyIllustration: A rugby coach responded to a controversial loss with sarcasm and aggression, distracting his team from their long-term plan. A year later, when facing the same opponent in the World Cup final, they had learned to stay focused on what mattered. Victory came not through revenge but through discipline and keeping to the right plan.

application:
  - "Before you make any decision, ask God: \"Does this further Your mission for me, or just my own comfort?\" Bring every plan — career, relationships, finances, even renovations — to Him in prayer first."
  - "When plans collapse unexpectedly, see it as an opportunity to realign your life around God's will instead of rebuilding the same structure that failed."
  - Remember that you are both cared for by a shepherd and subject to a king. Jesus represents you to God, but you also owe Him total allegiance and obedience.

toRemember: "God's plans span millennia without a single error — yours can too, if you submit every decision to the Priest-King who has already paid for your sin and holds your future in His hands."
