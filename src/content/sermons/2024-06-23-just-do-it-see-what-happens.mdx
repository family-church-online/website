# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Speaking the truth in love — not just once, but persistently — is what separates the gospel from flattery, the Word from the world, and real love from self-interest. It's costly, divisive, and absolutely necessary."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 4:12-15"
keyScriptureText: Brothers, I entreat you, become as I am, for I also have become as you are. You did me no wrong. You know it was because of a bodily ailment that I preached the gospel to you at first, and though my condition was a trial to you, you did not scorn or despise me, but received me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus. What then has become of your blessedness?

mainPoints:
  - title: Speaking the truth in love is selfless, not easy.
    body: "Paul became like the Galatians to reach them. He preached even when his body was wrecked. He didn't wait for comfort — he seized the opportunity in suffering."
  - title: False teaching flatters; the gospel confronts.
    body: The false teachers made much of the Galatians so the Galatians would make much of them. Truth divides; flattery seduces.
  - title: Real love perseveres in pain until Christ is formed.
    body: "Paul describes himself as being in \"the anguish of childbirth\" for them again. Love does not correct once and walk away — it fights, over and over, until the person is safe."

keyIllustration: "A matric hockey team played their final home game. The coaches instructed the younger players to pass to the seniors — even when they had better shots — so the seniors could score on their last day. One senior botched a penalty flick, was given a second chance, and scored. The team went wild. It was about them, not personal glory. That's what selfless love does: it sacrifices for the sake of the other, even at personal cost."

application:
  - "Discern what teaching makes much of: you, the teacher, or Jesus. If it's not Jesus, walk away."
  - "Don't avoid correction because it's uncomfortable. Love demands truth, even when it divides."
  - Persevere with those drifting from the gospel. Speak truth again and again, patiently, until Christ is formed in them.

toRemember: "When you speak the truth in love — even in weakness, even in suffering — God does a miracle. No church in Galatia without Paul's disease. No gospel without His sacrifice. Love the way you've been loved."
