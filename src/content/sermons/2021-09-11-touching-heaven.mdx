# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You don't need to settle for temporary relief or lower your standards to cope with a broken world — you've already come to a heavenly community where God rules justly, loved ones wait, and your hope is grounded in the eternal work of Christ."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 12:22-24 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But you have come to Mount Zion and to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, and to innumerable angels in festal gathering, and to the assembly of the firstborn who are enrolled in heaven, and to God, the judge of all, and to the spirits of the righteous made perfect, and to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant, and to the sprinkled blood that speaks a better word than the blood of Abel.

mainPoints:
  - title: The old covenant was tangible but distant.
    body: At Sinai, the Israelites could see fire, hear thunder, smell smoke — yet they were so separated by their sin that only Moses could approach God, and even he trembled with fear.
  - title: The new covenant brings you into a present, eternal community.
    body: "You've come to Mount Zion, where angels worship, the righteous are perfected, God rules justly, and your name is enrolled — not someday, but right now."
  - title: "Jesus' blood speaks a better word than Abel's."
    body: "Abel's blood cried out for justice; Christ's blood cries out that He took your punishment, opened the way, and made you part of a community that will never fracture or fade."

keyIllustration: "A diver separated from his group turned to see a Zambezi shark staring directly at him in murky water. His brain rifled through threat responses — fight, flight, freeze — before settling on freeze, paralysed until the shark moved off. The world is doing the same to us now: threatening us until we submit. But submission to Christ is the only response that doesn't leave us defeated."

application:
  - "Ask yourself daily: Is what I'm hoping in temporary or permanent? Will I thank God for this solution in ten thousand years, or will I regret it?"
  - Refuse to let ideological division fracture Christian community. Your friendship with another believer is an eternal bond, not contingent on politics or preferences.
  - Meditate on the reality of heaven. Your loved ones who died in Christ are as present there as you are here — seeing, hearing, worshiping, waiting for you to join them.

toRemember: "Hope is only as strong as what you place it in — and you've placed yours in a covenant sealed by the blood of Christ, a community that cannot be shaken, and a kingdom that is already real."
