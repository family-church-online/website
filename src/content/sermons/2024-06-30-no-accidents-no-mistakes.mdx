# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Christ alone is enough—not Christ plus your effort, your ritual, or your desperation. When you try to secure God's promises by human means, you risk losing the freedom you've already been given."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 4:21-23 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Tell me, you who desire to be under the law, do you not listen to the law? For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by a slave woman and one by a free woman. But the son of the slave was born according to the flesh, while the son of the free woman was born through promise.

mainPoints:
  - title: Not everyone who quotes the Bible is qualified to teach you.
    body: The false teachers in Galatia knew the law inside and out—but their knowledge led people into slavery, not freedom. Test everything against Scripture, like the Bereans in Acts 17.
  - title: "Abraham's story is your story."
    body: God promised Abraham a son when he was seventy-five. Twenty-five years later, Isaac was born. In between, desperation led to Ishmael—born according to the flesh, not the promise. Hagar represents the old covenant of law; Sarah represents the new covenant of grace. You are children of Sarah, born supernaturally by the Spirit.
  - title: Persecution comes with supernatural birth.
    body: "Just as Ishmael persecuted Isaac, those born according to the flesh will persecute those born according to the Spirit. Expect it. Suffering as a Christian isn't proof you're doing something wrong—it's proof you're living in freedom."
  - title: Christian freedom changes the motivation and the means of obedience.
    body: "You no longer obey to earn God's favor—you obey because He's already forgiven you and saved you from His wrath. The difference between a slave and a son: a slave obeys out of fear; a son obeys out of love."

keyIllustration: "A hundred-year-old couple—barren, past hope, biologically dead—has a child. That's not natural. That's supernatural. Isaac's birth wasn't just a miracle for Abraham and Sarah. It was a picture of what God does for every believer: He brings life out of death, hope out of impossibility, sonship out of slavery. Every Christian is born the same way Isaac was—by promise, by grace, by the Spirit."

application:
  - "Before you follow someone's teaching, healing method, or spiritual practice, ask: Does this point to Christ alone, or does it add requirements to the gospel?"
  - "If you've been praying for something for years and it hasn't happened, don't let impatience drive you to take shortcuts. God's timing is not wasted—it's purposeful, even when it's painful."
  - "Immerse yourself in Scripture daily. You can't discern deception if you don't know the Word intimately enough to recognize when it's being twisted."

toRemember: You are not a child of the slave, but of the free woman—born by promise, saved by grace, kept by the Spirit. Hold fast to that freedom, and never trade it for anything less.
