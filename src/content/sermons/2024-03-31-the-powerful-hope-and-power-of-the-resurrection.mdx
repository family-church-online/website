mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus won the victory on Friday, not Sunday.
    body: The cross was the victory—Jesus died without sin, which means He could pay for ours. The resurrection is the proof that the payment worked.
  - title: Thomas doubted because he was disappointed.
    body: "When God doesn't come through the way you expected, doubt feels justified. Jesus meets Thomas exactly where he is—inviting him to touch, to see, to be at peace."
  - title: The empty tomb is historical evidence.
    body: "For 2,000 years, no one has produced Jesus' body. His enemies had every motive to discredit the resurrection—but they couldn't, because He rose."
  - title: Belief is the only requirement.
    body: "You can't erase the hurtful words you said, the lust you entertained, the anger you nursed. That's why Jesus says: just believe. It's already paid. Stop balancing columns."

  - "If you're disappointed with God, bring it to Him. He's not afraid of your doubt. He invites you to see and touch and believe."
  - "Live out of gratitude, not guilt. Love others because you've been forgiven, not because you're trying to earn something."
