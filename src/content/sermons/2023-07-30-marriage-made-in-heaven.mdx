mainPoints:
  - title: Different responsibilities do not mean different value.
    body: Scripture says we are all one in Christ — male and female, Jew and Greek, slave and free — yet God ordains distinct roles. The world confuses responsibility with worth; Jesus does not.
  - title: Marriage has four God-given purposes.
    body: Emotional (companionship), social (children and family), physical (holy space for sexual intimacy), and spiritual (displaying Christ and the church to the world).
  - title: Husbands are called to sanctify and satisfy.
    body: Sanctify means teaching the Word and helping your wife become more like Jesus. Satisfy means treating her needs as your own — if she is not at peace, you are not at peace.
  - title: Wives submit as the church submits to Christ.
    body: "Not out of fear or coercion, but out of trust in a husband who is laying down his life for her good and God's glory."
  - title: When marriage is broken, fix yourself first.
    body: "Jesus teaches us to remove the plank from our own eye before addressing the speck in another's. Only after you have surrendered fully should you bring in others according to Matthew 18."

keyIllustration: "A video surfaced of eight police officers forcing a car off the highway, smashing its window, dragging out the occupants, and beating one unconscious. The reaction was visceral: outrage, fear, betrayal. Why? Because authority given to protect was used to abuse. That same dynamic explains why Ephesians 5 triggers discomfort — husbands have abused the authority God gave them. But when authority is used as God designed — to serve, protect, and sanctify — it becomes beautiful."

application:
  - "Husbands: Ask yourself daily — am I laying down my life for my wife? Am I studying God's Word so I can teach her? Am I prioritising her satisfaction over my own?"
  - "Wives: Trust is a response to sacrificial love. If your husband is surrendering his life for you, honour him by submitting as the church submits to Christ."
  - "Everyone: When conflict arises, look inward first. Are you demanding your rights or surrendering your will? Jesus teaches the latter."
