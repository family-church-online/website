mainPoints:
  - title: God Is the God of New Beginnings
    body: Unlike us, who can only rearrange what already exists, God creates genuinely new things — and His pattern in Scripture proves His plan has never changed.
  - title: "The Toledot Pattern Reveals God's Eternal Plan"
    body: "Eleven times in Genesis and three more times across the Old Testament, this Hebrew phrase marks the establishment of Israel, the priesthood, the kingship — all pointing to the eternal Messiah in Matthew 1:1."
  - title: Jesus Invites You Into That Plan Now
    body: By faith in Christ, you leave the futile ways of your ancestors and enter a purpose established before the foundation of the world — one that turns even your failures into His glory.
  - title: The Plan Culminates in New Heavens and a New Earth
    body: "God's unchanging purpose doesn't end with the millennium; it leads to eternity where He dwells with His people, wiping away every tear, making all things new."

keyIllustration: "Solomon, the wisest and wealthiest man who ever lived, tried everything — pleasure, learning, building projects, relationships — and called it all meaningless. We face the same choice: live for our own plans and end up like Solomon, or surrender to God's plan and find the meaning we're looking for."

application:

  - "Live today as someone already invited into eternity — you're not striving to earn God's favour; you're resting in a plan established before time began and secured by Jesus' blood."
