# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus builds His church by orchestrating people, timing, and outcomes—our job is to keep the faith, trust His timing, and step in or step back as He directs.
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 16:18 ESV"
keyScriptureText: I tell you, you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus builds His church—not human effort.
    body: Paul experienced setbacks, opposition, and eighteen months of hard ministry in Corinth. Yet the church thrived because Jesus was building it. No amount of human opposition can stop what He is building.
  - title: Your relationship with God comes before your work for God.
    body: Paul cut his hair to fulfill a vow and refused to stay in Ephesus despite an open door for ministry. He prioritised his personal commitment to God over visible ministry success. Ministry flows from intimacy with Jesus, not the other way around.
  - title: Trust God to work through others.
    body: "Paul left Priscilla and Aquila in Ephesus and moved on. Later, Apollos arrived—eloquent, scripturally trained, and passionate. God orchestrated the team. Paul didn't need to do it all because it was never his church to begin with."
  - title: The church thrives when disciples prepare for injustice and cover for one another.
    body: "Like the Springboks training to play with fourteen men, the church must anticipate setbacks and persecution. When one member is removed or sidelined, others step in without complaint. That's how Jesus' team wins."

keyIllustration: "The South African rugby team anticipated injustice before their match against the Six Nations champions. The coach trained them to play with one man less, knowing a red card was likely. When it happened, they didn't complain—they outscored their opponents 19–3 in the second half. They prepared to cover for one another. That's how the church must operate: anticipating hardship, trusting Jesus, and doing more than your part without needing recognition."

application:
  - "Examine your life: are you prioritising your personal relationship with God, or are you too busy serving Him to spend time with Him?"
  - Trust Jesus to build His church. Stop trying to control outcomes, timing, or who gets credit. He orchestrates everything.
  - Look around at the empty seats in your church or community. Who will you love, serve, and invite until they come to know Jesus?

toRemember: "At the end of his life, Paul didn't list his accomplishments—he said, \"I have kept the faith.\" That's what matters. Keep your faith, trust Jesus to build His church, and make disciples."
