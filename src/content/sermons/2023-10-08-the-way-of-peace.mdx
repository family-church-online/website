# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God commands His soldiers to put on peace before they enter battle, not to seek it after the fight is over—because His peace is the assurance that He has already secured the victory.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 6:14-15 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Stand therefore, having fastened on the belt of truth, and having put on the breastplate of righteousness, and, as shoes for your feet, having put on the readiness given by the gospel of peace.

mainPoints:
  - title: Roman sandals gave soldiers stability.
    body: Thick leather soles with metal studs protected feet from battlefield hazards and provided firm footing in hand-to-hand combat—spiritually, this represents standing fast in the truth of the gospel no matter what the enemy throws at you.
  - title: Roman sandals gave soldiers mobility.
    body: "Unlike heavy boots, these light sandals allowed armies to move quickly and adapt to changing battlefield conditions—spiritually, this represents staying agile against Satan's constantly shifting tactics of lies, deception, and false righteousness."
  - title: "God's peace is not the world's peace."
    body: The world offers temporary relief through distraction, substances, status, or power, but the turmoil returns worse than before. God offers assurance that you are His child and nothing can snatch you from His hand.

keyIllustration: "A sixteenth-century reformer named Nicholas Ridley was sentenced to death by burning at the stake for opposing an ungodly monarch. On the eve of his execution, his brother offered to spend the night with him to comfort him in his anguish. Ridley responded, \"Please don't. I plan to sleep as peacefully as ever, Lord willing.\" He had peace before the battle even began because his confidence was in God, not his circumstances."

application:
  - "Reclaim God's peace through inspection and confession (Psalm 139:23-24)"
  - "Rejuvenate God's peace by spending time with Him (Psalm 119:65)"
  - "Replenish God's peace by trusting Him for your victory (John 16:33)"
  - "Retain God's peace by focusing your mind on Him (Isaiah 26:3)"
  - "Reinforce God's peace by meditating on His promises and counting your blessings (Colossians 3:15)"

toRemember: Be watchful, stand strong in your faith, act like men, be strong—put on the sandals with the studs, stand up for what you believe, and go into war in peace, knowing the Lord has already secured your victory.
