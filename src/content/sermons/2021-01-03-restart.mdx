# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When everything changes and you can't go back, God's church doesn't stop — you become the church wherever you are, doing what Jesus commanded: Scripture, prayer, worship, service."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 3:16-17 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And when Jesus was baptized, immediately he went up from the water, and behold, the heavens were opened to him, and he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove and coming to rest on him; and behold, a voice from heaven said, \"This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "Jesus' baptism was the moment everything changed forever."
    body: The old covenant ended, the new covenant began. God the Father publicly announced His Son, introducing grace and truth just as He once introduced the law at Sinai. Nothing would ever be the same.
  - title: Jesus was baptized to fulfill all righteousness and secure ours.
    body: He humbled Himself to be counted as if He were a sinner so that those who repent and believe could be counted as if they are righteous. This is the great exchange — Christ taking our sin, giving us His peace.
  - title: "Massive change doesn't mean God has lost control."
    body: "When our plans fail and our future feels uncertain, God's plans remain unshaken. Jesus promised the gates of hell will not prevail against His church — no matter what the world looks like."
  - title: The church is what you do, not where you go.
    body: "Four things make you the church: reading and applying Scripture, praying constantly, worshiping in all forms, and serving others sacrificially. You can do all four anywhere — in your home, your workplace, your gym."

keyIllustration: "On New Year's Eve 2020, a man walked alone through a massive, well-lit shopping mall in Bloemfontein. Every shop was polished and stocked, music played over the PA system, but not another person was in sight. The eerie emptiness mirrored the year itself — luxury and normalcy still visible but utterly deserted. The illustration captured the fear of that moment: will things ever go back to normal? The answer Scripture gives is no — but God's church will thrive in the new normal."

application:
  - Commit to being the church in your home, your business, your community — wherever you are allowed to meet and minister.
  - "Don't wait for buildings to reopen. Start now: read Scripture together, pray for those in authority, worship in whatever form you can, serve those who desperately need it."
  - "When you break bread and drink the cup, let it be your sign of commitment: \"Lord, I am part of Your body. I will be Your church this year, no matter what happens.\""

toRemember: "The world was changed forever by what Christ did — more radically than any other change that will ever occur, because it's permanent and eternal. Never again will man be separated from God by sin."
