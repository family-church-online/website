# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Christ's sacrifice was perfect and permanent — He entered heaven once to intercede for you, and when He returns, it will not be to address sin again but to save those who are eagerly waiting for Him."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 9:27-28 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And just as it is appointed for man to die once, and after that comes judgment, so Christ, having been offered once to bear the sins of many, will appear a second time, not to deal with sin, but to save those who are eagerly waiting for Him.

mainPoints:
  - title: Christ entered heaven itself, not a copy.
    body: The earthly tabernacle was only a shadow of the true Holy of Holies — Jesus entered the real presence of God to intercede for us.
  - title: His sacrifice was once for all, not repeated annually.
    body: Because He is the perfect sacrifice, He dealt with sin permanently — not just covering it, but putting it away.
  - title: You die once, then face judgment.
    body: There is no reincarnation, no second chances — death is appointed, and what follows is eternal verdict.
  - title: Jesus will return to save, not to judge sin again.
    body: "He's coming back for those who are eagerly waiting, having already addressed sin at the cross."

keyIllustration: "A father stood outside a hospital delivery room while doctors determined whether his wife and newborn daughter would survive complications during labor. That agonising wait — staring at the closed door, not knowing the verdict — mirrors the ancient Israelites watching the high priest disappear behind the veil into the Holy of Holies. They waited to see if God had accepted the sacrifice for their sins. The illustration exposes the tension: we too are waiting for a verdict, but Christ has already interceded."

application:
  - Live with an eternal perspective — death is not the end, but the doorway to judgment, and your life now determines your eternity.
  - "Reject the cultural lie that you'll get another chance after death — God's Word is clear: you die once, then face judgment."
  - "Cultivate eager anticipation for Christ's return — if the thought of His coming fills you with fear rather than hope, examine whether you truly believe His sacrifice covers you."

toRemember: Jesus is in heaven now, interceding for you — and when He returns, it will be to save those who are eagerly waiting for Him.
