  - title: God uses three witnesses to confirm His will.

keyIllustration: "A sports loss leaves you crushed for a night—imagine that pit-of-the-stomach feeling lasting not hours but weeks, because someone told you the gospel you believed wasn't enough. That's what the Gentile believers in Antioch experienced when unnamed teachers from Jerusalem arrived and told them circumcision was required for salvation. The pain was swift; the resolution took a delegation, a council, scriptural debate, and a letter delivered by multiple witnesses. Quick damage, slow repair—that's the nature of false teaching."

application:

  - "Search the Scriptures. Ask whether what you're being told agrees with the whole counsel of God's Word."
  - Be patient. God will use the long wait between crisis and resolution to strengthen your faith and prepare you to encourage others.
