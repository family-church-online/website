mainPoints:
  - title: But God — the only voice that reaches the dead.
    body: "You were spiritually dead, unable to hear anything but Satan, the world, and your own sin. But God, through Jesus, spoke life into you with the same authority that raised Lazarus and the widow's son."
  - title: Rich in mercy because of great love.
    body: "Mercy is not getting what you deserve — God's kindness, forbearance, and patience holding back the punishment you've earned. Love is God sending His Son as the propitiation, paying the debt you could never pay."
  - title: Made alive and seated with Christ — by grace.
    body: "God didn't just forgive your debt; He raised you from the dead, canceled the record against you, put the enemy to shame, and seated you on the throne with Jesus. Not because you worked for it. Because of grace."
  - title: "You're a gallery — put on display for the world."
    body: God saved you so that in the coming ages, people would look at your life and see the Artist, not the artwork. Your transformation, your forgiveness, your hope — all of it points the world to the immeasurable riches of His grace.

keyIllustration: "Standing in front of a Michelangelo sculpture in Rome, lit to bring the marble to life, the realization hit: no one travels across the world to see a piece of rock. They come to see the work of an artist so skilled that even dead stone seems to breathe. Replicas exist everywhere, but they mean nothing. The artist is what makes it priceless. That's why God puts us on display — not because we're impressive, but because His grace is."

application:
  - "Stop striving to prove you're worthy of salvation. If it bothers you that the thief on the cross or the workers hired at the last hour got the same grace you did, you've missed the gospel. Grace can't be earned — that's what makes it grace."
  - "Live as a gallery of God's work. When people see how you forgive, love, hope, and endure, they should wonder about the Artist who transformed you. You go into the world not to impress them with your respectability, but to show them the One who raised you from the dead."
  - "Let God's kindness lead you to repentance. Mercy isn't meant to sweep sin under the carpet — it's meant to make you so grateful that you lift up the carpet and say, \"Lord, I don't want this anymore. Take it all.\""

toRemember: "You are alive not because you fixed yourself, but because God, rich in mercy and great in love, raised you from the dead by grace — and now He's put you on display so the world can see Him."
