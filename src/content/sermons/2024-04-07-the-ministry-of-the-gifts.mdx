mainPoints:
  - title: You cannot learn a spiritual gift—only receive it.
    body: The Holy Spirit gives each believer the specific gift He wills, and no one receives all the gifts. The gift makes the giver glorious, not the recipient important.
  - title: The body needs every single part.
    body: Some gifts seem flashy; others seem mundane. But Scripture lists administration and helping alongside prophecy and miracles because the church cannot function without every member contributing what the Spirit has given them.
  - title: Love is the point of every gift.
    body: Gifts are temporary—they will pass away when Christ returns. But love is eternal. If a gift is used for attention, status, or pride, it defeats the entire purpose. Pursue love first, then earnestly desire the gifts.
  - title: Gifts must be practiced in order.
    body: When the church gathers, spiritual gifts are to be exercised for building up the body—not in chaos, but in turn, with interpretation when needed, and always with the aim of encouraging and instructing one another.

keyIllustration: A sprinter loses their big toe—a tiny bone, barely noticeable—and suddenly drops significant time off their sprint. The smallest part of the body matters more than we realise. In the same way, the church cannot afford to dismiss any member or any gift as insignificant. Every part is needed.

  - When the church gathers, expect the Spirit to move through the body—not just through the pastor or elders, but through you.
