bigIdea: "Your life's true value isn't in what you leave behind on earth, but in whether you've invested in helping others follow Jesus — a legacy that can't be taken away."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 20:28 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Pay careful attention to yourselves and to all the flock, in which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers, to care for the church of God, which he obtained with his own blood.

mainPoints:
  - title: Lead by example.
    body: Scripture calls us to live in a way that makes others want to follow Jesus — serving with humility, declaring truth, and testifying to repentance and faith.
  - title: "Don't hide from your mortality."
    body: Accepting that your time is short gives urgency and weight to how you invest each day.
  - title: Guard against drawing people to yourself.
    body: Selfishness and covetousness make you want followers for your own benefit; generosity keeps your focus on leading people to Jesus for theirs.
  - title: Hand the responsibility to Jesus.
    body: "You can't save anyone — only He can build His church. Do your part faithfully, but trust Him with the results."

keyIllustration: "A parent wrote to a scientist, distressed that teaching her children about the sun's eventual explosion left them feeling hopeless. The scientist replied that when the sun explodes, its elements will scatter and eventually form new life. But this \"hope\" misses the point entirely — if everything you build is destined for cosmic dust, you need something that outlasts the sun itself."

application:
  - Every small act of obedience — turning the other cheek, forgiving quickly, speaking truth gently — is a deposit into a testimony others will one day want to follow.
  - Work hard to give, not to accumulate. The more you focus on helping the weak and being generous, the less covetousness controls you.
  - "If your past is full of failure, remember: God's power is perfected in weakness. The more messed up your page, the more glory He gets when He redeems it."

toRemember: "You won't live forever — but what you build in others for Jesus will."
