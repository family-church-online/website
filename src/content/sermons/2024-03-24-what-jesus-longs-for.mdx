# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God draws near to us at specific moments in our lives, offering salvation, conviction, or restoration — and we dare not waste them, because the consequences of ignoring His visitation are devastating.
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 19:41-42, 44"
keyScriptureText: "And when he drew near and saw the city he wept over it, saying, \"Would that you, even you, had known on this day the things that make for peace! But now they are hidden from your eyes… because you did not know the time of your visitation.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus fulfilled ancient prophecy on Palm Sunday.
    body: "Zechariah 9:9 foretold the Messiah riding a donkey into Jerusalem; Daniel 9:26 prophesied the timing to the decade. Jesus entered publicly, unmistakably, as the promised King."
  - title: Three responses to the Messiah — then and now.
    body: "Some worshiped Him. Some accused Him. Some didn't even show up. Every person today falls into one of those same three categories."
  - title: God weeps over rejection but never stops calling.
    body: Despite centuries of Israel rejecting His prophets, God sent His Son. When they rejected Him too, He wept — not in anger, but in grief. He still longs for us to return.
  - title: Persistent rejection leads to spiritual blindness.
    body: "Like Pharaoh's hardened heart, Jerusalem reached a point where God said, \"Enough.\" Opportunity has an expiration date. Ignoring God's visitation brings judgment."

keyIllustration: A family of bush babies emerged from a hole in the thatch one evening in the Waterberg, and in their curiosity, came closer and closer until they lined up on a branch just two meters away — so close you could almost touch them. It never happened again. That one moment when they came near was unrepeatable. God chooses times in our lives to come near to us, and we dare not waste them.

application:
  - "Ask God to draw near this Easter — don't wait. If there's something unresolved, something you've been pushing away, now is the time to deal with it."
  - "Reach out to someone who's not interested in Jesus, or someone hypocritical who's hurt you. Pray the Lord comes near to them and gives them another opportunity."
  - "Never believe the lie that it's too late. If Jesus wept over Jerusalem after centuries of rejection, He hasn't given up on you either. Humble yourself, confess, and He forgives."

toRemember: "Jesus weeps for you, loves you, and longs to bring you back — but there will come a time when those chances end, so don't blow it."
