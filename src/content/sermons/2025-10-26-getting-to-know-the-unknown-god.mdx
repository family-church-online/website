# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "There are only two kinds of people in the world: those who know the living God and those who don't—and God commands everyone everywhere to repent, because He has fixed a day to judge the world by the man He raised from the dead."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 17:24-25 ESV"
keyScriptureText: The God who made the world and everything in it, being Lord of heaven and earth, does not live in temples made by man, nor is he served by human hands, as though he needed anything, since he himself gives to all mankind life and breath and everything.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Paul's spirit was provoked by idolatry."
    body: "Athens was full of shrines—more idols than people—and Paul immediately saw through the architecture and culture to the spiritual emptiness beneath it. Idolatry isn't just statues; it's anything you worship instead of God."
  - title: Paul demolished idolatry in one sentence.
    body: "He told the Areopagus that the God who made everything doesn't live in temples or need human service—He gives life to all. That single statement intellectually undermined every Greek philosophy and every modern atheistic worldview."
  - title: God is both infinitely powerful and knowable.
    body: "The same God who created the universe—so vast we cannot comprehend it—and the human cell—so intricate we still don't fully understand it—is near to each of us and wants to be found."
  - title: Repentance is a command, not an invitation.
    body: "God doesn't RSVP; He summons. The times of ignorance are over. Now He commands all people everywhere to repent, because He has appointed a day of judgment."

keyIllustration: A mathematician calculated the odds of the universe spontaneously emerging from nothing at one in ten billion—some say one in a hundred billion. No rational person would bet their eternal future on those odds, yet atheists stake everything on cosmic accident. By contrast, if you believe God created the universe, a resurrection is trivial.

application:
  - "Examine your life for idols—career, comfort, lifestyle, reputation—and ask whether they've delivered the satisfaction they promised. Augustine said our hearts are restless until they rest in God."
  - "When you share the gospel, don't soften it. Paul gave the Areopagus the full, unequivocal truth: God made everything, commands repentance, and will judge the world. Some mocked, but some believed."
  - Remember that all other religions lack a living Saviour. Christianity alone offers a God you can know, who absorbed your sin on the cross and rose to defeat death.

toRemember: "When God looks at you, He doesn't see your sin—He sees Jesus, because Jesus bore your punishment on the cross. That's why the choice is not just obvious; it's the only choice that makes sense."
