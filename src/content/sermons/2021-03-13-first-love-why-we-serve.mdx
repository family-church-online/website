mainPoints:
  - title: "The church's commendation: toil, endurance, and discernment."
    body: Jesus praises Ephesus for refusing to tolerate evil, testing false apostles, and enduring patiently for His name — they stood for truth without compromise.
  - title: "The church's accusation: abandoned first love."
    body: Despite their orthodoxy and perseverance, they had become hard from fighting — holding to truth without holding to the heart of worship, devotion, and love for Christ that first compelled them.
  - title: "The church's remedy: remember, repent, return."
    body: "Jesus commands them to remember where they've fallen from, repent of their coldness, and return to the works they did at first — not more activity, but renewed affection."
  - title: "The consequence of refusing: lampstand removed."
    body: "The gates of hell cannot take the church, but Jesus Himself will remove a congregation's lampstand if they will not repent and return to their first love."

keyIllustration: An oven glove with a small hole renders the entire glove useless — one tiny gap and the whole hand gets burned. A young boy asked for the stained, damaged glove, washed it, and had his grandmother mend the hole. The church is called not only to point out the hole — to speak truth — but to take the glove, wash it, and mend it in love.

application:
  - Examine whether your ministry has become duty rather than devotion — are you still doing the things you did at first out of love for Jesus?
  - Repent where fighting for truth has hardened you instead of refining you — ask God to restore the joy and compassion that first marked your faith.
  - "Commit to speaking truth in love, not just speaking truth — the church is built up when we help, wash, and mend, not only when we point out what's wrong."

toRemember: Without the love of Christ building up inside us and welling over, we cannot possibly love each other — and a church that loses its first love loses its lampstand.
