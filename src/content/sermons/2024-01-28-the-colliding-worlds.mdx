# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The gospel of grace is not a man-pleasing message—it puts us in the dust as beggars before God, demanding we accept that there is nothing we can do to save ourselves except receive Christ's free gift."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 1:10-12 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "For am I now seeking the approval of man, or of God? Or am I trying to please man? If I were still trying to please man, I would not be a servant of Christ. For I would have you know, brothers, that the gospel that was preached by me is not man's gospel. For I did not receive it from any man, nor was I taught it, but I received it through a revelation of Jesus Christ."

mainPoints:
  - title: You cannot please both God and man.
    body: "A gospel that tells you you're a sinner in need of a Saviour—not a helper—offends human pride and is not designed to win popularity contests."
  - title: "Paul's gospel came by revelation, not tradition."
    body: His fanatical background as a Pharisee and his violent persecution of the church make it impossible that he invented Christianity or learned it from other men.
  - title: The gospel demands a collision.
    body: "At some point, the worldview you've constructed to please yourself must collide with the truth of Christ—and you must choose which world is really upside down."

keyIllustration: A hotel owner spends an entire weekend trying to impress a posh clientele with a gourmet meal, only to serve them trifle instead of duck after a series of disasters. No matter what he does, he cannot get it right. The illustration captures the futility of trying to please man when it comes to God—no amount of effort or good intentions can substitute for the gospel of grace.

application:
  - If you are building a personal religion from scraps of philosophy and selective scripture, stop waiting for calamity to force the collision—seek Jesus now before your heart hardens further.
  - "If you love Jesus, ask yourself: what are you doing today to turn your upside-down world the right way up? The gospel is not polite, and neither should your witness be."
  - "Check your gospel: are you preaching (or believing) a message that flatters human autonomy, or one that calls people to fall on their faces before a holy God?"

toRemember: "The gospel isn't asking for your commitment—God has nothing to gain from your old life. He is offering you His commitment, and you simply need to receive it."
