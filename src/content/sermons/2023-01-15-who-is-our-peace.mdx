# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus is our peace because He protects us from the enemy, reconciles us to one another, and—most critically—shields us from the wrath of God.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 2:13-16 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far off have been brought near by the blood of Christ. For he himself is our peace, who has made us both one and has broken down in his flesh the dividing wall of hostility by abolishing the law of commandments expressed in ordinances, that he might create in himself one new man in place of the two, so making peace, and might reconcile us both to God in one body through the cross, thereby killing the hostility.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus protects us from the enemy.
    body: "Satan, temptation, sin, and death—Christ delivers us from all that would separate us eternally from God's love."
  - title: Jesus removes hostility between people.
    body: The ceremonial law that divided Jew and Gentile has been abolished; culture and custom no longer have the power to separate those who belong to Christ.
  - title: Jesus reconciles us to God.
    body: This is the foundation of all peace—being made right with the Father through the cross so that we have access to Him by one Spirit.
  - title: Jesus commands us to preach peace.
    body: He is our peace, He created peace, and He preached peace—now He entrusts that same ministry of reconciliation to us.

keyIllustration: "A wildebeest calf with an injured knee limps through the bush while a jackal shadows the herd, patiently waiting for an opening. The adults surround the calf so completely that it lies down to rest, safe even though the predator is still visible. The herd doesn't remove the threat—they absorb it by refusing to give the enemy access."

application:
  - When conflict arises in your marriage or friendships, reconcile with God first—as you both draw near to Him, you draw near to each other.
  - "Stop withdrawing when you're broken. The body of Christ can only practice love if you let them; your need is their privilege."
  - Cultural identity is not neutral—if something in your culture keeps you from obeying Christ or loving another believer, it must be set aside.

toRemember: The wicked have no peace—not because God withholds it, but because peace is the fruit of the Holy Spirit, and it can only grow where Christ has made someone righteous.
