# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Jesus didn't just delay God's judgment — He replaced the entire sacrificial system by offering His body in perfect obedience, cleansing you from the inside and giving you His Spirit to empower joyful obedience."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 10:5-7 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Consequently, when Christ came into the world, he said, \"Sacrifices and offerings you have not desired, but a body have you prepared for me; in burnt offerings and sin offerings you have taken no pleasure. Then I said, 'Behold, I have come to do your will, O God, as it is written of me in the scroll of the book.'\""

mainPoints:
  - title: The old sacrifices could not cleanse sin.
    body: Year after year, Israel offered bulls and goats — not because God needed them, but to remind His people of their guilt and their need for a Savior. The sacrifices closed the door on immediate judgment but left the heart unchanged.
  - title: God prepared a body for Jesus to offer in perfect obedience.
    body: "The phrase \"a body have you prepared for me\" means God equipped the Messiah for obedience. Jesus took on flesh, lived without sin, and willingly laid down His life — not under compulsion, but in joyful submission to the Father's will."
  - title: "Christ's sacrifice was once for all."
    body: "When Jesus said \"It is finished,\" He meant the debt was paid in full. There are no more sacrifices needed, no more guilt to manage. The old system is abolished; the new covenant is established by His blood."
  - title: The Holy Spirit now empowers you to obey from the inside.
    body: In the Old Testament, the Spirit came upon certain individuals. Now every believer receives the Spirit — not just to forgive, but to regenerate, to create new desires, and to supply the power to carry them out.

keyIllustration: "A broken dishwasher was packed full of dirty dishes. It sounded like it ran, but when you opened the door, everything was still filthy — and after a few days, the smell was unbearable. The machine had to be removed and replaced with a new one that actually cleaned the dishes from the inside. The old covenant sacrifices were like closing the dishwasher door: they kept judgment at bay, but they couldn't wash the heart clean. Jesus didn't repair the old system — He replaced it entirely."

application:
  - "Rest in the knowledge that Christ's sacrifice is complete — you are not managing guilt, you are free from it."
  - "Respond to the finished work by offering yourself in joyful obedience: \"Here I am, Lord. Use me.\""
  - Walk in the power of the Holy Spirit, who gives you both the desire and the ability to obey God and live in purity.

toRemember: By His will you have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all — it is finished, it is paid in full, and you are free to love Him.
