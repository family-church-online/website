# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The blood of Christ does what animal sacrifice and human effort never could: it purifies the conscience from dead works so we can serve the living God in freedom."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 9:14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: How much more will the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit offered Himself without blemish to God, purify our conscience from dead works to serve the living God.

mainPoints:
  - title: Old Testament sacrifice had limits.
    body: The blood of goats and bulls purified the flesh ceremonially, allowing worshipers back into temple space, but it could not touch the conscience or deal finally with sin.
  - title: Jesus is the superior High Priest.
    body: He entered the true holy place not with animal blood but with His own, securing eternal redemption through the power of the eternal Spirit.
  - title: Dead works cannot silence the conscience.
    body: Whether religious rituals, self-punishment, compulsive behavior, or distraction, human effort to deal with guilt always fails.
  - title: "Christ's blood purifies the conscience completely."
    body: When we confess our sin and trust that His sacrifice is enough, we are freed from guilt and empowered to serve the living God.

keyIllustration: "A golfer standing over the first tee must clear his mind of every shanked shot he's ever hit. An athlete under a high catch must forget the last time he dropped it. Mental discipline can help you live in the present — but the conscience is a different beast. It wakes you at night decades later. No training or willpower can silence it permanently. Only the blood of Christ can."

application:
  - Confess your sin honestly to God — name what has been plaguing your conscience.
  - "Trust in faith that Jesus' blood is powerful enough. Stop believing your sin is stronger than His sacrifice."
  - Stop trying to punish yourself or earn peace through dead works. Accept the finished work of the cross.

toRemember: When you confess it and believe that Jesus Christ has paid for it, it is done. Stop trying the dead works to make it go quiet. Give it the Word of God to shut it up.
