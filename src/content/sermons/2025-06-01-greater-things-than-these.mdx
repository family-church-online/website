# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Whatever the church faces—persecution, loss, isolation—God's plan for her growth is not thwarted, because Jesus Himself intercedes for His people and promises the gates of hell will not prevail."

keyScriptureText: And I tell you, you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Peter's arrest deliberately mirrors Jesus' arrest."
    body: "Herod—the same king who mocked Jesus—arrests Peter during Passover, imprisons him with multiple squads of soldiers, and plans to bring him before the people after the feast. Every detail echoes Jesus' passion, showing that Peter is walking the same road his Master walked."
  - title: "The one striking difference: the church prays for Peter."
    body: "Scripture records no one praying for Jesus during His arrest and crucifixion; all His disciples fled. But when Peter is imprisoned, the church makes earnest prayer to God for him—showing that Jesus' intercession for His people has birthed a praying community."
  - title: Jesus continues to intercede for us right now.
    body: "Christ's ascension means He sits at the Father's right hand, pleading for you every moment of every day. The victory was won on the cross, but His intercession sustains His people through every spiritual battle."

keyIllustration: null

application:

  - "We are called to pray fervently for one another, especially for those under spiritual attack, because corporate prayer is the Spirit's weapon in the cosmic battle."
  - Whatever you face, Christ is interceding for you before the Father—your survival is not dependent on your strength, but on His faithful advocacy.

toRemember: The gates of hell will not prevail against His church—not because we are strong, but because Jesus prays for us and builds His kingdom through every storm we face.
