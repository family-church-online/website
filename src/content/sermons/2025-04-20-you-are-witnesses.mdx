# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus appeared to His disciples not just to prove He was alive, but to commission broken, doubting people as witnesses — empowered by the Holy Spirit — to proclaim repentance and forgiveness to the world.
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 24:44-48 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Then he said to them, \"These are my words that I spoke to you while I was still with you, that everything written about me in the Law of Moses and the Prophets and the Psalms must be fulfilled.\" Then he opened their minds to understand the Scriptures, and said to them, \"Thus it is written, that the Christ should suffer and on the third day rise from the dead, and that repentance for the forgiveness of sins should be proclaimed in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem. You are witnesses of these things.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Disbelief is a sin that must be repented of.
    body: "Even after hearing testimony from the women, the Emmaus disciples, and others, the eleven were terrified and thought they saw a ghost. Jesus rebuked them for their lack of faith because disbelief says \"I don't trust You\" to God."
  - title: Jesus kept His scars to prove His identity and His sacrifice.
    body: He could have healed them completely, but He chose to bear the marks of crucifixion forever — so that we would never forget what it cost to save us.
  - title: Everything in Scripture pointed to this moment.
    body: "Jesus opened their minds to see that His suffering, death, and resurrection were always part of God's plan — not chaos, but sovereign purpose working through human sin."
  - title: You are called to be a witness, empowered by the Holy Spirit.
    body: Jesus has done what only He can do; now He commissions you to do what He made you for — to proclaim repentance and forgiveness in His name, starting where you are.

keyIllustration: "A piece of rotting, crumbly, slimy wood — smelling like raw mushrooms, seemingly useless — was brought into view. But in the hands of the carpenter, it was transformed into something beautiful and functional. That is what Jesus does with broken, sinful people: He makes them useful for His glory."

application:
  - "Confess where you have doubted God's control, even after He has proven Himself faithful. Repentance restores your relationship with Him."
  - Stop disqualifying yourself from being a witness. If Jesus can use the disciples who abandoned Him, He can use you.
  - "Wait for the Holy Spirit's power before trying to witness in your own strength. You were never meant to do this alone."

toRemember: No piece of rotting wood is so broken that it cannot be used in the hands of the carpenter from Nazareth, Jesus Christ our Lord.
