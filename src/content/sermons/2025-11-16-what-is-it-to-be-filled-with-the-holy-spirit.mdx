mainPoints:
  - title: Receiving the Spirit happens at salvation.
    body: "If you belong to Jesus, you have the Holy Spirit dwelling in you—this is the non-negotiable mark of every believer (Romans 8:9; 2 Corinthians 1:21-22)."
  - title: Being filled with the Spirit is for empowerment.
    body: "The apostles were filled at Pentecost, then filled again when they testified, then filled again when they prayed—it's not a one-time event, it's ongoing surrender for service (Acts 4:8, 31; Acts 13:9)."
  - title: Spiritual gifts are given to serve, not to rank you.
    body: "The Spirit distributes gifts as He determines—not so you can show off, but so the body needs you. Don't let anyone tell you you're deficient because you don't have a certain gift (1 Corinthians 12:7-11)."
  - title: Complacency is the enemy of empowerment.
    body: "Settling for \"just saved\" means you'll stand before Christ having never asked for the power to do His work—and that's a tragedy, not a safe landing."

keyIllustration: "A courtroom comparison: an advocate is eloquent and convincing, but when a witness takes the stand and says, \"I was there. I saw it. That did not happen\"—the witness wins. Jesus told His disciples to wait for power because they needed to become witnesses, not just advocates. You can live with Jesus for years, see Him crucified and raised, and still not be a witness until you're empowered by the Spirit."

  - "Pray this for each other and for the church. The body needs every member operating in the Spirit's power—not isolated believers running on fumes."
