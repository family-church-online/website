  - title: "The Holy Spirit is God's engagement ring."
    body: "The Greek word for \"guarantee\" (arrabon) is the same word used today for an engagement ring. The Spirit isn't just a helper—He's proof that God has committed Himself to you forever. Like a seal on a letter, He marks you as belonging exclusively to God, and that seal cannot be broken."

keyIllustration: "A termite colony sends out winged ants to find mates from other colonies. If even termites can commit permanently, how much more has God committed to you? He has sealed you with His Spirit as His bride, forever."
