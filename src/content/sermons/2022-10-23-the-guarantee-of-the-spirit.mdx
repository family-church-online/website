# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The Holy Spirit seals believers permanently, guaranteeing that the inheritance God has promised is secure forever—not because of what we do, but because of what Christ has already done.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 1:13-14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: In him you also, when you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation, and believed in him, were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit, who is the guarantee of our inheritance until we acquire possession of it, to the praise of his glory.

mainPoints:
  - title: The Father chose us; the Son purchased us; the Spirit seals us.
    body: "Salvation requires the work of all three persons of the Trinity. The Father determined our adoption before the world began. Christ paid for us with His blood on the cross. Now the Holy Spirit seals us—permanently marking us as God's exclusive possession."
  - title: Believing the gospel requires humility.
    body: "The gospel is that Christ died for our sins, was buried, and was raised on the third day. Faith means believing in your heart and confessing with your mouth that Jesus is Lord. This is hard because it means admitting you are not enough on your own—you cannot fix yourself or earn God's approval."
  - title: "The Holy Spirit is God's engagement ring."
    body: "The Greek word for \"guarantee\" (arrabon) is the same word used today for an engagement ring. The Spirit isn't just a helper—He's proof that God has committed Himself to you forever. Like a seal on a letter, He marks you as belonging exclusively to God, and that seal cannot be broken."

keyIllustration: "A termite colony sends out winged ants to find mates from other colonies. Scientists don't understand how, but all colonies in an area release these ants at exactly the same time on the same day. Once two termites find each other, they burrow into the ground, seal the entrance, and remain committed to one another for thirty to fifty years—monogamous for life. If even termites can commit permanently, how much more has God committed to you? He has sealed you with His Spirit as His bride, forever."

application:
  - "You don't have to fear losing your salvation. The Holy Spirit isn't a temporary gift—He is God's permanent guarantee that you belong to Him and will receive your full inheritance."
  - Give God your whole heart. If you know you were destined for destruction but Christ purchased you with His blood, the only fitting response is to give Him everything—your affection, loyalty, focus, and devotion.
  - "Hold on to the gospel. Faith isn't a one-time decision; it's a lifelong holding on to the truth that Christ died for your sins, was buried, and rose again. When life gets hard, keep believing. By God's grace, you can."

toRemember: You are betrothed to the God of the universe—exclusively His, permanently sealed, eternally secure. Death does not part you from Him; it opens the final door to the most perfect union that can be.
