# ── PRESENTATION COPY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
tagLine: "Grace doesn't give you permission to sin — it gives you power to love like Jesus."
shortDescription: "You've been set free by grace alone — but what do you do with that freedom? You could use it as permission to live however you want, or you could let it shape you into someone who loves like Jesus. Paul says liberty without love is just license. True freedom comes when you respond to what God's done with gratitude, not entitlement."
subtitle: "You are saved by grace through faith alone — but that freedom comes with a choice: use it as permission to sin, or let it ignite a life of love."
hook: "If you're saved by grace and not by keeping the law, does that mean you can live however you want? Paul confronts the most dangerous misunderstanding of the gospel: the lie that freedom from the law means freedom from obedience. True freedom isn't doing whatever feels good — it's being empowered to love like Christ."

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You are saved by God's grace through faith alone — but true freedom isn't doing whatever you want. It's being empowered by the Holy Spirit to love God and love others out of gratitude for what Christ has done."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 5:13-15 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love serve one another. For the whole law is fulfilled in one word: you shall love your neighbor as yourself. But if you bite and devour one another, watch out that you are not consumed by one another."

mainPoints:
  - title: Grace sets you free in four ways
    body: — from guilt and shame, from the penalty of sin, from the power of sin, and from the burden of trying to please God through rule-keeping.
  - title: Freedom requires a response
    body: — you can either take it for granted and use it as permission to sin, or respond in gratitude by living in a way that honors God.
  - title: The whole law is summed up in love
    body: "— not warm feelings, but a decision of the will to serve God and others. Love God first, then love people — even the ones you don't like."
  - title: Liberty without love destroys churches and families
    body: "— legalism and license both lead to division. True freedom is liberty plus love, and it's contagious."

keyIllustration: "In 2005, a malnourished stray dog in Kenya, cared for by a struggling family, discovered an abandoned two-day-old baby girl in a forest. Instead of harming the child, the dog carried her through fields and busy roads back to the family's home and tucked her in with her own puppies. That one act of love rippled outward: the family was lifted out of poverty, the child was adopted into a loving home, and the dog became a national hero. Love, even in the most unlikely places, is contagious — and when empowered by the Holy Spirit, it changes everything."

application:
  - "Ask yourself: Am I responding to God's grace with gratitude or entitlement? Am I using freedom as permission to sin, or as power to love?"
  - "Stop trying to earn God's approval through rule-keeping. Instead, love Him with everything you have and let that overflow into how you treat others."
  - "When you struggle to love someone, remember: you can only love others because He first loved you. Ask the Holy Spirit for supernatural power to love people you don't even like."

toRemember: Liberty plus love equals freedom. Liberty without love is just a license to sin.
