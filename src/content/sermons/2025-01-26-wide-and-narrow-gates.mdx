# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Ananias and Sapphira didn't just lie to the apostles — they brought deception into the holy presence of God dwelling in the early church, and the only fitting response to sin in contact with holiness is death."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 5:3-4 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But Peter said, \"Ananias, why has Satan filled your heart to lie to the Holy Spirit and to keep back for yourself part of the proceeds of the land? While it remained unsold, did it not remain your own? And after it was sold, was it not at your disposal? Why is it that you have contrived this deed in your heart? You have not lied to man, but to God.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Sin always presents itself as something good.
    body: Ananias and Sapphira appeared generous on the surface, but their deception reveals how wickedness disguises itself as virtue.
  - title: "God's holiness is more central than we realise."
    body: "Scripture shows that no one can survive the presence of God's holiness in an unrighteous state — He is like the sun: life-giving from a distance, consuming up close."
  - title: "The early church carried God's presence like the temple."
    body: "Just as rivers of life flowed from the temple in Ezekiel's vision, the Holy Spirit in the early believers made them carriers of God's holiness into the world."
  - title: We diminish God over time.
    body: Our prayers grow dull, our faith becomes routine, and we present one version of ourselves publicly while hiding another version in secret — just like Ananias and Sapphira.

keyIllustration: A man holds a small marble in his hand. That marble represents the entire universe — seven hundred thousand kilometres per hour around the Milky Way, one orbit taking two hundred and forty million years. God holds all of it in the palm of His hand. When we pray, He invites us to ascend into His presence. This God, who technically should want nothing to do with us because of our sin, wants us to come close.

application:
  - "Examine your life honestly: are there two versions of you — one for church and one behind closed doors?"
  - "Enlarge God in your prayer life by imagining yourself ascending into His presence like Jacob's ladder."
  - "Don't avoid difficult scriptures. Rip off the band-aid and dig deeper — it's how you deconstruct false ideas about God and encounter Him truly."

toRemember: The greatest Christian challenge is that over time, God diminishes in our lives — help us to magnify Him in our minds, hearts, and spirits, in everything we do.
