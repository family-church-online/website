# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Baptism does not save you — only faith in Christ saves. But baptism is not optional: it's a command of Christ and a public declaration that you have been clothed in His righteousness, joined to His body, and committed to His concerns."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 3:26-27 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For in Christ Jesus you are all sons of God, through faith. For as many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ.

mainPoints:
  - title: Baptism is commanded, not optional.
    body: "Jesus instructs us to be baptised (Acts 2:38) and to baptise new disciples (Matthew 28:19). It's not an extra feature — it's an instruction."
  - title: Baptism does not save you — faith does.
    body: "Ephesians 2:8-9 says we are saved by grace through faith, not by works. The thief on the cross was never baptised, yet Jesus promised him paradise. Baptism is for those who are already saved."
  - title: "When you are baptised, you \"put on Christ\"."
    body: "This ancient imagery means: (1) your shame is covered, (2) you are clothed in His righteousness, (3) you are joined to His body, and (4) you commit to imitate Him and share His concerns."
  - title: Baptism is a public declaration, not a private transaction.
    body: "It's not just for you — it's for everyone who witnesses it. When someone is raised from the water, the church sees a picture of death, burial, and resurrection."

keyIllustration: "In Greek and Roman culture, young boys wore a toga with a red outline to show they were children. When a boy came of age, the men of the community would gather, remove his childhood clothes, and dress him in adult clothing. This ceremony marked his acceptance as a full member of society. Paul uses this imagery in Galatians: when you are baptised, you are \"clothed with Christ\" — publicly marked as belonging to Him and welcomed into His family."

application:
  - If you have put your faith in Christ but have never been baptised, you are called to obey this command — not to earn salvation, but to publicly declare what Christ has already done.
  - "If you are struggling with a sin you hate but can't conquer, that doesn't disqualify you from baptism. Repentance matters, not perfection. But if there's a sin you love and won't let go of, wait — baptism is for those who have put on Christ."
  - "Every day, remember your baptism: you are dead to the old you, alive in Christ, clothed in His righteousness, and committed to His concerns. Live like someone who belongs to Him."

toRemember: You are not your own. You belong to God and to His people, and you are committed to serving Him — not because baptism made it so, but because Christ did.
