# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "This body is a tent — temporary. But the work God calls you to do is eternal. Live like you're passing through, but with permanent purpose."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 14:24-28 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Then they passed through Pisidia and came to Pamphylia. And when they had spoken the word in Perga, they went down to Attalia, and from there they sailed to Antioch, where they had been commended to the grace of God for the work that they had fulfilled. And when they arrived and gathered the church together, they declared all that God had done with them and how He had opened the door of faith to the Gentiles. And they remained no little time with the disciples.

mainPoints:
  - title: Follow the pattern of Scripture, but be ready for God to change the plan.
    body: "Paul expected to return via Cyprus. He didn't. God works uniquely with each of us — look for patterns, but hold your plans loosely."
  - title: "Prepare with prayer and fasting for God's protection and provision."
    body: The church sent Paul and Barnabas out after fasting and prayer. Too often we undertake things without asking God or asking others to pray for us.
  - title: Persevere in the grace of God.
    body: "Paul completed the mission — two years, two thousand kilometres, left for dead — and came home. But he wasn't done. This was the first of three missionary journeys."
  - title: Pronounce the grace of God.
    body: "When they arrived, they declared what God had done with them. Testimony reminds you of God's faithfulness and encourages the Church. The tragedy is keeping silent."
  - title: Participate in peace with the body of Christ.
    body: "They stayed with the disciples \"no little time.\" We need each other. The mission field isn't always overseas — it's the school, the retirement village, the home group."

keyIllustration: "A family went camping with two tents — and discovered someone had moved the tent poles from one of them. They had a tent and an envelope in cold Plett weather. The point: you don't build an empire in a tent. It's temporary. This body is a tent. Don't invest everything in what won't last. There's a permanent home waiting — and a permanent purpose to live out while you're here."

application:
  - "Ask God where He's calling you to serve — in the local church, in the school, in the retirement villages, in the community around you."
  - "Join a ministry or home group. Get involved. Say \"pick me\" — don't wait to be asked."
  - Declare what God has done in your life. Your testimony encourages others and reminds you of His faithfulness.

toRemember: "We're passing through temporarily, but our purpose is permanent."
