# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God authenticates His work by pouring out the Holy Spirit on those we thought were unclean — and we are called to make disciples of all nations, not build walls between them.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 10:44-46 ESV"
keyScriptureText: While Peter was still saying these things, the Holy Spirit fell on all who heard the word. And the believers from among the circumcised who had come with Peter were amazed, because the gift of the Holy Spirit was poured out even on the Gentiles. For they were hearing them speaking in tongues and extolling God.

mainPoints:
  - title: God was already drawing Cornelius before Peter arrived.
    body: No one comes to Jesus unless the Father first draws them — and Cornelius, a Gentile, feared God and did good, but still needed to hear the gospel from Peter.
  - title: The Jews were chosen first to bring the knowledge of God to all nations.
    body: "Their rejection of the Messiah doesn't mean God is finished with them — it opened the door for Gentiles to enter, and one day the full number will be saved."
  - title: Peter preached the same gospel to Gentiles that he preached to Jews.
    body: Jesus was anointed by God, did good, healed the oppressed, was murdered, raised from the dead, and will judge the living and the dead — and all who believe receive forgiveness.
  - title: Baptism follows belief and the receiving of the Holy Spirit.
    body: Scripture shows that those who heard, believed, and were filled with the Spirit were then commanded to be baptized — not before, not as infants, but as a testimony to what God had already done.

keyIllustration: "When Peter walked into a Gentile house filled with Cornelius' friends and family — people he'd been taught never to touch, eat with, or socialize with — he was stepping into the most uncomfortable room of his life. But as soon as he began preaching the gospel, all the awkwardness disappeared. The joy of proclaiming Jesus erased everything else."

application:
  - We are called to be in the world but not of it — which means resisting the hatred dividing people by race, politics, and nationality, and instead making disciples of all nations.
  - "If you've been saved but not baptized, be baptized — because Jesus commanded it and Scripture shows it follows belief and the receiving of the Holy Spirit."
  - We need the discernment and power of the Holy Spirit to know when to withdraw from toxic influences and when to snatch someone out of the fire.

toRemember: "One day, people from every tribe and nation will worship Jesus together — so we'd better get used to it now, because we're going to spend eternity doing it."
