# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God wants to take you from the mud puddle to the beach—from being saved to being a worshipper who lives headlong for His glory.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 3:14-19 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For this reason I bow my knees before the Father, from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named, that according to the riches of his glory he may grant you to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in your inner being, so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith—that you, being rooted and grounded in love, may have strength to comprehend with all the saints what is the breadth and length and height and depth, and to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Worship in truth: the gospel foundation."
    body: "True worship begins at the cross, where we see our sin and God's holiness, confess our brokenness, and receive the atonement Christ made for us. Like Isaiah, we respond: \"Here I am, send me.\""
  - title: "Worship in the Spirit: the power to become worshippers."
    body: "The Holy Spirit moves in us to produce love, joy, and peace. We're called to seek the baptism of the Holy Spirit—not just to be saved, but to be filled, empowered, and made bold for God's purposes."
  - title: Present your body as a living sacrifice.
    body: "Worship isn't just singing—it's offering everything you have to God: your body, mind, house, family, wealth, time. This is spiritual worship, and it flows from gratitude for what He's done."

keyIllustration: "A child sits in a dirty alleyway, giggling and splashing in a mud puddle, delighting in the sensory experience. You see the joy and you're filled with joy—but across the road, just a hundred meters away, is the beach. Wouldn't you want to pick that child up and say, \"I know this is amazing, but let me show you what's really amazing\"? That's what God does for us. He doesn't condemn us for enjoying the mud—He invites us to the beach."

application:
  - "Stop sitting on the fence between the world and God's purposes. Leap headlong into the field He's calling you to—a life of worship, power, and unspeakable joy."
  - "If you've never experienced the love, joy, and peace of the Holy Spirit, sit at the foot of the cross and ask God to save you, fill you, and baptize you in His Spirit."
  - Devote yourself to praying for your church, your family, and your brothers and sisters—that they would become worshippers, not just churchgoers.

toRemember: God is able to do abundantly more than all we ask or think, according to the power at work within us.
