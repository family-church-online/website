# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Baptism doesn't clean you or save you — it declares that Christ's righteousness, not your own, is what carries you through God's judgment."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Peter 3:21-22"
keyScriptureText: Baptism, which corresponds to this, now saves you, not as a removal of dirt from the body, but as an appeal to God for a good conscience through the resurrection of Jesus Christ, who has gone into heaven and is at the right hand of God, with angels, authorities, and powers having been subjected to him.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Christ's perfection is what makes salvation possible."
    body: "Jesus lived a sinless life so that He could take our place and pay a debt He didn't owe for people who owed a debt they couldn't pay."
  - title: "God's patience is not passivity."
    body: He delays judgment to give every person a chance to turn and trust in Christ, but judgment is still coming for all who reject Him.
  - title: Baptism is a declaration, not a transaction.
    body: "The water doesn't cleanse you; it announces that you've been sealed into Christ by faith and are now hidden in Him through His death and resurrection."
  - title: The resurrection proves your sins were dealt with.
    body: "If Christ had stayed dead, His sacrifice would have been meaningless. His resurrection is God's receipt that the payment was accepted."

keyIllustration: "Eight people entered the ark before the flood. They weren't the eight most righteous, powerful, or deserving people on earth — Noah found favour with God, but Scripture says nothing about the righteousness of his wife, sons, or their wives. It was grace. God sealed them in, and the same water that destroyed the earth lifted the ark safely above the judgment. Baptism is the same picture: you're sealed into Christ by grace, and the same God who will judge the world holds you safely through it."

application:
  - "Stop trying to earn what's already been given by grace. Trust Christ's righteousness, not your own."
  - "If you've trusted in Jesus, be baptised in obedience — not to be saved, but because you already are."
  - "Rest in the permanence of Christ: He's alive, reigning, and nothing can undo what He's done for you."

toRemember: "Baptism doesn't make you righteous — it declares you're hidden in the only One who is, carried safely through judgment by His grace alone."
