  - title: "God's patience is not passivity."
    body: He delays judgment to give every person a chance to turn and trust in Christ, but judgment is still coming for all who reject Him.
  - title: Baptism is a declaration, not a transaction.
    body: "The water doesn't cleanse you; it announces that you've been sealed into Christ by faith and are now hidden in Him through His death and resurrection."
  - title: The resurrection proves your sins were dealt with.
    body: "If Christ had stayed dead, His sacrifice would have been meaningless. His resurrection is God's receipt that the payment was accepted."
