# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The church can be mobilised by the fear of God and the comfort of the Holy Spirit working together — the same people can be made fearful and comforted, and that combination equips them to go out with joy.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 9:31 ESV"
keyScriptureText: So the church throughout all Judea and Galilee and Samaria had peace and was being built up. And walking in the fear of the Lord and in the comfort of the Holy Spirit, it multiplied.

mainPoints:
  - title: The fear of the Lord mobilises you when circumstantial fear paralysed you.
    body: The disciples hid in Jerusalem, terrified of persecution. God sent their persecutor — converted — to show them His power and call them out.
  - title: Fear and comfort are not opposites; they work together.
    body: "The fear of the Lord is awe, fascination, and obedience. The comfort of the Holy Spirit is the assurance that you are held in God's mercy and empowered to act."
  - title: When you walk in fear and comfort, you get up and go.
    body: Peter left Jerusalem, went to Lydda, found a paralysed man, prayed in faith, and watched God heal him — and the whole region turned to the Lord.

keyIllustration: Stephen Lungu walked into a church tent in Zimbabwe with a group of armed soldiers, carrying petrol bombs, intending to annihilate the entire congregation. Jesus got hold of him in that service, brought him to his knees, and he gave his life to Christ. He spent the rest of his life ministering the gospel across Africa.

application:
  - Ask God to give you the fear of the Lord and the comfort of the Holy Spirit — then get up, go, look for someone who needs Jesus, and trust Him to work powerfully through you.
