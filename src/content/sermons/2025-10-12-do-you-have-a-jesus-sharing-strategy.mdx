mainPoints:
  - title: "You're not working alone."
    body: "Paul and Silas didn't plant churches in every town—they trusted the body of Christ and God's sovereignty to fill the gaps. Strategic mission matters, but so does trusting God's bigger plan."
  - title: Celebrate the small victories.
    body: "A few souls saved in Thessalonica before Paul fled wasn't failure—it was eternal life for those few. Don't let setbacks blind you to what God is doing."
  - title: Expect resistance.
    body: "Jealousy, violence, and opposition came every time Paul preached. If you're ready for it, it won't derail you—it's just part of the game."
  - title: Remember who you serve.
    body: Jesus is the ruler of the kings on earth. You work for the King of kings, and He will reward, restore, and make all things work according to His plan.

keyIllustration: "A child walks along a beach covered in thousands of dying starfish, picking them up one by one and tossing them back into the sea. A passerby says, \"What are you doing? You'll never get them all back. It's pointless.\" The child picks up another starfish and replies, \"This one doesn't think it's pointless,\" and tosses it back into the water. A few lives changed for eternity is never small."

application:
  - "Stop trying to do God's work alone. Find other believers, pray together, plan together—mission is a team sport."
  - "When setbacks pile up, deliberately celebrate the small wins. Write them down. Remember them. They're bigger than you think."
  - "Accept that resistance will come—jealousy, opposition, even violence. Prepare your heart for it, and you won't be derailed when it arrives."

toRemember: "You serve the King of kings—He doesn't lose, He will reward you, and He will be glorified."
