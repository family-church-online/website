You protected each person to make sure that your Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, would be born a descendant of David through Mary and legally through Joseph. We know, Father God, that he is God in the flesh. He is God that has become human. He always was, he always is, and he always will be the Son of God, our Lord Jesus Christ. Father, we no matter what goes wrong, no matter how things fall apart, we thank you that you are always here. Your words and your promises are true and we can trust you for forgiveness. So we come to you in the quiet of our hearts now Lord, and we just offer up our prayer of repentance to say, Lord, I'm sorry. I'm sorry I've tried to do this myself. I'm sorry I've been so afraid of the Grinches. I'm sorry I'm so so afraid of all the things that happen, when all I need to do is trust, be quiet, and let you heal me. Please hear our prayer now, Lord, in Jesus' name. Lord, as we come to the table we remember this is your body. As we have bread, we remember that you died for us. You, the Great Shepherd, laid down your life for us so that we could live. As we drink this grape juice, remember that this is your blood given for the forgiveness of our sins. We thank you that you are a just and holy God and that we can trust in your mercy. Please prepare our hearts to celebrate the table now Lord, prepare our minds and wash over us this amazing sense of Your peace and Your forgiveness and Your comfort. In Jesus name. Amen.
