mainPoints:
  - title: The tabernacle was designed to be temporary.
    body: God chose a tent, not a temple, to emphasise that this arrangement was never meant to last. After His presence left the physical temple in Ezekiel, He pointed forward to something better.
  - title: "The symbols inside pointed to both God's faithfulness and Israel's failure."
    body: "The manna, Aaron's staff, and the tablets represented God's provision, His chosen leadership, and His law — yet Israel grumbled, rejected, and broke all three."
  - title: No amount of ritual could cleanse the conscience.
    body: The priests laboured daily, offering sacrifices over and over. The high priest entered the Holy of Holies once a year with blood — but it had to be repeated annually because earthly blood cannot permanently cover sin.
  - title: Christ tore the curtain and opened the way permanently.
    body: When Jesus poured out His blood on the cross, He ended the cycle. The curtain ripped. Access to God is no longer limited to one man, one day, one year. It is open to all who believe.
