# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The church exists to love and worship God and to love others so they can love and serve God too—and we do this through four daily practices: prayer, worship, scripture, and serving."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 22:37-39"
keyScriptureText: "Jesus replied, \"Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.\" This is the first and greatest commandment. And the second is like it: \"Love your neighbor as yourself.\" All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments."

mainPoints:
  - title: Love God with everything.
    body: "This goes beyond emotion—it requires obedience, steadfast devotion, and a commitment to following God's will in every area of life."
  - title: Love others as yourself.
    body: Love is not passive feeling; it is active service, reflected in intentional outreach, charity, and help for those facing challenges.
  - title: Worship expresses our purpose.
    body: We were created not to avoid punishment, but to be worshippers—loving, enjoying, and glorifying God in spirit and in truth.
  - title: Honor and obey God above all.
    body: Obedience is a profound expression of love, aligning our lives with His teaching and making us salt and light in a dark world.

keyIllustration: Corrie and Betsie ten Boom were sent to a Nazi concentration camp for hiding Jews during World War II. In that place of despair, they held worship services, shared Scripture with fellow prisoners (many of them Jews), prayed for one another—and even for their enemies. They brought light into the darkest place imaginable by living out prayer, worship, scripture, and service.

application:
  - "Before making any decision, ask: \"Is this in obedience to God? Will this bring me closer to Him or further away?\""
  - Make the four pillars—prayer, worship, scripture, serving—part of your daily life, not just your Sunday routine.
  - Share burdens with one another, pray for each other, and encourage each other by lifting one another up in love.

toRemember: "We are called to be salt and light in a dark and tasteless world—obedience to God's commands is what makes us both."
