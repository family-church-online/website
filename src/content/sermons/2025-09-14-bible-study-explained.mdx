# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Scripture is God speaking—not just about morality or history, but about Himself, about you, and about Jesus—and when you learn to ask the right questions, it reprograms your mind with truth the world cannot touch.
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 24:27, 32 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And beginning with Moses and all the prophets, he interpreted to them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself… They said to each other, \"Did not our hearts burn within us while he talked to us on the road, while he opened to us the scriptures?\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Scripture reveals your heart to you because your heart is deceitful.
    body: "Jeremiah says the heart is desperately ill and deceiving above all else. You will hide from yourself what Scripture exposes—so ask, \"What is this passage showing me about myself that I don't want to see?\""
  - title: All Scripture is about Jesus, not just the New Testament.
    body: Jesus Himself said Moses and the prophets wrote about Him. If you only read the New Testament, you miss what the apostles assumed you already knew from the Old. Every passage reveals something about what He has done.
  - title: Scripture is God-breathed, carried along by the Spirit, and absolutely trustworthy.
    body: "2 Peter 1:20-21 says prophets were driven by the Spirit the way a ship is driven by a storm—utterly carried along. 2 Timothy 3:16 says all Scripture is breathed out by God. It is not one voice among many; it is the only voice you can trust completely."
  - title: To study Scripture rightly, ask three questions of every passage.
    body: "What does this teach me about God? What does this teach me about myself and other human beings? What does this teach me about Jesus and what He has done? If you ask these faithfully, you follow Scripture's own method of interpretation."

keyIllustration: "Two disciples walked the Emmaus road, broken and disappointed after the crucifixion. Jesus appeared but kept them from recognizing Him. He listened to their pain, then called them foolish for not believing the prophets. He spent hours walking them through Moses and the prophets, showing them how it all pointed to His suffering and glory. Only after He revealed Himself at the table did they say, \"Didn't our hearts burn when He opened the Scriptures?\" The living Christ was in front of them—but what set their hearts on fire was the Word explained."

application:
  - "Audit your media diet: are you consuming more bad words (news, social media) than good words (Scripture)? If so, make a concrete change—read the Bible before you check your phone, limit news to once or twice a week, and replace scrolling time with a reading plan."
  - Join or start a Bible study group where you can read a passage together and ask the three diagnostic questions aloud. Accountability and community keep you from just confirming what you already think.
  - "If you're not on a daily Bible reading plan, get on one today. The church website has resources—don't let another week pass where you remain biblically malnourished."

toRemember: If you fill your mind with what is true, honorable, just, pure, lovely, and commendable—and practice what you see in Scripture—the God of peace will be with you.
