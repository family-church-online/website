keyScriptureRef: "1 Thessalonians 1:2-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: We give thanks to God always for all of you, constantly mentioning you in our prayers, remembering before our God and Father your work of faith and labor of love and steadfastness of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.

mainPoints:
  - title: Work of faith.
    body: "True faith isn't just something you believe — it's something you act on. Your obedience proves your trust in Jesus is real."
  - title: Labor of love.
    body: "You don't work to earn salvation; you work out of gratitude for what Jesus has already done. It's a joy, not a transaction."
  - title: Steadfastness of hope.
    body: Jesus is coming back. Hold on to that certainty, ignore the lies, and live every day like He could return today.

keyIllustration: "A child on the first day of school sits alone, afraid, waiting desperately for home time. But the children who thrive are the ones who stop passively waiting and start engaging — making friends, doing the work. The Thessalonians were like anxious schoolchildren longing for Jesus' return. Paul told them: don't just sit there afraid. Get busy doing what God called you to do."

application:
  - "Root yourself daily in God's Word so you can discern truth from lies."
  - Cut out distractions that steal your focus from Kingdom work — even good things can pull you away.
  - Share the gospel and live as a testimony of His love, knowing He could return at any moment.

toRemember: "Jesus is coming back. Stay ready. Stay busy. And keep working out of gratitude for what He's already done."
