# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus is the eternal, sinless High Priest who intercedes for us perfectly — and He is the only one who can save us.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 7:25-26 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Consequently, he is able to save to the uttermost those who draw near to God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for them. For it was indeed fitting that we should have such a high priest, holy, innocent, unstained, separated from sinners, and exalted above the heavens.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus is the perfect priest prophesied from the beginning.
    body: Psalm 110 foretold a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek — not from the line of Aaron, but eternal, without beginning or end. Jesus fulfills this perfectly.
  - title: He is the only mediator between God and humanity.
    body: Human priests died and had to offer sacrifices for their own sins first. Jesus lives forever, is without sin, and offered Himself once for all. There is no other way to God.
  - title: He is alive and interceding for you right now.
    body: "Every other religious figure, ancestor, or saint is dead. Jesus conquered death, rose from the grave, and sits at the Father's right hand pleading for you by name."

keyIllustration: "A family adopted two kittens from a rescue haven. The cats had been saved, fed, sheltered — but only when they were brought into a home did they experience what it meant to be known by name, held, loved, and truly safe. Many of us live thinking we're fine, that we don't need saving — until we experience the love of God and realize what we were missing all along."

application:
  - Stop turning to dead ancestors, saints, rituals, or worldly comforts for salvation. There is one God and one mediator — Jesus Christ.
  - Acknowledge your need for rescue. The works of the flesh — anger, envy, division, idolatry — separate us from God. We cannot save ourselves.
  - Call on Jesus by name. He is alive, He hears you, and He intercedes for you perfectly. This Christmas, let Him be your Saviour — not just a figure in a nativity scene.

toRemember: You have a living High Priest who gave His life for you, rose again, and is interceding for you right now — call on Him, and let Him save you.
