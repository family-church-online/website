# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "True commitment to God is demonstrated through three inseparable actions: sacrifice (worship that costs you something), submission (honouring the authority He places over you), and supplication (prayer for the gospel to advance)."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 13:15-16 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Through him then let us continually offer up a sacrifice of praise to God, that is, the fruit of lips that acknowledge his name. Do not neglect to do good and to share what you have, for such sacrifices are pleasing to God.

mainPoints:
  - title: Sacrifice — Worship that costs you
    body: "God doesn't need your worship, but He is worthy of it. True worship includes praising Him, sharing what you have, and serving others. Commitment without sacrifice is just a promise you never intended to keep."
  - title: Submission — Obeying godly authority
    body: "God places spiritual leaders over us to shepherd, not dominate. If you can't receive the authority of leaders you can see, how will you receive the authority of God you don't always see?"
  - title: Supplication — Praying for the gospel to advance
    body: We are commanded to pray earnestly and continually — not just for ourselves, but for the gospel to move and save those for whom it was sent. Without this, we are not functioning as the church.

keyIllustration: "A small bird was observed carrying something white in its beak, flying to a different tree, and deliberately dropping it — bird droppings from her nest. She was removing waste far from her chicks to protect them from predators. This small, unglamorous act of sacrifice illustrates what commitment looks like: doing what costs you something, not because it's comfortable, but because it protects and serves those you love."

application:
  - "Worship isn't just what you do on Sunday morning — it's how you use every gift God has given you with gratitude, and how you share those gifts with others."
  - If you struggle with authority, ask God to help you see that honouring godly leadership is honouring Him.
  - "Commit to praying regularly and specifically for the gospel to advance — in your community, in the persecuted church, and in places you'll never see."

toRemember: "Jesus offered the ultimate sacrifice, obeyed the Father's authority, and intercedes for us still. When commitment feels too costly, remember — He did it first, and He empowers you to do the same."
