# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus has already determined the outcome — we are being carried through the storm, not abandoned in it.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 23:11 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "The following night the Lord stood by him and said, \"Take courage, for as you have testified to the facts about me in Jerusalem, so you must testify also in Rome.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus has already decreed the outcome.
    body: "Before Paul ever boards the ship, Christ tells him he will reach Rome. Human authorities think they are making decisions, but God's plan was set long before."
  - title: "The storm is not out of God's control."
    body: "The same Greek word for \"driven along\" by the storm is used to describe how the Holy Spirit carries Scripture writers and how Christ upholds the universe. What looks like chaos is divine steering."
  - title: "Hope abandoned is faith's opportunity."
    body: God allows the storm to strip away all human strength and hope so that Paul and the others must rely entirely on Him — not on experts, not on favorable winds, not on themselves.
  - title: "Paul's intercession saves lives."
    body: God grants Paul the lives of all 276 people on board — not because they deserved it, but because Paul asked. Christian prayer matters far more than the world knows.

keyIllustration: "The preacher describes nearly drowning during a dive on the south coast — holding onto wreckage as currents threatened to sweep him into open ocean, nose bleeding from stress, helpless until someone else set up a rescue line. That terror taught him the ocean's power — and what it means to need carrying."

application:
  - When circumstances scream that God has lost control, return to His promises — not to what you see around you.
  - "Pray for those around you, even when they won't listen to your counsel. God may save them because you asked."
  - Faith grows strong by believing what God has said and acting as if it is true — especially when nothing confirms it yet.

toRemember: "When you believe God's promises, you fulfill the purpose for which you were created: to glorify Him by trusting His word."
