# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You cannot fully belong in Christ's Kingdom until you've left the world behind—exclusion from what feels safe is the only path to inclusion in what lasts forever."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 13:13-14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Therefore let us go to him outside the camp and bear the reproach he endured. For here we have no lasting city, but we seek the city that is to come.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Follow Leaders Who Speak God's Word"
    body: Imitate those who faithfully proclaim Scripture, especially those who gave their lives for the gospel, because their authority comes from the message they carry, not themselves.
  - title: "Jesus Never Changes—His Gospel Doesn't Either"
    body: "Cling to the unchanging gospel of grace; don't mix it with the world or let shifting teachings dilute your strength, which comes only from knowing you're chosen by Christ."
  - title: We Have an Altar They Cannot Eat From
    body: "Just as the Day of Atonement sacrifice was burned outside the camp, Jesus suffered outside the city to sanctify you—you're called to leave the comfortable and join Him there."
  - title: Bear the Reproach Christ Endured
    body: "Going all in means facing rejection, exclusion, and persecution for Jesus, but that's where true belonging begins—not in the world's acceptance but in the family of God."

keyIllustration: "A young man arrives at military conscription, stripped of everything familiar—clothes, hair, routine, friends, identity. Over nine weeks, brutal training breaks down every external comfort until he belongs to a platoon. The bond becomes so deep he doesn't want to go home alone anymore, but together. The point: you don't get inclusion without first being excluded from everything you once knew."

application:
  - "Stop trying to straddle the world and the Kingdom. You can't belong in both. Choose fully where you stand."
  - "Recognise your true family: the brothers and sisters in Christ around you, not the world's approval or old comforts."
  - "When you face rejection for following Jesus, remember it's not unexpected—He told you it would happen, and it means you belong to Him."

toRemember: "This world is not your home—it's temporary, passing away. Your lasting city is the New Jerusalem, where God dwells with His people forever, and every tear is wiped away."
