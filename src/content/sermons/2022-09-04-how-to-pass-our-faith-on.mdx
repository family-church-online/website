# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God calls you precious not because of what you've done but because He gave His Son in exchange for you—and that truth is the one worth passing on to the next generation."
keyScriptureRef: "Isaiah 43:1-4 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But now thus says the Lord, he who created you, O Jacob, he who formed you, O Israel: \"Fear not, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by name, you are mine. When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; and through the rivers, they shall not overwhelm you; when you walk through fire you shall not be burned, and the flame shall not consume you. For I am the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior… Because you are precious in my eyes, and honored, and I love you, I give men in return for you, peoples in exchange for your life.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Ask three questions when you read Scripture.
    body: "What does the passage tell us about God? What does it tell us about people? What does it tell us about Jesus and what He's done for us? This keeps you from cherry-picking verses out of context."
  - title: Isaiah 43 was written to exiled Israel—but it still speaks to you.
    body: "The character of God doesn't change, human waywardness doesn't change, and the gospel Jesus fulfills is foreshadowed even here: God exchanges nations for Israel just as He will exchange His Son for you."
  - title: Jesus makes you part of the flock.
    body: "In John 10, He says He has other sheep not of Israel's fold—and those sheep hear His voice. You are known by name, loved, and protected by the Good Shepherd."
  - title: Your value comes from what God gave for you.
    body: "Not your achievements, looks, or family—but the fact that God traded His Son's life for yours. That's your worth."

keyIllustration: "A team game was played where children passed a ball row by row until it reached the front. The point: faith is passed person to person, generation to generation, just like that ball—but only if everyone plays their part. If you want the next generation to value what God has done, you have to practice your faith every single day."

application:
  - Stop basing your worth on what you do or how you look. You are precious because God says so—and He proved it on the cross.
  - "Read the Bible honestly. Don't rip verses out of their original context. Ask the three questions every time."
  - Pass faith on by living it twenty-four-seven. The next generation learns not from what you say but from watching you celebrate with God, grieve with God, and make decisions with God.

toRemember: You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies—sin and death must watch as you dine with the Shepherd who gave everything for you.
