# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The gospel comes directly from Jesus, and its authenticity is proven both by independent witnesses preaching an identical message and by the radical transformation it produces in every life that truly receives it.
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 1:11-12 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "For I would have you know, brothers, that the gospel that was preached by me is not man's gospel. For I did not receive it from any man, nor was I taught it, but I received it through a revelation of Jesus Christ."

mainPoints:
  - title: Paul received the gospel directly from Jesus, not from human instruction.
    body: He insists he spent only fifteen days with Peter and James — far too short to learn what he preaches. His message came by revelation, not training.
  - title: Independent witnesses preach the identical gospel, proving it comes from God.
    body: "The twelve apostles, Jesus' brothers James and Jude, and Paul all received the same message through completely different means. This consistency testifies to its divine origin."
  - title: The gospel must radically turn your life around.
    body: "Paul went from murdering Christians to preaching the faith he once tried to destroy. If the gospel hasn't changed you, either you haven't received the true gospel or you haven't trusted it."
  - title: God pursues you first and empowers the transformation.
    body: "You don't find Jesus — He finds you. The Father draws you, plants the desire for change, and the Holy Spirit recreates you as a servant of God. The struggle against sin is supernaturally empowered."

keyIllustration: "Stephen Lungu grew up abandoned, violent, filled with hatred for anything to do with Jesus. Trained as a Marxist guerrilla, he was sent to blow up a bank but found a tent revival instead. He decided to plant explosives around the tent and murder everyone inside. But first, he thought, let's go in for two minutes and mess with them. He walked in with his gang, knife ready, mocking the worship — and the gospel found him. A young woman from Soweto gave her testimony. A pastor read Romans 6:23. Stephen broke down weeping, laid down his weapons, and gave his life to Christ. Years later, praying at an outreach, he prayed for a woman's healing. She was healed, accepted Christ, then said, \"I have one more thing — you're my son.\" The mother who had abandoned him at six years old. The gospel that turned a would-be church bomber into an evangelist also brought forgiveness and reconciliation with the one who had hurt him most."

application:
  - "If you call yourself a Christian but see no change, ask yourself: have I really understood and trusted the gospel? Come for prayer and help."
  - "If you're yearning for transformation but it feels slow, be patient but not complacent. The desire itself proves God is pursuing you, and change will come."
  - "The gospel is not insurance or club membership — it's supernatural recreation. You are meant to become a completely new creation."

toRemember: The same gospel that turned a murderer into an apostle and a bomber into an evangelist will radically change you too — because that is the power of Jesus Christ.
