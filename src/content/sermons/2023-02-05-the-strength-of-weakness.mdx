# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God doesn't need your strength—He displays His wisdom and power most clearly when you admit your weakness and trust in His grace instead of your ability."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 3:8-10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: To me, though I am the very least of all the saints, this grace was given, to preach to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ, and to bring to light for everyone what is the plan of the mystery hidden for ages in God who created all things, so that through the church the manifold wisdom of God might now be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places.

mainPoints:
  - title: Pride traps us in two ways.
    body: "Either we beat ourselves up for failing to meet our own standard, or we take credit when things go well—both responses reveal we're trusting our strength instead of God's."
  - title: "The gospel reconciles God's righteousness and grace."
    body: A righteous judge must punish sin, but God becomes flesh to take that punishment Himself so He can be both just and merciful—this is the power we rest in.
  - title: "God equips you for what He's called you to do."
    body: "You don't have to prove yourself or manufacture strength—He created you for a purpose and will provide what you need to fulfill it."
  - title: "The church displays God's wisdom to angels."
    body: "It's not isolated strong individuals who teach the heavenly realms about God—it's the weak, broken church loving and obeying together that reveals His manifold wisdom."

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - Stop trying to be strong enough on your own. Admit your weakness to God and trust Him to accomplish what only He can do through you.
  - "Don't believe the lie that your past disqualifies you. Paul persecuted the church and called himself the least of the apostles—yet God used him powerfully because it was never about Paul's strength."
  - "Work hard, but recognize it's God's grace at work in you. Your commitment increases when you realize the pressure is off—you're not holding everything together, He is."

toRemember: "When you feel beaten and finished, remember: God is not done with you—His power is made perfect in your weakness, and He works all things together for good to those who love Him."
