We pray Father for your grace, for your love and for your mercy to minister into our hearts this morning to wash us clean, to empower and strengthen us not as one person, but as the church. The wonderful beautiful body of Christ working together to glorify you and to teach even angels the depth of your unsearchable wisdom. Hear our prayer, Lord, as we confess our sin to you, as we seek your forgiveness, your assurance, and your peace. In Jesus' name. Amen.
