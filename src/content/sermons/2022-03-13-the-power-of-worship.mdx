# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Worship is not confined to Sunday—it's the foundation of daily Christian life and the most powerful response to suffering, anxiety, and darkness."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 16:25-26 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "About midnight Paul and Silas were praying and singing hymns to God, and the prisoners were listening to them. Suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken, and immediately all the doors were opened and everyone's bonds were unfastened."

mainPoints:
  - title: The four walls of the church are Scripture, prayer, worship, and serving.
    body: These are not separate activities but overlapping realities that form the foundation of daily Christian life—they protect the church from being shut down by persecution, lockdowns, or crisis.
  - title: Worship culminates everything we do as believers.
    body: Reading Scripture, praying, serving others, baptism, breaking bread—all of it is an act of declaring the goodness and glory of God.
  - title: Paul and Silas worshipped in prison not for their own release but for the salvation of others.
    body: They prayed for the prisoners and the jailer, and God answered by bringing an earthquake, a conversion, and a baptism—their freedom was the side effect, not the goal.
  - title: "Worship in the night is God's gift to pierce the darkness."
    body: When anxiety suffocates you and Scripture memory or prayer feel impossible, singing praise to God becomes the way out—not because it changes your circumstances, but because it fixes your eyes on Him.

keyIllustration: "A man lies awake in the night, suffocating under anxiety and pain. Scripture meditation doesn't calm him. Reflecting on the gospel doesn't bring peace. Then he begins to sing a worship song he knows—its words pierce the darkness and restore his soul. This is the power of worship in the night: it's God's weapon when nothing else works."

application:
  - Learn worship songs now—memorise the words—so that in your darkest hour, you have a weapon to pierce the night.
  - If you only worship on Sunday, you are not prepared for the crises, lockdowns, or suffering that will come. Practice Scripture, prayer, worship, and serving every day.
  - "Turn your prayers outward: stop praying only for your own relief and start praying for the salvation and freedom of those watching you suffer."

toRemember: "When you begin to practice Scripture, prayer, worship, and serving, it's catchy—and it transforms not only your life but the lives of everyone watching you."
