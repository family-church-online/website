# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When believers become spiritually exhausted, the greatest danger isn't physical collapse—it's spiritual isolation, and the only remedy is the body of Christ meeting together to carry each other home."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 12:12-13 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Therefore, lift your drooping hands and strengthen your weak knees, and make straight paths for your feet, so that what is lame may not be put out of joint, but rather be healed.

mainPoints:
  - title: Spiritual exhaustion has visible symptoms.
    body: "Drooping hands, weak knees, and wandering feet aren't just physical—they're pictures of prayerlessness, inconsistency, and drift. The writer of Hebrews diagnoses the church and says: you can still recover, but you must act now."
  - title: The call to holiness and peace is impossible alone.
    body: "We're commanded to pursue peace with everyone and holiness before God at the same time—a tension only sustainable by the Holy Spirit and the support of other believers. Isolation guarantees failure."
  - title: Three compromises destroy endurance.
    body: "Under pressure, believers are tempted to return to legalism (trying to earn God's favour), turn to false gods (coping mechanisms that replace Christ), or deny their faith entirely (selling their inheritance like Esau for temporary relief)."
  - title: Most people quit before they have to.
    body: "The \"wall\" in long-distance running is primarily psychological, not physiological. The same is true spiritually—most believers who fall away do so not because they couldn't continue, but because they stopped believing they could. Someone running beside you can turn that around."

keyIllustration: "In the Comrades Marathon, runners organise into \"buses\"—pace groups led by experienced runners who keep the group on track to finish within a certain time. If you fall off the bus, you're unlikely to finish. If you pick the wrong bus or the leader fails, the whole group falls apart. The church is meant to function this way: believers keeping pace together, encouraging those who falter, and ensuring no one is left behind when the finish line comes into view."

application:
  - If you cannot meet in person, you must intentionally meet with others online—do not watch services alone.
  - "When you see someone's hands dropping or knees wobbling spiritually, reach out immediately. Do not assume they'll recover on their own."
  - Reject the lie that you can rest your way back to spiritual health. Endurance is built in community, not in isolation.

toRemember: "We have only twelve hours of daylight to do the work of Jesus, and He's returning—don't be the one who sees the finish line from a distance and realises it's too late."
