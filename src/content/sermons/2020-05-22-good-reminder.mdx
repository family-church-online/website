mainPoints:
  - title: "Angels announced three critical moments in Jesus' mission."
    body: "At His birth, the resurrection, and the ascension — each time declaring God's faithfulness and sealing the certainty of His plan."
  - title: The disciples were told to wait for power, not timing.
    body: "When they asked if Jesus would restore Israel's kingdom, He redirected them: you'll receive the Holy Spirit, and you'll be witnesses — that's what matters when I return."
  - title: Jesus ascended bodily and will return bodily.
    body: "The same Jesus who was lifted up into the clouds, obscured from their sight, will return the same way — physically, gloriously, with heaven's armies."

keyIllustration: "A person once said they would have loved to witness the ascension — just to see Jesus' face as He returned to the Father, knowing He had achieved everything He was sent to do. That moment captures the heart of Ascension Day: total victory, perfect obedience, and the restoration of His eternal throne."

application:

  - "When you're discouraged by delay, remember: God's plans never fail. The same certainty behind Jesus' birth and resurrection guarantees His return."
  - "Stop gazing at what you've lost. You have everything you need — the Holy Spirit — to be His witness until He comes back."
