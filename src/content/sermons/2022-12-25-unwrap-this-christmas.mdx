# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Jesus' judgment isn't something to fear if you've already unwrapped yourself before Him — confessed your sin, trusted His forgiveness, and let Him begin making you like Himself."
keyScriptureRef: "1 John 1:9 ESV"
keyScriptureText: If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus is qualified to judge.
    body: He became fully human, faced every temptation you face, yet never sinned — so He knows your struggle and judges with perfect fairness.
  - title: The verdict depends on whether you trust Him.
    body: Those who reject the Judge fear His return; those who trust Him celebrate it, because He took their judgment on Himself at the cross.
  - title: You are the gift that needs unwrapping.
    body: "Stop hiding behind the wrapper of who you want people to think you are — confess what's really inside, and let Him transform it."
  - title: When He returns, the world finally works.
    body: "His judgment isn't just personal; it's cosmic — He will make all things right, and that reign will never end."

keyIllustration: "A wrapped present under the tree has endless potential — it could be anything you want. But once opened, the truth is revealed. We live like wrapped gifts, hoping no one sees what's really inside. Jesus says, \"I've already seen it, and I still love you. Now let Me transform what's in there.\""

application:
  - Stop pretending before God. Confess specifically — the lies, the grudges, the hidden sins — and trust that He is faithful to forgive and cleanse.
  - "Let the promise of His return reframe your stress. The world doesn't work now, but it will — and permanently."
  - "Celebrate judgment as good news: if you're in Christ, condemnation is finished, and transformation has begun."

toRemember: "When you unwrap yourself before Jesus now, judgment is done — forever. You'll spend eternity singing with the rivers and the hills because there is no condemnation for those who are in Christ."
