# ── PRESENTATION COPY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
tagLine: "The early church didn't fake unity—they were possessed by a Spirit that made selflessness irresistible."
shortDescription: "When the Spirit descended at Pentecost, He created something impossible: a community that craved God more than comfort, shared possessions without compulsion, and praised with simplicity. You can't manufacture that kind of church. But you can pray for it. What would it look like if that same Spirit moved in your community today?"
subtitle: "The Spirit doesn't create religious clubs—He births communities that hunger, fear, share, and praise in ways that expose our self-interest as poverty."
hook: What made the early church impossible to ignore? Not signs and wonders alone, but a community so radically transformed that generosity replaced greed, awe replaced ritual, and joy replaced striving. Can the same Spirit produce that today?

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The greatest miracle of Pentecost was not tongues of fire or the sound of rushing wind—it was the birth of a community whose Spirit-fueled love made selfishness obsolete.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 2:42-45 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And they devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers. And awe came upon every soul, and many wonders and signs were being done through the apostles. And all who believed were together and had all things in common. And they were selling their possessions and belongings and distributing the proceeds to all, as any had need."

mainPoints:
  - title: A Spirit-filled community craves the things of God.
    body: When the Spirit regenerates you, what was once boring becomes the source of insatiable hunger—Scripture, fellowship, prayer, and worship shift from duty to delight.
  - title: A Spirit-filled community walks in the fear of God.
    body: Awe is the proper response to an awesome God. We are called not to fake His power, but to revere His holiness whether He performs miracles or not.
  - title: A Spirit-filled community shares selflessly.
    body: "They weren't under compulsion—their hearts had been so transformed that the needs of others became more pressing than clinging to possessions."
  - title: A Spirit-filled community worships joyfully.
    body: Receiving their food with glad and simple hearts, they overflowed in praise because God Himself was their portion—not abundance, not security, but God alone.

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "Examine whether your hunger for God's word and God's people is growing or fading—that appetite is a mark of the Spirit's work, not your willpower."
  - Stop trying to manufacture what only the Spirit can produce. Pray fervently that He would break into your community as He did at Pentecost.
  - Ask whether contentment and generosity mark your life, or whether accumulation and self-protection still rule your heart.

toRemember: There is nothing we can do to reproduce a Spirit-filled community beyond sowing good seeds and watering them with fervent prayer that the same Spirit who transformed them would transform us.
