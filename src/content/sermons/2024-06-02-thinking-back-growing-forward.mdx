# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God uses the past to teach us, not to trap us—He purchased your freedom so you could live as His child today, trusting that He wastes nothing.
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 4:6-7 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And because you are sons, God has sent the Spirit of His Son into our hearts, crying, \"Abba! Father!\" So you are no longer a slave, but a son, and if a son, then an heir through God."

mainPoints:
  - title: Remember where you were.
    body: "Before Christ, we were enslaved—either to the law's demands or the world's elementary principles, constantly trying to earn God's approval."
  - title: Remember what God has done.
    body: At exactly the right time, God sent His Son to live under the law, die for us, and purchase our adoption as His children.
  - title: Celebrate where you are now.
    body: "You are not a slave but a son, indwelt by the Holy Spirit, able to call God \"Abba, Father\" in every moment of need."
  - title: "Trust where He's taking you."
    body: God never wastes anything—every trial, every uncertainty is being used to conform you to the image of Christ.

keyIllustration: "A visual was shown of a broken, weathered pallet next to a beautifully painted one. When God looks at you, He doesn't see the broken pallet—He sees the beautiful one, because you are covered in Christ. And sometimes the best thing you can do is let someone sit on you, bearing their weight for God's glory, trusting that He is using even that for eternal purposes."

application:
  - "Stop asking why the former days were better. The present is in God's hands, and He is doing something new right now."
  - "Don't try to paint over your brokenness. Accept that God sees you as covered in Christ, and live from that identity."
  - "When chaos tempts you to retreat, remember: you are adopted, indwelt by the Spirit, and an heir of God's kingdom."

toRemember: You have a God who loves you, who purchased you, who created you for a purpose—go and enjoy serving Him, even in pain, knowing He will not waste it.
