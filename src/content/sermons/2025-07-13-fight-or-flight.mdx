This sermon asks: when does faithfulness mean staying under fire, and when does it mean moving on to the next town?"

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Faithfulness to the gospel means persevering boldly under opposition until God leads you forward—and your obedience now determines your eternal reward.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 14:3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: So they remained for a long time, speaking boldly for the Lord, who bore witness to the word of His grace, granting signs and wonders to be done by their hands.

mainPoints:
  - title: Start with those already searching.
    body: Paul and Barnabas began in synagogues among people who knew Scripture and sought God—building momentum before reaching the unreached.
  - title: The gospel always divides.
    body: Jesus said He came to bring a sword, not peace—truth forces a response, even splitting families and uniting unlikely enemies against Him.
  - title: "Opposition met with \"so they remained.\""
    body: "When rejection came, Paul and Barnabas didn't retreat—they stayed longer and spoke bolder, and God confirmed their witness with miracles."
  - title: Know when to move forward.
    body: "After sustained faithfulness, the Spirit directs you to the next town—don't waste time where hearts are closed when others are waiting to hear."

keyIllustration: "A rugby team faces the breakdown—the chaotic pile where the ball is contested and players throw their bodies in with strength and bravery. Last week they were stopped; this week they persevered at the breakdown and won 45-0. Spiritual opposition is the same: you can't win without engaging the fight, and perseverance under pressure changes the outcome."

application:
  - "Don't soften the exclusivity of Christ—if someone doesn't have the Son, they don't have the Father, no matter their religion or sincerity."
  - Ask God for power when opposition comes—expect signs, boldness, and wisdom as you remain faithful under pressure.
  - Release guilt over people who reject you—move forward to those who will listen; there are too many towns left to reach.

toRemember: "Jesus is returning at an hour you don't expect—your faithfulness now determines your eternal reward and responsibility in His Kingdom forever."
