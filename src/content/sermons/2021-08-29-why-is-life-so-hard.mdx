# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God disciplines you not because He's angry, but because He loves you too much to leave you the way you are—His goal is to make you like Jesus."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 12:5-6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: My son, do not regard lightly the discipline of the Lord, nor be weary when reproved by Him, for the Lord disciplines the one He loves and chastises every son whom He receives.

mainPoints:
  - title: Consider who has suffered with you.
    body: Jesus endured the worst suffering humanity could inflict—betrayal, torture, death—not just to show solidarity, but to take on Himself the consequences of your sin and mine.
  - title: You are adopted, not automatically a child.
    body: "Scripture is clear: we are made in God's image, but we become His children only when He purchases us from slavery to sin and adopts us through Christ."
  - title: All suffering has purpose if you belong to God.
    body: "Romans 8:28 guarantees that for those who love Him, every hardship, every tear, every ache will be used to conform you to the image of Jesus—nothing is wasted."
  - title: Discipline is healing, not punishment.
    body: "The story of the blind man in Mark 8 shows Jesus leading him out of the crowd, touching him progressively, patiently—this is how God disciplines: in love, over time, dealing with what needs to be dealt with."

keyIllustration: "A child forced to sit in a car for six hours might call it torture—until you tell them the beach is waiting at the end. The suffering doesn't change; the perspective does. Similarly, when you suffer in the gym, your lungs burn and your muscles ache, but you endure it gladly because you know it's making you stronger. God's discipline works the same way: the pain is real, but so is the purpose."

application:
  - "Stop asking \"Why me?\" and start asking \"Who is with me?\"—then spend time with Him."
  - "Pray this: \"Lord, don't let me waste this suffering. Work it for good. Make me like Jesus.\""
  - "Take others to Jesus the way the blind man's friends did—invite them to church, share sermons, ask God to touch them."
  - "Discipline your children in love, not anger, so they can one day trust God's correction."

toRemember: You were bought from a slave market, washed, clothed, and adopted—God will not leave you unchanged, because He loves you too much for that.
