  - title: All suffering has purpose if you belong to God.
    body: "Romans 8:28 guarantees that for those who love Him, every hardship, every tear, every ache will be used to conform you to the image of Jesus—nothing is wasted."
  - title: Discipline is healing, not punishment.
    body: "The story of the blind man in Mark 8 shows Jesus leading him out of the crowd, touching him progressively, patiently—this is how God disciplines: in love, over time, dealing with what needs to be dealt with."

God's discipline works the same way: the pain is real, but so is the purpose."
