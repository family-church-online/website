# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Christ is above every power, authority, and name — and as His body, the church is seated with Him in that position of absolute victory and intimacy.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 1:21-23 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Far above all principality and power and might and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in that which is to come. And He put all things under His feet, and gave Him to be head over all things to the church, which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all.

mainPoints:
  - title: "God's redemptive plan has four parts:"
    body: "Election (chosen before creation), justification (forgiven through Christ's blood), sanctification (the walk of transformation by the Spirit), and glorification (face to face with Christ in eternity)."
  - title: Sanctification is the journey between position and practice.
    body: "In Christ, you are perfectly justified — that's your position. But in practice, you still sin, repent, and grow. The Holy Spirit empowers this walk, convicting you of sin, making God real, opening Scripture, and filling you with love."
  - title: Christ is above every name.
    body: Principalities, powers, demons, financial woes, health crises, addiction, political leaders, worldly philosophies — if you can name it, Christ has authority over it. Everything will bow before Him.
  - title: "The church is Christ's body."
    body: This is not a distant relationship. The Head (Christ) and the body (the church) are intimately connected. He knows your sore thumb. He knows every struggle, every infirmity, and He will never leave you.

keyIllustration: "Climbing a mountain on a cloudy day, you reach what you think is the summit — then the clouds lift for a moment, revealing a vista so magnificent your mind can't take it all in. Then the clouds return. That's what it's like with God's truths. Sometimes the Holy Spirit gives you a glimpse of the staggering reality of who Christ is, and other times you walk step by step in faith, trusting what you saw."

application:
  - Stop trying to manage your crises alone. Christ is above every named threat in your life — bring it to Him first, not as a last resort.
  - "Make space for Jesus. John the Baptist said, \"He must increase, I must decrease.\" If you want to walk closer to your position in Christ, you must displace the world to make room for Him."
  - Rely on the Holy Spirit to open your eyes. You cannot grasp these truths in your own strength — pray for the eyes of your heart to be enlightened.

toRemember: Christ knows your struggles better than you do — and He promises to never leave you. Walk forward in the confidence that you are part of His body, seated with Him above every power.
