    body: Principalities, powers, demons, financial woes, health crises, addiction, political leaders, worldly philosophies — if you can name it, Christ has authority over it. Everything will bow before Him.

    body: This is not a distant relationship. The Head (Christ) and the body (the church) are intimately connected. He knows your sore thumb. He knows every struggle, every infirmity, and He will never leave you.
