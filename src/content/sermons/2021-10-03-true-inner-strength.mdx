# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

keyScriptureText: "Keep your life free from the love of money, and be content with what you have, for he has said, \"I will never leave you nor forsake you.\" So we can confidently say, \"The Lord is my helper; I will not fear; what can man do to me?\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Strength comes from serving your church community.
    body: "When you're struggling, the natural impulse is to turn inward—but Scripture says look outward. Serve other believers. Visit those in prison. Show hospitality. You'll find that helping others lifts you out of your own pain."
  - title: "Guard your marriage—it's a pillar of resilience."
    body: "Whether you're married or single, Scripture calls you to honour the covenant of marriage. Sexual purity isn't just about obeying rules—it's about protecting the strength God designed marriage to provide in times of crisis."
  - title: "The only unshakeable strength is Christ's hold on you."
    body: "Communities can be scattered. Marriages can be broken. But no one can snatch you from the Father's hand. That is the one reality no loss, persecution, or crisis can ever take from you."

keyIllustration: A friend mentioned that a middle ear infection was causing dizziness and blurred vision. It seemed strange—until learning about the vestibular ocular reflex, a lightning-fast neural link between the inner ear and the eyes. When your head moves, your inner ear tells your eyes to move in the opposite direction so you stay focused. If the connection fails, your vision blurs. The ear and the eye need each other. So does the body of Christ. You are not useless—you are essential. The church cannot function without you.

application:
  - "If you're struggling right now, stop isolating. Look for opportunities to serve someone else in your church—help with a meal, visit someone lonely, pray for someone in need. Watch what happens to your own sense of strength."
  - "If you're married, guard your covenant. If you're single, honour the marriage bed you may one day share by keeping yourself sexually pure now."
  - "Cultivate gratitude for what you have, not resentment for what you've lost. Meditate on the reality that Christ holds you and will never let you go."

toRemember: "You will only know true inner peace when you have the freedom to help others for no other reason than because God told you it's the right thing to do—and His blessing of peace, power, and strength will come with obedience."
