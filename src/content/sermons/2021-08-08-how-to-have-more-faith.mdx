takeaways:
  - Faith is a gift from God, not something you manufacture through effort

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Faith isn't something you accumulate for yourself—it's a gift from God that multiplies when you pass it on to others through your words, your obedience, and your willingness to trust Him in adversity."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 11:31 ESV"
keyScriptureText: By faith Rahab the prostitute did not perish with those who were disobedient, because she had given a friendly welcome to the spies.

mainPoints:
  - title: Where does faith come from?
    body: "Faith is initiated by God—it comes from hearing His Word (Romans 10:17) and is given to us by the Holy Spirit as a gift (1 Corinthians 12:9)."
  - title: How does faith grow?
    body: "Faith multiplies when you use what you've been given: by speaking God's Word to others, by obeying Him in the small unseen decisions, and by sharing your story across generations."
  - title: What is the purpose of faith?
    body: Faith exists to save lives eternally and restore our relationship with God—not to make us more comfortable, but to draw others into His Kingdom.

keyIllustration: "A pagan prostitute named Rahab sheltered Israelite spies because she had heard what God did to Egypt. That single act of faith didn't just save her life—it planted seeds that grew through generations, eventually producing Boaz, who showed mercy to Ruth, whose lineage led to King David and ultimately to Jesus Christ. One woman's obedience in a moment of crisis became the thread God wove into the Messiah's arrival."

application:
  - "Stop asking \"How can I get more faith?\" and start asking \"Who can I share my faith with today?\" The question shifts from accumulation to multiplication."
  - "Do the small things right in front of you—forgive the person who wronged you, stand up for someone being bullied, speak truth when it's costly. Let God handle the big things."
  - Use this season of trial to grow faith in others. When this pandemic ends, let there be more people headed to heaven because you chose to obey.

toRemember: "Faith doesn't come from trying harder—it comes from trusting deeper and sharing wider."
