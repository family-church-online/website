# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God orchestrated every detail of history so that Jesus would be born exactly where and when Micah predicted—and your response to that gift determines whether your time on earth will be wasted or redeemed.
keyScriptureRef: "Micah 5:2 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But you, O Bethlehem Ephrathah, who are too little to be among the clans of Judah, from you shall come forth for me one who is to be ruler in Israel, whose coming forth is from of old, from ancient days.

mainPoints:
  - title: God loves us beyond comprehension.
    body: From the moment of the fall, He planned to reunite with us—no matter the cost, no matter our brokenness.
  - title: God has a plan, and it unfolds with impossible precision.
    body: Seven hundred years of history, a Roman census, a pregnant woman on a donkey—all orchestrated so Jesus would arrive in Bethlehem exactly as foretold.
  - title: God loves humble beginnings and humble endings.
    body: "Jesus was born poor, lived without status, and died poor—by worldly standards a failure, but by God's standard the epitome of success."
  - title: Jesus obeyed the Father completely.
    body: "He came to do the Father's will, and that same call to obedience extends to us."

keyIllustration: "A CEO explains to his ambitious young protégé that time is like an airplane ticket. Each seat has value only if it's occupied. Once the plane takes off, empty seats drop to zero value—the opportunity is gone forever. Missed time with family, missed moments of obedience to God, cannot be recovered. Fill your plane with passengers, and know which missed opportunities you can live with and which you cannot."

application:
  - Love God back by getting to know Him—read Scripture, pray, worship, develop a real relationship.
  - Trust His plan instead of trying to control outcomes in your own strength.
  - Stop measuring success by career, wealth, or status; focus on changing hearts and making an eternal difference.
  - Learn obedience by listening to what God wants from you, not what the world demands.

toRemember: "The scribes knew the prophecy but never went to Bethlehem. Don't let knowledge replace relationship, or busyness replace obedience."
