# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: When you stand in the truth no matter the cost, Jesus stands to acknowledge you before the Father.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 7:56 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Behold, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God.

mainPoints:
  - title: "The Fight: False Accusations Are Weapons of the Enemy"
    body: "Stephen was full of the Spirit, doing great signs, when the Synagogue of the Freedmen accused him of blasphemy. False accusations come when you're spiritually strong and fruitful — designed to sow doubt, fear, and derail your mission. Recognise them as tactics of the enemy, but do not be afraid. The Holy Spirit will give you the words to say in that very hour."
  - title: "The Counter Attack: Brutal Honesty Rooted in Scripture"
    body: "Stephen responded with a systematic walk through Scripture — from Abraham to the temple — showing that Moses, the law, and the temple all pointed to Jesus. Then he delivered the brutal truth: \"You stiff-necked people — you murdered the Messiah you've been waiting for.\" He wielded the sword of the Spirit with precision and refused to compromise, even knowing the cost."
  - title: "The Victory: Jesus Stands"
    body: "Jesus is seated at the right hand of the Father — except in this moment. When Stephen declared the truth, Jesus stood to acknowledge him before the angels of God. Stephen's martyrdom was not defeat but victory. His witness impacted Saul, scattered the church, and spread the gospel across the Mediterranean. God uses all things for His glory and the good of those who believe."

keyIllustration: Two young girls performed a simple acrobatic dance after an extraordinary act. Nervous and stumbling, they were visibly struggling — until the entire audience began clapping in rhythm, unprompted. The girls relaxed, found their stride, and finished strong to roaring applause. Their father stood and ran to embrace them, overwhelmed with pride. This illustrates what Jesus does when we stand for Him under pressure — He stands to cheer us on and acknowledge us before the Father.

application:
  - When falsely accused, trust in the Lord. Recognise the lies as schemes of the enemy and respond in truth, regardless of the consequences.
  - Do not compromise when confronted with the cost of standing with Jesus. The Holy Spirit will equip you with wisdom and courage in the moment.
  - Remember that your life is a play on a stage — there will be ups and downs, applause and jeers. If you stay true to the end, Jesus will be standing, waiting to acknowledge you before the Father.

toRemember: "If you stay true to the end, Jesus will be standing, waiting to acknowledge you before the Father's angels."
