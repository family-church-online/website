# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The gospel alone saves us by faith — baptism does not save, but it is the obedient, worshipful declaration that salvation has already happened.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 10:43-44 ESV"
keyScriptureText: To him all the prophets bear witness that everyone who believes in him receives forgiveness of sins through his name. While Peter was still saying these things, the Holy Spirit fell on all who heard the word.

mainPoints:
  - title: Being good is not enough.
    body: Cornelius was devout, generous, and God-fearing — but he still needed to hear the gospel. Good works cannot save us; we fall short of the glory of God.
  - title: The gospel is the power of God for salvation.
    body: "Peter simply preached the gospel: Jesus died for our sins, was buried, and was raised on the third day. Nothing else was required — Cornelius and his household believed and received the Holy Spirit immediately."
  - title: Baptism is obedience, worship, and a sign.
    body: It does not save, but it seals and declares what has already happened. You enter the new covenant by being born again — and baptism is the sign of that new birth.

keyIllustration: "A wedding ring doesn't make you married — your covenant commitment before witnesses does. The ring is the sign and seal. In the same way, baptism doesn't save you — faith in the gospel does. Baptism is the visible declaration to the world that you belong to Jesus."

application:
  - Stop trying to earn your salvation. Rest in the finished work of Christ — your standing before God is based on His grace, not your goodness.
  - "Memorise the gospel: 1 Corinthians 15:1-6. Know what saves you and be ready to share it with others."
  - "If you've believed the gospel but haven't been baptised, obey Christ's command — declare publicly what has already happened in your heart."

toRemember: God loves you exactly the way He finds you, but He loves you too much to leave you that way.
