# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's covenant with Abraham was established by faith and sealed by promise — and no law introduced 430 years later could cancel what God had already ratified."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 3:17-18 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "This is what I mean: the law, which came 430 years afterward, does not annul a covenant previously ratified by God, so as to make the promise void. For if the inheritance comes by the law, it no longer comes by promise; but God gave it to Abraham by a promise."

mainPoints:
  - title: Even human covenants cannot be broken once ratified.
    body: "Paul appeals to what the Galatians already understand: a covenant, once sealed, cannot be added to or annulled — and God's covenant is far more binding than any human agreement."
  - title: "God's promise to Abraham pointed to one offspring: Christ."
    body: The Abrahamic covenant was never about many descendants earning their way to God, but about one descendant — Jesus — through whom all nations would be blessed by faith.
  - title: The Law did not replace the promise.
    body: "The Mosaic Law came centuries after Abraham and served to separate Israel from surrounding nations, but it was never the means of salvation — that was always by faith in God's promise."
  - title: The inheritance comes by promise, not performance.
    body: If salvation depended on keeping the Law, the promise would be void — but God gave the inheritance to Abraham as a gift, which is why it requires faith, not works.

keyIllustration: "A Canadian railway company needed land owned by an Indigenous chief named Crowfoot. After years of negotiation, they offered him a lifetime pass to travel anywhere on the railway for free — a gold-plated promise engraved on a plaque he wore around his neck for the rest of his life. He treasured the promise. But he never once redeemed it. We do the same with God's promises — we carry them, admire them, but never cash them in."

application:
  - Stop trying to perform your way into favour. You are already grafted into the promises made to Abraham through faith in Christ.
  - "Read God's promises and apply the ones that speak to your current condition — He gave them to be used, not admired."
  - Pray based on what God has already promised — prayer is taking God at His word and asking Him to do what He said.

toRemember: "God doesn't fail on His promises — and His greatest promise is that those who believe in Jesus will be reunited with Him, as it was in the beginning."
