# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You can't control what the world does, but you can control whether it robs you of the love, joy, and peace God has given you — by redirecting your thoughts through praise, petition, and obedience."
keyScriptureRef: "Philippians 4:6-7 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.

mainPoints:
  - title: Rejoice in the Lord always.
    body: "Even when circumstances are terrible, you can choose to celebrate the fact that God loves you and has saved you — that's the joy of your salvation, and it's always available."
  - title: Let your gentleness be known to everyone.
    body: "Meekness is not weakness — it's controlled power. Even when anger feels justified, God calls you to respond with gentleness because He is near and in control."
  - title: Reject anxiety through three-part prayer.
    body: When your mind races, bring it back through praise (worship Him), supplication (give Him your requests), and thanksgiving (name His gifts).
  - title: "Meditate God's way."
    body: "Christian meditation isn't emptying your mind — it's filling it with what is true, honorable, just, pure, lovely, and commendable. Fix your thoughts on God's character and His works."
  - title: Practice what you know.
    body: "Obedience deepens your experience of God's love. When you do what He commands, you experience Him in ways you can't access through knowledge alone."

keyIllustration: "A man, angered by cultural blasphemy, leaves home still carrying that anger. In a parking lot, another driver cuts him off. Though he makes no verbal response and avoids eye contact, the anger on his face provokes a hostile confrontation. The takeaway: unresolved anger — even when justified — leaks out and attracts more anger, robbing you of peace and making you an instrument of hostility rather than peace."

application:
  - When something provokes you, stop before you act. First, bring it to God in praise, petition, and thanksgiving. Let Him recalibrate your thoughts before you engage the world.
  - "Gentleness isn't weakness — it's trust that God is near, in control, and strong enough to defend you. You don't have to retaliate to be safe."
  - "Practice filling your mind with Scripture when anxiety spirals. Pray the Psalms. Name God's past faithfulness. Let truth overwhelm your racing thoughts."

toRemember: "When you fill your mind with the things of God and fill your life with obedience to His Word, the God of peace will be with you — and that's where you'll find His love, His joy, and His peace."
