# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God reveals Himself to us everywhere, and worship is the response of surrendering your body, mind, and will to His glory every single day.
keyScriptureRef: "Romans 12:1-2 ESV"
keyScriptureText: I appeal to you therefore, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is your spiritual worship. Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that by testing you may discern what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect.

mainPoints:
  - title: Worship is a response, not an event.
    body: "Scripture shows us God's glory first—His character, His work, His mercy—then calls us to respond. Worship happens when we see Him clearly and surrender accordingly."
  - title: The fear of the Lord is awe-filled surrender.
    body: "From Abraham to John, every encounter with God's presence involved falling on their face—not running away. True fear of God is being terrified, fascinated, and in love all at once."
  - title: Surrender your body, mind, and will daily.
    body: Worship in spirit and truth means offering God your actions, your thoughts, and your choices. What you tune into shapes what you become.

keyIllustration: "The human ear was explored in intricate detail—how earwax protects, how the cochlea splits sound into frequencies, how balance and vision work together reflexively. The two-centimeter ear canal is perfectly tuned to hear human speech, worship, and creation's sounds. This design is no accident: God made us to perceive Him and respond in awe."

application:
  - "Daily ask: What am I tuning into? Limit news and world noise. Prioritize Scripture, prayer, creation, and worship."
  - "Surrender your body each morning: \"Lord, use my hands, voice, and presence for Your glory today.\""
  - "Renew your mind constantly by soaking in God's Word and choosing to see His goodness in everything."

toRemember: You are made to see God, hear God, and respond in worship—not just on Sunday, but in every moment, with everything you are.
