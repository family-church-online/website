# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You get to know God not by demanding answers to your prayers, but by watching Him work — even when His purposes look nothing like what you expected.
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 1:68-72 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, for He has visited and redeemed His people and has raised up a horn of salvation for us in the house of His servant David, as He spoke by the mouth of His holy prophets from of old, that we should be saved from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us; to show the mercy promised to our fathers and to remember His holy covenant.

mainPoints:
  - title: Zechariah was faithful but unprepared.
    body: "He knew the scriptures, served as a priest, and lived blamelessly — but when Gabriel announced the Messiah's coming, all Zechariah could think about was his own disappointment. Obedience without relationship leaves you unable to recognise God when He shows up."
  - title: "God's grace meets us in our self-centeredness."
    body: "Zechariah's problem wasn't that he lacked a child. His problem was sin — and God's plan was to send Jesus to pay for it. Nine months of silence forced Zechariah to stop talking and start watching God work. That's where transformation happened."
  - title: You know God by watching Him work.
    body: Just as you only truly know someone when you see them in their element, you know God by observing how He fulfills His promises, works through pain, and accomplishes His purposes. The Bible shows you God at work — then you look for Him working in your own life.

keyIllustration: A woman lost her son to a drunk driver. Instead of bitterness, she gave her grief to God — and eventually visited the young man in prison over and over until his release. Though past adoption age, she unofficially adopted him as her son. Together they traveled across the United States, ministering about the dangers of drunk driving and the power of forgiveness. She lost a son to tragedy and gained one by grace. This illustrates how God does not waste our suffering — He redeems it for His purposes when we trust Him.

application:
  - "Read the Bible daily to see God's character and how He works — this is how you learn to recognize Him in your own circumstances."
  - "When prayers go unanswered, resist the temptation to become inward-focused. Ask God to show you what He's doing, even if it's not what you expected."
  - Seek God with all your heart — through prayer, worship, and service — and trust His promise that those who seek Him will find Him.

toRemember: God will not waste your pain. He will use it for His glory if you trust Him — because that is who He is.
