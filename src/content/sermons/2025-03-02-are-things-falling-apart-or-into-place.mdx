# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: What looks like the church falling apart under persecution is actually God planting His people so the gospel spreads — and your suffering is never wasted when surrendered to Him for His purposes.
keyScriptureRef: "2 Corinthians 1:6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: If we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation. And if we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which you experience when you patiently endure the same sufferings that we suffer.

mainPoints:
  - title: Scattered to Be Planted.
    body: "The Greek word for \"scattered\" means planted, not discarded — persecution forced believers out of Jerusalem into Judea and Samaria, exactly as Jesus had commanded in Acts 1:8, turning exile into mission."
  - title: Ordinary People, Extraordinary Gospel.
    body: "The early church exploded in growth not because of apostles and leaders alone, but because everyday believers like Philip (who started by serving food to widows) went everywhere \"gospeling the gospel\" with no special training required."
  - title: "Your Suffering Isn't Wasted."
    body: God wastes nothing — not one tear, not one fear, not one rejection — and when you surrender your pain to Him, He uses it to bring comfort, salvation, and freedom to others.

keyIllustration: A town in Alabama built a monument to an agricultural pest — a boll weevil — that destroyed their cotton economy. Forced to pivot, they discovered peanuts were a far better crop, and the town prospered beyond what cotton ever brought. What looked like destruction became the doorway to flourishing.

application:
  - "Stop waiting for the \"right time\" or special qualifications to share your faith — you already know what Jesus did for you, and that's all you need to start."
  - "Ask God not to waste your suffering, but to use it for someone else's joy, faith, healing, or freedom."
  - Obey in the small, ordinary things — serving, showing up, speaking up — and trust God to do the miraculous through you.

toRemember: "If you're a Christian, you're going to suffer — but when you suffer obediently and faithfully in the name of Jesus, none of it will be wasted, because God uses it to bring peace, freedom, and eternal life to others."
