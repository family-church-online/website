# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus did not merely tolerate children—He rebuked those who tried to keep them away, because children belong to the kingdom and model the kind of faith that enters it.
keyScriptureRef: "Mark 10:13-16 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And they were bringing children to him that he might touch them, and the disciples rebuked them. But when Jesus saw it, he was indignant and said to them, \"Let the children come to me; do not hinder them, for to such belongs the kingdom of God. Truly, I say to you, whoever does not receive the kingdom of God like a child shall not enter it.\" And he took them in his arms and blessed them, laying his hands on them."

mainPoints:
  - title: "Blessing children puts God's name on them."
    body: "The Aaronic blessing (Numbers 6) is God's instruction to invoke His love, grace, and mercy over His people—and to mark them as belonging to Him."
  - title: Jesus is both Lion and Lamb.
    body: He is the conquering King who will return in fury and the gentle Shepherd who welcomes children into His arms. We cannot know Him fully if we ignore either side of His character.
  - title: The disciples hindered; Jesus was indignant.
    body: When the disciples tried to protect Jesus from interruption, He rebuked them. Children are not distractions—they are building blocks of the temple of the living God.
  - title: Childlike faith is the only kind that enters the kingdom.
    body: To receive the gospel like a child means to trust without questioning, to believe without demanding proof, to accept that Jesus is enough.

keyIllustration: "A woman who had bled for twelve years spent everything on doctors and found no help. She believed that if she could just touch Jesus' garment, she would be healed. That same desperate faith drove parents to bring their children to Jesus—not for a ritual, but for a relationship. They knew that if Jesus touched their children, everything would be different."

application:
  - "Bless your children daily using the Aaronic blessing—speak God's name over them and claim them as belonging to Him."
  - As a church, never hinder a child from coming to Jesus. Love them, welcome them, and show them Christ through how you treat them.
  - "Receive the kingdom like a child: put away complexity, trust simply, and believe that Jesus is enough."

toRemember: "The thief on the cross asked only one thing: \"Jesus, remember me when You come into Your kingdom.\" When we bring our children to Jesus, we need nothing more than that—if He knows them and touches them, it is enough."
