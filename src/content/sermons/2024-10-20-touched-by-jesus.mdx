mainPoints:
  - title: "Blessing children puts God's name on them."
    body: "The Aaronic blessing (Numbers 6) is God's instruction to invoke His love, grace, and mercy over His people—and to mark them as belonging to Him."
  - title: Jesus is both Lion and Lamb.
    body: He is the conquering King who will return in fury and the gentle Shepherd who welcomes children into His arms. We cannot know Him fully if we ignore either side of His character.
  - title: The disciples hindered; Jesus was indignant.
    body: When the disciples tried to protect Jesus from interruption, He rebuked them. Children are not distractions—they are building blocks of the temple of the living God.
  - title: Childlike faith is the only kind that enters the kingdom.
    body: To receive the gospel like a child means to trust without questioning, to believe without demanding proof, to accept that Jesus is enough.

keyIllustration: "A woman who had bled for twelve years spent everything on doctors and found no help. She believed that if she could just touch Jesus' garment, she would be healed. application:
  - "Bless your children daily using the Aaronic blessing—speak God's name over them and claim them as belonging to Him."
