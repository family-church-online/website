  - title: The Purpose of Leadership Is Equipping, Not Performing Ministry.
    body: "Leaders exist to train the saints to do the work of service. Ministry is not limited to clergy — every believer is called to full-time Christian service in their context. The process is: leaders equip → saints serve → the body is built up."
  - title: Spectator Christianity Produces Three Dangers.
    body: When believers do not serve, they remain spiritually immature (underdeveloped through lack of exercise), unstable (tossed by religious fads and false teaching), and gullible (unable to discern truth from cultish deception).
