# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Christ gave leadership gifts to the church not to do the ministry for the congregation, but to equip believers to do the work of service themselves until we all reach spiritual maturity.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 4:11-14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And he gave the apostles, the prophets, the evangelists, the shepherds and teachers, to equip the saints for the work of ministry, for building up the body of Christ, until we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to mature manhood, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ, so that we may no longer be children, tossed to and fro by the waves and carried about by every wind of doctrine, by human cunning, by craftiness in deceitful schemes.

mainPoints:
  - title: The Five Leadership Gifts and Their Biblical Definitions.
    body: "Apostles were commissioned directly by the risen Christ and laid the foundation of the church (no longer active today). Prophets received direct revelation from God to speak to the early church (also fulfilled). Evangelists bring the gospel to those who don't know Christ. Shepherds (pastors) teach, lead, guide, and care for the congregation. Teachers instruct believers in Scripture and doctrine."
  - title: The Purpose of Leadership Is Equipping, Not Performing Ministry.
    body: "Leaders exist to train the saints to do the work of service. Ministry is not limited to clergy — every believer is called to full-time Christian service in their context. The process is: leaders equip → saints serve → the body is built up."
  - title: Spectator Christianity Produces Three Dangers.
    body: When believers do not serve, they remain spiritually immature (underdeveloped through lack of exercise), unstable (tossed by religious fads and false teaching), and gullible (unable to discern truth from cultish deception).

keyIllustration: "A new pastor arrives at a church. After his first sermon, an elderly woman asks him to interpret a difficult scripture passage for her. The pastor refuses — not to be rude, but to equip. He says, \"I won't tell you what the scripture means, but I will help you find the meaning in the scripture.\" This illustrates the biblical model of equipping over dependency."

application:
  - Stop waiting for staff to do the ministry. Identify your spiritual gifts and find a place to serve in building up the body of Christ.
  - If you have an idea for how the church can serve the local community, speak up. The elders are looking for initiatives that serve the area, involve families, and engage the congregation.
  - Before taking communion, examine yourself. Confess unrepentant sin, deal with anger or doubt, and ensure your heart is right before the Lord.

toRemember: The gospel is not merely something to come to church to hear, but something to go from the church to tell — and we are all appointed to tell.
