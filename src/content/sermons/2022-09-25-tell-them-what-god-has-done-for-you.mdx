# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Jesus' priority is for you to preach the gospel of how He saved you from death, sin, and the wrath of God—not just the miracles, but the Savior."
keyScriptureRef: "Mark 5:19-20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And he did not permit him, but said to him, \"Go home to your friends and tell them how much the Lord has done for you, and how he has had mercy on you.\" And he went away and began to proclaim in the Decapolis how much Jesus had done for him, and everyone marveled."

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus delivers, then teaches.
    body: "The man freed from demons wasn't just exorcised—he sat at Jesus' feet, receiving the good news of the kingdom before he was sent out."
  - title: One testimony can change a region.
    body: When Jesus returned to the Decapolis, the people who once begged Him to leave now brought the sick and begged Him to heal—because one man obeyed and told his story.
  - title: Jesus wants you to preach the gospel, not just the miracle.
    body: "Later, Jesus tells those He heals not to tell anyone—not because miracles don't matter, but because people were coming for signs instead of salvation."

keyIllustration: "A woman diagnosed with pancreatic cancer told her Jewish endocrinologist she had prayed to make it to her daughter's matric. When he asked what her prayer was now, she said, \"Please, Lord, can I make it to her wedding?\" At that exact moment, the church bells next door began to chime. Both sat in stunned silence before the doctor said, \"From your lips to God's ears.\""

application:
  - "Your testimony doesn't need to be dramatic to be powerful—faithfulness from childhood is just as much a story of God's mercy as deliverance from demons."
  - When Jesus sends you out, your job is to tell them what God has done for you—not to stay silent, not to wait for permission, but to obey and speak.
  - "Baptism is a public declaration that you were dead and now you're alive forever—it's not about what you've experienced, but about who has saved you."

toRemember: Every day is a gift, and this is the day that the Lord has made—so tell them what God has done for you.
