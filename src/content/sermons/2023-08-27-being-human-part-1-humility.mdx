# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You cannot grow closer to Christ, submit to God's will, or obey Him unless you are first humbled—emptied of yourself and filled with Him."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 9:35 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And Jesus went throughout all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues and proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every affliction.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Humility is God's plan for your salvation."
    body: Being humbled leads to surrender; surrender leads to obedience. God uses humility to transform you into the image of Christ—not to diminish you, but to make room for His power in your weakness.
  - title: Pride separates you from God, from others, and from reality.
    body: "It creates a false view of yourself, makes you jealous of others' success, and convinces you that you don't need God. Pride is the cancer behind most of our sin."
  - title: Jesus lived a life of perfect humility.
    body: "He—Creator of all—became human, submitted to the Father's will, and healed the very people who would demand His death. His humility led to obedience, even to death on a cross."
  - title: God sometimes humbles us when pride goes unchecked.
    body: "When we don't see our pride—especially in areas where we think we're humble—God graciously intervenes. The process is painful but it's always a gift, preparing us for greater usefulness in His kingdom."

keyIllustration: "A golfer leading a tournament by one stroke was congratulated by a friend before the final hole. He lost focus, shanked the next shot, and lost the tournament. Years later he admitted: \"I started focusing on myself instead of the task I was given.\" Pride shifts our attention from God's work to our own importance—and we lose what matters most."

application:
  - "Ask God to show you where pride hides in your life—especially in areas where you think you're humble."
  - "When God humbles you through painful circumstances, receive it as grace. He's preparing you for greater fruitfulness."
  - Measure yourself by the faith God has given you, not by comparison with others or by your own achievements.

toRemember: God can do remarkably more with your life than you ever could on your own—but only when you are empty of you and full of Him.
