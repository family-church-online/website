# ── PRESENTATION COPY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
tagLine: What feels like Christian tolerance may be the one thing Christ demands you stop.
shortDescription: "Tolerance is one of culture's highest values — but can it become a sin? When false teaching creeps into the church dressed as compassion or open-mindedness, Christ says: enough. He sees where you live, commends your faithfulness, and calls you to repent of the one thing you thought was virtuous — looking the other way."
subtitle: Christ commends faithfulness under persecution but charges the church at Pergamum — and us today — to repent of tolerating those who teach spiritual compromise.
hook: Your culture calls it open-mindedness and your instinct calls it kindness — but what does Christ call it when His people stop confronting false teaching? Is tolerance ever a form of betrayal?

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The church is called not to passive tolerance but to active faithfulness — to repent of compromise, warn the deceived, and trust that the same word that will one day judge the world is holding them together right now.
keyScriptureRef: "Revelation 2:16 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "\"Therefore, repent. If not, I will come to you soon and war against them with the sword of my mouth.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Faithfulness is seen and honoured.
    body: Christ knows exactly where His people live, names the evil surrounding them, and commends every way they have held fast to His name — even under the threat of death.
  - title: Tolerating compromise is itself a sin.
    body: When teachers draw people toward syncretism, other gods, or sexual immorality, remaining silent is complicity; Christ calls the entire church — not only the teachers — to repent of it.
  - title: The sword that judges also sustains.
    body: The word of Christ that will destroy His enemies is the same word that feeds, vindicates, and holds the faithful moment by moment — the hidden manna and the white stone of acquittal.

keyIllustration: "Reading your own MRI report is used to frame the letter to Pergamum: confronting the clinical language of what is wrong feels brutal and frightening, but the discomfort exists so that what needs healing can be found and treated. Christ's correction is not cruelty — it is the careful diagnosis of a physician who has already borne the very suffering He asks us to face."

application:
  - Before adopting any alternative therapy or spiritual practice, trace its origins — what appears culturally neutral may carry spiritual allegiances incompatible with Christ.
  - When you see false teaching drawing others in your community away from Christ, speak up — your silence is not kindness but abandonment of those who need to be warned.

toRemember: Whatever this world withholds from you because you belong to Christ — He holds a white stone with your name on it, and what He gives, no one can take away.
