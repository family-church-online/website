# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The darkness you left behind isn't neutral — it's a destructive spiral that callouses your heart, blinds your mind, and makes you insatiable for impurity. You were pulled into the light by the blood of Christ, and the only way to stay there is to guard your heart and worship Him."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 4:17-19"
keyScriptureText: Now this I say and testify in the Lord, that you must no longer walk as the Gentiles do, in the futility of their minds. They are darkened in their understanding, alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance that is in them, due to their hardness of heart. They have become callous and have given themselves up to sensuality, greedy to practice every kind of impurity.

mainPoints:
  - title: Futile thinking leads nowhere.
    body: "Without God's wisdom transforming your mind, everything you pursue — no matter how noble it seems — has no eternal value. You need His truth to make your life count."
  - title: Sin darkens your understanding of everything.
    body: "It doesn't just cloud your morality; it warps your logic, your compassion, your ability to solve problems. Left to ourselves, we become what we worship — and worshiping the world makes us more like the world."
  - title: A hardened heart becomes insatiable.
    body: Calloused hearts crave more and more depravity because sin is never satisfied. The heart of stone God replaced with a heart of flesh must be guarded, or it will harden again.
  - title: Jesus is the only light that never goes out.
    body: "No matter how dark the world gets, you are held in His light. Worship changes what you crave. Turn toward Him, and you'll love the light."

keyIllustration: "A couple exhausted after a long day finally settles in for much-needed sleep — only to have a neighbor's loud party begin at 8pm. Just as frustration peaks, a power outage silences the music. Sometimes even load shedding can feel like grace. But the darkness Paul warns about has no silver lining — it destroys eternally."

application:
  - "Guard your heart by presenting your body to God as an instrument of righteousness, not as a tool for your own pleasure or the world's patterns."
  - When darkness surrounds you — whether in culture, circumstances, or your own struggles — turn to worship. Sing, read Scripture, meditate on His truth. Worship changes the heart.
  - Remember that you belong to Him. No grid collapse, no cultural depravity, no personal failure can take away the fact that you are His beloved, bought with His blood, seated in the light.

toRemember: Jesus is the light of life — whoever follows Him will not walk in darkness.
