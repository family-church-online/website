# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The more aware you are of what you've been forgiven for, the more deeply you will love Jesus and experience His peace."
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 7:47 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Therefore I tell you, her sins, which are many, are forgiven — for she loved much. But he who is forgiven little loves little.

mainPoints:
  - title: Two sinners, one dinner table.
    body: "Simon the Pharisee and the sinful woman both sit before Jesus — one weeping in gratitude, the other judging in pride — because only one knows she's been forgiven."
  - title: All debts are equally unpayable.
    body: "Whether you owe fifty days' wages or five hundred, if you can't pay, you can't pay — the size of your sin doesn't change your guilt, but your awareness of it determines your gratitude."
  - title: Forgiveness sets you free, not just pardoned.
    body: "Jesus tells the woman \"Your faith has saved you. Go in peace\" — she's not just forgiven the consequences of sin, but freed from its control over her life."

keyIllustration: "A car stops at a traffic light. A man emerges from the bush with a gun, creeps to the back door where a baby sits in a car seat, grips the handle — then suddenly stops and runs. The driver never sees it. His wife in the car behind watches it all unfold. Later that night, when she tells him what happened, he breaks down weeping — not from fear, but from gratitude. He hadn't known what he'd been rescued from. The illustration demonstrates why knowing the depth of what you've been saved from produces overwhelming thankfulness rather than indifference."

application:
  - "Stop comparing your sin to others' — all of us are equally unable to pay our debt without Christ, regardless of how \"respectable\" our lives appear."
  - "Be honest with God about the full extent of your brokenness — hiding from it doesn't protect you, it blocks you from experiencing the joy of forgiveness."
  - "If your faith feels flat, ask the Lord to show you what you've been forgiven for — gratitude flows from awareness."

toRemember: "Until you're willing to become a mess before Jesus, you'll never truly hear Him say, \"Go in peace. Your sins are forgiven.\""
