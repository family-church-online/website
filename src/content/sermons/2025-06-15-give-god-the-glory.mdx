# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Herod murdered, imprisoned, and tried to destroy the church—then demanded worship as a god. Jesus rose from the grave, sits on the eternal throne, and promised that hell itself will not prevail against His church. You can trust the One who finishes what He starts.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 12:23-24 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Immediately an angel of the Lord struck him down, because he did not give God the glory, and he was eaten by worms and breathed his last. But the word of God increased and multiplied.

mainPoints:
  - title: Herod positioned himself as saviour while attacking the church.
    body: Dressed in silver robes, sitting on a throne, receiving worship as a god—while murdering James, arresting Peter, and starving the people of Tyre and Sidon during a famine.
  - title: Flattery and pride work together and God detests both.
    body: "The people shouted \"the voice of a god, not a man\" because they wanted food. Herod soaked it up because he wanted glory. Both were utterly insincere."
  - title: "God's humblest creature humbled the proudest king."
    body: The same angel who struck Peter to save him struck Herod to judge him. Herod was eaten by worms and died—while Jesus rose from the grave and His body never saw decay.
  - title: The church multiplied while Herod disappeared.
    body: Every persecutor of the church—every dictator, president, king—comes and goes. But Jesus sits at the right hand of God, and the gates of hell will not prevail against His church.

keyIllustration: "A historian named Josephus recorded that Herod wore a metallic silver robe designed to catch the sunlight as he sat on his throne and delivered his speech. While speaking, he was struck with unbearable abdominal pain. The contrast is deliberate: the greatest king in all his pomp and power was brought low by the humblest of God's creatures. The worm illustrates the final word on human pride—dust returns to dust, but Jesus rose from the dead."

application:
  - Stop fearing the persecutor. They will come and go. Jesus is eternal and He does not fail.
  - "Guard against flattery and pride in your own life. God cut off Herod for refusing to give Him the glory—check where you're soaking up attention that belongs to Him."
  - "Trust that Jesus finishes what He starts. Two thousand years later, we're still worshiping. He's still building His church. He will carry you through whatever threatens you today."

toRemember: "The persecutors will come and go, but Jesus Christ is alive—and He will declare when it's finished. The gates of hell will not prevail."
