# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: True peace is not the absence of the storm — it is the presence of Jesus in your vessel, calming the waters from the inside.
keyScriptureRef: "2 Thessalonians 3:16-18 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Now may the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times in every way. The Lord be with you all. I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. It is a sign of genuineness in every letter of mine. It is the way I write. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus is the Lord of peace — not just a provider of peace.
    body: He experienced grief, loss, anxiety, and anger, yet remained the embodiment of peace. His peace is perfect, perpetual, and unbroken.
  - title: The world offers peace through avoidance; Jesus offers peace through His presence.
    body: Modern solutions tell you to empty your mind and escape. Jesus says the waves are real — now walk to Me through them.
  - title: "God's grace is not just blessing — it is God's riches at Christ's expense."
    body: Every good thing you are able to do flows from grace. Peace is not earned; it is received through belief in who Jesus is and what He has done.

keyIllustration: In 1750, two earthquakes shook London within ten days. When seismologists predicted a third, more violent quake, panic gripped the city. John Wesley gathered his followers in a chapel, read Psalm 46 aloud, and prayed together. In the middle of citywide chaos, that group experienced a peace like never before — not because the danger passed, but because they invited Christ into the vessel.

application:
  - Stop trying to make the waves disappear. Invite Jesus into your vessel — your body, your life, your chaos — and let Him calm the waters from within.
  - "Practice the three disciplines that brought peace to John Wesley's community: gather with others, read Scripture aloud, and pray for one another in the storm."
  - "Ask yourself: What mark am I leaving? Paul left no empire, no possessions — but he turned the world upside down through love and perseverance in Christ."

toRemember: When Jesus is in your vessel, the waters become calm — not because the storm stops, but because He is with you in it.
