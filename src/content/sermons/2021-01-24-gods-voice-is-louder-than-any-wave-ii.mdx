# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "To truly honor and obey God's Word, we must move beyond reading to a rhythm of study, trust, application, memorisation, and teaching—these five practices transform Scripture from information into life."
keyScriptureRef: "Psalm 119:145-148 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "With my whole heart I cry; answer me, O Lord! I will keep your statutes. I call to you; save me, that I may observe your testimonies. I rise before dawn and cry for help; I hope in your words. My eyes are awake before the watches of the night, that I may meditate on your promise."

mainPoints:
  - title: Scripture reveals three essential things
    body: It shows us who God is, who we are (in our brokenness), and who Jesus is (as the solution that reconciles us to God).
  - title: Ask three diagnostic questions of any passage
    body: What does it tell me about God? What does it tell me about my own need? What does it tell me about Jesus and what He has done?
  - title: Use the START framework
    body: "Study Scripture (with the Holy Spirit's help), Trust what it says, Apply it immediately, Remember it (through memorisation), and Teach it to others."
  - title: Application is not optional
    body: "Until we apply what we've learned, we haven't truly learned it—and we risk growing cold toward it."

keyIllustration: "Three types of people assemble Christmas gifts: the prepared (read instructions first), the semi-prepared (read while assembling), and the hands-on learner (figure it out as you go). No matter your learning style, Scripture is the one instruction manual we all need—and it's sufficient for knowing God, understanding ourselves, and learning how to live."

application:
  - Begin using the three diagnostic questions in your personal Bible reading and group studies.
  - Commit to memorising one verse this week, then meditate on it throughout your day.
  - "Identify one person you can teach—a child, a friend, someone newer in faith—and share what you're learning."

toRemember: "Length of days and years of life and peace will be added to you when your heart keeps God's commandments—not through mere knowledge, but through devoted practice."
