# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Eternal life is not earned through religious transactions or careful calculation—it is a relationship with Jesus built on repentance, recognition of His perfection, and humble surrender.
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 23:42-43 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And he said, \"Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom.\" And he said to him, \"Truly, I say to you, today you will be with me in paradise.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Idolatry is manipulation disguised as worship.
    body: Demetrius manipulated crowds and exploited religion for personal gain. Modern idolatry does the same—treat God as a means to get what you want.
  - title: Transactional religion is not Christianity.
    body: "Every other religion says \"do this, get that.\" Faith in Jesus is not risk-reward calculation—it's knowing the God who saved you while you were still a sinner."
  - title: Genuine faith begins with recognizing your need.
    body: The thief on the cross admitted he deserved death, declared Jesus sinless, and asked only to be remembered—no bargaining, only surrender.
  - title: Jesus offers assurance to those who truly seek Him.
    body: "Eternal life is relational, not transactional. You don't earn it by saying the right words—you receive it by knowing the One who knows you."

keyIllustration: "A silversmith named Demetrius gathered a mob to riot against Paul—not because he cared about the goddess Artemis, but because Paul's gospel threatened his income. The crowd swelled to chaos in a stadium; most didn't even know why they were there. Demetrius didn't care—he manipulated empty, angry people to serve his own ends. That is idolatry: using religion to get what you want, regardless of the cost to others."

application:
  - Ask God to search your heart and reveal whether your faith is genuine surrender or self-serving transaction.
  - Confess your need for a Saviour—admit you cannot earn your way to heaven.
  - Trust that Jesus knows your name, numbered every hair on your head, and offers you eternal life not because you deserve it, but because He is merciful.

toRemember: Eternal life is not a destination you bargain for—it is knowing the God who wrote your name on His palm before you were born.
