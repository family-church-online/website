# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God has written a story for your life before you were born, and He will finish what He starts—but you must choose whether to stay in His script or burn it and write your own.
keyScriptureRef: "Jeremiah 1:5 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, and before you were born I consecrated you; I appointed you a prophet to the nations.

mainPoints:
  - title: God foreknows and predestines, yet commands us to choose.
    body: "Both are true. God planned Jeremiah's life before birth, yet Jeremiah had to decide to obey. We cannot fully reconcile sovereignty and free will—we humbly accept both."
  - title: "Jeremiah stayed in God's story despite overwhelming resistance."
    body: "He was threatened, thrown in pits, and tempted to quit. But he said God's word was like \"a burning fire shut up in my bones\"—he could not stop speaking it."
  - title: "King Jehoiakim despised God's Word and cut himself out of the story."
    body: "He literally cut up the scroll and burned it. His name was removed from the genealogy of Jesus. You cannot rewrite Scripture and expect to remain in God's script."
  - title: "If you want to stay in God's story, immerse yourself in His Word daily."
    body: Not casually, but systematically—studying, applying, and obeying. This is how God reveals your part in His script, one day at a time.

keyIllustration: A murder mystery dinner party where each guest was given a character and a script. The evening worked beautifully when everyone stayed in their part, but fell apart when people drifted in and out of character. God has given you a script—will you stay in it, or ad-lib your own story?

application:
  - "Decide today: are you in God's story, or your own? If you desire to know Him and love Him, that desire comes from Him—and He will finish what He started."
  - "Commit to daily Scripture reading and study, using proper methods that honor the Bible as God's inerrant Word."
  - Join or start a Bible study group at Family Church to learn how to apply Scripture correctly to your hardest life questions.

toRemember: "Your plans can be overthrown in an instant, but God's script has never failed—and it ends in perfect love, restoration, and eternity with Him."
