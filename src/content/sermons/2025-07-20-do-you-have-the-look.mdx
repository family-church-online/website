# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You cannot be healed by what you refuse to see broken, and you cannot receive grace while still trying to earn approval.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 14:9-10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "He listened to Paul speaking. And Paul, looking intently at him and seeing that he had faith to be made well, said in a loud voice, \"Stand upright on your feet.\" And he sprang up and began walking."

mainPoints:
  - title: "The crippled man heard what he couldn't do; the crowd heard what they already knew."
    body: The man born lame had spent his life confronting his helplessness — he knew he could never fix himself. The crowd had spent their lives confident in their own stories and gods. Humility is what opens the door to grace.
  - title: We learn by comparing new information to what we already believe.
    body: This is normal — but it becomes deadly when we stop letting God challenge what we think we know. The crowd saw a miracle and immediately translated it into their existing mythology. Faith requires setting aside certainty.
  - title: Paul and Barnabas tore their robes and rushed into the crowd.
    body: "They refused to let glory intended for Jesus rest on them for even a moment. The urgency wasn't just theological — it was survival. When Herod accepted worship, he was struck down. Pride destroys ministry faster than doubt ever will."
  - title: "Even after correction, the crowd could \"scarcely\" be restrained."
    body: "They were held back, not turned around. Restraint is not repentance. God doesn't want to just keep you from your old sins — He wants to turn you toward something better."

keyIllustration: "A fish pond springs a one-centimetre leak. Within days it drains dangerously low. The gardener prays and searches — because no repair product in the world can fix a leak you haven't found. The right product, applied to the right place, seals underwater without draining the pond or risking further damage. Sin works the same way: unless you let God show you where the break is, no amount of effort will stop the drain."

application:
  - "Stop filtering scripture through what you already agree with. Ask God to show you what you don't want to see."
  - Acknowledge that every breath, every provision, every good thing comes from Him — not your effort.
  - "Surrender the belief that if you just try harder, you can earn God's approval or fix what's broken."

toRemember: "You enter the kingdom of heaven for one reason only: Jesus died for you, took your sin, and gave you His righteousness. That's it. Live like someone who believes that."
