# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God wants to open the eyes of your heart so you can truly see — not just know about — the hope, inheritance, and resurrection power that are already yours in Christ.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 1:18-20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Having the eyes of your hearts enlightened, that you may know what is the hope to which he has called you, what are the riches of his glorious inheritance in the saints, and what is the immeasurable greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of his great might that he worked in Christ when he raised him from the dead and seated him at his right hand in the heavenly places.

mainPoints:
  - title: "The Hope You're Called To"
    body: "Your hope is not just eternal life in general — it's being made like Jesus: eternal, adopted as God's child, empowered to do His will, and loved by the Father exactly as Jesus is loved. This hope purifies you even now as you become more like Him."
  - title: The Riches of His Inheritance
    body: "You are not just receiving an inheritance from God — you are God's inheritance. The Father has given you as a gift to His Son. You are Christ's treasured possession, valued enough to be His eternal bride, His adopted sibling, His blood-bought inheritance."
  - title: The Immeasurable Power Toward You
    body: "The same power that raised Jesus from death and seated Him at God's right hand is at work in you now. This power raised you from spiritual death, empowers you as a witness, sustains you in daily endurance and joy, and will one day seat you on the throne with Christ."

keyIllustration: "A peacock's tail contains hundreds of feathers that must grow to precise lengths, at exact angles, layered with keratin of varying thicknesses to create iridescent patterns — all without the bird's awareness or trial-and-error. The brilliance isn't just in the beauty you see, but in the Creator behind it. Similarly, you can know the facts about God's work in your life without truly seeing the genius, artistry, and overwhelming love behind it all. Paul prays that the Holy Spirit would help you see what's really there."

application:
  - "Stop living like the power is off. Many believers walk in the dark — frustrated, powerless, spiritually exhausted — when resurrection power is already available. Ask for it. Trust you've received it. Use it."
  - "Move from advocate to witness. The Holy Spirit doesn't just give you better arguments — He gives you the power to speak from what you've actually seen with the eyes of your heart. That kind of testimony saves lives."
  - "Let your hope purify you now. You will be made like Jesus — that's the promise. Let that future reality change how you live today: enduring, patient, joyful, and empowered to overcome."

toRemember: "You've been raised from spiritual death, given as a gift to the Son of God, and seated with Christ on His throne — don't waste the immeasurable power already working in you."
