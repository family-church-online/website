# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God has promised a blessing to everyone who reads, hears, and keeps Revelation — and that blessing is yours if you will hold on to your faith in Jesus while history moves toward its completion.
keyScriptureRef: "Revelation 1:3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Blessed is the one who reads aloud the words of this prophecy, and blessed are those who hear, and who keep what is written in it, for the time is near.

mainPoints:
  - title: Read it whole.
    body: Revelation is designed to be read from start to finish in a single sitting — only then do you experience the full journey from terror to encouragement and grasp the complete picture of where history is going.
  - title: "Know where you're going."
    body: "The book reveals three things: the glory of Jesus as He truly is, the honest state of the church right now, and the future events leading to His return, the defeat of evil, and the perfection of creation."
  - title: Hear it and keep it.
    body: "To \"hear\" is to receive God's word with genuine desire — not just process the sound. To \"keep\" it is to hold fast to faith in the gospel of Jesus Christ all the way to the end, which is what the Holy Spirit empowers you to do."

keyIllustration: "A small boy sits calmly doing his homework on a plane being battered by a severe storm, while a large soldier beside him grips the armrest in terror. When pressed, the boy explains: \"My dad's the pilot.\" The illustration captures what it means to keep doing your homework — living faithfully — while the world around you is in chaos, because you know who is in control of where you are going."

application:
  - Read the whole book of Revelation in one sitting this week — it takes 45 minutes to an hour, and God has specifically promised to bless those who do.
  - Hold fast to your faith in the gospel of Jesus Christ. The Holy Spirit will take care of the obedience; your part is not to let go of what you believe about who He is and what He has done.
  - Share what you are hearing from this series — those who take the word of Revelation out into the world are given a specific blessing for doing so.

toRemember: Keep doing your homework while the storm rages — because you know the Pilot, and you know exactly where this flight is going.
