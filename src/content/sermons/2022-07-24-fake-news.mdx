# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When deception is everywhere — in media, in false teaching, even in forged letters claiming apostolic authority — your protection is not better information but deeper immersion in God's Word and His sovereignty."
keyScriptureRef: "2 Thessalonians 2:1-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Now concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our being gathered together to Him, we ask you, brothers, not to be quickly shaken in mind or alarmed, either by a spirit or a spoken word, or a letter seeming to be from us, to the effect that the day of the Lord has come. Let no one deceive you in any way.

mainPoints:
  - title: The Thessalonians were deceived by a forged letter.
    body: "Someone impersonated Paul and told them the day of the Lord had already come, throwing them into panic because they thought they'd been left behind and forgotten."
  - title: Paul corrects the lie with two clear signs.
    body: The day of the Lord cannot come until the rebellion (apostasy) occurs and the man of lawlessness (Antichrist) is revealed — neither of which had happened yet.
  - title: The Antichrist is a spiritual counterfeit.
    body: "Like the false cleaner fish that mimics the real one to exploit vulnerable prey, the Antichrist will imitate Christ, demand worship, and deceive many — but only for an appointed time under God's sovereign restraint."
  - title: Focus on the real, not the fake.
    body: "When you consume more news than Scripture, you become vulnerable to deception. Daily engagement with God's Word anchors you in truth when everything else is propaganda."

keyIllustration: "A diver encounters a cleaning station on the reef where fish queue up to have parasites removed by cleaner wrasse and shrimp. But lurking nearby is a comb-tooth blenny — a fake cleaner fish that mimics the real one's appearance and dance, then bites chunks out of the unsuspecting fish seeking help. It's a perversion of what God designed for healing, exactly like the Antichrist's counterfeit of Christ."

application:
  - "Choose what you consume. If you spend more time reading news than Scripture, you're feeding your mind on distortion instead of truth. Consider limiting news intake to once or twice a week."
  - "Test everything against Scripture. When you see a teaching online or receive a forwarded message, check it against God's Word before circulating it. Time setters are upsetters — don't be shaken by false predictions."
  - Use the reading plans available on the church website. If you struggle to read daily, start with the provided plans and let Scripture reminders come to you through the church app.

toRemember: "God is restraining the Antichrist until the appointed time. You are not abandoned, not forgotten, and not outside His sovereign control — so fill your mind with His Word, not the world's lies."
