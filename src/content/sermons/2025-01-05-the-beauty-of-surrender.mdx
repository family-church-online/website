# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Real surrender to God isn't passive acceptance — it's wrestling with Him in honest struggle until you're humbled enough to cling to Him for blessing, not control."
keyScriptureRef: "Genesis 32:24-26 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Jacob was left alone. And a man wrestled with him until the breaking of the day. When the man saw that he did not prevail against Jacob, he touched his hip socket, and Jacob's hip was put out of joint as he wrestled with him. Then he said, \"Let me go, for the day has broken.\" But Jacob said, \"I will not let you go unless you bless me.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: God initiates the wrestle when you reach the end of yourself.
    body: "Jacob couldn't bargain or scheme his way out. God pulled him aside — alone, terrified, powerless — and met him there. Wrestling isn't punishment; it's transformation."
  - title: True surrender means refusing to let go even when God humbles you.
    body: "Jacob's hip was dislocated, but instead of running, he clung harder and said, \"I will not let you go until you bless me.\" That's faith — not demanding your way, but trusting His goodness even in pain."
  - title: "The mark of God's discipline becomes the mark of your identity."
    body: "Jacob walked with a limp for the rest of his life. Every step reminded him: God touched me. God humbled me. God blessed me. His descendants carried that memory in their diet. Some encounters with God change you permanently."

keyIllustration: "Lion cubs and kittens fight constantly — rough, relentless wrestling that looks violent. But it's not cruelty; it's formation. Without that struggle, they never learn to hunt, balance, or survive. Jacob's wrestle with God wasn't about punishment — it was about preparing him for what was coming. The struggle we hate is often the training we desperately need."

application:
  - "Stop asking God to remove every struggle and start asking what He's teaching you through it."
  - "When hardship comes, don't focus on who caused it — focus on the sovereign God who allowed it and is using it to shape you."
  - You can be brutally honest with God about your pain and still trust Him — Job did, Habakkuk did, Jesus did.

toRemember: "Whatever you face in this new year, don't let go of God — even if He has to dislocate your hip to get your attention."
