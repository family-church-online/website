mainPoints:
  - title: Address the problem (A).
    body: "Don't ignore false teaching. Engage directly, ask questions, and go to the source. Follow the Matthew 18 pattern: speak first, involve others if needed, then bring it to the church."
  - title: Concentrate on the mission (C).
    body: "Even while traveling to confront error, Paul and Barnabas stopped along the way to share stories of God's work among the Gentiles, bringing joy to every church they visited. Problems are opportunities to glorify God."
  - title: Trust all of Scripture (T).
    body: False teaching arises when someone takes one part of Scripture and ignores the rest. Read the whole Bible, because every part reveals Jesus and keeps the gospel in balance.
  - title: Saved by faith, show evidence (S).
    body: You are saved by faith alone—not by what you do, wear, eat, or how you worship. But genuine faith will always produce visible fruit as the Holy Spirit works in you.
  - title: Set them free (S).
    body: The gospel liberates people from the burden of earning their salvation. Never add conditions that Jesus did not require. Point people to grace, not to rules.

keyIllustration: "A woman convicted of her sin came to Jesus and could only weep at His feet, washing them with her tears and drying them with her hair. Religious leaders judged her as unworthy. Jesus said, \"Your faith has saved you. Go in peace.\" She was set free by belief alone—no ceremony, no requirement, just faith."

application:
  - Test every teaching you hear—even from respected sources—against the full counsel of Scripture, not just isolated verses.
  - "When problems arise, use the ACTS framework: Address, Concentrate, Trust, Saved by faith / Set them free. Problems become opportunities to glorify God."
  - "Stop judging other believers by whether they look or act like you. Ask instead: Do they believe the gospel? Is the Holy Spirit producing fruit in their life?"

toRemember: "God already knew the problem you're facing, and He has already prepared the solution. Trust Him, focus on the mission, and set people free with the gospel—never enslave them with conditions Jesus never required."
