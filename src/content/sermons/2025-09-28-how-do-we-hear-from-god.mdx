mainPoints:
  - title: "A closed door is God's guidance too."
    body: "We love being told what to do, but hate being told what not to do—especially when our heart is in it and we're equipped and ready. But when you're doing God's work, a \"no\" is just as much direction as a \"yes.\""
  - title: Stay the course until God changes it.
    body: "Paul didn't sit down and wait for instructions. He kept moving west. When you run out of options, that's when God gives you the vision—because that's when you trust Him most instead of your own plans."
  - title: God orchestrates salvation.
    body: "He sent Paul to Lydia, but He also prepared Lydia's heart to receive the message. The Lord opened her heart—and then saved her entire household. Your job is to keep heading west and sharing the gospel."

keyIllustration: "A woman named Abigail intercepted David when he was about to massacre an entire household in anger. Afterwards, David said, \"Blessed be the Lord who sent you this day to meet me—you kept me from blood guilt.\" God sent someone to say no, and David thanked Him for it. A closed door can save your life."

application:
  - "Stop asking \"why\" and \"how\" when doors close. Ask instead: \"Lord, what do You want me to do now?\""
  - "Keep reading. Keep praying. Keep telling people about Jesus. If you don't get a new vision, keep going in the direction you're already going."
  - "Trust that God is saving people through your faithfulness—even when you can't see it yet."

toRemember: "Sometimes it's easier to discern the will of God than to accept it—but when you do, He does something magnificent."
