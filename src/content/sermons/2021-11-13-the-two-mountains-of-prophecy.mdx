# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You walk through the valley between Christ's suffering and His return with inexpressible joy — not because you can see the way ahead, but because the prophets, apostles, and the Holy Spirit have revealed enough of who Jesus is and what He has done to anchor your faith in the grace of God."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Peter 1:8-9"
keyScriptureText: Though you have not seen him, you love him. Though you do not see him now, you believe in him and rejoice with joy that is inexpressible and filled with glory, obtaining the outcome of your faith, the salvation of your souls.

mainPoints:
  - title: The two mountains of prophecy frame your present reality.
    body: "The Old Testament prophets saw the mountain of Christ's suffering and the mountain of His glory in the distance. You stand between them, in the valley, where the view is obscured by shadows."
  - title: "The precision of fulfilled prophecy guarantees the certainty of what's to come."
    body: "David and Isaiah described crucifixion centuries before it existed. Zechariah and Ezekiel pinpointed the Mount of Olives as the place of Christ's return. If the first mountain was fulfilled with total accuracy, so will the second."
  - title: Study prophecy humbly, not to fix dates but to know grace.
    body: "The religious elite missed Jesus even though prophecy pointed directly to Him. The apostles still didn't understand the timeline after the resurrection. You are called to search Scripture for the grace revealed in the gospel, not to build a prophetic system that makes you feel secure."
  - title: You can rejoice without seeing because you know the God who keeps His word.
    body: "The prophets searched and inquired. The apostles asked, \"When, Lord?\" The angels long to look into this salvation. You have been given what they yearned for: the revealed gospel of a suffering Saviour who will return in glory."

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "Stop demanding to know when or how God's plan will unfold. Your calling is not to decode timelines but to trust the grace you've been shown and walk faithfully through the valley."
  - "When life falls apart and you can't see how God could use it for good, remember: Scripture says you will know all things work together for good, not that you will see it in the moment."
  - "Prepare for Christ's return not with fear but with readiness. He came once as the suffering servant. He is coming again as the commander of heaven's armies to judge the earth."

toRemember: You have not seen Him, but you love Him — and though you do not see Him now, you rejoice with inexpressible joy, because the prophets, the apostles, and the Holy Spirit have revealed enough of His grace to carry you through the darkest valley.
