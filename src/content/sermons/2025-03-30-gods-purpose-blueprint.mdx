# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Your true purpose is revealed only through Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit equips you to walk the hard, narrow road despite fierce resistance.
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 7:13-14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Enter by the narrow gate. For the gate is wide and the way is easy that leads to destruction, and those who enter by it are many. For the gate is narrow and the way is hard that leads to life, and those who find it are few.

mainPoints:
  - title: Purpose is revealed in Christ
    body: — Without Jesus, we are spiritually blind like Saul, using our talents but reaping destruction. Only when we understand who Jesus is do we see the narrow gate and begin our true purpose.
  - title: Expect resistance on the journey
    body: — External opposition (people, careers, addictions), internal resistance (doubt, fear, lies we believe), and even resistance from the church will arise. Satan does not want you fulfilling your purpose in Christ.
  - title: The Holy Spirit carries you through
    body: — God the Father orchestrates all events, Jesus shows you the narrow gate, and the Holy Spirit guides, protects, and strengthens you every step of the way.

keyIllustration: "A traffic guard in Johannesburg stands in the intersection directing cars. For those brief moments, life feels purposeful — waving, dancing, guiding. The rest of his day he waits, ignored, barely surviving. Yet in that intersection, he experiences what every human craves: a sense that his life matters. This is the ache we all carry — the yearning for purpose that was broken when we were cast out of Eden."

application:
  - We must trust God the Father, believe in Jesus Christ the Son, and follow the Holy Spirit — this is the pattern for living out our purpose.
  - "Brokenness is not wasted. The extent of what has hurt you may correlate to the extent God can use you. Broken people can endure reproach for Christ's sake that others would shun."
  - Ask God to show you what He wants you to do with your gifts and talents for His Kingdom, not for your own glory.

toRemember: When we come to realise who Jesus is and what He has done, then we start to understand what our gifts and talents are for and how we can use them in the Kingdom.
