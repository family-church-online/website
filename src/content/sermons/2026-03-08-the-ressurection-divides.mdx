# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Your job is not to persuade or close the deal—your job is to testify to the facts about Jesus, and the Holy Spirit will do the rest.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 23:11 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "The following night the Lord stood by him and said, \"Take courage, for as you have testified to the facts about me in Jerusalem, so you must testify also in Rome.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: The Sanhedrin knew who Jesus was but refused to submit.
    body: They acknowledged His miracles, knew His power, and feared His influence—but chose their positions of authority over Him.
  - title: Paul testifies about resurrection and the room erupts in division.
    body: The resurrection is not a neutral topic. It divides because it confronts every claim to self-rule and demands accountability beyond the grave.
  - title: Jesus stands by Paul in his darkest moment.
    body: After two failed attempts to preach the gospel, when Paul feels like a failure, Jesus appears and affirms his work—not his success, but his faithfulness to testify to the facts.
  - title: Our job is to testify to the facts, not close the deal.
    body: "We are marketers, not salesmen. We present the truth about Jesus—who He is, what He did, how He changes lives—and the Holy Spirit alone can bring someone to say \"Jesus is Lord.\""

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - Stop measuring your witness by whether people accept Jesus. Measure it by whether you testified to the facts faithfully.
  - Expect opposition. If you testify about the resurrection, some people will violently reject it—not because you failed, but because the message threatens their autonomy.
  - "When you feel like a failure, remember Jesus stands by you. He doesn't measure your worth by results. He measures it by your faithfulness to testify."

toRemember: The Holy Spirit closes the deal. You testify to what Jesus has done. Let Him do the rest.
