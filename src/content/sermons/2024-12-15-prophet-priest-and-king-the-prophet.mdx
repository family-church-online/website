# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus is not just a prophet—He is the Prophet whom every Old Testament prophet pointed to, the final and perfect revelation of God, the only way to eternal life.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 1:1-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Long ago, at many times and in many ways, God spoke to our fathers by the prophets, but in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son, whom he appointed the heir of all things, through whom also he created the world. He is the radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his nature, and he upholds the universe by the word of his power. After making purification for sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high.

mainPoints:
  - title: A Prophet Speaks From God.
    body: "Prophets like Moses, Samuel, Elijah, and Elisha spoke God's word to His people—but Jesus is the final, perfect Word made flesh."
  - title: A Prophet Is Authenticated By God.
    body: Moses was authenticated with signs and wonders; Jesus gave sight to the blind—a miracle no Old Testament prophet ever performed—proving He is more than a prophet.
  - title: Prophets Are Mistreated and Murdered.
    body: Jerusalem put the prophets to death, and Jesus predicted His own crucifixion—then rose on the third day, exactly as He said.
  - title: A Prophet Reveals the Future.
    body: "Jesus foretold His death, resurrection, ascension to the Father's right hand, and return—and we can trust those promises because He has already fulfilled the first ones perfectly."

keyIllustration: A bird in the Amazon vibrates its wings 107 times per second to produce a sound no other bird makes—using two specially designed feathers, one a comb and the other a plectrum, that work together like a guitar string. Without all the elements together, nothing works. The design is irreducibly complex, yet some still refuse to see the Designer. In the same way, Christmas can distract us from seeing Jesus clearly—the one every detail of Scripture points to.

application:
  - Eternal life is not a transaction—it is knowing God and Jesus Christ whom He has sent. Spend this Advent asking Jesus to reveal Himself to you more deeply.
  - "Answer the question Jesus asks: \"Who do you say that I am?\" Your eternity depends on it."
  - "Don't let Christmas traditions distract you from celebrating the only way to the Father—Jesus Christ, the Way, the Truth, and the Life."

toRemember: "Every day on this earth is an opportunity given to you by the Father to know Jesus better—don't waste it."
