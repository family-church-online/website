# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God still gives gifts of prophecy and tongues to His church, but they must be used according to Scripture's clear instructions: one speaker at a time, interpretation required, all things done for building up, and above all, in love."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Corinthians 14:1-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Pursue love, and earnestly desire the spiritual gifts, especially that you may prophesy. For one who speaks in a tongue speaks not to men but to God, for no one understands him, but he utters mysteries in the Spirit. On the other hand, the one who prophesies speaks to people for their upbuilding and encouragement and consolation.

mainPoints:
  - title: Old Testament prophecy was inerrant; New Testament prophecy is for edification.
    body: Old Testament prophets who spoke falsely were put to death. Today, prophecy is given to build up, exhort, and comfort — it never contradicts Scripture.
  - title: Tongues are directed to God; prophecy is directed to people.
    body: "When we speak in tongues, we speak mysteries to God in the Spirit. When we prophesy, we speak God's encouragement to His people."
  - title: Not all have the same gift.
    body: The Spirit distributes gifts individually as He wills. You do not need to speak in tongues to be Spirit-filled. Do not despise the gifts God has given you because someone else has a different one.
  - title: All things must be done decently and in order.
    body: Only two or three may speak in tongues or prophesy in a gathering. There must be interpretation. One person speaks at a time. Everything is weighed against Scripture.

keyIllustration: A shoemaker attended a service where Charles Spurgeon preached. Spurgeon pointed directly at him and said he was a shoemaker who kept his shop open on Sundays and had taken ninepence the previous Sunday — fourpence profit. The man was stunned; it was true. He realized God had spoken to him through the preacher, closed his shop the next Sunday, and eventually came to faith in Christ. Prophecy brings conviction and discloses the secrets of the heart.

application:
  - Do not forbid speaking in tongues, and earnestly desire to prophesy — but do all things according to Scripture.
  - "If someone prophesies, test it: does it align with God's Word? Is it for building up, exhortation, or comfort?"
  - Pray before using any gift. Ask God to protect His people from misuse and to use you in love.

toRemember: The greatest gift is love — without it, every other gift is nothing.
