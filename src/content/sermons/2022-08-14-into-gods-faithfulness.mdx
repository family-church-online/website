mainPoints:
  - title: Paul Asks the Thessalonians to Pray for Mission.
    body: "He's heading into hostile territory, facing wicked men with no faith in Christ, and he needs the word of the Lord to go ahead of him."
  - title: The Lord Is Faithful—Immovably, Steadfastly, Reliably.
    body: "The Hebrew concept behind \"faithful\" is emet: truth, sturdy, unshakable. No matter how many times we fail, He never has an off day."
  - title: Children Need to Be Brought to Jesus, Not Fixed First.
    body: "Jesus said, \"Let the children come to me.\" They didn't need to become adults. In fact, adults need to become like them."
  - title: Saturate Your Home in Scripture.
    body: Deuteronomy 6 commands us to teach the Word diligently—when you sit, walk, lie down, rise. Write it on your doorposts. Make it unavoidable.

keyIllustration: "A couple had walked through years of struggle and prayer before their son was born. The entire congregation had prayed for them long before they ever met. When the child was finally brought to the church for blessing, the moment became a living picture of God's orchestration—His faithfulness carrying them through every dark season, now culminating in this public act of bringing the child to Jesus."

application:
  - Bring your children to Jesus exactly as they are—no preparation needed.
  - "Ask God to direct your heart into His love and the steadfastness of Christ—you can't manufacture this yourself."
  - "Make Scripture unavoidable in your home: speak it, write it, live it in front of your children daily."

toRemember: The Lord is faithful. He will establish you and guard you—and that faithfulness is the only ground you need to stand on as you raise children, follow Christ, and face the darkness.
