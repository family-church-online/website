# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The breastplate of righteousness is not your effort to be good — it is Christ's perfect righteousness given to you by faith, and Satan cannot penetrate it."
keyScriptureRef: "Romans 3:21-24 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But now the righteousness of God has been manifested apart from the law, although the Law and the Prophets bear witness to it — the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ for all who believe. For there is no distinction: for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God, and are justified by his grace as a gift, through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus."

mainPoints:
  - title: Attacks do not mean abandonment.
    body: Job, Peter, and Jesus Himself were all attacked by Satan — not because God rejected them, but because they walked faithfully. If you are under fire, it does not mean you are out of favour.
  - title: "Satan's goal is always the same."
    body: "Every temptation, every hardship, every accusation has one aim: to sever your relationship with God. He isolates you, then tells you God has abandoned you. The answer is to run toward God, not away."
  - title: Self-righteousness is as deadly as despair.
    body: "Job was blameless in God's eyes, yet fell into defending his own record. You cannot outweigh your sin with good deeds, and you cannot stand before God claiming you are basically fine. Both roads end in death."
  - title: You must be broken down to be built up.
    body: "Like a caterpillar dissolving into liquid before it becomes a moth, we must admit total spiritual bankruptcy before God can clothe us in His righteousness. The posture that saves is: \"God, be merciful to me, a sinner.\""

keyIllustration: "A moth's metamorphosis requires the caterpillar to dissolve almost entirely into liquid before being rebuilt into a new creature. The process is irreducibly complex — it cannot happen in gradual steps or the organism dies. Similarly, we cannot gradually make ourselves righteous. We must be entirely undone, admitting our sin, before God can remake us in Christ's righteousness."

application:
  - "Every morning, put on the breastplate by praying Romans 3:21-24 and declaring that your standing with God is based on Christ's work, not yours."
  - "When the accuser lists your sins, agree with him — and then point him to the cross. \"Yes, I am a sinner. That is why Jesus died for me.\""
  - Memorise Scripture so that in the moment of accusation, you have the truth ready to counter the lie.

toRemember: You are not a worm trying to become a butterfly — you are a worm remade into something miraculous by the power of God, and nothing the enemy says can undo what Christ has done.
