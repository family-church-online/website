# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Everything created by human hands will be shaken and removed, but those who listen to God's voice will inherit an eternal kingdom that cannot be dissolved."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 12:26-27 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "At that time his voice shook the earth, but now he has promised, \"Yet once more I will shake not only the earth but also the heavens.\" This phrase, \"Yet once more,\" indicates the removal of things that are shaken—that is, things that have been made—in order that the things that cannot be shaken may remain."

mainPoints:
  - title: "Refusing God's voice has eternal consequences."
    body: If those who rejected Moses on earth did not escape judgment, those who reject God Himself—speaking through Jesus—will face far greater accountability.
  - title: Everything created will be shaken and removed.
    body: "Human technology, infrastructure, wealth, empires, even the heavens themselves will be violently shaken until only what is unshakable remains—God's eternal kingdom."
  - title: An unshakable kingdom is already available.
    body: Through Jesus, access to a kingdom of justice and righteousness that cannot be dissolved is offered to us now—this is the foundation of all hope.
  - title: Our response should be reverent worship.
    body: In light of receiving this unshakable inheritance, acceptable worship means living with reverence, awe, and an eternal perspective rather than clinging to temporary things.

keyIllustration: "Imagine the fabric of time and space as a picnic blanket. On it rests the earth with all its people, homes, communities, and the entire universe—galaxies, stars, solar systems. Now picture someone with the power to wield that blanket taking it in His hands and shaking it violently, the way you'd shake out a dirty picnic blanket. Everything—tectonic plates, oceans, stars, galaxies—is flung off and dissolves into nothing. Then the Father takes that fabric and fashions it into a divine, unshakable kingdom. That's the kingdom being offered."

application:
  - Stop placing your ultimate security in careers, finances, or earthly structures destined to be shaken.
  - "Listen for God's voice in the silence rather than letting the noise of this world drown Him out."
  - Accept that Jesus Christ lived, died, and rose again—and that His love for you is greater than any other.

toRemember: When everything around you shakes, the kingdom that cannot be shaken is yours through Christ—set your sights there.
