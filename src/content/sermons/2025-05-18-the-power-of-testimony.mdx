# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Your testimony has power not because your experience was dramatic, but because you obeyed God's instruction, confirmed it through Scripture and the body of Christ, and testified to His glory so others could come to repentance."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 11:18 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "When they heard these things, they fell silent and they glorified God, saying, \"Then to the Gentiles also God has granted repentance that leads to life.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Look for the opportunity.
    body: Opportunity usually comes in uncomfortable circumstances — criticism, pushback, or when life crashes after a spiritual high.
  - title: Be prepared to hear the Holy Spirit.
    body: Live a disciplined life of prayer and obedient service; make space for God to speak instead of expecting Him to interrupt your schedule.
  - title: Ask the right question.
    body: "When God gives a vision or instruction, don't ask \"What does this mean?\" — ask \"What do You want me to do?\""
  - title: Embrace the body.
    body: God works through multiple people simultaneously to confirm His word; involve other believers and check everything against Scripture.
  - title: Expect God to confirm His word.
    body: The Holy Spirit validates obedience in ways that align with Scripture and the prophetic promises already given.
  - title: "Testify and celebrate God's glory."
    body: Share your testimony in a way that glorifies God and includes the full gospel, calling people to repentance and faith in Christ alone.

keyIllustration: An American pastor, Josh Sullivan, was kidnapped at gunpoint from his church in the Eastern Cape. Held for six days, he was finally rushed out of the house by his captors — but inexplicably fell twice, unable to stand, delaying their escape. As they pulled out of the driveway, police who had gotten lost looking for another address randomly pulled in to ask for directions. A firefight ensued; the captors were killed, and Josh was found unharmed in the car. The police had no idea he was there — but Jesus always knew where he was.

application:
  - If you want God to speak, stop waiting for Him to interrupt your day — proactively create space for Him through prayer and obedience.
  - When you sense God leading you, involve the body of Christ and confirm it against Scripture before you act.
  - "Never share your testimony without sharing the gospel: we have all sinned, deserve God's wrath, and can only be saved by faith in Christ's death and resurrection."

toRemember: "Your testimony doesn't need to be dramatic to be powerful — it just needs to be obedient, confirmed by Scripture, and told for the glory of God and the repentance of others."
