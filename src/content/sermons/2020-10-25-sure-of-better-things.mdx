# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The Holy Spirit assures believers of their salvation through three witnesses: the promises of Scripture, the experience of God's worth and love, and the fruit of works motivated by love for Him."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 6:9-10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Though we speak in this way, yet in your case, beloved, we feel sure of better things, things that belong to salvation. For God is not unjust so as to overlook your work and the love that you have shown for his name in serving the saints, as you still do.

mainPoints:
  - title: The Witness of the Word
    body: Scripture is inspired, trustworthy, and filled with promises you can rely on — from justification by grace to the bodily resurrection of Christ.
  - title: The Witness of the Worth
    body: "The Holy Spirit pours God's love into your heart, filling you with love for Him and assuring you that you are His child."
  - title: The Witness of the Works
    body: When you serve others out of love for God, the Holy Spirit produces fruit — love, joy, peace, patience — confirming you belong to Him.

keyIllustration: "When a believer sits broken and despondent, questioning whether they've failed God too deeply, God sends real people with real problems, asking for help. As they pray together and serve one another, they see God work in the person in front of them — and that becomes the clearest witness to God's love for His children."

application:
  - "Don't treat salvation like cosmic insurance — cultivate a living relationship with God through daily study of His Word, worship, and service."
  - "When doubt and fear creep in, declare God's worth even when it feels uncomfortable — watch how love for Him grows and overshadows the fear."
  - Get involved in the work of the kingdom — making disciples, serving the church, praying with others — because this is where you see God work most powerfully.

toRemember: "It's not empty words that give us eternal salvation assurance — it's a living, breathing relationship with God, worked out day by day with perseverance and faith, trusting in His promises."
