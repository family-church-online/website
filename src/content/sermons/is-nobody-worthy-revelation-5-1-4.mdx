hook: "A sealed scroll rests in God's right hand, but a search of heaven, earth, and everywhere below turns up no one worthy to open it. Why would that reduce a man standing before the throne to loud, breaking tears?"
takeaways:
  - "Understand that the sealed scroll represents God's plan—coming judgment, the fulfilment of prophecy, a will handed to its heir, and the deed to a purchased world."
  - "Recognise why John weeps loudly: if no one can open the scroll, the death, sin, and suffering of the world have no end."
  - "See that opening the scroll requires someone fully human yet completely without sin, since only the sinless may carry out judgment."
  - "Grasp why Jesus alone is worthy—He shared our humanity, remained pure, and ransomed people for God by His blood."
  - "Learn to view His return as both an act of mercy and an act of justice, where righteousness and peace meet in one act."
audience:

  - You quietly wrestle with whether Jesus can really be the only way back to God.
  - "You long for the world's wrongs to be put right but are afraid of the judgment that comes with it."
