# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: An unbelieving heart is not passive doubt—it is active rebellion that leads to falling away from God, and it must be fought daily through mutual encouragement and holding fast to His Word.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 3:12-13 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Take care, brothers, lest there be in any of you an evil, unbelieving heart, leading you to fall away from the living God. But exhort one another every day, as long as it is called today, that none of you may be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.

mainPoints:
  - title: The Seriousness of Unbelief
    body: "Scripture calls unbelief evil because it arrogantly rejects God's revealed power and promises, mirroring Pharaoh's defiant hardness and Israel's refusal to trust despite witnessing miracles."
  - title: "The Solution: Mutual Exhortation"
    body: "God's remedy is not private discipline alone but daily, intentional encouragement within the church—believers holding one another accountable to stay in the Word and resist spiritual drift."
  - title: The Consequence of Falling Away
    body: Perseverance to the end is not optional; those who do not hold fast their original confidence have no share in Christ, making daily faithfulness a matter of eternal significance.

keyIllustration: "A physical education teacher described how, during lockdown, he and several friends created a Zoom group to train together. Without that mutual accountability, he admitted he would have abandoned fitness entirely. The same principle applies spiritually: isolation breeds drift, but intentional community keeps us pressing forward."

application:
  - Commit to reading Scripture daily and invite someone to hold you accountable to that rhythm.
  - Reach out to another believer this week—by phone, video, or in person—and ask how you can encourage them in their faith.
  - Identify one area where pride or self-reliance has crept in, confess it, and ask God to soften your heart.

toRemember: Today is the day to exhort, encourage, and hold fast—because an unguarded heart hardens slowly, and the stakes are eternal.
