# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Before God uses us mightily, He humbles us completely—this is the pattern of Christ's own victory and the way He distributes gifts to His church."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 4:7-10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But grace was given to each one of us according to the measure of Christ's gift. Therefore it says, \"When he ascended on high he led a host of captives, and he gave gifts to men.\" (In saying, \"He ascended,\" what does it mean but that he had also descended into the lower regions, the earth? He who descended is the one who also ascended far above all the heavens, that he might fill all things.)"

mainPoints:
  - title: "Christ's ascension follows His descent."
    body: Jesus humbled Himself, became human, obeyed to the point of death—and was then exalted above every name. Absolute surrender preceded absolute victory.
  - title: Believers are gifted individually for the common good.
    body: "Each member of the church receives grace according to the measure of Christ's gift—different gifts, one Spirit, one purpose: unity in diversity."
  - title: We are captives given as gifts.
    body: Christ conquered us as His enemies, made us His own, and now gives us to the church to serve and build it up. You are not your own—you were bought at a price.
  - title: God plows the field before the harvest.
    body: "Humbling, suffering, and obscurity are often God's preparation for fruitfulness, not evidence of His displeasure. The plow precedes the seed."

keyIllustration: "A.W. Tozer's \"The Plow\"—the fallow field is safe, smug, and undisturbed, but it never experiences growth or fruit. The cultivated field is broken open by the plow, violent and painful, but that breaking allows the seed to fall and new life to emerge. When you feel plowed, God is preparing you for fruitfulness."

application:
  - Stop resisting the process of being humbled—whether through suffering, disappointment, or obscurity—and trust that God is at work preparing you for His purposes.
  - Surrender the outcome of your life to God, knowing that faithfulness and obedience matter more than achievement or recognition.
  - Recognise that your spiritual gifts are given by grace, for the common good of the church, not for personal validation or comparison with others.

toRemember: If you want absolute victory, you must absolutely surrender—and that surrender is not defeat, but the doorway to being used powerfully by God.
