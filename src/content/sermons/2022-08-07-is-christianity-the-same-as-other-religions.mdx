# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Christianity is radically distinct from every other religion because it proclaims a Triune God who saves you by grace, not performance—Father who chose you, Son who died for you, Spirit who sanctifies you.
keyScriptureRef: "2 Thessalonians 2:13-14"
keyScriptureText: But we ought always to give thanks to God for you, brothers beloved by the Lord, because God chose you as the firstfruits to be saved, through sanctification by the Spirit and belief in the truth. To this he called you through our gospel, so that you may obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

mainPoints:
  - title: Christianity worships the Creator, not the created.
    body: Every religion that elevates angels, demons, nature, or human tradition above the one true God exchanges truth for a lie—and Scripture names that as the fundamental deception.
  - title: Salvation comes from the whole Triune God.
    body: You are beloved by the Lord Jesus, chosen by God the Father, and sanctified by the Holy Spirit—no other faith proclaims one God in three persons working together to save you.
  - title: You are saved by grace, not by works.
    body: "Christianity flips the religious script: you don't earn God's favor by doing good—God gives you eternal life as a gift, then transforms you to live in gratitude and obedience."
  - title: Deception will increase, but God will establish you.
    body: Whether subtle lies in the church or blatant rebellion in the culture, you are called to hold fast to the truth—and God Himself will protect, comfort, and strengthen you.

keyIllustration: A rove beetle infiltrates army ant colonies by mimicking their appearance and scent so perfectly that researchers must count thousands of ants to spot a single imposter. The beetle lives among them undetected, feeding on their eggs from the inside. Deception in the church works the same way—it looks almost right, sounds almost biblical, but subtly devours truth from within.

application:
  - Stand firm in Scripture as your sole authority—human tradition, personal experience, and cultural pressure do not carry the same weight as the Word of God.
  - Know what you believe about the Trinity, the gospel, and salvation by grace so you can recognize teaching that contradicts it.
  - "Don't be afraid of the cultural drift or rising deception—God has chosen you, sealed you by His Spirit, and will establish you in every good work and word."

toRemember: The God of all grace who has called you to His eternal glory in Christ will Himself restore, confirm, strengthen, and establish you.
