# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: When Christ returns, those who know Him will enter the wedding feast, and those who do not—no matter how religious or informed—will be shut out forever.
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 25:13"
keyScriptureText: Watch therefore, for you know neither the day nor the hour.

mainPoints:
  - title: Preparedness cannot be transferred.
    body: The wise virgins could not share their oil because a lifetime of relationship with God cannot be handed over in a moment of crisis.
  - title: The oil is the fruit of the Holy Spirit in you.
    body: Having the Spirit is not a one-time event but an ongoing daily reality that produces faith, obedience, and endurance when the wait is long and the pressure is real.
  - title: "Jesus' warning: \"I do not know you.\""
    body: Entry into the kingdom is not about doctrinal knowledge or church attendance—it is about a living, growing relationship with Christ that He recognises.

keyIllustration: A student arrived at his final exam fully prepared in every external way—on time, hydrated, equipped—but realised upon opening the paper that five years of neglect had left him utterly unprepared to answer the questions. No amount of last-minute readiness could replace the daily work he had failed to do. This is the difference between being present and being prepared.

application:
  - "Stop waiting for the \"right time\" to prioritise God. Today is the day to seek Him, know Him, and build the relationship that will sustain you when trials come."
  - Guard against cold love. Lawlessness and injustice can make your heart hard. Choose to minister in love to both victims and perpetrators—because all need Jesus.
  - Do not rely on information about God. A relationship with Him is grown daily through prayer, Scripture, worship, and obedience—not collected once and stored.

toRemember: Forget your past failures. Today, make your relationship with God through Jesus Christ your priority, so that when He returns—whether at midnight or midday—you will know Him and He will know you.
