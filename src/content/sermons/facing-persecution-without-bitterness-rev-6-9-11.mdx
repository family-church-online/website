[35:52] Father, thank you that you are righteous and holy, worthy of all our praise. Thank you, Lord, that you see everything. You know everything. You tell the disciples that not one hair of their head will perish, that every hair is numbered, that not even a little sparrow falls from the heavens without you having known about it. Not only do you know about it, Lord, but you've made all the plans that we need to help us through it, to give us purpose through it, to reward us through it, and to reward us eternally after it.

And Father, thank you that our Lord Jesus Christ is not asking us to persevere through persecution from a distance. It's something he did, something he chose. Lord Jesus, thank you for choosing to die. Thank you for choosing to be persecuted, to be ostracized, to be mocked, to be beaten, to be rejected even though you did nothing wrong. Thank you that whatever we feel, whatever we're afraid of, we know you've been through it, and you will never leave us alone, and you will carry us through it.

[36:21] Your grace is enough. Thank you. In Jesus' name. Amen.
