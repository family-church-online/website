# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Your freedom in Christ—purchased by His blood—is worth fighting for, because it rests on the gospel alone, not on human additions or conditions.
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 2:4-5 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Yet because of false brothers secretly brought in—who slipped in to spy out our freedom that we have in Christ Jesus, so that they might bring us into slavery—to them we did not yield in submission even for a moment, so that the truth of the gospel might be preserved for you.

mainPoints:
  - title: The gospel is non-negotiable.
    body: Paul took Titus—an uncircumcised Greek believer—to Jerusalem to prove that salvation is by faith alone, not faith plus anything.
  - title: "True freedom is the power to do what's right."
    body: "Freedom in Christ isn't license to do whatever you want—it's liberation from sin's control and the fear of death, and access to God at any time."
  - title: Your fights have spiritual significance.
    body: "Whether you're battling illness, rejection, or exhaustion, these struggles are part of a larger mission to glorify God and advance the gospel."
  - title: Stand firm, but stay soft.
    body: Defending truth sometimes requires conflict, but hardness of heart dishonors God—compassion must remain even in the fight.

keyIllustration: "A child learning to ride a bicycle finally gets freedom when the training wheels come off. Imagine someone insisting those wheels go back on after the child has learned to ride—absurd and frustrating. False teachers were doing exactly that to the Galatian church: trying to put back the \"training wheels\" of Old Testament law after Jesus had given them freedom."

application:
  - "Don't compromise the gospel—not for comfort, not for unity, not to avoid conflict. The gospel is worth the fight."
  - "Remember that your struggles have purpose. God wastes nothing, and He only lets you into a fight because He's going to do something with it."
  - "While you fight for truth, remember the poor—don't let theological battles harden your heart to human need."

toRemember: "The peace and freedom waiting for you in eternity is so far beyond what you can imagine that every fight you endure now is worth it—and the gates of hell will not prevail against Christ's church."
