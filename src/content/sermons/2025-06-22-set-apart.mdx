# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God reveals your calling when you stop asking what to do and start becoming the kind of person who can hear Him — through worship, service, prayer, and Scripture.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 13:2-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "While they were worshiping the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, 'Set apart for me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them.' Then after fasting and praying, they laid their hands on them and sent them off."

mainPoints:
  - title: The church at Antioch was a messy, diverse family.
    body: It included a Jewish priest passionate about reaching Gentiles, a black man named Simeon, a Libyan named Lucius, and a man who grew up with the king who mocked Jesus — yet the Holy Spirit spoke clearly to them because they were unified in worship and service.
  - title: "If you want to hear from God, don't do more — be more."
    body: "The Holy Spirit spoke while they were worshiping and fasting, not while they were strategizing or planning. Hearing God isn't about adding spiritual tasks; it's about cultivating a lifestyle of worship, prayer, Scripture, and service."
  - title: You are not your own — you were bought at a price.
    body: "The call to be \"set apart\" is a reminder that if you belong to Christ, you no longer belong to yourself. Your life has a purpose beyond survival, beyond comfort, beyond your own plans."
  - title: The church confirms and sends what God initiates.
    body: "The Holy Spirit identified Barnabas and Saul, but the church laid hands on them and sent them out. Your calling is personal, but it's never private — the body of Christ plays a role in affirming, equipping, and supporting you."

keyIllustration: "A Jewish priest, a black man, a Libyan, and a childhood friend of a murderous king walked into a church. It sounds like the setup to a joke, but it's the story of Antioch — a city so dark and debauched that fleeing Christians thought they could hide there. Yet God planted a church in that chaos, brought together the most unlikely group of leaders, and used them to launch the greatest missionary movement in history. The diversity wasn't a problem to manage; it was the kindling God needed to start a fire that would burn across the globe."

application:
  - "If you're asking \"What does God want me to do?\" — start by asking \"Who does God want me to become?\" Serve somewhere. Worship daily. Open Scripture. Pray. The call comes to those who are already moving."
  - "Your differences aren't disqualifications — they're the diversity God uses to reach the world. Don't wait to have it all together before you serve; God uses messy, scrambled-up people."
  - "You were bought at a price. You don't belong to yourself. Wake up tomorrow and ask: \"What eternal purpose do I get to live for today?\" not \"How do I just survive today?\""

toRemember: "You're not here to survive — you're here for a reason, and that reason might very well be that today, somebody you love learns about Jesus and comes to eternal life because of what God gave you to give them."
