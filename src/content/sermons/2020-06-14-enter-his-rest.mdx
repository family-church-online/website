# ── PRESENTATION COPY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
tagLine: "Rest isn't found in perfect circumstances. It's found in believing God when everything falls apart."
shortDescription: "You've seen God do impossible things before. So why is it so hard to trust Him now? When life collapses — when loved ones die, when income vanishes, when the future looks impassable — unbelief keeps you from the rest God promises. Peace doesn't mean comfort. It means trusting Him in the storm."
subtitle: The Israelites saw miracle after miracle, yet refused to trust God when it counted — and their unbelief cost them the rest He promised.
hook: "Why do we struggle to believe God in the crisis when we've already seen Him come through before? The wilderness generation knew God's power — they'd walked out of Egypt on dry ground through the Red Sea — yet when the moment came to enter the Promised Land, they balked. What does their failure teach us about entering God's rest today?"

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Entering God's rest is not about perfect circumstances — it's about trusting His promises when everything around you says they're impossible."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 3:19 ESV"
keyScriptureText: So we see that they were unable to enter because of unbelief.

mainPoints:
  - title: "The wilderness generation saw God's power but refused to trust Him."
    body: "It wasn't one incident — they rebelled against God repeatedly over forty years, and their unbelief ultimately disqualified them from entering the Promised Land."
  - title: "Faith that you had once doesn't count — what matters is the faith you have now."
    body: Like fitness that fades without maintenance, faith must be present and active, not merely remembered.
  - title: "God's rest is available in the middle of suffering, not just at the end."
    body: "Horatio Spafford lost everything — property, daughters, security — yet wrote \"It is well with my soul\" in the place where his children drowned, because he trusted God's promises over his circumstances."
  - title: Your choices carry consequences for those who depend on you.
    body: The men of Israel forfeited the Promised Land, and their families suffered forty years in the wilderness as a result.

keyIllustration: "A man named Horatio Spafford built a successful property business and invested in the poor and marginalized. He lost everything in the great Chicago fire. He sent his wife and four daughters on a family vacation to England; their ship was struck and sank in the Atlantic, killing all four daughters. His wife sent a two-word telegram: \"Saved alone.\" When crossing the ocean himself, he asked the captain to show him the exact spot where the ship went down. There, over the graves of his daughters, he wrote the hymn \"It Is Well with My Soul.\" Peace like a river, sorrows like sea billows — whatever the circumstances, he could say his soul was at rest because he believed God's promises."

application:
  - When circumstances feel impossible, return to the promises of God in Scripture and remind yourself of what He has already done.
  - Encourage your brothers and sisters in Christ — share how God has been faithful to you, because testimony is a weapon against despair.
  - If you cannot find rest, reach out to other believers and ask them to pray with you and strengthen your faith.

toRemember: "Entering God's rest begins the moment you believe — and the more deeply you trust, the more powerfully you experience His peace, even in the storm."
