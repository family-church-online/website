# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God saves by faith alone, and saving faith declares itself immediately in baptism — not after you've cleaned up, not after you've waited long enough, but as soon as you believe."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 10:44-47 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "While Peter was still saying these things, the Holy Spirit fell on all who heard the word. And the believers from among the circumcised who had come with Peter were amazed, because the gift of the Holy Spirit was poured out even on the Gentiles. For they were hearing them speaking in tongues and extolling God. Then Peter declared, \"Can anyone withhold water for baptizing these people who have received the Holy Spirit just as we have?\""

mainPoints:
  - title: God saves you to make you a witness, not just to rescue you.
    body: "The disciples saw Jesus die and rise, yet Christ told them to wait in Jerusalem for the Spirit — because without the Spirit's power, you're only an advocate with arguments, not a witness with fire."
  - title: Baptism is the immediate, visible response to invisible salvation.
    body: Cornelius and his household received the Spirit before they asked, before they were baptised, before they cleaned up — because they heard and believed. Nothing else was required. Nothing else should delay you.
  - title: Baptism places you into a new family, not just a new belief system.
    body: "Peter — an Orthodox Jew who wouldn't eat with Gentiles — stayed with Cornelius for days. The gospel doesn't just change your theology; it changes who you eat with, live with, and call family."

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "If Jesus has saved you, obey Him publicly in baptism — don't wait for perfect understanding, perfect behaviour, or perfect timing. Faith acts."
  - Ask the Holy Spirit to baptise you with power for witness — being saved is not the same as being equipped to serve and testify with boldness.
  - Welcome the baptised into the mess and beauty of church family — baptism is entry into a community that eats together, stays together, and loves imperfectly together.

toRemember: The God who drew you to faith will equip you for witness — but you must take the step of obedience before you feel ready.
