# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You are saved by grace through faith in Christ alone—and the moment you add anything to that, you lose everything.
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 5:1 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For freedom Christ has set us free. Stand firm therefore, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Stand firm in the gospel—don't go back to earning your way."
    body: If you trust in your own obedience or religious performance to make you right with God, you have rejected grace and severed yourself from Christ.
  - title: "False teaching doesn't look dangerous at first."
    body: It comes in small doses, through respected voices, and feels harmless—but a little leaven leavens the whole lump. What starts as a minor compromise ends in deadly error.
  - title: God has given you elders to protect you from false teaching.
    body: "Their job is to teach the truth, identify error, and keep the church from drifting. Bring your questions to them. Don't assume every teaching labelled \"Christian\" is safe."

keyIllustration: "A family of tiny klipspringer antelope is cornered on a granite boulder by a pack of wild dogs. The dogs press in from every side, lunging, barking, trying to provoke them into moving. The young antelope watch the elders—who stay absolutely still on the one impossible spot the dogs can't reach. If they panic and run, they're lost. But if they stay where they are, they're safe. The Galatian church faces the same choice: stay in the truth of the gospel, or be chased off by false voices into deadly ground."

application:
  - "Stop listening to any teaching that says Jesus plus something equals salvation. If it's not in Scripture, tested by Scripture, and confirmed by your church elders, leave it alone."
  - When suffering comes, resist the lie that God is punishing you for lack of faith or obedience. You are saved by grace—your circumstances do not determine your standing with God.
  - "Take the teachings you're consuming—devotionals, podcasts, social media posts—to your elders and ask: Is this sound? Does this align with the gospel? Don't assume; ask."

toRemember: "Jesus Christ is enough. His death and resurrection are enough. His grace is enough. Don't let anyone take that from you."
