# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Remembering God's faithfulness is the antidote to life's perfect storms—and it requires children to ask their elders, and elders to faithfully tell the truth about who God is."
keyScriptureRef: "Deuteronomy 32:7 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Remember the days of old. Consider the years of past generations. Ask your father, and he will tell you. Your elders, and they will teach you.

mainPoints:
  - title: All of us are called to remember.
    body: Gratitude comes from remembering what God has done; remembering His faithfulness equips us to face the perfect storms of life—when multiple crises hit at once.
  - title: Children and youth are called to ask.
    body: "When you struggle with identity, purpose, or pressure, don't turn to your screen—go to your parents and elders. They may not know what you're into, but they know you in ways no one else can."
  - title: Parents and elders are called to tell.
    body: But before we can tell, we must listen. We must hear not just the question, but the question behind the question. Teaching happens through relationship, experience, play, and prayer—not textbook instruction pasted onto their lives.

keyIllustration: "A fisherman faces a perfect storm when gale-force winds combine with torrential rain and extreme cold. Any one challenge is manageable—but all three at once create disaster. We all face perfect storms: a job loss during a recession, a family illness during a move, a breakup alongside rejection at school. When multiple crises strike at once, remembering who God is and what He has done becomes our anchor."

application:
  - "Young people: Take your questions about identity, purpose, and faith to your parents and elders—not to social media. They formed you, they know you, they love you in ways no influencer ever will."
  - "Parents: Don't replace yourself with a screen. Get involved in your children's world. Listen first, then tell. Pray with them for the specific struggles they're facing. Teach them through experience, not just information."
  - "Everyone: When life overwhelms you, remember what God has done—for all humankind since the beginning, and specifically in your own life. Remembering is the foundation of gratitude, and gratitude is the cure for sin."

toRemember: God is strong enough to exalt in monotony—perhaps He is younger than we are, and His delight in making each daisy again and again is what sustains the world; we must teach our children with that same tireless joy.
