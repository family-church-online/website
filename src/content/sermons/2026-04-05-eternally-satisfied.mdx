mainPoints:
  - title: Isaiah predicts both death and resurrection.
    body: "He writes of a grave—but also of someone who will \"see His offspring\" and \"prolong His days.\" Dead men don't see. This is prophecy of resurrection."
  - title: The resurrection is proof that Jesus was sinless.
    body: Death could not hold Him because He owed nothing. His resurrection is the evidence that His sacrifice was effective and complete.
  - title: The resurrection is the gift that makes faith possible.
    body: "God didn't just save us—He gave us proof we could trust. Abraham believed a promise. We're offered an empty tomb and eyewitness testimony."
  - title: You are His offspring.
    body: "When Isaiah says \"He shall see His offspring,\" he's talking about all who believe. You are the result of Christ's suffering—and the reason He is satisfied."

keyIllustration: "A condemned criminal hanging beside Jesus stood up for Him when no one else would. He said, \"We deserve what we're getting, but this man has done nothing wrong.\" If Jesus had sinned, He couldn't have paid your debt—He would have owed His own. But being perfect, He was able to pay what you could not."

application:

  - "The table isn't a place to mourn—it's a declaration that He is alive, interceding for you, and coming back."
