# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God has strategically positioned you in communities where you have access — and with the Holy Spirit's power and knowledge of Scripture, you're equipped to be the bridge that connects those communities to Christ."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 16:1-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Paul came also to Derbe and to Lystra. A disciple was there, named Timothy, the son of a Jewish woman who was a believer, but his father was a Greek. He was well spoken of by the brothers at Lystra and at Iconium. Paul wanted Timothy to accompany him, and he took him and circumcised him because of the Jews who were in those places, for they all knew that his father was a Greek.

mainPoints:
  - title: Timothy was positioned at the intersection of two worlds.
    body: Jewish mother, Greek father — he belonged nowhere and everywhere, which made him the perfect bridge between Jew and Gentile.
  - title: "Paul's decision to circumcise Timothy was about access, not salvation."
    body: "Unlike Titus (a full Gentile), Timothy's circumcision removed a cultural barrier without compromising the gospel."
  - title: Growth always involves discomfort.
    body: "The early church couldn't stay comfortable — Jew and Gentile had to learn to worship together. Neither can we."
  - title: "You have what Timothy had: access, Scripture, and the Spirit."
    body: "God doesn't wait for you to be an expert. He uses you where you are, with what you know, empowered by His presence."

keyIllustration: "A man accidentally attended the wrong wedding — didn't know a soul, sat through the entire ceremony thinking he was late to the right one, then bolted once he realised his mistake. Four years later, the bride tracked him down through social media. His unplanned presence became a story of connection, and now they're friends. Sometimes God grants you access to places you never intended to enter — and what you do with that access matters."

application:
  - "Take inventory: Where has God given you access? Your workplace, sports team, school, neighbourhood — those aren't random. They're mission fields."
  - "Stop waiting to \"know the Bible well enough.\" Timothy started immediately. Read Scripture, ask the Holy Spirit for power, and start where you are."
  - "Be a human magnet. You may be the only person who can connect a particular community to the church — don't waste that privilege."

toRemember: "The Great Commission wasn't given to elders and pastors — it was given to every single one of us, to go and make disciples wherever God has placed us."
