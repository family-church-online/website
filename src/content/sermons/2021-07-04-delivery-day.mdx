# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "After suffering so much for Jesus, don't shrink back in fear — hold on in faith because delivery day is coming and His promises are worth the wait."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 10:35-38"
keyScriptureText: "Therefore do not throw away your confidence, which has a great reward. For you have need of endurance, so that when you have done the will of God you may receive what is promised. mainPoints:
  - title: "Don't waste what you've already suffered."
    body: "These believers had been publicly shamed, lost property, and stood with others in prison — all because they knew they had \"a better possession and an abiding one.\" To turn back now would throw away everything they endured."
  - title: "Remember what you're waiting for."
    body: "God has made specific promises: the crown of life, becoming like Christ when we see Him, a place prepared by Jesus, our names confessed before the Father, ruling with Christ on His throne, and the end of all tears, death, mourning, and pain."
  - title: "Hold on in faith — He's coming soon."
    body: "The \"coming one\" will not delay. God is looking for those who will wait for Him in faith, not shrink back in fear. When the Son of Man returns, will He find faith on earth?"

keyIllustration: "A child waits for a gift ordered online, checking daily for the courier. The longer the wait, the greater the anticipation. The gift isn't just arriving — it's stretching joy across days, allowing the child to share the excitement, to talk about what's coming. That's what we do with God's promises: we hold them, talk about them, anticipate them, knowing delivery day is certain."

application:
  - "Don't let the length of the wait make you doubt the certainty of the promise. God's delivery day is coming."
  - "When suffering tempts you to turn back, remember what you've already endured and what you're waiting for."
  - Live by faith, not fear. Shrinking back — a fearful, cowardly turning away — is the opposite of holding on in confidence.

toRemember: "The child you delivered will someday deliver you — from suffering, from fear, from death itself. Hold on. He's coming."
