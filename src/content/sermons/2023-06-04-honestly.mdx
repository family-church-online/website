mainPoints:
  - title: Truth in your words.
    body: "Lying isn't only speaking falsehood—it's also refusing to speak truth when you should, curating appearances, or staying silent to avoid discomfort."
  - title: Truth in your emotions.
    body: "Unaddressed anger and bitterness don't fade—they fester. When you hide what you feel, you give the devil an opportunity to destroy relationships."
  - title: Truth in your work.
    body: Honest work means integrity in what you produce, not just avoiding theft. Half-hearted effort or cutting corners is a form of dishonesty.

keyIllustration: "A cable tie demonstrates the progressive trap of dishonesty. Each click tightens the hold—you can't go backwards. Every lie forces another lie to cover it, and another, until you're bound and the blood supply is cut off. The only way out is a single decisive cut. That's what Christ does: truth liberates in one stroke what lies have slowly strangled."

application:
  - "Stop thinking of yourself as \"basically honest\" if you curate your image, dodge confrontation, or coast at work."
  - "When you're angry with someone, deal with it quickly and openly—otherwise bitterness will grow and wreck the relationship."
  - Remember that God despises lies as much as murder. Revelation 22 excludes liars from the kingdom alongside murderers and idolaters.

toRemember: "You cannot cut yourself free from the trap of lies, but the Spirit of truth indwells you to convict, forgive, and liberate you for God's glory."
