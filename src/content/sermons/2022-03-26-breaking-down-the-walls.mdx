# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God sent spies into Jericho not for military intelligence, but to rescue a Canaanite prostitute who placed her faith in Him—revealing that the gospel has always been for the most unlikely, and that the walls separating us from the lost are flattened by God so we can walk in and fetch those He has already marked for salvation.
keyScriptureRef: "Joshua 2:9-11 ESV"
keyScriptureText: I know that the Lord has given you the land, and that the fear of you has fallen upon us, and that all the inhabitants of the land melt away before you. For we have heard how the Lord dried up the water of the Red Sea before you when you came out of Egypt… For the Lord your God, He is God in the heavens above and on the earth beneath.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Rahab's faith was genuine because it led to radical action."
    body: She risked execution to hide the spies because she feared God more than her own king. Hebrews 11 honors her faith; Matthew 1 places her in the lineage of Jesus Christ.
  - title: The red cord foreshadows the blood of Christ.
    body: Just as Rahab and her family were saved by staying in the room marked with the red cord, so all whom God has chosen are marked by the blood of Jesus and will not be harmed when judgment comes.
  - title: "God sent the spies to save Rahab—and to build Israel's faith."
    body: "The spies returned saying, \"Truly the Lord has given all the land into our hands, and also all the inhabitants of the land melt away because of us.\" Those words were Rahab's. The one they rescued inspired the entire nation."
  - title: You are positioned where you are to fetch the people God has already chosen.
    body: The church exists not to hide behind walls but to walk through the rubble of walls God has flattened and bring home those He has marked—no matter how unlikely they seem.

keyIllustration: "When Israel invaded Jericho, the entire wall collapsed inward—except for one small section marked by a red cord, where Rahab and her family waited. The spies walked through the chaos, opened the door, and said, \"Come.\" God orchestrated an earthquake, flattened a fortress, and left standing only the one room He had marked for salvation. That's the gospel: God breaks down every impossible barrier to rescue the ones He has chosen."

application:
  - "If you think you deserve salvation more than Rahab did, you're wrong. If you think you're too broken for God to use you, you're also wrong. Salvation is not about your résumé; it's about His sovereign choice and your faith response."
  - "God has positioned this church in Fourways—behind the walls of gated communities—not by accident. Between 5-7% of this area regularly attends church. The walls are massive. But God flattens walls. Your job is to walk in and fetch the people He has already marked."
  - "The most unlikely people you reach will become the ones who build your faith. Evangelism is not about your cleverness or strategy. God knows where the Rahabs are. He will bring them. You just need to be ready to walk through the rubble and say, \"Come.\""

toRemember: "God didn't choose you because you were clean—He chose you, marked you with His red cord, and now sends you to fetch the messy, the unlikely, and the impossible, because that's exactly what He did for you."
