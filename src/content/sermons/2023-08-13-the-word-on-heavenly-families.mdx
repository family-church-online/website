# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God designed family roles to reveal the gospel: we trust His promises first, then obedience becomes possible by His grace."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 6:1-4 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right. \"Honor your father and mother\" (this is the first commandment with a promise), \"that it may go well with you and that you may live long in the land.\" Fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord."

mainPoints:
  - title: Children obey in the Lord because it is right.
    body: Obedience under parental authority is designed to be voluntary and willing, motivated by honour rather than fear.
  - title: The fifth commandment links obedience to a promise.
    body: "\"Honour your father and mother\" is the first human-centred commandment, and it comes with the promise of long life and flourishing — a pattern that points to the gospel."
  - title: The structure reveals the centre.
    body: "Ephesians 6:1-4 is a chiasm: the first and last verses mirror each other (roles of children and parents), the second and second-last mirror each other (commandment and promise), leaving the centre point as the key truth — belief in God's promise enables obedience to His command."
  - title: Parents must discipline without provoking anger.
    body: Discipline in love, not frustration, reflects how God disciplines those He loves; it builds closeness rather than resentment.

keyIllustration: "A marine biologist examines dried algae cells under a microscope and discovers diatoms — intricate, symmetrical silica skeletons more beautiful than architecture, hidden inside something common and overlooked. Just as God places beauty at the heart of the ordinary, Scripture's structure hides the gospel at the centre of everyday family instructions."

application:
  - "Honour your parents not to earn blessing, but because God's promise enables it — even when obedience is difficult."
  - "Discipline your children only after you've dealt with your own anger; godly correction restores relationship."
  - Recognise that when family roles break down, society decays — protecting biblical family structure protects the gospel witness.

toRemember: "Your deeds follow you to heaven — they don't lead you there. Faith in God's promises leads; obedience follows."
