# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God has been preparing opportunities for you for millennia — your job is to receive His word with joy, test it against Scripture daily, and seize what He's placed in front of you today."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 17:11-12 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Now these Jews were more noble than those in Thessalonica; they received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so. Many of them therefore believed, with not a few Greek women of high standing as well as men.

mainPoints:
  - title: "There's no time like the present."
    body: Paul fled Thessalonica by night, arrived in Berea, and went straight to the synagogue — no recovery period, no delay. When God opens a door, the Bereans seized it immediately.
  - title: "God's sovereign preparation runs deeper than we imagine."
    body: "Babylon destroyed the temple and scattered Jews across the known world, planting synagogues everywhere. Alexander Hellenized the region so everyone spoke Greek. Rome built roads and brought peace so the gospel could travel safely. Paul's midnight journey was the culmination of centuries of divine setup."
  - title: Noble reception means enthusiastic testing.
    body: "The Bereans welcomed Paul's teaching with arms wide open — but they didn't stop there. They fact-checked everything against Scripture, daily. Eagerness without discernment is dangerous; discernment without eagerness is lifeless."
  - title: Belief leads to immediate action.
    body: "The Bereans didn't just agree intellectually — they believed, served, and within three chapters were traveling with Paul on mission. When you grab hold of what God has prepared, you don't sit still."

keyIllustration: "Imagine being invited to a braai where your friends have spent hours preparing: tidying the space, setting up music, preparing food, creating a beautiful environment for conversation and celebration. Then imagine walking in, glancing around, and saying, \"There's rugby on — I'll see you guys later,\" and leaving. You'd never do that. Yet how often do we walk into opportunities God has been preparing for centuries and treat them as optional, inconvenient, or something we'll get to later?"

application:
  - Receive teaching with enthusiasm, but test everything against Scripture — not once, but daily.
  - "Recognise that today is not random; it's the culmination of God's sovereign preparation. Ask Him what He's set in front of you right now."
  - When you believe, act immediately. The Bereans moved from belief to service without pause.

toRemember: "God doesn't waste anything — not empires, not exile, not your circumstances. He's been preparing good works for you to walk in. The only question is whether you'll seize them today."
