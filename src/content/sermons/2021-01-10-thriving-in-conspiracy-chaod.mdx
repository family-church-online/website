# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus warned His disciples about wars, false prophets, and chaos — not to fuel anxiety, but to anchor them in His sovereignty so they would be spiritually ready, relationally faithful, and missionally focused when He returns.
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 24:6-8 ESV"
keyScriptureText: See that you are not alarmed, for this must take place, but the end is not yet. For nation will rise against nation and kingdom against kingdom, and there will be famines and earthquakes in various places. All these are but the beginning of the birth pains.

mainPoints:
  - title: Be Astute (vv. 4-5).
    body: "Jesus warns: don't be led astray, and don't be alarmed. Conspiracy theories are like scratching a mosquito bite — the relief is short-lived, and the itch only gets worse. Evaluate everything through Scripture, not speculation."
  - title: "Accept God's Sovereignty (v. 6)."
    body: "\"This must take place.\" Chaos doesn't mean God is out of control. When we stop fighting what is and trust His plan, we move from panic to peace and from obsession to obedience."
  - title: "Hold onto God's Assurances (v. 14)."
    body: "The gospel will be preached to all nations before the end comes. Nothing — not governments, pandemics, or persecution — will stop God's mission. That includes your part in it."
  - title: Be Alert, Not Obsessed (vv. 42-44).
    body: Jesus calls us to watch for His return, but not to become paralyzed by it. Alertness means spiritual readiness — prayer, Scripture, obedience — not endless speculation.
  - title: Apply What Jesus Taught (Matthew 25).
    body: "The parables that follow show us how to live while we wait: stay spiritually prepared (ten virgins), use your gifts faithfully (talents), and serve others tangibly (sheep and goats)."

keyIllustration: "A mosquito bite on the knuckle itches intensely. You know you shouldn't scratch it, but the relief feels irresistible — until the itch gets worse. Conspiracy theories work the same way. They promise clarity and control, but the more you dig, the more anxious and distracted you become. Jesus offers something better: trust in His sovereignty and focus on His mission."

application:
  - Stop scratching. If conspiracy content is feeding your anxiety rather than your faith, step back. Plug into worship, Scripture, and community instead.
  - Stay faithful in the small things. Reach out to a neighbor. Pray with a friend. Study the Bible with your household. These are the works Jesus will reward.
  - "Use your gifts now. Whether it's art, teaching, compassion, or wealth — Jesus will ask what you did with what He gave you. Don't wait for perfect conditions to serve Him."

toRemember: "Jesus didn't reveal the future to fuel our fear — He revealed it to anchor our faith. The only way we truly fail is when we give up on Him."
