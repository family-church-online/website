# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You grow up as a Christian not by learning alone, but by practicing truth and love together with others in the body of Christ—because spiritual maturity requires both knowledge and experience.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 4:15-16 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Rather, speaking the truth in love, we are to grow up in every way into him who is the head, into Christ, from whom the whole body, joined and held together by every joint with which it is equipped, when each part is working properly, makes the body grow so that it builds itself up in love.

mainPoints:
  - title: We are vulnerable to false teaching because our hearts are deceitful.
    body: Scripture warns us that false teachers will secretly bring destructive heresies into the church. Without spiritual maturity and community, we are easily deceived—especially when tired, angry, or isolated.
  - title: Growing up spiritually means becoming more like Jesus—and it requires other people.
    body: You only learn patience when someone irritates you. You only learn to love when loving is hard. A body of individually gifted believers who never practice together cannot function as the church.
  - title: Speaking the truth in love is the key to growing together.
    body: Truth without love is cruelty. Love without truth is hypocrisy. The gospel message—repent, believe, and be baptized—must be spoken with sacrificial care, both to the world and to one another in the church.

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "Check the teaching you're listening to—podcasts, books, videos—against Scripture. If a teacher distorts the gospel, stop listening, no matter how good they sound."
  - "Commit to studying God's Word daily. Spend at least a third of your screen time in Scripture and prayer—you cannot discern lies if you do not know the truth."
  - "Practice speaking truth in love with other believers. Ask how someone really is. Tell them what challenged or convicted you. Offer to pray. Don't isolate—you need the body, and the body needs you."

toRemember: You grow up by growing out—spiritual maturity is forged in community, where truth and love collide and transform us into the likeness of Christ.
