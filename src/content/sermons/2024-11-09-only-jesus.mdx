# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The greater fear drives out the lesser fear — when you fear God more than outcomes, you become unstoppable.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 4:19-20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But Peter and John answered them, \"Whether it is right in the sight of God to listen to you rather than to God, you must judge, for we cannot but speak of what we have seen and heard.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Only Jesus has authority over life and death.
    body: "He laid down His life and took it up again — something no one else in history can claim. The Sadducees denied resurrection because if there's nothing after death, death becomes the ultimate weapon of control."
  - title: Only Jesus takes unremarkable people and does remarkable things.
    body: Peter and John were uneducated, common men. The religious leaders saw they had been with Jesus — and that was the only explanation for their boldness and power.
  - title: Only Jesus is the way to be saved.
    body: "There is no plan B. One God, one mediator, one name under heaven by which we must be saved. This isn't exclusion — it's an open door for anyone who will come."
  - title: The fear of God is the antidote to every other fear.
    body: "Jesus said fear Him who can destroy both body and soul in hell. The fear of God is the full spectrum: awe of His power, terror of His wrath, and dread of ever being separated from Him for a moment."

keyIllustration: "A diver caught in a storm at thirty meters clung to a wreck until his hands bled, terrified it was his last moment — but the fear didn't stop him from diving again, because he loved the ocean. Fear and love aren't opposites. The fear of God works the same way: awe of His power, love of His presence, and dread of separation all at once."

application:
  - "Stop lying awake terrified of what's coming next. Ask yourself: who do I belong to? Who saved me? Who is bigger than every outcome I'm afraid of?"
  - "Don't let anyone tell you to keep Jesus personal and quiet. If you've seen Him, if you've heard Him, you can't unsee or unhear it — and you were never meant to."
  - "When the world tries to terrorize you, remember: they can only kill the body. After that, you're in the presence of Jesus forever. That's all they've got."

toRemember: There is one and only one Jesus — and nothing in all creation, visible or invisible, angel or demon, can ever separate you from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus.
