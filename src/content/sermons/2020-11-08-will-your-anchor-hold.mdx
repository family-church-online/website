# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Your faith is not anchored in the storm around you — it is anchored in the throne room of God, where Jesus stands interceding on your behalf, holding you firm no matter what rages in the world.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 6:19-20 NIV"
keyScriptureText: We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure. It enters the inner sanctuary behind the curtain, where our forerunner, Jesus, has entered on our behalf.

mainPoints:
  - title: "God's promises stand."
    body: He swore by Himself because there is no one greater — it is impossible for God to lie. His word to Abraham came to pass, and His word to you will too.
  - title: We have an anchor for our soul.
    body: An anchor keeps a ship from drifting, holds it steady in the storm, and pulls it to safety when grounded. Your faith does the same — because it is anchored in heaven.
  - title: Jesus has entered the inner sanctuary on our behalf.
    body: The curtain in the Holy of Holies was torn from top to bottom when Jesus died — now your anchor is secured in the very presence of God, where Jesus intercedes for you.

keyIllustration: "A burned child in a hospital lay in agony until a teacher arrived saying the school had sent her to teach him nouns and adverbs. Suddenly, he began to eat and recover. When asked why, he said: \"If they sent a teacher, I must not be dying.\" Hope — even a small word — changes everything."

application:
  - When promises are broken around you, cling to the One whose promises never fail.
  - "Your job is not to stop the storm — it's to hold on to the anchor that cannot be moved."
  - Death is not the end — to be absent from the body is to be present with the Lord. This life is the dot; eternity is the line that never stops.

toRemember: Build your life on the promises of God — since His promises are unbreakable, your hope will be unshakable.
