# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Jesus didn't avoid suffering or wait for it to end before continuing His mission — He embraced it as the very path through which God's glory would be revealed and our salvation accomplished."
keyScriptureRef: "2 Corinthians 4:17-18 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For this light momentary affliction is preparing for us an eternal weight of glory beyond all comparison, as we look not to the things that are seen but to the things that are unseen. For the things that are seen are transient, but the things that are unseen are eternal.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus chose suffering throughout His entire life, not just at the cross.
    body: "From Herod's infanticide to abandonment by His family to constant rejection by religious leaders, His life was marked by intentional, prophesied suffering that He willingly embraced."
  - title: "Palm Sunday didn't bring relief — it escalated the conflict."
    body: "When Jesus finally revealed His identity publicly, He didn't withdraw into comfort; He immediately confronted the religious leaders in the temple, knowing it would lead to His death within days."
  - title: "Jesus' compassion kept Him fighting when human logic said quit."
    body: He wept over Jerusalem not because of wounded pride, but because He grieved the consequences they would face for rejecting Him — and He continued His mission anyway.
  - title: We waste suffering when we wait for it to end before obeying God.
    body: If Jesus had perfect obedience and still faced continuous hardship, why do we expect our lives to be different? The worst thing we can do with suffering is let it stop us from doing what God has called us to do.

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "Stop waiting for your circumstances to improve before you serve God — chances are, there's another trial around the corner, and you'll never do what He made you for."
  - Pray not for the removal of suffering, but for the preservation of your faith and the continuation of your obedience in the middle of it.
  - Trust that God pours His treasure into cracked jars of clay precisely to show that the power belongs to Him, not us.

toRemember: "Whatever you're facing right now, it's transient and temporary — but what God brings from it is eternal, with a weight of glory you cannot even comprehend."
