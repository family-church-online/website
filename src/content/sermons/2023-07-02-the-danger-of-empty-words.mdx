# ── PRESENTATION COPY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
tagLine: Discontentment is the first step toward moral destruction. Gratitude is the way back.

Gratitude is the antidote—and the path back to God."

hook: "Why does God forbid coveting when every other commandment deals with actions? # ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Empty words promise that sexual sin, greed, and discontentment are harmless, but they lead to destruction and the wrath of God—gratitude is the antidote that anchors us in His provision and keeps us from deception.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 5:6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience.

mainPoints:
  - title: Empty words deceive us about the seriousness of sin
    body: "The culture bombards us with messages that sexual immorality, covetousness, and impurity are normal or even praiseworthy, but these empty words mask a deadly trajectory that ends in God's wrath."
  - title: Discontentment is the first step toward destruction
    body: "Romans 1 reveals the pattern: refusing to thank God leads to futile thinking, idolatry, and eventual moral collapse—sexual sin and greed are not isolated behaviors but symptoms of a heart that has turned from God."
  - title: Gratitude is the antidote to covetousness
    body: "Thanksgiving anchors us in God's provision and goodness; when we daily focus on what we have rather than what we lack, we cut off the root of discontentment that feeds idolatry and immorality."
  - title: Christ offers rescue from the wrath we deserve
    body: "The gospel does not minimize sin's danger but provides the only escape—through faith in Jesus, we are reborn as children of God, filled with the Holy Spirit, and kept safe from judgment."

keyIllustration: "A child tears open a large, foil-wrapped Easter egg, expecting it to be solid chocolate, only to discover it's hollow—beautiful and promising on the outside but empty inside. This is how empty words work: they promise fulfillment, freedom, and satisfaction, but lead only to disappointment and destruction."

application:
  - Wake up each day and consciously thank God for what you have—interrupt the cycle of discontentment before it takes root.
  - Identify where culture is lying to you about sexuality, ambition, or contentment, and actively reject those empty words with Scripture.
  - "If you're struggling with sexual sin or addiction, bring it into the light—confess it to a trusted believer and seek help before it destroys you."

toRemember: "Thank You, Lord—these two words, spoken daily with sincerity, are your shield against the deception that begins with wanting what you don't have and ends in destruction."
