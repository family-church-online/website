# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You were saved not just for forgiveness, but for holiness — and the moment you belong to Christ, you've already left this world in your heart, even if your feet are still planted here."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Thessalonians 4:3-5"
keyScriptureText: "For this is the will of God, your sanctification: that you abstain from sexual immorality; that each one of you know how to control his own body in holiness and honor, not in the passion of lust like the Gentiles who do not know God."

mainPoints:
  - title: God chose you to be holy.
    body: Before the foundation of the world, He determined that His people would be sanctified — set apart, made pure, and prepared to stand blameless before Him.
  - title: Sexual purity is a picture of covenant faithfulness.
    body: "Marriage was designed by God to reflect the relationship between Christ and the church, which is why sexual immorality is so serious — it's a lived contradiction of the gospel."
  - title: Holiness requires both patience and urgency.
    body: God is the one who sanctifies you, so you must be patient. But Jesus is coming back, so you must also fight sin now and flee temptation actively.
  - title: Knowing God protects you from immorality.
    body: The deeper your relationship with God, the less power temptation has over you, because intimacy with Him satisfies what loneliness and lust cannot.

keyIllustration: "A man takes a new job and gives notice at his old one. The moment he resigns, everything changes — even though he still shows up for a month. His heart has already left. His employer knows it, and sometimes asks him to leave early because he's no longer really there. In the same way, the instant you are saved, you leave this world. Your fears, ambitions, and hopes change. You are already gone, even though your body is still here."

application:
  - "Proactively flee from what tempts you — whether it's a route you drive, a show you watch, a person you speak to, or a thought pattern you entertain."
  - Grow your relationship with God by serving Him and watching Him work, because you know Him most deeply when you see Him move.
  - "Remember that anything outside of God's design for marriage — including lust, pornography, and fantasies — is sexual immorality, and God will hold you accountable."

toRemember: Human history is the long, terrible story of man trying to find something other than God to make him happy. A holy person gets rid of the other things and is satisfied in God.
