# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The gospel is not just a policy that kicks in when you die—it is the only source of the love, joy, and peace you desperately need right now, and until you draw those from Christ alone, you will keep turning to false substitutes that leave you empty.
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 5:16-17 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh. For the desires of the flesh are against the Spirit, and the desires of the Spirit are against the flesh, for these are opposed to each other, to keep you from doing the things you want to do.

mainPoints:
  - title: You were saved for something, not just from something.
    body: God did not rescue you simply to keep you out of hell—He saved you to glorify Him, to love others, and to proclaim His excellencies to a broken world.
  - title: Every human action is a pursuit of love, joy, or peace.
    body: The works of the flesh—sexual immorality, anger, envy, addiction—are all attempts to find love, joy, or peace apart from God, and they will always fail.
  - title: The fruit of the Spirit is not behavior—it is supply.
    body: Love, joy, and peace are not things you produce; they are realities you receive from God through daily relationship with Him, and patience, kindness, and self-control flow naturally from that supply.
  - title: Walking by the Spirit requires honest self-examination and daily dependence.
    body: You must look honestly at the brokenness inside you, confess your desperate need for God, and choose to rejoice in your salvation, pray with thanksgiving, and meditate on what is true, honorable, and pure.

keyIllustration: "A person who has been given a perfectly cooked gourmet hamburger with all their favorite ingredients and condiments will begin to salivate just hearing it described in detail—the body responds to what the mind dwells on. In the same way, when you focus on the brokenness of the world, you feel broken; but when you meditate deeply on the truth of Christ's love and your salvation, your mind, emotions, and actions begin to change."

application:
  - Stop separating salvation from daily life—invite Jesus into every unmet need, every fear, every hunger, and ask Him to be the source of love, joy, and peace you are chasing.
  - Identify where you are pursuing love, joy, or peace in counterfeit forms—substances, approval, control, comfort—and confess them as idolatry.
  - "Practice Philippians 4:4-9 daily: choose to rejoice in your salvation, bring every anxiety to God in prayer with thanksgiving, and fix your mind on what is true, honorable, just, pure, lovely, and commendable."

toRemember: Jesus is enough—but you will only experience His sufficiency when you invest daily in your relationship with Him, believing that He alone can satisfy the deepest hunger of your heart.
