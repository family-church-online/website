# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Your faith doesn't save your family, but it does create a covering of prayer, witness, and blessing that sets them apart until they can choose Christ for themselves."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Corinthians 7:14 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For the unbelieving husband is made holy because of his wife, and the unbelieving wife is made holy because of her husband. Otherwise your children would be unclean, but as it is, they are holy.

mainPoints:
  - title: "\"Made holy\" means set apart, not saved."
    body: "The word sanctified in this passage doesn't mean the unbeliever is saved by someone else's faith—it means they're set apart for special attention, placed under a covering of prayer and witness."
  - title: Your children are under your umbrella.
    body: "Children in a believing home are \"holy\" because they watch you pray, study Scripture, deal with conflict biblically, and bless them—they have a front-row seat to what Jesus does in a life."
  - title: Baptism is obedience, not salvation.
    body: "Scripture teaches we are saved by faith alone—the thief on the cross was never baptized. Baptism is a public declaration: \"I have died with Christ and been raised to new life.\""
  - title: We cover others until they can stand on their own.
    body: Just as a stranger can share an umbrella in the rain, believers extend spiritual covering—prayer, witness, blessing—until the unbeliever is ready to choose Jesus for themselves.

keyIllustration: "A man waits seven hours in the rain at Home Affairs, standing in a queue that wraps around the parking lot. A stranger in front of him notices he has no umbrella and invites him to share his. Two completely different people—but one extends his covering to protect the other. That's the picture of what a believer does for an unbeliever in their home: you don't carry them, but you shelter them under the covering of your faith until they're ready to hold their own umbrella."

application:
  - Pray daily for your children and unbelieving spouse—your prayers are powerful and effective because you stand in right relationship with God.
  - "Let your family see you follow Jesus: study Scripture in front of them, deal with conflict biblically, bless them, and speak openly about what God is doing in your life."
  - "Don't rely on infant baptism or any ritual to secure your child's salvation—your first task as a parent is to make disciples of your children by inviting them under your covering until they can choose Jesus for themselves."

toRemember: "You can't save anyone—but you can cover them in prayer, witness, and love until they're ready to say yes to Jesus."
