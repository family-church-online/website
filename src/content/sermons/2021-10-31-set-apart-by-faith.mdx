  - title: "\"Made holy\" means set apart, not saved."

  - title: Baptism is obedience, not salvation.
    body: "Scripture teaches we are saved by faith alone—the thief on the cross was never baptized. Baptism is a public declaration: \"I have died with Christ and been raised to new life.\""
  - title: We cover others until they can stand on their own.
    body: Just as a stranger can share an umbrella in the rain, believers extend spiritual covering—prayer, witness, blessing—until the unbeliever is ready to choose Jesus for themselves.
