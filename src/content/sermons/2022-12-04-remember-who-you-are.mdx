# ── PRESENTATION COPY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
tagLine: "You're not saved because you work for God—you work because God already saved you."
shortDescription: "You know you're saved by grace, not works—but what if you've got it backwards? Salvation isn't the reward for doing good; doing good is what you're saved for. When you remember who God is and what He's done, you stop earning approval and start living out the inheritance He prepared before the world began."
subtitle: We are saved by grace for good works—not the other way around.
hook: "Why does the idea of grace—unearned, undeserved favour—still feel so unfair? If salvation isn't something we work for, what's the point of obedience, service, or trying to live right? This sermon reorders the relationship between grace and works: you're not saved because you serve God; you serve because God already saved you."

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "We are saved by God's grace through faith—not by works—so that we can do the good works God prepared for us before the foundation of the world."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 2:8-10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast. For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them.

mainPoints:
  - title: Salvation is a gift—not a wage.
    body: "Grace is God giving us what we don't deserve, salvation is the gift, and faith is how we receive it—but none of it is earned."
  - title: We are saved for good works, not by good works.
    body: "God didn't save you so you could sit idle; He saved you so you could live out the works He already planned for you."
  - title: Remembering who God is changes everything.
    body: "Just as Israel had to remember God's faithfulness to receive their inheritance, we must remember His grace to live out ours."

keyIllustration: "In Deuteronomy, Moses repeats the command to \"remember God\" fifteen times. Before Israel entered the Promised Land, God reminded them of slavery in Egypt, the parting of the Red Sea, the pillar of cloud and fire—everything He'd done. They couldn't receive their inheritance without remembering who they were. The same is true for us: we cannot walk in the works God prepared if we forget who He is and what He's done."

application:
  - "Stop trying to earn what's already been given—your salvation is secure in Christ."
  - "If you resent others who seem to \"get away with\" less effort, you've misunderstood grace."
  - "Good works aren't random acts of kindness—they're a way of life in your home, work, and relationships."

toRemember: He who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Christ Jesus—your faith may waver, but His faithfulness does not.
