# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God assembled millions in Jerusalem not just to witness the arrival of the Messiah, but to set the stage for the cross—and ultimately, for His return, when an innumerable multitude will worship Him forever.
keyScriptureRef: "Revelation 7:9-10"
keyScriptureText: "After this I looked, and behold, a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb, clothed in white robes, with palm branches in their hands, and crying out with a loud voice, \"Salvation belongs to our God who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb!\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "God's timing is perfect."
    body: Jesus had twelve hours in a day—no one could cut His mission short at eleven fifty-three. He would complete His work exactly when the Father ordained it, not when the Pharisees decided.
  - title: God orchestrates history down to the smallest detail.
    body: "Millions gathered at Passover, witnesses spread the news of Lazarus, the crowd knew Psalm 118 and Zechariah 9:9 by heart—all converging at the exact moment to proclaim, \"Hosanna! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!\""
  - title: Palm Sunday was the announcement, not the climax.
    body: "The crowd wasn't just there to welcome Jesus—they were there to witness the cross, the greatest act of love in history, and to become witnesses who would proclaim His death until He returns."
  - title: "You get to choose which crowd you're in."
    body: "The disciples who didn't understand but kept following. The witnesses who faithfully told what they'd seen. The curious who wanted to see for themselves. Or the Pharisees who saw the miracles but chose their own power instead."

keyIllustration: "Historical records show that at one particular Passover, 600,000 lambs were slaughtered in Jerusalem. With families sharing each lamb, that means four to six million people crammed into the city. When word spread that the man who raised Lazarus from the dead had arrived, a massive wave of humanity flooded the streets, all chanting the same Messianic cry from Psalm 118. This wasn't a small parade—it was the largest stage in history, set for the greatest act of love the world would ever see."

application:
  - "When life is a roller coaster and you don't understand what God is doing, keep going. The disciples didn't get it at the time either, but when Jesus was glorified, they remembered—and their faith was strengthened."
  - "Your story matters. The witnesses who saw Lazarus raised didn't think they'd influence millions, but by faithfully sharing what they'd seen, they set the stage for the Triumphal Entry."
  - "Don't be a curious bystander. Come to the Lord's table not out of cultural habit, but because the gravity of what He's done has blown you away—and you're ready to proclaim His death until He returns."

toRemember: "Palm Sunday was huge—but there's something bigger coming, and you're invited to be part of the crowd no one can number, standing before the throne with palm branches in your hands, crying, \"Salvation belongs to our God and to the Lamb!\""
