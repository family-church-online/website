# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You can recite Psalm 23, but only those who know Jesus as the good shepherd — who restores wandering souls and conquers death — will dwell in the house of the Lord forever.
keyScriptureRef: "John 10:11"
keyScriptureText: I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd gives His life for the sheep.

mainPoints:
  - title: The shepherd provides and protects.
    body: "David knew God as \"my shepherd\" — the One who fed him in green pastures, refreshed him by still waters, and gave him everything he needed. Like Anatolian shepherd dogs bred to bond with and guard sheep, God protects His own."
  - title: The shepherd restores wandering souls.
    body: "Sheep wander. So do we. Restoration comes through repentance — stopping your own way and returning to the shepherd — and faith in Christ's death and resurrection. Only He can make you right with God again."
  - title: The shepherd leads through death.
    body: "Christ is the only one who journeyed through death and came out victorious. If you belong to Him, He will personally lead you home. You won't fear death if you know the shepherd, because He knows the way."

keyIllustration: A climber ascending Mount Everest or Kilimanjaro depends entirely on a Sherpa — someone who knows the route, has walked it many times, and can lead through sudden weather changes. In the same way, believers need Christ to guide them through the valley of the shadow of death. He is the only One who has conquered death and returned. He knows the way home.

application:
  - Ask Christ to restore your soul through repentance and faith — turn from going your own way and believe that He died for your sins and rose to give you new life.
  - If death frightens you, make the journey with the shepherd. He will take you home. Your assurance comes from knowing Him, not just knowing the psalm.

toRemember: "The promise of God's provision, sins forgiven, a constant companion, and a home in heaven only applies to those who know the good shepherd. Do you know Him?"
