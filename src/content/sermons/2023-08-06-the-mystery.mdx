# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Marriage was designed before sin to reveal the gospel — Christ leaving the Father, becoming flesh, giving His life to create His bride, the church.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 5:31-32 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Therefore a man shall leave his father and mother and hold fast to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh. This mystery is profound, and I am saying that it refers to Christ and the church.

mainPoints:
  - title: Marriage is to take precedence.
    body: Your spouse comes before your parents, before your children, before every other human relationship — you are one flesh with no one else.
  - title: Marriage is to be permanent.
    body: God Himself joins husband and wife; what He has joined, no one should separate. Marriage is exclusive and lifelong.
  - title: Marriage is profound.
    body: "This mystery was hinted at in Genesis 2 and revealed in the New Testament: marriage pictures Christ and the church."
  - title: Marriage was prepared in advance.
    body: God instituted marriage in Genesis 2, before the fall, to show His rescue plan before humanity even knew they needed rescuing.

keyIllustration: "A man survived a catastrophic car crash and endured years of reconstructive surgery. Every time surgeons needed bone for grafting, they harvested it from his ribs. When he finally asked why they always used his ribs, the doctor explained: the rib is the only bone in the body surrounded by a membrane so rich in stem cells that when the bone is removed and the membrane is closed, the rib regenerates. Thousands of years before medicine understood this, Genesis 2 records that God took a rib from Adam to create Eve — and then closed up the flesh. Adam gave a rib to give life to Eve; Christ gave His life to give life to the church. And when they pierced His side, blood and water flowed out."

application:
  - "Husbands: love your wife sacrificially as Christ loved the church — lay your life down for her sanctification and wellbeing."
  - "Wives: respect and honour your husband, especially when it's difficult, out of reverence for Christ."
  - When you live out biblical marriage — even imperfectly, in the power of the Holy Spirit — you become a living picture of the gospel to a watching world.

toRemember: God prepared the table before you knew you needed it — marriage reveals a Saviour who loved you before you even knew His name.
