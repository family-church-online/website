# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's priority for you is not your comfort, health, or happiness—it's your holiness, because without holiness you will not see the Lord when Christ returns."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Thessalonians 3:12-13 ESV"
keyScriptureText: May the Lord make you increase and abound in love for one another and for all, as we do for you, so that he may establish your hearts blameless in holiness before our God and Father at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all of his saints.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Paul's passion is the church's holiness, not their comfort."
    body: "When Timothy brought good news that the Thessalonians' faith was holding strong despite persecution, Paul said, \"Now we live if you are standing fast in the Lord.\" He didn't pray for their protection or ease—he prayed for their faith, love, and holiness."
  - title: Holiness means being set apart, not fitting in.
    body: "To be holy is to be single-minded, dedicated, and separate from the world—devoted entirely to God's purposes. Christians are called to be \"weird\" in a culture that pressures everyone to blend in."
  - title: False teachers make sin comfortable.
    body: "The church is full of voices that preach comfort, prosperity, and happiness as proof of faith. But Scripture is clear: God's will is your holiness, not your wealth or ease. Sensuality, materialism, and worldliness are repackaged as acceptable when they should be cut away."
  - title: "Without holiness, we won't see the Lord."
    body: "Hebrews 12:14 says to \"strive for the holiness without which no one will see the Lord.\" Jesus is returning with His holy ones—we need to learn to fit in with them, not the world."

keyIllustration: "A family on holiday walked into a local pub wearing a Bulls rugby jersey—in the heart of Eastern Cape farming country, where rival team loyalty runs deep. The room went silent. The temptation was to go back to the car and change shirts to avoid conflict. But you don't have to betray who you support just to fit in. In the same way, Christians are called to stand out, not blend in—even when it's uncomfortable."

application:
  - Pray differently for yourself and others—prioritise holiness, faith, and love over comfort, health, and prosperity.
  - "Don't pursue happiness at the expense of holiness. God may bless you with prosperity or allow you to suffer—both are tools to make you holy."
  - "Submit to God's refining work, even when it hurts. Like a gangrenous finger that must be cut off by a doctor, we can't make ourselves holy—but we must stay still and let Him do the surgery."

toRemember: We are not here to be happy, wealthy, and healthy—we are here to be holy, set apart, and ready for the day Jesus returns.
