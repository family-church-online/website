# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Living faith twenty-four-seven means stopping to trust, refusing to lean on your own understanding, and acknowledging God—every time you face a decision, a delight, or a disaster.
keyScriptureRef: "Proverbs 3:5–6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Three triggers: decisions, delights, disasters."
    body: "Whenever any of these three things happen, stop and think of the three words from Proverbs—trust, don't lean, acknowledge."
  - title: Acknowledge first, then trust.
    body: "Before you react, phone anyone, or make a plan, acknowledge God's sovereignty and thank Him. Then ask for His help and refuse to lean on your own reasoning."
  - title: Faith is caught, not taught.
    body: The best way to transfer faith to children is to model it—stopping in real time to pray, trust, and give thanks in front of them.

keyIllustration: "A last-minute venue change for Friday evening youth threatened to derail the entire event. The natural response was frustration and insistence on keeping the original space. Instead, acknowledging God's sovereignty and trusting Him opened the door to a much better venue—the multipurpose hall the team had always wanted but could never book. What looked like a disaster became a delight because of one simple pause to trust and acknowledge."

application:
  - "Memorise Proverbs 3:5–6 and rehearse it every time you face a decision, receive good news, or encounter a setback."
  - Train yourself to stop and pray before reacting—even in small, everyday moments like choosing what to eat or responding to a rude driver.
  - Model trust in front of your children by praying aloud when delights or disasters happen, so they see faith in action.

toRemember: "Without realising it, you've already memorised this verse—now keep applying it until trusting God becomes your first instinct, not your last resort."
