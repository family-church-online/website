mainPoints:
  - title: The Servant is first Israel, then Jesus.
    body: "Isaiah calls Israel God's servant, chosen to bless the nations — but when Israel fails, the prophecy shifts to One born from Israel who will bring them back and become a light to every nation."
  - title: "If the Servant is Jesus, Scripture is God's supernatural word."
    body: "Isaiah's precision — written 700 years before the cross — proves these are not human guesses but God speaking through men carried along by the Holy Spirit."
  - title: Jesus took our place.
    body: He was pierced for our transgressions, crushed for our iniquities. The punishment that brought us peace fell on Him. His wounds healed us. This is substitutionary atonement — God laid our sin on Him so we could receive His righteousness.
  - title: All are saved by faith — Jew and Gentile alike.
    body: "Israel's rejection opened the door for the Gentiles. When the Church is complete, Israel will be grafted back in. God's gifts and calling are irrevocable, and His plan is to have mercy on all."
