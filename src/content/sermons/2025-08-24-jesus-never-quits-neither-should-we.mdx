mainPoints:
  - title: Discipleship requires follow-through.
    body: "Paul and Barnabas returned to every city they'd preached in—even where they'd been stoned—because making disciples means more than a one-time conversation. It means teaching, guarding, and walking with people as they grow."
  - title: "Failure doesn't disqualify you; quitting on grace does."
    body: "Paul refused to take John Mark because he'd abandoned the mission in Pamphylia. Barnabas insisted on giving him a second chance. Their sharp disagreement split them apart—but doubled the mission field and sent John Mark back to the very place he'd quit."
  - title: "Jesus knows what it's like to want to quit."
    body: "In Gethsemane, Jesus was \"sorrowful even to death\" and begged the Father to let the cup pass from Him. He understands your temptation to walk away—and He didn't quit, so His grace won't let you either."
  - title: Grace turns failure into fuel.
    body: "Peter denied Jesus three times, yet Jesus prayed for him beforehand and restored him. Peter later called John Mark \"my son\" and likely poured that same grace into him. John Mark went from rejected assistant to the author of the Gospel of Mark, strengthening believers for two thousand years."

keyIllustration: "A boy was taken to elite cricket coaching after struggling at school. Unprepared for the intensity, he was running hills, doing push-ups, and being shouted at—a far cry from the \"everyone's a winner\" atmosphere he was used to. Halfway across the field after practice, he said, \"I'm not coming back to that one.\" His father explained that the coach wasn't punishing him—he was growing him. That night, when asked what happened, the boy said simply, \"I grew.\" The times you hurt most are often the times you grow most."

application:
  - "Stop trying to serve God in your own strength. Confess where you're out of your depth, and surrender it to Him."
  - "If you've quit before, grace gives you another chance. Get back on the horse. Jesus hasn't quit on you."
  - "When you're ready to walk away, remember Gethsemane: Jesus chose the cross so you could choose to keep going."
