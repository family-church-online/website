mainPoints:
  - title: Draw near in faith.
    body: "Jesus' blood tore down the barrier between you and God. Guilt and shame no longer have to hold you at a distance. Come with full assurance that you are washed clean and welcomed into His presence."
  - title: Hold fast in hope.
    body: Faith looks back at what Jesus has done; hope looks forward to His promises. Let that hope energise you daily as you wait for eternal life, resurrection, and a new heaven and earth.
  - title: Stir up one another in love.
    body: Faith and hope are not ends in themselves—they move you to action. Meet with other believers not just to be encouraged, but to encourage them toward love and good works.

keyIllustration: "A compound bow needs the arrow loaded (faith), the string drawn back (hope), and the release (love). Faith places trust in God's character; hope builds energy from His promises; love pulls the shot off by serving others. All three work together—missing any one leaves you powerless."

application:
  - Ask the Holy Spirit to separate your view of your earthly father from your understanding of the Heavenly Father—God does not fail, forget, or abandon you.
  - Draw closer to God than you have ever drawn to anyone. He wants more intimacy with you than you have yet experienced.
  - "When you gather with believers—in church, in a small group, over a meal—shift your mindset from \"What will I get?\" to \"Who can I stir up to love and good works?\""

toRemember: Faith, hope, and love endure forever—even in eternity, your trust in God and your hope in His goodness will only grow deeper as He reveals more of Himself to you.
