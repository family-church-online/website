# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God has written a story for history and for your life, and no interruption, suffering, or enemy can edit it. Your identity is not found in what happens to you, but in whose you are — and that cannot be taken away.
keyScriptureRef: "1 Corinthians 10:13 ESV"
keyScriptureText: No temptation has overtaken you that is not common to man. God is faithful, and He will not let you be tempted beyond your ability, but with the temptation He will also provide the way of escape, that you may be able to endure it.

mainPoints:
  - title: The world uses suffering to erase your identity.
    body: "Babylon's strategy was deliberate: siege, starvation, kidnapping, re-education, diet control, and renaming. Daniel lost his home, his freedom, his culture, and even his name. The goal was to break him and rebuild him as a tool of the empire. This is still happening today — culturally, politically, spiritually."
  - title: Every dilemma has a third way when you trust God.
    body: "Daniel faced a brutal choice: eat the king's food (which involved pagan worship) or die. But when he prayed, God revealed a third option — vegetables and water. The eunuch agreed to a test, and God made Daniel healthier than everyone else. What looks like a rock and a hard place is never the end of the options when God is involved."
  - title: "God's story cannot be interrupted."
    body: "Seventy years later, Daniel is still praying, still faithful. God sends Gabriel to reveal the future — four world empires will rise and fall exactly as predicted, and a final kingdom (the kingdom of Christ) will crush them all and fill the earth. This is not metaphor. This is God's fixed, unshakable plan for history."

keyIllustration: "Jen Bricker was born without legs to Romanian Olympic gymnast parents who gave her up for adoption because they could not accept her disability. Adopted by a Christian family, she was raised with the identity that she was fully capable and deeply loved by God. She became a world-renowned acrobat and tumbling champion — competing without legs. She later discovered her biological sister was Dominique Moceanu, an Olympic gold medalist she had idolized as a child. Jen's story illustrates that your identity is not determined by what you lack or what was done to you, but by whose you are."

application:
  - When you face pressure to compromise your faith for survival, security, or acceptance, cry out to God. He promises a way of escape from every temptation to sin.
  - Do not let circumstances, loss, or suffering redefine who you are. You are defined by Christ alone, and that identity cannot be stripped away.
  - "Trust that God's story for history — and for your life — is unshakable. Interruptions are not edits. They are acts in the same story, authored by Him."

toRemember: "You are God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which He prepared beforehand that you should walk in them."
