# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Empty religion looks godly on the outside but lacks the power to transform because it is built on misplaced love—love for self, money, and pleasure instead of love for God.
keyScriptureRef: "2 Timothy 3:1-5 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But understand this, that in the last days there will come times of difficulty. For people will be lovers of self, lovers of money, proud, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy, heartless, unappeasable, slanderous, without self-control, brutal, not loving good, treacherous, reckless, swollen with conceit, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God, having the appearance of godliness but denying its power. Avoid such people.

mainPoints:
  - title: "The problem: misplaced love."
    body: Humanity was created in union with God, but the fall broke that union and left a void we try to fill with egotism, materialism, and hedonism instead of with God.
  - title: "The danger: false teachers."
    body: "Teachers who appear godly but deny God's power prey on people desperate for salvation, leading them astray with messages that lack truth and credibility."
  - title: "The solution: love God above all."
    body: "Jesus commands us to love God with all our heart, soul, and mind—when we do, we can't be led astray by the things of this world."
  - title: "The response: seek Jesus and verify sources."
    body: "Spend time in Scripture, validate what you're learning against God's Word, and share the truth as you discover it."

keyIllustration: "A photographer arrived at an airport to document California wildfires and jumped into a small Cessna, assuming the pilot was ready. Mid-flight, the photographer asked the pilot to make several low passes over the fires. The pilot responded, \"Why?\" The photographer said, \"I'm a photographer—I'm here to take pictures.\" After a long pause, the pilot asked, \"You mean you're not the instructor?\" Both men had placed their trust in someone who couldn't deliver what they needed. Air traffic control guided them safely back, but when we place our trust in sources that lack credibility—talk show hosts, prosperity preachers, our own feelings—we risk destruction instead of safety."

application:
  - "Check Paul's list in 2 Timothy 3:2-4 against your own life—where are you placing your love? When you love God first, He directs your love for yourself, possessions, and pleasure into healthy expressions."
  - "If you're angry at God or struggling to pray, start the conversation anyway. Let Him know how you feel. Job began with \"Why?\" and through conversation with God, arrived at \"Who is God?\""
  - "Read the Bible consistently, verify the credibility of teachers and sources you're learning from, and share the truth as you discover it with others who need it."

toRemember: Our closeness to God is never close enough, and our work on this earth never ends until we are called to be with our Father.
