# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You cannot build a relationship with God. You can only respond to the God who has already chosen to build a relationship with you—and that response begins with being alone with Him.
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 1:15-17 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But when he who had set me apart before I was born, and who called me by his grace, was pleased to reveal his Son to me, in order that I might preach him among the Gentiles, I did not immediately consult with anyone, nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away into Arabia.

mainPoints:
  - title: God initiates everything.
    body: "He set you apart before birth, called you by grace, and revealed His Son to you. You didn't find Him—He found you. Every step of your salvation is His work, not yours."
  - title: You are saved for a purpose, not just from something.
    body: "God didn't rescue you so you could coast. He set you apart for His glory, to bring hope and new life to others. Knowing your purpose changes how you wake up every morning."
  - title: Solitude with God is non-negotiable.
    body: Paul went away to Arabia. Jesus prayed all night. Peter was alone on the roof. Abraham, Elijah, John—every disciple needed one-on-one time with the Father. You need it too.
  - title: Your calling mirrors the twelve apostles.
    body: "They were set apart, called, trained, and sent. So are you. The only difference is you don't write Scripture—but you carry the same Spirit and the same authority to live powerfully for His kingdom."

keyIllustration: "A cricket coach watches a five-year-old stumble onto the field in oversized gear, dragging a bat twice their size. The coach sees something—potential—and sets that child apart. Over the years, that kid gets extra attention, harder training, more focus. Setting apart isn't passive. It's intentional, active preparation for a purpose. That's what God does with you."

application:
  - "Carve out time to be alone with God—no distractions, no devices, just you and Him. If you don't know where to start, just show up. He does the rest."
  - "Stop waiting to feel qualified. God doesn't call the equipped; He equips the called. You are already set apart. Live like it."
  - "Take responsibility for your own relationship with God. Church, home groups, and pastors can't live it for you. You must respond to what He's already doing in your life."

toRemember: "There is only one thing standing between you and the same calling as Peter, Paul, or Mary—your response. What's stopping you?"
