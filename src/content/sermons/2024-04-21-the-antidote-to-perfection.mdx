# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You cannot earn God's grace through rule-keeping or spiritual effort — you receive it by faith, and that faith transforms you from the inside out."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 3:6-7 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Just as Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness. Know then that it is those of faith who are the sons of Abraham.

mainPoints:
  - title: Abraham was made righteous by faith, not works.
    body: God declared Abraham righteous when he believed the promise of countless descendants — five hundred years before the law was given to Moses. Faith, not obedience, is what counts us as children of Abraham.
  - title: "If you rely on works, you're held to a perfect standard."
    body: "God's standard is zero or one hundred — no middle ground. Perfection means never hurting anyone, never wanting anyone hurt, loving and forgiving instantly, giving without expectation. None of us can meet that standard."
  - title: Christ became the curse so we could be free.
    body: Jesus took the curse of the law — hung on a tree, as Deuteronomy foretold — so that the blessing of Abraham (freedom by faith) could come to all who believe. The law was temporary; His sacrifice is final.
  - title: "Grace produces good works — it doesn't require them first."
    body: "When you receive grace by faith, something changes inside you. You stop working to earn God's approval and start working because you've already received it. The order matters: faith first, works follow."

keyIllustration: "A group of rebellious high school boys at a discipleship camp trashed their dorm room — mattresses on the floor, soap graffiti on mirrors, wet towels in the corner. Instead of punishing them, two camp leaders cleaned the room spotlessly and announced it as the day's winner. The boys, expecting consequences, were floored. They found the leaders, apologised, and were so moved by that unearned grace that they won the contest every day for the rest of the week. Grace didn't excuse rebellion — it transformed it."

application:
  - Stop measuring your standing with God by your performance. You are already counted righteous through faith in Christ — nothing you do can add to or subtract from that.
  - "Reject the lie that stronger faith fixes all problems. God does not withhold healing, provision, or breakthrough because you haven't believed hard enough. His grace is not a reward for faith — it's a gift received by faith."
  - "Let grace free you from the pressure to be perfect. You are free to fail in a world that won't allow it, free from endless comparison, free to rest because Jesus already won."

toRemember: "When you stop trying to earn what's already been given, you're finally free to become the person God created you to be."
