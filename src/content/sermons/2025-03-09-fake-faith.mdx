# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Believing in Jesus doesn't save you — trusting Him as your only hope does. Simon believed and was baptized, yet Peter told him his heart wasn't right before God. The thief on the cross shows us what saving faith actually looks like: confessing we deserve God's wrath, recognizing Jesus as our sinless substitute, and asking Him to remember us — not for what we can get, but because we have nowhere else to go."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 8:20-23 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But Peter said to him, \"May your silver perish with you, because you thought you could obtain the gift of God with money! You have neither part nor lot in this matter, for your heart is not right before God. Repent, therefore, of this wickedness of yours, and pray to the Lord that, if possible, the intent of your heart may be forgiven you. mainPoints:
  - title: Not all belief is saving belief.
    body: Simon believed, was baptized, and followed Philip — but when he tried to buy spiritual power, Peter exposed his heart. He wanted Jesus to give him what he wanted, not to surrender to what Jesus wanted. Even demons believe in God and tremble; saving faith requires repentance and trust.
  - title: Scripture commands us to test our faith.
    body: "Paul says \"Examine yourselves to see whether you are in the faith.\" This isn't about paralyzing doubt — it's about clarity. If we discover our faith is faulty, we can repent and cry out to Jesus for real faith. If we discover it's genuine, we can rest in assurance and stop second-guessing our salvation."
  - title: The thief on the cross shows us what saving faith looks like.
    body: "He confessed he deserved God's wrath. He recognized Jesus as sinless and perfect. He asked Jesus to remember him — not to rescue him from the cross, but simply to remember him in His kingdom. That's it. That's the faith that saves."

keyIllustration: null

application:
  - "Test your faith with the three questions from the thief: Do I believe I deserve God's wrath? Do I believe Jesus is sinless and my only hope? Am I trusting Him alone, or am I trying to use Him to get what I want?"
  - If you realize your faith has been about managing life instead of surrendering to Christ, confess that now. Ask Him to break your pride and show you what real faith looks like.
  - "If you pass the test, stop living in fear and doubt. Jesus said to the thief, \"Today you will be with me in paradise.\" If you trust Him, that's your promise too."

toRemember: The thief had nothing to offer Jesus but his sin and his need — and that was enough. If your faith looks like his, you are saved.
