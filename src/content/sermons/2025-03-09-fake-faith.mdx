mainPoints:
  - title: Not all belief is saving belief.
    body: Simon believed, was baptized, and followed Philip — but when he tried to buy spiritual power, Peter exposed his heart. He wanted Jesus to give him what he wanted, not to surrender to what Jesus wanted. Even demons believe in God and tremble; saving faith requires repentance and trust.
  - title: Scripture commands us to test our faith.
    body: "Paul says \"Examine yourselves to see whether you are in the faith.\" This isn't about paralyzing doubt — it's about clarity. If we discover our faith is faulty, we can repent and cry out to Jesus for real faith. If we discover it's genuine, we can rest in assurance and stop second-guessing our salvation."
  - title: The thief on the cross shows us what saving faith looks like.
    body: "He confessed he deserved God's wrath. He recognized Jesus as sinless and perfect. He asked Jesus to remember him — not to rescue him from the cross, but simply to remember him in His kingdom. That's it. That's the faith that saves."
