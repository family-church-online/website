# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Waiting is hard because of pain, powerlessness, and pointlessness — but Jesus doesn't waste anything. He calls us to enjoy what we have now, trust His power, and turn on the afterburners in the holding pattern."
keyScriptureRef: "John 2:4-5 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And Jesus said to her, \"Woman, what does this have to do with me? My hour has not yet come.\" His mother said to the servants, \"Do whatever he tells you.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus waited thirty years before His ministry began.
    body: "He lived a normal, mundane life for three decades — working, providing, protecting His family. Nothing was wasted. He knows what it's like to wait."
  - title: The wedding miracle points to the cross.
    body: "Six stone jars used for ritual washing are filled with water and become wine — the old purification system replaced by the blood of Christ. All of this on \"the third day.\""
  - title: Jesus is the Creator.
    body: "He didn't just transform water — He created wine from nothing, just as He created plants and separated land from water on the third day in Genesis. The word \"good\" echoes creation itself."
  - title: Pain has purpose when you know the mission.
    body: Soldiers lose limbs and need less morphine than civilians in accidents, because they know why they suffered. When you know God has a purpose, pain becomes bearable.
  - title: True joy is found in salvation, not circumstances.
    body: "Jesus didn't come to fix a wedding — He came to invite us to the wedding feast of the Lamb. Rejoice that your name is written in heaven."

keyIllustration: "An Israeli F-15 pilot collided mid-air with another jet during a training exercise. The other plane exploded. His aircraft spiralled toward the ground, fuel spraying everywhere. He had no idea how bad the damage was. Instead of bailing out, he turned on the afterburners — accelerating toward the earth — and regained control. He landed the plane ten miles away. Only then did he discover one entire wing had been torn off. Investigators refused to believe he'd flown it that way. But he had. When you're plummeting out of control, that's when you turn on the afterburners."

application:
  - Stop wishing your life away. Joy is available right now — in the birds, the clouds, the faces of those you love, the food on your table.
  - Turn on the afterburners. Use this time to draw closer to God, pray more, serve more, study His Word, make disciples.
  - Trust that nothing is wasted. God is preparing you, shaping you, using every painful, pointless-feeling moment for His glory and your good.

toRemember: "Jesus doesn't want us to miss a single moment — there is fullness of joy waiting for us right where we are, if we abide in Him."
