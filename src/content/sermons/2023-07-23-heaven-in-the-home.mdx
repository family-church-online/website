# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: To establish heaven in your home, stop settling for substitutes and be filled with the Holy Spirit through worship, gratitude, and submission.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 5:15-18 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Look carefully then how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, making the best use of the time, because the days are evil. Therefore do not be foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is. And do not get drunk with wine, for that is debauchery, but be filled with the Spirit.

mainPoints:
  - title: "The Principle of Promptness:"
    body: Do what you know God is asking you to do—now. Procrastination dishonours God and steals opportunities that may never return.
  - title: "The Principle of Priority:"
    body: "What is urgent is not always what is important. Discern God's will by asking (in prayer), seeking (with open eyes), and knocking (by taking action)."
  - title: "The Principle of Power:"
    body: You cannot build heaven in your home in your own strength. The Holy Spirit alone provides the power to live wisely and love sacrificially.
  - title: "The Practice of Worship, Gratitude, and Submission:"
    body: Be filled with the Spirit by addressing one another in psalms and hymns, giving thanks always, and submitting to one another out of reverence for Christ.

keyIllustration: A traveller arrives in a foreign country, exhausted and starving. The quickest option is a drive-through burger—high flavour, instant relief. It tastes incredible in the moment. But later, when a real burger is made with care—quality meat, caramelised onions, melted cheese, toasted buns—the contrast is undeniable. The substitute promised satisfaction but delivered emptiness. Alcohol, convenience, control—they all work the same way. They imitate the joy, community, and peace the Holy Spirit offers, but they reverse it. Real joy requires real investment.

application:
  - "Number your days. Ask God each morning: if this is my last day, how will I treat my family, spend my time, and steward my opportunities?"
  - "Commit to the four walls of being the church: Scripture, prayer, worship, and serving. These aren't optional add-ons—they are the structure that holds everything together."
  - "Stop saying you don't have time to pray. A lumberjack with fifty trees and one axe doesn't skip sharpening the blade. Prayer sharpens everything else."

toRemember: If you belong to Jesus, act like it—while there is still time.
