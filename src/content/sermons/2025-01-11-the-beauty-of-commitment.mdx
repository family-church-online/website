# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "True commitment to God isn't a one-time decision or a desperate bargain — it's a daily surrender that begins in honest wrestling, is tested when He answers, and is proven when you follow through no matter the cost."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Samuel 1:10-11 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "She was deeply distressed and prayed to the Lord and wept bitterly. And she vowed a vow and said, \"O Lord of hosts, if you will indeed look on the affliction of your servant and remember me and not forget your servant, but will give to your servant a son, then I will give him to the Lord all the days of his life, and no razor shall touch his head.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Wrestling with God is the path to true surrender.
    body: Hannah prayed with vexation, frustration, and anger — not polite religious language. She was honest about her pain, and that honesty became the foundation for genuine commitment.
  - title: "God's mercy meets us even when we approach Him imperfectly."
    body: Whether Hannah was bargaining or expressing gratitude in advance, God answered her prayer not because she did it perfectly, but because He is gracious and faithful.
  - title: "Following through on your vow releases God's greater blessing."
    body: Hannah gave Samuel back to God at age four. In return, God gave her three more sons, two daughters, and a prophet who would bless Israel for generations.
  - title: The real blessing is knowing God deeply.
    body: "Hannah's greatest gift wasn't children — it was experiencing God's faithfulness in a way that changed her forever and inspired others, including Mary, the mother of Jesus."

keyIllustration: "A diver separated from his group in dark water faces a theoretical limit called the particle horizon — a point beyond which information can never be retrieved, no matter how advanced the technology. Similarly, as finite human beings, there are limits to what we can understand about God's purposes. We will never fully comprehend why He closes some doors and opens others. But just as the ocean beyond the particle horizon still exists, God's infinite knowledge and perfect plan extend far beyond what we can see or understand. Our part is not to know everything — it's to trust the One who does."

application:
  - "If you've made a promise to God, don't run from it. Ask Him for the strength to keep it, even if it's costly."
  - "Be honest with God in your pain. Wrestling isn't a lack of faith — it's often the beginning of true surrender."
  - Commit to raising your children to know God, not just raising them well. The greatest gift you can give them is a deep relationship with Him.

toRemember: Every day is a new beginning when you commit yourself to God — forgetting the failures of the past, trusting His forgiveness, and looking forward to the glory of serving Him with everything you have.
