# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Purpose is not found by waiting for clarity—it's forged through five daily practices that align you with God's plan even when you can't see the destination."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 11:6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And without faith it is impossible to please him, for whoever would draw near to God must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who seek him.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Gratitude: Give your best to God"
    body: "Abel offered the firstborn and the fat portions—not leftovers. Gratitude fuels generosity. The more you focus on what you've been given, the stronger your desire to give back becomes."
  - title: "Fascination: Walk with God for the sake of knowing Him"
    body: "Enoch didn't just worship—he pursued. He was so obsessed with God that God took him. Carve out space to seek God purely because you want to know Him better."
  - title: "Fear: Build something out of reverent awe"
    body: "Noah built the ark because he feared God's power and judgment. Biblical fear is both trembling at His power and being terrified of separation from Him. Build up the church—people—before the fire tests your work."
  - title: "Obedience: Leave what God tells you to leave"
    body: Abraham left his home not knowing where he was going. Obey God in the small decisions—honesty, humility, faithfulness—and the big decisions will align. If Scripture says stop, stop today.
  - title: "Trust: Conceive something eternal"
    body: Sarah considered God faithful and received power to conceive. What legacy of faith will you pass on? Invest your trust in another person—share your faith so it outlives you.

keyIllustration: "A genealogy lists death after death—\"and then he died\"—until the seventh name: Enoch. He didn't die. God took him. Why? Because Enoch walked with God. He was so fascinated, so obsessed with knowing God, that God brought him home early. The contrast between Enoch and everyone else in Genesis 5 illustrates what happens when you pursue God purely for the sake of knowing Him."

application:
  - "For the next three weeks, practice this five-step rhythm: give something out of gratitude; carve out time to seek God purely to know Him; ask what you need to build and who you need to build up; identify what you need to leave; trust God for what He wants you to conceive."
  - Join the church in fasting and praying every Tuesday for three weeks. Fast food, media, or whatever you choose—and use that hunger to express your dependence on God.
  - "Obey in the small things today. The direction of your life is the sum of your small obediences. Don't wait for a big decision to align you—align yourself now."

toRemember: The greater fear always drives out the lesser fear—when you fear God rightly, the fear of tomorrow loses its grip.
