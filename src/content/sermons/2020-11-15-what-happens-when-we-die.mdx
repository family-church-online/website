# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When you die, you are instantly in Christ's presence — and the posture of the thief on the cross shows us exactly how to be sure we'll be with Him forever."
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 23:42-43 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And he said, \"Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom.\" And he said to him, \"Truly, I say to you, today you will be with me in paradise.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "You die once — immediately into Christ's presence"
    body: "There is no unconscious sleep, no reincarnation, no waiting period. Jesus tells the thief, \"Today you will be with me.\" That word \"with\" matters more than any description of paradise."
  - title: Your future includes a resurrected body
    body: "You will be like Jesus in His resurrected state — tangible, recognizable, able to eat and drink, no longer subject to pain or death. You won't become an angel; you'll remain you, perfected."
  - title: "How to be sure you're going there"
    body: "The dying thief shows us three postures: recognize you deserve condemnation, acknowledge Jesus is sinless and able to save, and humbly ask Him to remember you in His kingdom."

keyIllustration: "A snowboarder stands alone on an Austrian mountaintop — fresh snow, blue sky, absolute beauty — yet feels empty because no one he loves is there to share it. That same night, he stays in a grimy motel with cigarette burns in the sheets and pine cones banging on the roof all night. Which would he choose to return to? The motel — because his wife was with him. Heaven is not first about what it looks like; it's about being with Jesus."

application:
  - Stop fearing unconscious nothingness or anxious waiting — the moment you die, if you belong to Christ, you are with Him.
  - "Humble yourself like the thief: admit your guilt, trust Jesus alone to save you, and ask Him simply to remember you."
  - If a dying criminal could witness to Christ on a cross, you can tell someone else how to be with Jesus forever.

toRemember: "Where you're going matters less than who you'll be with — and you are never separated from the love of God in Christ Jesus."
