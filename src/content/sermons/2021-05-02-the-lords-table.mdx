# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Jesus took the punishment you earned for your sin, and at the Lord's Table we remember His sacrifice and celebrate the new covenant He made with us."
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 22:15-16"
keyScriptureText: "And he said to them, \"I have earnestly desired to eat this Passover with you before I suffer. For I tell you, I will not eat it until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus took your punishment.
    body: "Every sin earns death, but Jesus stepped in and took the penalty in your place, just like the Passover lamb's blood saved Israel's firstborn from death."
  - title: The new covenant brings three promises.
    body: God puts His law in our hearts so we want to obey Him, He gives us a personal relationship with Him, and He forgives our sins and remembers them no more.
  - title: "The Lord's Table is a declaration."
    body: "When we take the bread and cup, we're telling the world we believe Jesus did this for us and that we're walking with Him into an incredible future."

keyIllustration: "A schoolboy waits in his teacher's classroom, knowing he's about to be caned for something he deserved. Just as the punishment is about to fall, a stranger knocks, whispers to the teacher, and takes the boy's place. The boy walks free while the stranger receives the blows he earned. That's what Jesus did at the cross — He took the punishment we deserved."

application:
  - Stop carrying guilt over confessed sin — God has forgotten it and you should too.
  - "Come to the Lord's Table remembering that Jesus was eager to do this for you."
  - "Walk in the freedom of knowing death has passed over you because of Jesus' blood."

toRemember: "Jesus didn't just pay for your sin — He walked out of that place of punishment, put His arm around you, and said, \"Come, we've got an incredible future ahead of us.\""
