# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's plans for your life are sovereign and unchanging, even when He requires you to make dramatic turns you never expected."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 10:28 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And he said to them, \"You yourselves know how unlawful it is for a Jew to associate with or to visit anyone of another nation, but God has shown me that I should not call any person common or unclean.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "God's plan has always included all nations."
    body: "From Abraham to the Gospels to Acts, God's strategy was to bless Israel so that through them, all nations would know Him. The gospel to the Gentiles wasn't Plan B—it was always the plan."
  - title: When you seek God, you will find Him.
    body: "Cornelius didn't know Jesus, but he prayed and gave to the poor. God promises that anyone who seeks Him with their whole heart will find Him, regardless of background or mistakes."
  - title: Salvation comes only through Jesus.
    body: "Cornelius couldn't find his own way to God. God sent Peter to bring the message of Jesus, because there is no other mediator between God and humanity."
  - title: Obedience often requires stepping out of your comfort zone.
    body: "Peter faced massive personal and cultural consequences for entering a Gentile's home. He obeyed anyway, because God had made it clear this was the next step in His plan."

keyIllustration: "A comparison is drawn between Peter and Jonah. Both left from Joppa. Both were sent to Israel's enemies. Jonah resisted, then obeyed reluctantly and saw the Ninevites repent. Peter obeyed willingly and saw Cornelius and his household believe. Even Peter's father was named Jonah—pointing to how this moment was no accident, but part of a centuries-long story God had been writing."

application:
  - "Trust that even when your life takes a drastic turn, God's plan for you hasn't changed. Ask Him for the strength to make the turn He's calling you to."
  - "Build genuine relationships with people who don't know Jesus, not to judge them but to deliver the gospel. Inclusion matters because it creates space to share the only message that can save."
  - "If you're struggling to obey God in something He's made clear, share it with others and ask for prayer. You weren't meant to make hard turns alone."

toRemember: You are here for a reason—to go to the people nobody else can reach and tell them about the One apart from whom no one can know God.
