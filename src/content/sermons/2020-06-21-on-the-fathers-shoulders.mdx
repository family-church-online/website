# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Fathers are called to lead their households in righteousness—not by their own strength, but by faith in the Father whose shoulders carry the weight.
keyScriptureRef: "Genesis 18:19 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For I have chosen him, that he may command his children and his household after him to keep the way of the Lord by doing righteousness and justice, so that the Lord may bring to Abraham what he has promised him.

mainPoints:
  - title: God held the men accountable.
    body: Only the men of war—20 years and older, numbered in the census—were barred from entering the Promised Land. They had been given the responsibility to trust God and lead their families in, but they refused.
  - title: Their failure affected everyone.
    body: "Though God did not punish the women and children directly, the entire nation wandered forty years in the wilderness because of the men's faithlessness. Consequences ripple through families."
  - title: Fathers today face similar pressures.
    body: "Culture reduces fathers to mere providers or depicts them as incompetent. Gender confusion blurs identity. Men are left asking: Who am I? What is my role?"
  - title: Scripture defines fatherhood clearly.
    body: A father is called to lead by example in righteousness, protect and carry his family, love his wife sacrificially as Christ loved the church, and teach and discipline his children in the Lord.
  - title: We cannot do it in our own strength.
    body: "Just as the Israelite men failed when they tried to fight without God's blessing, fathers today cannot carry the weight alone. Salvation begins by faith and continues by faith—God works in us both to will and to work for His good pleasure."

keyIllustration: A father let his son skateboard down a steep road, thinking he could warn him of oncoming cars. But the noise of the wheels and the wind made it impossible for the boy to hear. A car passed dangerously close. The father realised he had failed to protect his son—a vivid picture of how fathers can fail even when present, and how the weight of responsibility feels unbearable when carried alone.

application:
  - Stop trying to perfect yourself by your own effort. You were saved by faith—continue in faith, trusting God to change you.
  - Acknowledge where you have failed as a father, husband, child, or spouse. Confess it to God and trust Him to carry what you cannot.
  - "If you are a single mother or a father under crushing pressure, know this: the burden is not on your shoulders alone. God is your help and shield."

toRemember: "The title is not \"On the father's shoulders\"—it is \"On the Father's shoulders.\" God works in you to will and to work. Trust Him, obey Him, and let Him carry the weight you were never meant to bear alone."
