mainPoints:
  - title: Make the most of time created by difficult situations.
    body: "The riot in Ephesus looked like disaster but became the launching point for Paul's next mission — three months in Corinth, the book of Romans written, churches strengthened."
  - title: Make the most of time to deal with difficult situations.
    body: "Paul didn't ignore the mess in Corinth or Rome; he stepped into it with teaching and writing that would shape the church for centuries."
  - title: "Make the most of time with God's people."
    body: Paul brought a team of elders on the road not just for accountability but for discipleship — long miles became a mobile seminary.
  - title: "Make the most of time to speak God's word."
    body: Paul preached until midnight in Troas not because he loved his own voice but because the urgency of the gospel demanded it — Jesus came to preach, and so must we.
  - title: "Make the best use of time to prioritize God's purpose."
    body: Paul skipped Ephesus to reach Jerusalem by Pentecost because he knew where God was sending him next — obedience to the mission anchors every other decision.

keyIllustration: "A preacher spent weeks praying about buying a stylus for his tablet — a small thing, but he couldn't shake the sense to wait. Finally one day, peace came. He walked into the store and while waiting for stock, met two children in the corner. One was terrified, alone, panicking. The arrival of the preacher calmed him completely. Later the mother said her son had been falling apart until he walked past. God orchestrated a five-hundred-rand purchase to position one man at the exact moment a child needed him most."

application:
  - "Wake up tomorrow and pray: \"Lord, these twelve hours are Yours. Lead me where You want me, hook me up with who You want me to meet, and do with me what You will.\" Then accept whatever shape the day takes."
  - Stop treating disruption as disaster. When plans fall apart, ask what God is making space for instead.
  - "Don't underestimate the power of preached Scripture. It is living, active, and eternal — never dismiss a chance to hear it or speak it."

toRemember: "You don't need to see the whole plan — you just need to trust the One who does, surrender the day, and stay ready."
