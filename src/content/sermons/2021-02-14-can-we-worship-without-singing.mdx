# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You were created to worship God — not because He needs you, but because worshipping Him is the purpose for which you exist, the means by which you become like Christ, and the only thing that will satisfy your soul.
keyScriptureRef: "1 Peter 1:8-9 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Though you have not seen Him, you love Him. Though you do not now see Him, you believe in Him and rejoice with joy that is inexpressible and filled with glory, obtaining the outcome of your faith, the salvation of your souls.

mainPoints:
  - title: You exist to worship God.
    body: "After twelve chapters of searching for meaning, Solomon concludes: \"Fear God and keep His commandments, for this is the whole duty of man.\" The fear of God is not terror — it's breathless awe before a God so holy and powerful you can't look away."
  - title: "God doesn't need your worship — He wants it."
    body: "The Trinity was perfectly satisfied before creation. God made you not because He was lonely, but because He wanted you to know Him. That changes everything: your worship isn't obligation, it's privilege."
  - title: You become what you worship.
    body: "Whatever you give your heart to shapes you. Worship God through Christ, and you become like Jesus. That's not a Sunday activity — it's the purpose of your whole life."

keyIllustration: "A rugby coach explained why he recruits so many South African players: \"They're not just big and powerful — they're deeply spiritual men with a higher purpose. They don't play for themselves or the money. They play to glorify God. When you've got a guy like that, you've got a warrior.\" Worship isn't contained in a song — it spills into every tackle, every project, every conversation."

application:
  - "Ask yourself: have I ever felt joy so overwhelming I couldn't express it when I thought about what Jesus has done for me? If not, that's where the journey begins."
  - "Worship is not singing louder — it's living for His glory in everything you do. Your work, your relationships, your gifts — all of it can be an act of worship."
  - Only the Holy Spirit can turn you from a rebel into a worshipper. Ask Him to do it.

toRemember: "You were not made to survive, achieve, or impress. You were made to know the God who saved you — and to live every moment ablaze with gratitude for what He's done."
