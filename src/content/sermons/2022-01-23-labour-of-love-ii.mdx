# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When your life is scrambled beyond recognition and you're convinced you can't go on, the cure isn't fixing your circumstances — it's knowing with unshakeable certainty that God chose you, He has a plan for you, and He will finish what He started, with or without your comfort."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Thessalonians 1:4-5 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For we know, brothers loved by God, that he has chosen you, because our gospel came to you not only in word, but also in power and in the Holy Spirit and with full conviction.

mainPoints:
  - title: We know God has chosen us because His Word came to us.
    body: Election begins with hearing the gospel — not just the words, but the power of the Holy Spirit working through it.
  - title: We know God has chosen us because we received the message and its messengers.
    body: "Have you believed the gospel? Have you said yes to the one who told you about it? That's the second unmistakable sign."
  - title: We know God has chosen us because the Holy Spirit used the gospel to change us.
    body: Real change looks like repentance (turning from idols to serve the living God), receiving assurance from the Holy Spirit, and laboring in love by imitating Christ so that others will imitate you.

keyIllustration: "A scrambled Rubik's Cube was used to illustrate life. When you look at it jumbled, you want it fixed instantly — but the way to solve it is not to stare at it and try to force it into place. The way to solve it is to learn the simple moves and apply them over and over, step by step, until the solution emerges. That's what faith looks like: follow the small, obedient moves God shows you, trust the process, and when you look up, He will have solved what you could never solve on your own."

application:
  - "Stop trying to fix your scrambled life in your own strength. Ask God what simple, faithful steps He's calling you to take — and take them, even when you can't see the big picture."
  - "If you're wondering whether God has chosen you, look for the signs: Have you heard the gospel? Have you believed it? Has it changed you? If yes, you are His — no matter what your circumstances say."
  - "Repent of the idols you're still holding — unforgiveness, self-reliance, comfort-seeking — and turn fully to the living God, trusting that He chose you and will finish what He started."

toRemember: "When you're broken on the ground and can't trust yourself anymore, that's when everything turns — because that's when you finally stop trying to solve your own life and let the One who chose you do what only He can do."
