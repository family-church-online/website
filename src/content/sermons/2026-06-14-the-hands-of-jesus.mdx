mainPoints:
  - title: "Don't judge God by your circumstances."
    body: When life turns hard, run toward Him rather than reading resentment into His character; you signed up for a spiritual war, and war is never fair.
  - title: Trust His word over your eyes.
    body: As with Moses, God shows us His character rather than a picture to interpret; what we merely see, we so often misread.
  - title: You belong to Jesus and His church.
    body: The hands in the vision are pierced yet protecting; you were purchased to serve His church, not to sit comfortably within it.

keyIllustration: "A video of two bulldogs shows that a dog who has never been struck will not flinch when its owner feigns a blow; it simply trusts the hand. So it is with the hand of Christ: full of power to judge and destroy, yet laid gently on the fearful with the words, \"Do not be afraid.\""

application:
  - When hardship comes, draw nearer to God rather than pulling back or blaming Him.
  - "Go to Scripture to learn God's character instead of drawing conclusions from your situation."
  - "Choose and serve a church by asking \"Who needs me there?\", not merely \"Where am I comfortable?\""

toRemember: The hands that were pierced for you are the hands that now hold you, and no one can snatch you out of them.
