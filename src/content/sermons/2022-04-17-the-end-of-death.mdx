# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The resurrection is the single event that proves Christianity is true—if Christ wasn't raised, faith collapses; if He was, everything He said can be trusted."
keyScriptureRef: "1 Corinthians 15:12-15 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Now if Christ is proclaimed as raised from the dead, how can some of you say there is no resurrection of the dead? But if there is no resurrection of the dead, then not even Christ has been raised. And if Christ has not been raised, then our preaching is in vain and your faith is in vain. We are even found to be misrepresenting God, because we testified about God that he raised Christ, whom he did not raise if it is true that the dead are not raised.

mainPoints:
  - title: The Gospels were written by eyewitnesses within one or two decades of the events.
    body: Too soon for myth to replace fact, and too public for lies to survive scrutiny.
  - title: Biblical and extra-biblical historians confirm that followers believed Jesus was raised bodily.
    body: "Josephus, Tacitus, and Pliny the Younger documented the belief, even though they weren't Christians."
  - title: "No evidence in two thousand years has proven the tomb wasn't empty."
    body: If the body had been found, Christianity would have been destroyed in its first week.
  - title: No one has provided a better explanation for the empty tomb.
    body: The swoon theory, stolen body theory, and hallucination theory all collapse under scrutiny.
  - title: The apostles went from terrified and hiding to brazen and bold overnight.
    body: "Something radical changed them—most died as martyrs rather than deny what they'd seen."
  - title: No other historically attested instance exists of anyone being raised from the dead after three days.
    body: Mythology has gods who rise, but no verified human being has ever done what Jesus did.
  - title: Believers in every age testify to a life-changing relationship with Jesus.
    body: "The resurrection isn't just history—it's present power transforming lives today."

keyIllustration: "Sir Lionel Luckhoo holds the Guinness World Record for the most consecutive defence murder trial acquittals—245 in a row. After forty-two years practising law around the world, he said: \"The evidence for the resurrection of Jesus Christ is so overwhelming that it compels acceptance by proof which leaves absolutely no room for doubt.\""

application:
  - "You don't have to be afraid of death—Jesus proved it's not the end."
  - "Your faith isn't blind; it's built on the most scrutinised event in history."
  - Because Jesus rose, you can trust everything else He said about forgiveness, eternal life, and your future with Him.

toRemember: If God is for us—if He died and rose for us—then nothing can stand against us.
