# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

keyScriptureRef: "Isaiah 53:7-8 (read by the Ethiopian)"

mainPoints:
  - title: God arranges divine appointments through simple obedience.
    body: "An angel told Philip to go to a desert road. He didn't know why. But when he got there, God had already positioned a searching official reading the exact passage of Scripture that points to Jesus."
  - title: "Your value isn't determined by human acceptance."
    body: The Ethiopian had traveled thirty days to worship in Jerusalem, only to be excluded because of his ethnicity and condition. Yet God sent a personal evangelist to meet him in the desert and reveal that he was loved, chosen, and purposed.
  - title: Faithful service in the small things prepares you for the significant things.
    body: Philip started by serving tables. His obedience in the mundane led to supernatural encounters, miraculous transportation, and eventually a settled ministry where he raised four prophesying daughters and hosted the apostle Paul.

keyIllustration: "A man seated in the back of a car on a long journey has no control over the route, the speed, or the destination. He can only trust the driver. If he spends the journey complaining, fighting, and making the back seat miserable, the drive doesn't change—but his experience does. Philip learned to make the back seat peaceful by trusting God's direction, even when it led through deserts, persecution, and uncomfortable encounters. That posture of trust allowed God to take him exactly where he needed to be."

application:
  - "Stop waiting for the grand plan. Start obeying in the small things: serve somewhere, love someone difficult, resist a temptation today."
  - "If you feel rejected or worthless, know this: God orchestrated a thirty-day journey, a desert road, an ancient prophecy, and a supernatural encounter just for one man. You are seen. You are valued. You have purpose."
  - "Ask God each morning: \"What appointment have You arranged for me today? Whose life will You touch through my obedience?\""

toRemember: "You don't need to know where God is taking you. You just need to obey where He's leading you today."
