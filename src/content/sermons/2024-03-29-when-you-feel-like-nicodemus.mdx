# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Jesus' death on the cross was complete victory—not defeat requiring a resurrection comeback—and we are called to rest in His finished work rather than exhaust ourselves trying to fix what He has already accomplished."
keyScriptureRef: "Colossians 2:13-15"
keyScriptureText: And you, who were dead in your trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, God made alive together with Him, having forgiven us all our trespasses, by canceling the record of debt that stood against us with its legal demands. This He set aside, nailing it to the cross. He disarmed the rulers and authorities and put them to open shame, by triumphing over them in Him.

mainPoints:
  - title: Nicodemus came to Jesus confused, trying to understand in his own wisdom.
    body: "He wanted to know how to be born again, but Jesus revealed salvation is God's work, not human achievement—the Spirit works where He wills, not through our striving."
  - title: "Nicodemus tried to defend Jesus, not realizing God's plan included the crucifixion."
    body: When he stood up for Jesus before the Pharisees, he was unknowingly trying to stop what God had ordained—the very arrest and suffering that would secure our salvation.
  - title: "Nicodemus brought 75 pounds of spices to anoint Jesus' body—an act of love, but unnecessary."
    body: "He honored Jesus' death, yet God had promised His Holy One would not see decay. Our sincere efforts don't change God's sovereign plan, but they reveal our hearts."
  - title: The cross was total victory, not defeat.
    body: "When Jesus said \"It is finished,\" He had already triumphed over sin, death, and demonic powers. The resurrection was evidence of victory won, not a comeback after defeat."

keyIllustration: Just as Moses lifted up the bronze serpent in the desert and the Israelites only had to look at it to be healed—no matter where they were in the camp, no striving required—so the Son of Man was lifted up on the cross. All we must do is believe. Healing, salvation, victory—all of it accomplished by looking to Him in faith, not by our frantic efforts to achieve it.

application:
  - Stop lying awake trying to control what only God can control. Bring your anxiety to Him and ask for the peace of knowing He is sovereign over every detail.
  - You are not saved, sanctified, or made righteous by your effort—believe and receive what Christ has already finished on the cross.
  - Serve with all your heart, but serve empowered by the Holy Spirit, not by anxious striving in your own strength.

toRemember: "When you think you've been beaten, when you think you've lost the war, give it to the Lord—because the most atrocious things that happen can become the stage for God's greatest victories."
