# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Nostalgia tempts you to retreat into a sanitized past, but Jesus—who is Apostle and High Priest—calls you to hold fast to your confession and boast in your hope, no matter what you've lost."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 3:1-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Therefore, holy brothers, you who share in a heavenly calling, consider Jesus, the apostle and high priest of our confession, who was faithful to him who appointed him, just as Moses also was faithful in all God's house. For Jesus has been counted worthy of more glory than Moses—as much more glory as the builder of a house has more honor than the house itself."

mainPoints:
  - title: Nostalgia distorts reality.
    body: We remember the good and forget the pain, creating a past that never existed—making it impossible to return to and doubly painful to miss.
  - title: Jesus is both Apostle and High Priest.
    body: Moses represented God to the people; Aaron represented the people to God. Jesus does both—He reconciles us fully.
  - title: "Moses was faithful in God's house; Jesus is faithful over it."
    body: Moses was a servant testifying to what was coming; Jesus is the Son who built the house and rules it.
  - title: You belong to His house if you hold fast.
    body: Material loss can be regained, but if you abandon faith, you lose everything that cannot be recovered.

keyIllustration: Colonel Sanders lost everything repeatedly—businesses, careers, relationships—through economic collapse, pandemics, and world wars. He rebuilt again and again. Material loss is devastating but recoverable. Faith abandoned, however, is the one loss that costs you eternity.

application:
  - Resist the temptation to idealize the past; it will trap you in grief for something that never was.
  - Meditate on Jesus daily—read Scripture, pray, and ask what it reveals about God, yourself, and what Jesus has done.
  - "Hold fast to your confession; don't let persecution, loss, or hardship loosen your grip on faith."
  - "Boast in Jesus boldly; telling others what He's done for you anchors you in joy and strengthens your own faith."

toRemember: You are His house if you hold fast to your confidence and boast in your hope—because Jesus is not just greater than Moses; He is the builder, the Son, and the Savior who reconciles you to God.
