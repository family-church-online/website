# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When you align your life with God's single unchanging purpose through faith in Christ and acts of genuine love, He will guide your path no matter how many plans change along the way."
keyScriptureRef: "Romans 12:9-13 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Let love be genuine. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good. Love one another with brotherly affection. Outdo one another in showing honor. Do not be slothful in zeal, be fervent in spirit, serve the Lord. Rejoice in hope, be patient in tribulation, be constant in prayer. Contribute to the needs of the saints and seek to show hospitality.

mainPoints:
  - title: Many plans, one purpose.
    body: "Your mind will change its plans constantly, but God's single purpose for your life will stand regardless of circumstances or other people's interference."
  - title: Faith precedes obedience.
    body: You cannot obey God properly without first believing in Jesus Christ—only through the Holy Spirit can you obey with the right motives and the necessary strength.
  - title: Love genuinely, starting here.
    body: Scripture directs us to begin acts of genuine love within the church community, not with distant or abstract acts of service.
  - title: Gratitude enables obedience.
    body: When you feel too tired or irritated to love others, gratitude for what God has already done is the fastest way to realign your heart and actions.

keyIllustration: "Moses sat at a well in Midian with nothing but the clothes on his back, his life in ruins. When shepherdesses arrived and were bullied away from the water by other shepherds, Moses had one small choice: step in and help, or ignore it. He chose to help. That single act of genuine love led to a marriage, a family, a job tending sheep in the very wilderness he would later lead two million people through. God used forty years of obscurity to prepare Moses for his calling—all because of one small decision to love."

application:
  - Ask God daily for the strength to obey in the small decisions, knowing He is assembling the bigger picture while you focus on faithfulness.
  - "When facing a decision Scripture doesn't address directly, ask: \"What does genuine love look like here?\" and trust that obedience will align you with God's will."
  - Start with the people in the room—serve, honor, and contribute to the needs of your local church community as the foundation of your obedience.

toRemember: While you work on the small pieces of obedience, kindness, and gratitude, God is putting the whole picture together.
