tagLine: You can love people and still refuse to tolerate what is destroying them.
shortDescription: Your church community may be warm, generous, and growing — yet still tolerate a voice that is quietly leading people away from God. Jesus sees it all, and He holds leaders and congregations accountable. This passage draws the line between love that confronts and love that enables, and shows what genuine faithfulness costs.
subtitle: A church can excel in love and still be rebuked — because genuine love requires the courage to confront what destroys it.

# ── ABOUT LISTS ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
takeaways:
  - "Jesus' burning eyes mean He sees your works, your motives, and your silence — nothing is hidden from the one who searches mind and heart."
  - Tolerating false teaching is not love; it is the neglect that allows spiritual destruction to spread through a community.
  - "\"Judge not\" does not mean silence — it means confronting with humility, having first examined yourself."
  - Those who hold fast and conquer will be given authority to rule with Christ over the nations in His coming kingdom.

audience:
  - "You've been told Christians must never confront anyone — and something in you suspects that's not the whole story."
  - "You've watched someone drift toward teaching that doesn't line up with Scripture and weren't sure whether to say something."
  - You find yourself tolerating things you know are wrong because you fear being seen as judgmental.

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: A church that loves well must also discern boldly — because Christ, who sees into every heart, calls His people to hold fast, confront faithfully, and inherit the authority He has promised.
keyScriptureRef: "Revelation 2:23 ESV"
keyScriptureText: I am he who searches mind and heart, and I will give to each of you according to your works.

mainPoints:
  - title: Christ sees and judges everything.
    body: His eyes like flames of fire are not decoration — they declare that He sees into every heart, rewards every faithful act, and holds every compromise to account.
  - title: Love without discernment enables destruction.
    body: Thyatira was growing in love and service, yet Jesus rebukes them for tolerating a teacher who was seducing His people into idolatry and immorality — and shows how unchecked false teaching spreads from teacher to follower to devoted disciple.
  - title: Hold fast and inherit the authority He promises.
    body: "To those who conquer — who keep His works until the end — Christ promises authority over the nations in His coming kingdom, and gives Himself: the bright morning star."

keyIllustration: "Someone who ignored serious symptoms for too long, endured painful treatment, and came out the other side sits down with a friend who now has the same symptoms. The question is simple: do you say \"don't worry about it,\" or do you tell them what you know? That is what faithful confrontation looks like — not superiority, but hard-won love that refuses to let someone walk into unnecessary suffering."

application:
  - When a voice promises deeper spirituality through practices borrowed from other religions or secret knowledge, the response Christ calls for is not curiosity — it is to hold fast to what you have.
  - When you see someone being drawn away from faithfulness to Christ, the loving response is not silence. Remove the log from your own eye first, then speak — humbly, carefully, and with courage.

toRemember: Christ is the jealous husband of His church — He sees everything, loves without limit, and is coming to rule all nations; hold fast to Him, and you will inherit the authority He promises.
