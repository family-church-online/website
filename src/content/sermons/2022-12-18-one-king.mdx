# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus came first as the humble servant-King to change hearts; He will return as the warrior-King to bring justice — and both images reveal a King worthy of worship.
keyScriptureRef: "Psalm 98:4-6 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Make a joyful noise to the Lord, all the earth. Break forth into joyous song and sing praises. Sing praises to the Lord with a lyre, with a lyre and the sound of melody, with trumpets and the sound of the horn. Make a joyful noise before the King, the Lord.

mainPoints:
  - title: The Old Testament prophesied two comings.
    body: Isaiah 63 shows a warrior-King; Isaiah 53 reveals a suffering servant. Israel expected both in one arrival — and missed the first.
  - title: "Jesus' first coming as the servant-King shows His character."
    body: Born in a manger, riding a donkey into Jerusalem — He came in humility, peace, and patience to change hearts.
  - title: "Jesus' second coming as the warrior-King guarantees justice."
    body: Revelation 19 shows Him returning on a white horse, faithful and true, to judge sin and right every wrong.

keyIllustration: "A Burchell's Coucal called every morning for two months — beautiful, elusive, impossible to spot. After weeks of searching, one exhausted afternoon the search ended: there it was, perched heavily on a branch, exactly as hoped for. Jesus has been calling since the fall — and when you finally see Him, He's everything you hoped for and more."

application:
  - "Jesus is God's plan for salvation — He loves you more than you can love Him, and He gave up His throne to be with you."
  - "Everyone needs Jesus as King — even those who've never heard His name respond when they see Him."
  - Worship Him now — this Christmas, make a joyful noise, tell others, and give Him the honor He deserves.

toRemember: "Jesus is unlike any other king: humble, patient, just, sovereign — and calling you to worship Him."
