# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God delights in turning tables at the last possible moment—but while you wait, you must trust Him alone, refuse vengeance, stay devoted to His purpose, and forgive daily.
keyScriptureRef: "Genesis 50:20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: As for you, you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good, to bring it about that many people should be kept alive, as they are today.

mainPoints:
  - title: Trust God and turn everything over to Him.
    body: Joseph learned not to place the burden of ultimate trust on any human being—not Potiphar, not the cupbearer, not even his father—but on God alone.
  - title: Wait for God to turn it around.
    body: For thirteen years Joseph endured slavery, false accusation, and imprisonment, knowing that only God could reverse the injustice—and He did, in one day.
  - title: "Don't take God's place."
    body: "Joseph refused both revenge and credit, saying \"Am I in the place of God?\" He knew vengeance and vindication belong to the Lord."
  - title: "Stay devoted to God's purpose."
    body: "Whether managing Potiphar's household, the prison, or Egypt's food supply, Joseph did his work with excellence, trusting God would use it for His plan."
  - title: Take every opportunity to forgive.
    body: "Even when his brothers cooked up another lie after Jacob's death, Joseph wept and forgave again—because forgiveness is a daily decision, not a one-time event."

keyIllustration: "A chess master has his opponent two moves from checkmate. Then the referee steps forward, spins the board 180 degrees, and suddenly every winning position belongs to the losing player. That's what it means when God turns tables—He waits until the very last move, then reverses everything in an instant."

application:
  - "Stop waiting for human rescue and turn your impossible situation over to God daily—He's the only one who can carry that weight."
  - "When you finally have power over those who hurt you, choose forgiveness over revenge, because God's justice is better than yours."
  - Do the work in front of you with excellence, even in prison, because God uses faithful obedience to position you for His reversal.

toRemember: Whether you see the tables turn in this lifetime or the next, God is faithful—and waiting on Him is never wasted.
