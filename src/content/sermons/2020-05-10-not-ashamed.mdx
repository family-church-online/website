# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus endured suffering not because He was weak, but to become the perfect Savior who brings you to glory — and He is not ashamed to call you His family.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 2:10-11 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For it was fitting that he, for whom and by whom all things exist, in bringing many sons to glory, should make the founder of their salvation perfect through suffering. For he who sanctifies and those who are sanctified all have one source. That is why he is not ashamed to call them brothers.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus was perfected through suffering to bring you to glory.
    body: His redemption required Him to share your flesh, your temptations, your struggles — and to die in your place without sin.
  - title: You share one source with Christ.
    body: You have the same Father, the same flesh, and the same Holy Spirit — which is why Jesus is not ashamed to call you His brother or sister.
  - title: God never hid His face from His Son.
    body: "Psalm 22 reveals that even on the cross, God the Father did not abandon Jesus — He was proud of His Son's obedience and heard His cry."
  - title: Jesus proclaims your name in the congregation.
    body: He declares you before the Father and sings praise alongside you, welcoming you into His family without shame or hesitation.

keyIllustration: A managing director loudly proclaimed in an office that tragedy proved there is no God — and the speaker remained silent, ashamed and uncertain. That moment of retreat illustrates how persecution and doubt can cause us to shrink back from proclaiming Christ.

application:
  - When you doubt or drift from the Word, return in humble repentance and ask God to rebuild your confidence and boldness.
  - Proclaim your love for Christ through how you act and speak — even in isolation, there are ways to declare you belong to Him.
  - "Take the Lord's table seriously as a proclamation of Christ's death, not a dull routine — it declares you are not ashamed of Him."

toRemember: Jesus is not ashamed to call you His family — so do not be ashamed to proclaim Him to the world.
