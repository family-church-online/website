# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: To deliberately sin after receiving knowledge of the truth is to reject the ultimate sacrifice of Jesus—there is no other sacrifice left, only the terrifying expectation of judgment.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 10:26-27 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For if we go on sinning deliberately after receiving knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains a sacrifice for sins, but a fearful expectation of judgment, and a fury of fire that will consume the adversaries.

mainPoints:
  - title: "Apostasy is not struggling with sin—it's rejecting Jesus after knowing Him."
    body: "The warning is written to Christians who understood the sacrifice of Jesus but chose to walk away. This is moral apostasy: turning your heart from God while claiming to know Him."
  - title: After Jesus, there is no backup plan.
    body: Under the Law of Moses, Israel had annual sacrifices. Jesus was the ultimate sacrifice. If you reject Him after knowing what He did, there is nothing else—only judgment.
  - title: Apostasy is a threefold rejection of the Trinity.
    body: It tramples on the Son of God, profanes the blood of the covenant, and outrages the Spirit of grace. It is repugnant to God—deliberate disrespect for all He is and all He has done.
  - title: "God's justice is both terrifying and comforting."
    body: "While apostasy brings vengeance, God's justice means that all wrongs will be made right. Trusting that God will judge gives us peace when surrounded by injustice."

keyIllustration: Imagine witnessing an adult trampling on your child—innocent and vulnerable—lying on the ground. What emotion does that stir in you? What do you want to do? To have knowledge of the truth and reject it is the equivalent of trampling on Jesus. If that image stirs your heart, how do you think God the Father feels when He sees His Son trampled on?

application:
  - "If you've received knowledge of the truth, you stand at a crossroads: walk the easy path of the Pharisees—turning your back on God—or walk the difficult road of faith that leads to everlasting life with Him."
  - Struggling with recurring sin is not apostasy. Apostasy is knowing the truth and deliberately choosing to disregard, disrespect, and turn away from God entirely.
  - When surrounded by injustice, trust that God will set all things straight—even if not in this lifetime. His justice is certain.

toRemember: It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God—but for those who stay strong in faith and turn to Him for refuge, sin no longer poses any threat.
