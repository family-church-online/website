# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: True value is found not in earthly recognition or achievement, but in being a faithful servant beloved by God and called by Christ to encourage His church.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 6:21-22 ESV"
keyScriptureText: So that you also may know how I am and what I am doing, Tychicus the beloved brother and faithful minister in the Lord will tell you everything. I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know how we are and that he may encourage your hearts.

mainPoints:
  - title: All Scripture matters, including the obscure parts.
    body: Every word is God-breathed and profitable for teaching. The closing of Ephesians — naming a little-known servant — reveals profound truths about eternal value.
  - title: Who commends you matters more than what they say.
    body: "Paul, who gave up wealth, status, and safety for Christ, called Tychicus \"beloved brother\" and \"faithful minister.\" These are character words, not performance metrics."
  - title: "Being \"beloved\" is a matter of identity in Christ."
    body: You become beloved not through achievement, but through faith in Jesus. God makes His home in those who love Him and keep His word.
  - title: Faithfulness means longevity in obedience.
    body: Tychicus walked thousands of kilometres, served for decades, and obeyed without hesitation. His character was proven over time, not in one heroic moment.
  - title: Jesus is the ultimate character witness.
    body: "He gave up heaven to become a servant. If He calls you \"beloved\" and \"faithful,\" nothing else matters — not for time, but for eternity."

keyIllustration: "A sixty-year-old tendon made twenty-eight tackles in the Rugby World Cup final. Peter-Steph du Toit's father donated his own hamstring tendon so his son could recover from a devastating knee injury in time to play. The procedure worked so well it became a breakthrough treatment. The father sat in a wheelchair, strapped up, praising God — while his ligament helped win the World Cup. No one knew. But that hidden sacrifice changed everything."

application:
  - "Stop seeking approval from the world and start living for the \"well done\" of Jesus."
  - "Find someone you don't know and encourage them — this is why we gather as a church."
  - Ask God to change your heart from seeking fame or legacy to seeking faithfulness in small, unseen obedience.

toRemember: Something small you do today, in obedience and love, can change the world — and Jesus sees it all.
