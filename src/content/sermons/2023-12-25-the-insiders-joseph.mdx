# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God speaks differently to each person, but immediate obedience — even when it's costly — is how you get to know Him deeply."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 1:24-25 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "When Joseph woke from sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord commanded him: he took his wife, but knew her not until she had given birth to a son. And he called his name Jesus."

mainPoints:
  - title: God speaks to us individually.
    body: "Mary saw an angel face to face; Joseph received dreams. God already knows how to reach you — stop looking for someone else's burning bush."
  - title: What God says matters more than what people think.
    body: Joseph faced public shame, but he obeyed immediately. Fearing God more than public opinion is the beginning of true freedom.
  - title: The right time to obey is now.
    body: "Joseph rose the same night he was warned, walked thirty days through the desert with a newborn, and trusted God's timing without hesitation."
  - title: God is stronger and wiser than any threat.
    body: "Even when told to return to a country where Herod's family wanted Jesus dead, Joseph trusted that God's protection was greater than human danger."

keyIllustration: "A man engaged to be married discovers his fiancée is pregnant — and the child isn't his. In that culture, the most zealous religious leaders would have executed her under Moses' law. Joseph considered divorcing her quietly to avoid the shame. But when God spoke, Joseph chose God's voice over his reputation, took Mary as his wife immediately, and raised the Son of God — even though it meant a lifetime of whispers and judgment. Obedience cost Joseph everything society valued, but gave him something no one could take away: intimacy with God Himself."

application:
  - "Stop waiting for the \"right time\" to obey what God has already told you. Delayed obedience is disobedience."
  - "Ask yourself: Am I more afraid of what God thinks or what people think? Let that question expose where you're still seeking human approval over God's."
  - "Read Scripture to know God, not just to learn facts. Ask three questions: What does this show me about God? What does it show me about humanity? What does it show me about Jesus and what He's done for me?"

toRemember: "Getting to know God will be difficult, but it's worth it — because that relationship can never be taken away from you, not in all eternity."
