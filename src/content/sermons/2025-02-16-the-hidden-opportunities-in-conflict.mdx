# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God uses conflict in the church not to destroy it, but to redirect focus, multiply ministry, and invite ordinary people into extraordinary purpose through the power of the Holy Spirit.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 6:2-4 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And the twelve summoned the full number of the disciples and said, \"It is not right that we should give up preaching the word of God to serve tables. Therefore, brothers, pick out from among you seven men of good repute, full of the Spirit and of wisdom, whom we will appoint to this duty. But we will devote ourselves to prayer and to the ministry of the word.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "Conflict exposed a deeper problem:"
    body: The apostles were distracted from their God-given calling to prayer and the Word by good but delegable tasks.
  - title: "The solution multiplied ministry:"
    body: Seven Spirit-filled believers were chosen to serve, freeing the apostles to preach and creating new opportunities for Philip and Stephen to move into powerful ministry.
  - title: "Service, not status, unlocks calling:"
    body: Faithfulness in small, unglamorous tasks — washing cups, handing out food — positions you to be trusted with greater things.
  - title: "The result was explosive growth:"
    body: As priorities were realigned and people were empowered to serve, the Word spread and even many priests came to faith.

keyIllustration: "A diver separated from his group in the ocean notices a chariot-like vehicle approaching underwater. He signals wildly, and the driver stops. The passenger inside — a high official — asks if he knows the way back. The diver explains the route, and as they part, the official is baptised right there in the water. Weeks later, entire communities in the official's homeland are hearing the gospel. It all started because the diver was willing to serve in an ordinary, unglamorous role — guiding lost swimmers back to shore."

application:
  - "Ask yourself honestly: Are you serving somewhere in the body of Christ, or are you waiting for something \"significant\" enough? Faithfulness in washing communion cups or welcoming guests positions you for what God has next."
  - "If you're overwhelmed by demands that pull you away from your calling, it may be time to delegate — not because the tasks don't matter, but because others need the opportunity to serve and grow."
  - "When conflict erupts, don't just try to suppress it. Ask what God might be exposing or redirecting through it."

toRemember: "The Lord doesn't need superheroes — just willing, broken, ordinary people ready to give themselves so He is glorified."
