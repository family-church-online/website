# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

keyScriptureRef: "Isaiah 12:2 ESV"

mainPoints:
  - title: Joseph used listening and obedience as his strongest tools
    body: "God didn't give Joseph a sword to protect Jesus—He gave him dreams and instructions. When the angel warned Joseph to flee to Egypt, immediate obedience became more powerful than Herod's entire army."
  - title: "Mary's song was built from Scripture she had memorized"
    body: "When joy and fear overwhelmed her, Mary didn't invent new words—she sang the promises of God she already knew. Her Magnificat echoes Hannah's song and Moses' song because God's Word was already alive in her heart."
  - title: Scripture memorized becomes your song in the darkest night
    body: "When pain or fear makes prayer impossible, the verses you've hidden in your heart become the song that strengthens you. Even when you can barely think, God's truth recited over and over pushes back the darkness."

keyIllustration: "A man named Horatio Spafford lost his wife and daughters when their ship sank. In the deepest grief imaginable, he penned the hymn \"It Is Well with My Soul.\" His strength didn't come from pretending the pain wasn't real—it came from singing the truth that Jesus died and rose again, and that he would one day be reunited with those he loved. The song gave him perspective to say, \"Yes, I'm hurting—but it is still well with my soul.\""

application:
  - Memorize Scripture now so it becomes your song when fear paralyzes you later
  - "Trust that simple obedience to God's Word is more powerful than any strategy you can devise"
  - "Let God's truth recited over and over be the light that pushes back your darkest nights"
