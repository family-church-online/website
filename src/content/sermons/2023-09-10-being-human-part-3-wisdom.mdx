# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Wisdom isn't processing God through your knowledge—it's processing your world through knowledge of God, and that begins with prayer."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 9:37-38 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Then he said to his disciples, \"The harvest is plentiful, but the laborers are few; therefore pray earnestly to the Lord of the harvest to send out laborers into his harvest.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus showed His disciples how to do the work before He sent them.
    body: "He walked with them through towns and villages, teaching and healing, modeling ministry before commissioning them. True leadership doesn't just tell—it shows."
  - title: "We've gotten wisdom backwards."
    body: "We process God through our knowledge instead of processing our knowledge through God. The world has \"nuclear giants and ethical infants\"—brilliance without wisdom, power without conscience."
  - title: Prayer equips us for mission.
    body: "Jesus didn't tell His disciples to strategize or recruit—He told them to pray. Prayer gives us time to consider the task, understand what's required, and wait to be sent rather than rushing ahead in our own zeal."
  - title: People need to be shown who Jesus is.
    body: "Many haven't rejected Christ—they simply haven't seen Him. Your neighbor, your coworker, your friend may be waiting for someone to show them the light in the darkness."

keyIllustration: "A man named Omar Bradley, a major general in World War II, addressed a crowd in 1948 and said: \"We have grasped the mystery of the atom and rejected the Sermon on the Mount. Man is stumbling blindly through spiritual darkness while toying with the secrets of life and death. The world has achieved brilliance without wisdom, power without conscience. Ours is a world of nuclear giants and ethical infants.\" His words ring even truer today—we have more information than ever, but we lack the wisdom to live by."

application:
  - Pray about your mission before you act. Ask God to show you who needs to see Him through you, and wait to be sent rather than rushing ahead.
  - Stop assuming people already know who Jesus is. They may have heard the name, but they need someone to show them who He truly is.
  - Seek knowledge of God above all else. Let that shape how you interpret the world around you—not the other way around.

toRemember: The harvest is still plentiful, the laborers are still few, and the mission still begins with prayer.
