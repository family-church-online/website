# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "If you have truly learned Christ — meaning you've been born again by the Holy Spirit — you cannot go back to your old way of life. Transformation is not cosmetic; it's a total renewal of mind that shows itself in how you live."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 4:20-24 ESV"
keyScriptureText: But that is not the way you learned Christ — assuming that you have heard about him and were taught in him, as the truth is in Jesus, to put off your old self, which belongs to your former manner of life and is corrupt through deceitful desires, and to be renewed in the spirit of your minds, and to put on the new self, created after the likeness of God in true righteousness and holiness.

mainPoints:
  - title: Learning Christ is a one-time event, not ongoing education.
    body: "Like a child who suddenly learns to ride a bike, there is a moment when everything converges — knowledge, the Spirit's work, and rebirth — and you become able to obey God. That's when you \"learned Christ.\""
  - title: "You can't hear God's voice until He speaks directly to you."
    body: Just as Lazarus was dead until Jesus called him out of the tomb, we are spiritually dead until Christ calls us to life. Hearing His voice is the beginning of salvation.
  - title: Putting off the old self requires partnership with the Spirit.
    body: "Transformation happens when you cooperate with the Holy Spirit's work by disciplining your thoughts, setting your mind on heavenly things, and refusing to let worldly passions dominate."
  - title: What your mind is focused on determines what pulls you off course.
    body: "If your thoughts are anchored in Christ's mission — making disciples, raising children to know Him, reaching the lost — distractions lose their power over you."

keyIllustration: "A father accidentally cut his finger while trying to open medication for his daughter on a dark morning during load shedding. On the way to school, a taxi driver deliberately cut him off in traffic — a provocation that would normally trigger anger. But because his mind was entirely focused on his bleeding finger, he didn't even notice the offense. Later he realized: what his mind was set on changed the entire outcome of that moment. If we keep our minds set on Christ and His purposes, the enemy's provocations lose their grip."

application:
  - Ask the Holy Spirit to renew your mind daily, then cooperate by actively setting your thoughts on things above — not passively waiting for change to happen to you.
  - "Guard your heart and keep your passions few. Don't let hobbies, relationships, or pleasures slowly pull your focus away from making disciples and serving Christ."
  - "If you're drifting, don't wait — bring other believers around you and ask for prayer and accountability before you lose your way entirely."

toRemember: "You were bought at a great price — not just to be saved, but to do God's work in this world. Don't let the enemy distract you or pull you back into darkness."
