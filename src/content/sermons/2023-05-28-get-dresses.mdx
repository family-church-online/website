  - title: Learning Christ is a one-time event, not ongoing education.
    body: "Like a child who suddenly learns to ride a bike, there is a moment when everything converges — knowledge, the Spirit's work, and rebirth — and you become able to obey God. That's when you \"learned Christ.\""
  - title: "You can't hear God's voice until He speaks directly to you."
    body: Just as Lazarus was dead until Jesus called him out of the tomb, we are spiritually dead until Christ calls us to life. Hearing His voice is the beginning of salvation.
  - title: Putting off the old self requires partnership with the Spirit.
    body: "Transformation happens when you cooperate with the Holy Spirit's work by disciplining your thoughts, setting your mind on heavenly things, and refusing to let worldly passions dominate."
  - title: What your mind is focused on determines what pulls you off course.
    body: "If your thoughts are anchored in Christ's mission — making disciples, raising children to know Him, reaching the lost — distractions lose their power over you."

keyIllustration: "A father accidentally cut his finger while trying to open medication for his daughter on a dark morning during load shedding. But because his mind was entirely focused on his bleeding finger, he didn't even notice the offense. Later he realized: what his mind was set on changed the entire outcome of that moment. If we keep our minds set on Christ and His purposes, the enemy's provocations lose their grip."

  - "If you're drifting, don't wait — bring other believers around you and ask for prayer and accountability before you lose your way entirely."
