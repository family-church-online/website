mainPoints:
  - title: You were darkness, now you are light.
    body: "Paul doesn't say you were in darkness. You were darkness — you cooperated with it, hid in it, preferred it. Now you are fundamentally different. This is not reformation. This is re-creation."
  - title: Do not partner with those who are still in darkness.
    body: Friendship, yes. Love, yes. Service, yes. But partnership — joint responsibility, joint risk, shared mission — requires shared identity. Light and darkness do not share identity.
  - title: Light exposes darkness simply by being present.
    body: "You don't need to preach at people to expose darkness. A missionary was killed for never evangelizing — because her presence alone made people want what she had. When light is real, it reveals."
  - title: Be careful even when speaking about darkness.
    body: Talking about sin in too much detail can re-contaminate us. Jude says to save others by snatching them from the fire, but show mercy with fear, hating even the garment stained by the flesh.

keyIllustration: "Light is both particle and wave — and physicists still don't understand how. Worse, light behaves differently when observed. Some now claim this proves human consciousness shapes reality, that we determine truth. But believers know better. We don't need to master quantum mechanics to know who Jesus is. He is the light of the world, and His wisdom is the only truth we need to live with purpose."

application:
  - Evaluate your partnerships — business, relational, financial. Are you yoked to darkness in ways that compromise your ability to walk as light?
  - Stop trying to fit into a world that no longer makes sense to you. The disorientation you feel is evidence the transformation is real.
  - Ask the Lord to show you His will in the grey areas. Walking as children of light requires discernment, and discernment requires surrender.

toRemember: Not all the darkness in the universe can extinguish even the tiniest bit of light. You are that light. And the darkness has not overcome it.
