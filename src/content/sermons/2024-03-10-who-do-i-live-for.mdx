# ── ABOUT LISTS ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
takeaways:
  - That your old guilt-laden self died with Christ and no longer defines you
  - That Christ now lives in you by His Spirit, empowering you to live differently
  - "That living by faith means trusting in Christ's finished work, not your own effort"
  - That you belong to Him—body, time, choices—because He bought you with His life
  - That gratitude, not guilt or ambition, becomes the motive for surrender

audience:
  - You wake up weighed down by guilt over sins you know God has forgiven
  - You feel powerless against temptation and wonder if real change is possible
  - "You're living for your career, reputation, or comfort instead of the One who gave Himself for you"
  - "You need to be reminded that your life is not your own—and why that's good news"

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You have been crucified with Christ—freed from guilt and sin's control—so that you might live not for yourself, but for the One who loved you and gave Himself for you."
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 2:20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: I have been crucified with Christ. It is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me. And the life I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me.

mainPoints:
  - title: You died with Christ—your guilt is cancelled.
    body: When you believed, your old self was crucified with Him; the debt you owed is gone, and you are no longer condemned.
  - title: "Christ now lives in you—sin's control is broken."
    body: You are not powerless anymore; the Holy Spirit dwells in you, empowering you to live in a way you never could on your own.
  - title: You live by faith in the Son of God, not by sight.
    body: Faith is not confidence in yourself or your circumstances; it is trust in Christ, who loved you and gave Himself for you.
  - title: "To add anything to grace is to say Christ's death wasn't enough."
    body: If you could be made right by keeping the law or doing good works, then Jesus died for no purpose.

keyIllustration: A South African rand note belongs not to the person holding it, but to the Reserve Bank—you cannot legally deface it or destroy it because it is not yours. In the same way, if you belong to Christ, your body, your time, your choices are not your own. You were bought with a price—His life given for yours.

application:
  - Stop living under the weight of guilt for sins Christ has already paid for—you died with Him, and that old you is gone.
  - "Wake up each morning and ask, \"Lord, what are we going to do today?\"—because your life belongs to Him, not to you."
  - Serve Him not to earn His love, but out of gratitude for the love He has already shown you.

toRemember: You are not your own. You were bought with a price. Glorify God in your body.
