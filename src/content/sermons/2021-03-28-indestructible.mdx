# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus is an indestructible high priest who lives forever, intercedes for you now, and has the power to cleanse what you cannot fix yourself.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 7:23-25 ESV"
keyScriptureText: The former priests were many in number, because they were prevented by death from continuing in office, but He holds His priesthood permanently, because He continues forever. Consequently, He is able to save to the uttermost those who draw near to God through Him, since He always lives to make intercession for them.

mainPoints:
  - title: The law could not make anyone perfect.
    body: It exposed our inability to obey God in our own strength and acted as a guide until Christ came to fulfil it fully.
  - title: Jesus is a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.
    body: "Unlike the Levitical priesthood, which was limited by death and personal sin, Jesus' priesthood is based on His indestructible life — He was raised from the dead and lives forever."
  - title: Jesus is interceding for you right now.
    body: Because He is alive and seated at the right hand of the Father, He is actively speaking on your behalf, taking your pain and sin to God and securing your forgiveness and restoration.
  - title: No human authority can save you from death.
    body: "Self-help programmes, gurus, and even religious leaders cannot conquer death — only Jesus defeated it, proving He alone has the authority and power to bring you into God's presence."

keyIllustration: A diver separated from his group in murky water begins to panic, checking his oxygen and searching frantically for the surface. His training tells him to stop, breathe, and trust his equipment — but panic overrides reason. Only when he forces himself to pause and trust does he find his way back. In the same way, when stress and regret overwhelm you, the world offers frantic solutions — but only pausing and surrendering to Christ brings true peace.

application:
  - Stop turning to substitutes for peace — alcohol, pornography, self-help programmes — and ask Jesus to cleanse the exchange tables you have set up in the temple of your body.
  - Daily surrender your life to Christ, acknowledging that you are not your own and that only He can heal the wounds of your past and calm your fears about the future.
  - "Trust that Christ's word and work are enough — He lives, He intercedes, and He will carry you through every struggle by the power of His Holy Spirit."

toRemember: Christ in you is the hope of glory — and because He is indestructible, He will make you indestructible too.
