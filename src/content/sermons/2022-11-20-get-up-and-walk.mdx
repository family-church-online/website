# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "You cannot appreciate the miracle of salvation until you understand you were spiritually dead—incapable of saving yourself—and that only Jesus' command to \"rise\" brought you to life."
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 2:1-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And you were dead in your trespasses and sins in which you once walked, following the course of this world, following the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that is now at work in the sons of disobedience, among whom we all once lived in the passions of our flesh, carrying out the desires of the body and the mind, and were by nature children of wrath, like the rest of mankind.

mainPoints:
  - title: You were dead in sin.
    body: Not sick, not struggling—dead. Spiritually unable to respond to God, obey Him, or save yourself. Sin earns death, and every human being faces this verdict until Jesus intervenes.
  - title: Three enemies constantly draw you away.
    body: "The world system glorifies sin and rejects God's authority. Satan, the prince of the power of the air, deceives you into believing you can be your own god. Your own sinful desires—the passions of the flesh—compel you toward rebellion from within."
  - title: You are either a child of God or a child of wrath.
    body: "There is no neutral ground. God's wrath rests on humanity because of sin, and only through faith in Jesus Christ are you transferred from death to life, from wrath to adoption."
  - title: Jesus alone raises the dead.
    body: "Just as He commanded Jairus' daughter to rise, He speaks life into your spiritual death. You contribute nothing but the corpse. He does all the work of rescue, healing, and restoration."

keyIllustration: "A woman was attacked by a leopard at a game lodge. Her carotid artery was punctured, her femoral artery destroyed. By all accounts, she should have died. But a child screamed, a staff member called for help, a man sprinted 500 meters and kicked the leopard off unarmed, a paramedic on-site stemmed the bleeding, a helicopter happened to be nearby, surgeons rebuilt her leg. Months later, against all odds, she walked down the aisle at her wedding. The illustration reveals that rescue required a chain of miracles—none of which she could perform for herself. She was powerless. God orchestrated every detail. This is the picture of salvation: you are the victim, helpless and dying, and God mobilises heaven and earth to raise you to life."

application:
  - "Stop pretending you have it together. The church is not a gathering of respectable people—it's a community of rescued corpses who know they owe everything to Jesus."
  - "If you've never surrendered to Christ, today is the day. Admit you are spiritually dead and ask Jesus to raise you. Believe He died for your sins and was raised to give you life."
  - "Once raised, your job is to carry the message to others still dead in sin. You may be the child who screams for help, the one who makes the call, or the one who holds the umbrella in the storm. Play your part in God's rescue mission."

toRemember: "You were dead. Jesus said \"get up\"—and only by His power do you walk."
