Heal us, restore us, refresh us, renew and perfect us spiritually as you did physically for that man affected forty years. Only you can do that, Lord Jesus. Be glorified today in helping us to trust in your son, our Lord Jesus Christ. Be glorified today in us receiving the full power and promise of the Holy Spirit. Be glorified today in turning us, each one of us, from our wickedness for the glory of your name, now and forever. In Jesus' name, amen.
