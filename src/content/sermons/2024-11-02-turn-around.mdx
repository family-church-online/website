# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus died not merely to forgive your sin, but to bless you by turning you from your wickedness—repentance is the fruit and the purpose of grace.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 3:25-26 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "You are the sons of the prophets and of the covenant that God made with your fathers, saying to Abraham, 'And in your offspring shall all the families of the earth be blessed.' God, having raised up his servant, sent him to you first to bless you by turning every one of you from your wickedness."

mainPoints:
  - title: Cling to Christ, not your independence.
    body: "The healed man didn't run off to show what he could do—he clung to Peter and John, praising God. Gratitude is expressed in dependence, not distance."
  - title: "Your worst failure didn't break God's plan."
    body: "Peter tells the crowd they killed the Author of Life—but that God's sovereign plan was never derailed. Our sin is real, but it's not sovereign."
  - title: Repentance is the blessing, not the burden.
    body: "God raised Jesus to bless Abraham's offspring by turning them from wickedness. Forgiveness without transformation is incomplete—the goal is Christlikeness."
  - title: Affliction can bear fruit for eternity.
    body: "One man's forty years of suffering became the platform for thousands to repent. God redeems what He permits."

keyIllustration: "A man born lame sat outside the temple gate for forty years—never walking, never dancing, never running. After Peter healed him in Jesus' name, his story became the catalyst for over two thousand people to repent and believe. His decades of affliction, surrendered to Christ, were transformed into eternal fruit. What if your suffering is not wasted, but appointed for the salvation of others?"

application:
  - Stop clinging to self-sufficiency and start clinging to the church—God sends skin and bone to comfort you.
  - Let conviction lead you to repentance, not shame. The Holy Spirit convicts in order to heal, not to crush.
  - Surrender your affliction to God. Ask Him to use it for the comfort and salvation of others, not just your own relief.

toRemember: Jesus took His affliction on the cross and turned it into eternal life for us—He can do the same with yours.
