# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Jesus came humbly once and will return in power to overthrow all opposition — and until that day, you are called to fix your eyes on Him and remain totally dependent on His strength.
keyScriptureRef: "Zechariah 9:9 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion! Shout aloud, O daughter of Jerusalem! Behold, your king is coming to you; righteous and having salvation is he, humble and mounted on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey."

mainPoints:
  - title: Zechariah prophesied two arrivals separated by centuries.
    body: "In verses 9 and 10 of chapter 9, Zechariah foretold both Christ's first coming on a donkey and His second coming to rule from sea to sea — with thousands of years between them."
  - title: Jerusalem welcomed Alexander the Great but rejected Jesus.
    body: Two hundred years before Christ, Alexander rode into Jerusalem on a warhorse and was welcomed; five hundred years later, Jesus rode in on a donkey and was rejected by the religious elite.
  - title: Jesus will return to the Mount of Olives and split it in two.
    body: "Zechariah 14 foretells the day when Christ's feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, causing a massive earthquake that will create a valley for His people to escape through."
  - title: Until He returns, your job is to hold on and not let go.
    body: You are not called to independence but to total dependence — fixing your eyes on Jesus through daily prayer, Scripture, worship, and obedience no matter what pressure you face.

keyIllustration: "A twelve-year-old girl struggling to dive deeper than five metres held onto the instructor's jacket straps and fixed her eyes on his face. She never looked away. As they descended past her barrier — six metres, ten, fifteen — she stayed locked in. Only when they reached the reef and she finally looked around did the joy and gratitude flood in. The point: she never let go. Neither should we."

application:
  - Stop defaulting to self-reliance when pressure increases. Grab hold of Jesus, fix your eyes on Him, and refuse to let go — no matter how deep the pressure or how dark the circumstances.
  - "If God is calling you into service or deeper study, don't wait. Enrol in a small group, commit to daily Scripture and prayer, and obey what the Holy Spirit is saying now."
  - "Don't be distracted by fear or deceived by lies about the end times. Jesus told us to stay ready by doing what He's called us to do — and that means being faithfully present in His Word and His presence every single day."

toRemember: "The time is coming when Jesus will quiet every enemy, take you by the hand, and say, \"Come, let's go have a look\" — and you will swim hand in hand with Him through the glories of His perfected creation for all eternity."
