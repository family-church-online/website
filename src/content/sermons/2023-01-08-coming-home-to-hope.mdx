# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Without faith in Jesus Christ as the Son of God who died in our place and rose for the forgiveness of our sins, we have no hope and no God — and the most loving thing we can do is speak that truth.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 2:12-13 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Remember that you were at that time separated from Christ, alienated from the commonwealth of Israel and strangers to the covenants of promise, having no hope and without God in the world. But now in Christ Jesus, you who once were far off have been brought near by the blood of Christ.

mainPoints:
  - title: We are told to remember our past exclusion.
    body: Paul commands Gentile believers to recall when they were mocked, alienated, and treated as outsiders by the Jews — not to reopen wounds, but to show the magnitude of grace. Remembering where we came from magnifies what Christ has done.
  - title: Without Christ, we had no God.
    body: "The word translated \"without God\" is atheist — not someone who denies God's existence, but someone who has no relationship with Him. Every religion that rejects Jesus as the Son of God leaves people in that state, no matter how devout they are."
  - title: Jesus is God, or He is nothing.
    body: "Scripture is unambiguous: Jesus claimed to be equal with God, accepted worship, and said salvation depends on believing in Him. If He is not God in the flesh, His death cannot save us — only God can pay for our sins because only He can forgive them."
  - title: The gospel is offensive because it is exclusive.
    body: "Saying Jesus is the only way sounds intolerant, but it is the most generous offer in existence: salvation is a free gift, not something you earn. Pretending all paths lead to God is not compassionate — it leaves people lost."

keyIllustration: "A young pregnant woman entered an abortion clinic, shielded by staff so protesters couldn't reach her. But one woman's voice broke through: \"Your baby has ten fingers and ten toes.\" Inside the clinic, those words haunted her. She changed her mind. Years later, her son David met her and brought a book filled with letters from people whose lives she'd touched by giving him life. The same offensive words that hurt her had saved him."

application:
  - Accept that without faith in Jesus Christ as the Son of God, people have no hope — even if they are deeply religious.
  - Speak the truth in love, knowing that what feels offensive to the hearer may be the very thing that saves their life.
  - "Reject the world's accusation that Christianity is intolerant; the gospel is the only message that offers salvation by grace, not by human effort."

toRemember: "Jesus says, \"Come home. You belong to Me. I bought you with My blood. I love you. I forgive you. You are My child.\""
