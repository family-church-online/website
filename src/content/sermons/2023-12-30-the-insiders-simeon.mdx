# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Simeon was ready for the Messiah because he devoted his life to waiting for God's promises—and when Jesus arrived, the Holy Spirit led him to the exact moment he needed to be there."
keyScriptureRef: "Luke 2:25-26 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Now there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon. This man was righteous and devout, waiting for the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit was upon him, and it had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he would not see death before he had seen the Lord's Christ."

mainPoints:
  - title: Simeon was characterized by what he was waiting for.
    body: "He wasn't waiting for comfort or success—he was waiting for God's promise to console Israel. What you're waiting for in the year ahead reveals what you truly value."
  - title: God gave Simeon a personal promise.
    body: The Holy Spirit revealed to Simeon that he would see the Messiah before he died. God keeps His promises—and He speaks personally to those who seek Him with all their heart.
  - title: Simeon was led by the Spirit to the right place at the right time.
    body: "He didn't plan his day around his own agenda. He went to the temple \"in the Spirit,\" and walked in just as Mary and Joseph brought Jesus in. Being led by the Spirit requires setting your mind on God daily."
  - title: Jesus is a dividing line.
    body: Simeon warned Mary that her son would cause the fall and rise of many in Israel—some would embrace Him, others would reject Him violently. Following Jesus brings both deep love and costly opposition.

  - "Review your goals for the year and ask: what do I really want? If your deepest desire isn't centered on God, this is the year to let Him reshape your heart."
