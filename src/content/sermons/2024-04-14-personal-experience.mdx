Paul confronts the Galatians (and you) with one devastatingly simple question: Did the Spirit work in you because you deserved it, or because you believed?"
subtitle: If the Holy Spirit came to you by faith alone, why would you try to continue the Christian life by human effort?

If you started in the Spirit, you cannot finish in the flesh."

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: If you received the Holy Spirit by faith when you heard the gospel, you cannot now be perfected by your own effort—God began the work in you, and He will finish it.
keyScriptureRef: "Galatians 3:2-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Let me ask you only this: Did you receive the Spirit by works of the law or by hearing with faith? Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now being perfected by the flesh?"

mainPoints:
  - title: Paul appeals to their personal experience.
    body: "He asks: when you received the Holy Spirit, was it because you obeyed the law, or because you believed the gospel? Their own memory testifies to the truth of grace."
  - title: You cannot finish what the Spirit began.
    body: If God started the work of salvation in you by His Spirit, you cannot now take over and complete it by human effort. Dependence on God is not a stage—it is the entirety of the Christian life.
  - title: Legalism wastes what Christ has done.
    body: "To add human effort to the finished work of the cross is to say Christ's death was not enough. That is tragic beyond measure."
  - title: God supplies the Spirit and works miracles by faith, not works.
    body: "The Spirit's ongoing work in the believer—His presence, power, and miracles—is sustained by faith in the gospel, not by rituals or rule-keeping."

keyIllustration: "When the Israelites were dying from snakebites in the wilderness, God told Moses to lift a bronze serpent on a pole. Anyone who looked at it would live. They didn't have to do anything—just look. Jesus said, \"As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in Him may have eternal life.\" Salvation comes from looking to Christ crucified, not from doing anything to earn it."

application:
  - When you feel spiritually dry or distant from God, go back to the cross. Meditate on what Christ endured for you. That love—not your effort—changes you.
  - Stop trying to impress God or manipulate Him with your obedience. He gave you His Spirit by grace. Trust Him to sustain you by that same grace.
  - "If you're battling sin, remember: you cannot defeat it in your own strength. Confess your dependence, look to the cross, and ask the Spirit to do what only He can do."

toRemember: He who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ.
