# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God allows the shaking of all temporary things so that what cannot be shaken — His Word, His promises, and our eternal relationship with Him — may remain and give us peace.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 12:26-27 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Yet once more I will shake not only the earth but also the heavens. This phrase, \"yet once more,\" indicates the removal of things that are shaken—that is, things that have been made—in order that the things that cannot be shaken may remain."

mainPoints:
  - title: "The Jewish Christians' world was being shaken to its core."
    body: Hebrews was written before 70 AD to believers facing riots, siege, mass starvation, and the impending destruction of the Jerusalem temple — everything they had trusted in was being torn apart.
  - title: There are five progressive warnings about falling away from the faith.
    body: "Don't drift from the Word by neglecting it. Don't doubt the Word. Don't become dull to it. Don't deliberately disobey it. Don't reject it outright. Each step leads further from peace and closer to judgment."
  - title: The shaking is purposeful.
    body: God allows temporary, material things to be shaken so that eternal realities — His Word, His Son, His unshakable kingdom — are revealed as the only secure foundation.
  - title: Draw near to the Word through the four pillars.
    body: Engage in scripture, prayer, worship, and service. These are not optional extras — they are how the church remains the church, no matter what is shaken around us.

keyIllustration: Imagine all the office pot plants around the world sitting neglected during lockdown — no one watering them, no one tending them. They slowly wither and die from neglect. We are no different spiritually. If we neglect the Word of God, we will drift, doubt, and eventually die spiritually. Regular watering from scripture is not optional — it is how we survive.

application:
  - "Do not let this season of upheaval numb you to God's Word. Use reading plans, devotions, study guides — whatever keeps you actively engaged with scripture every single day."
  - Recognize that God is shaking your temporary comforts to reveal what is eternal. Ask Him to help you release what cannot last and cling to what cannot be moved.
  - "Return to the four pillars: scripture, prayer, worship, and service. Wherever you are, however isolated, you can still be the church by doing these things."

toRemember: You will not just survive this shaking — you will find peace in it if your eyes remain fixed on Jesus and your heart remains rooted in His unchanging Word.
