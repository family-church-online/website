# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Christmas is not just about celebrating the birth of Jesus—it's about surrendering to the King He came to be."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 2:1-2 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Now after Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of Herod the king, behold, wise men from the east came to Jerusalem, saying, \"Where is he who has been born king of the Jews? For we saw his star when it rose and have come to worship him.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "Jesus is Prophet: He speaks the Word of God."
    body: "Unlike the prophets who said \"thus says the Lord,\" Jesus claimed to be God speaking directly. He reveals the truth about who we are and what we need."
  - title: "Jesus is Priest: He offers Himself as the sacrifice."
    body: He intercedes for us, takes the punishment we earned, and reconciles us to the Father by grace through faith.
  - title: "Jesus is King: He demands our allegiance."
    body: The wise men worshipped Him. Herod feared Him. We must choose whether to surrender control of our lives to the King of Kings, or cling to autonomy like Herod did.

keyIllustration: "A massive rugby player told the speaker, \"If anyone gives you trouble, just call me.\" But even that security didn't inspire surrender. Only when the speaker understood who Jesus is—Creator, Saviour, King—did true, joyful submission become possible. Surrendering to Jesus isn't a loss of freedom; it's the peace of letting the King drive."

application:
  - Stop trying to impress God with good works. Trust Him as King and let Him lead your life.
  - "Ask yourself: am I receiving Jesus like the wise men, or resisting Him like Herod?"
  - Remember that Jesus is returning—not to suffer, but to rule. Those who surrender to Him now will reign with Him forever.

toRemember: "It's one thing to say Jesus is King. It's another to kneel before Him in joy, trusting that He will do a better job of running your life than you ever could."
