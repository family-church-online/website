# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: God allows false prophets into places of power not because He is absent, but so that when His servants arrive, the contrast between demonic deception and Holy Spirit authority becomes unmistakable—and leaders hungry for truth are astonished by His word.
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 13:9-12 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "But Saul, who was also called Paul, filled with the Holy Spirit, looked intently at him and said, \"You son of the devil, you enemy of all righteousness, full of all deceit and villainy, will you not stop making crooked the straight paths of the Lord? And now, behold, the hand of the Lord is upon you, and you will be blind and unable to see the sun for a time.\" Immediately mist and darkness fell upon him, and he went about seeking people to lead him by the hand. Then the proconsul believed when he saw what had occurred, for he was astonished at the teaching of the Lord."

mainPoints:
  - title: Everyone is being led by something or someone.
    body: "You may think you're self-made, but your decisions are shaped by desires, fears, past pain, or voices you've trusted—the question is whether you're following the right leader."
  - title: Opposition intensifies when you bear fruit.
    body: "Paul and Barnabas didn't encounter the false prophet until they were making an impact; if your obedience to God isn't costing you anything, it may not be threatening the enemy."
  - title: "God's faithfulness outlasts our unfaithfulness."
    body: "Despite Israel's repeated rejection, Paul always preached to the Jews first—showing that God keeps His promises even when His people don't keep theirs."
  - title: The enemy positions false prophets near seats of power.
    body: "Bar-Jesus (Elymas) wasn't working in obscurity—he was advising the governor, blinding him to truth, because Satan knows influence multiplies deception."
  - title: "God's word astonishes more than any miracle."
    body: Sergius Paulus believed when he saw Elymas struck blind, but he was astonished by the teaching of the Lord—because the greatest miracle is the gospel that saves eternally.

keyIllustration: "A rugby player who has a phenomenal game attracts the hardest hits from the opposition—because he's dangerous. If you're useless on the field, falling over, can't catch the ball, the defence ignores you. The moment you start bearing fruit for God, expect the enemy to hit harder—not because you're losing, but because you've become a threat."

application:
  - Stop chasing miracles and cry out for the word of God—it will astonish you and anchor you when opposition comes.
  - If you lead others, especially many people, expect loneliness and the temptation to trust the wrong voices; submit to Scripture and the Holy Spirit for wisdom.
  - "Don't be surprised when doing the right thing attracts attacks; opposition is proof you're advancing the kingdom, not evidence you're off course."

toRemember: The servant of God filled with the Holy Spirit will always dwarf and make a mockery of demonic power—because He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world.
