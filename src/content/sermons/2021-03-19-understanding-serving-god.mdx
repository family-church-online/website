# ── PRESENTATION COPY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
tagLine: What if every task you perform today was an act of worship or an act of sin?
shortDescription: "You know Jesus died for you — but do you serve Him like He's Lord? Every moment, every task, every decision is either worship or disobedience. Nothing is secular when Christ is King. No reward is possible when you serve in your own strength."
subtitle: Nothing in your life — work, family, leisure, struggle — is secular when Jesus is Lord.
hook: "Can you really serve God with your whole heart when you're stuck in traffic, buried in work emails, or scrubbing dishes? If Jesus is truly Lord, then there is no such thing as secular service — only worship or disobedience."

# ── ABOUT LISTS ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
takeaways:
  - Jesus is Lord over every area of life — not just Sundays
  - God rewards faithfulness in small, hidden tasks more than spectacular visible ones
  - Serving in your own strength produces nothing eternal; abiding in Christ bears much fruit
  - Humility, not ambition, is the mark of Christ-like service
  - The Holy Spirit is available to anyone who asks — no matter how weak you feel

audience:
  - "You've split your life into \"spiritual\" Sundays and \"secular\" weekdays"
  - "You're exhausted from serving God in your own willpower"
  - You wonder whether the ordinary, hidden tasks of life matter to God
  - You want to understand what it means to make Jesus Lord — not just Saviour

# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Every action — whether at work, at home, or at church — must be done as an act of worship to Christ the Lord, or it becomes sin.
keyScriptureRef: "Colossians 3:23-24 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters, since you know that you will receive an inheritance from the Lord as a reward. It is the Lord Christ you are serving.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus Christ is the center of Christian faith — not the church, Bible, or any religious activity.
    body: Our faith is in the person of Jesus, and we are His disciples, following His example in every area of life.
  - title: Serve God with all your heart, willingly, faithfully, and in humility.
    body: "Joseph's life demonstrates this — whether enslaved, falsely accused, or imprisoned, he served as an act of worship, and God honored him."
  - title: Apart from Christ, you can do nothing.
    body: Service in your own strength is futile; only those who abide in the vine will bear lasting fruit.

keyIllustration: A man leased a farm and prepared the soil to plant seeds. Before sowing, he gathered all the workers — even those on the far end of the field — into a circle. They prayed together, acknowledging that though they plowed and scattered seed, only God could give life to what they planted. The farm produced remarkable crops — not because of technique, but because every step was an act of worship.

application:
  - "Stop dividing life into \"spiritual\" and \"secular\" — if Jesus is Lord, then everything you do is either worship or sin."
  - Ask the Father for the Holy Spirit if you feel weak or uncertain — He promises to give Him to anyone who asks.
  - Pursue faithfulness in small, hidden tasks rather than chasing recognition or spectacular service.

toRemember: It is enough that Jesus died — and that He died for you.
