# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Jesus' death activated a last will and testament naming you as an heir—ransoming you from sin's slavery, giving you the Holy Spirit now, and reserving an imperishable inheritance in heaven."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 9:15-17 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Therefore he is the mediator of a new covenant, so that those who are called may receive the promised eternal inheritance, since a death has occurred that redeems them from the transgressions committed under the first covenant. For where a will is involved, the death of the one who made it must be established, for a will takes effect only at death since it is not in force as long as the one who made it is alive.

mainPoints:
  - title: Jesus Is the Mediator Between You and God
    body: "You were God's enemy, deserving only wrath. Christ takes your hand and the Father's, reconciling you by His blood."
  - title: You Were Enslaved; Jesus Paid the Ransom
    body: "Real freedom is not doing whatever you want—it's being free from the compulsion of your own will. Jesus purchased that freedom with His life."
  - title: The Holy Spirit Is Your Present Inheritance
    body: At Pentecost, the will was activated. The Spirit convicts, regenerates, equips, and prays for you—reversing Babel, uniting the scattered, and making God known.
  - title: An Imperishable Kingdom Awaits You
    body: The Spirit is now; the kingdom is coming. God has kept an inheritance in heaven—unfading, undefiled, imperishable—for all who endure.

keyIllustration: "Sitting at the reading of a last will and testament is a mix of grief and gratitude—you hear the voice of someone you've lost making provision for you in their death. Hearing the gospel is like that: Jesus, having died, speaks directly to you through His will, naming you as an heir and leaving you an inheritance purchased at the cost of His life."

application:
  - If you have never repented and trusted Christ, today is the day to accept your inheritance. The will has been read—say yes.
  - Stop trying to earn what has already been paid for. Your freedom, your Spirit, your hope—they are gifts, not wages.
  - "When trials come, remember: they are not wasted. The Spirit uses them to build your faith and bring Christ victory over Satan in your life."

toRemember: The Holy Spirit is yours now. The kingdom is kept for you in heaven. Whatever you are suffering, it is not wasted—God will use every tear for unthinkably wonderful things.
