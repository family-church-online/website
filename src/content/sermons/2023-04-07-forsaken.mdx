# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Jesus' cry of abandonment wasn't confusion or despair — it was the fulfilment of prophecy and the moment He bore the wrath of God in our place, completing the payment for sin so we'd never face separation from the Father."
keyScriptureRef: "Mark 15:34"
keyScriptureText: "And at the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, \"Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?\" which means, \"My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?\""

mainPoints:
  - title: "Jesus quotes Psalm 22 to explain what's happening."
    body: After three hours of supernatural darkness, He directs witnesses to a thousand-year-old prophecy that foretold His mocking, piercing, nakedness, thirst — proving His death was no accident but the planned rescue of God.
  - title: "Jesus wasn't just a victim — He was a volunteer."
    body: He knew every detail beforehand, could have called twelve legions of angels to stop it, but chose to lay down His life because the Scriptures said it must be so.
  - title: "The cross wasn't only physical suffering — it was God's wrath poured out."
    body: "Sin's penalty isn't just death but eternal separation from God's love. Jesus faced that separation so we never would, enduring what we deserved in a moment of unfathomable anguish."
  - title: "\"It is finished\" means the debt is paid in full."
    body: "The Greek word tetelestai was used when taxes were stamped \"paid\" — Jesus declared the punishment complete, the record of sin cancelled, and the way back to the Father opened forever."

keyIllustration: "A student waiting to be caned for his wrongdoing suddenly hears a knock at the door. Someone whispers to the teacher, taps the student's shoulder, and says, \"You can go.\" Confused, he leaves — but as the door closes, he hears the blows fall. The substitute was the teacher's own innocent son. That's a pale shadow of the Father punishing His sinless Son in our place."

application:
  - "You can stop trying to pay for your own sin — Jesus declared \"tetelestai,\" paid in full."
  - "Hell is not a place where demons rule; it's where God's justice is satisfied, and Jesus took that for you."
  - "When you come to Communion, you're not mourning a victim — you're celebrating a Victor who chose the cross to bring you home."

toRemember: The God of the universe — hands that flung stars into space — offered Himself to suffer not only human cruelty but divine wrath, so that you would never be forsaken.
