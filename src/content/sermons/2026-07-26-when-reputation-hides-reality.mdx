# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Spiritual life cannot survive on reputation — you need to wake up to what God actually sees and let Him cleanse what is dying before it dies completely.
keyScriptureRef: "Revelation 3:1b ESV"
keyScriptureText: I know your works. You have the reputation of being alive, but you are dead.

mainPoints:
  - title: Reputation is not reality.
    body: The church at Sardis was celebrated by everyone and condemned by Jesus; popular approval is not evidence of spiritual health, and mistaking one for the other is precisely how a church dies.
  - title: Complacency kills slowly.
    body: A good reputation acts like sleep — it silences the alarm while love for others, desire to obey, prayer, and honesty quietly stop working.
  - title: Waking up is a daily choice.
    body: Jesus calls the church to remember what they received, keep it, and repent — not as a one-off crisis but as a daily return to honesty before God, cooperating with what He is doing in us.

keyIllustration: A trusted royal advisor served his king faithfully for years while concealing a deep personal grievance. The moment an opportunity came, he defected and offered counsel designed to destroy the very man he had appeared to serve. No one around the king could see the hidden resentment — illustrating how utterly invisible our true spiritual state can be, even to those closest to us, and how only God sees what is really there.

application:
  - "Take time this week to sit alone before God and pray: \"Lord, show me what I cannot see — what is dying, what needs to change.\" Trust that the One asking this of you is the same One who already loves you enough to clothe you in His righteousness."

toRemember: A church God has to call dead is not one the world despises — it is one the world praises; let His verdict, not theirs, be the one that wakes you.
