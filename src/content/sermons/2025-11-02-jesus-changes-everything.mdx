# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "When you reach your breaking point, Jesus doesn't demand superhuman strength—He provides exactly what you need, surrounds you with His people, and works powerfully through your weakness to accomplish eternal purposes."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 18:9-10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "And the Lord said to Paul one night in a vision, \"Do not be afraid, but go on speaking and do not be silent, for I am with you, and no one will attack you to harm you, for I have many in this city who are my people.\""

mainPoints:
  - title: Even giants break—Paul arrives burned out.
    body: After rejection in Athens, Paul reaches Corinth exhausted, afraid, and alone. Instead of immediately preaching, he searches for people and begins making tents—seeking community and practical support before resuming ministry.
  - title: Jesus meets you in layers of provision.
    body: God sends Aquila and Priscilla for shelter and work, then Silas and Timothy with financial support and partnership. Each provision builds on the last, equipping Paul not for comfort but for the mission God assigned him.
  - title: Your weakness becomes His platform.
    body: "Paul's inability to carry the work alone reveals what was always true: Jesus sustains His servants. The fruitfulness that explodes in Corinth—Crispus and his household, many Corinthians believing—comes not from Paul's strength but from abiding in Christ."
  - title: God protects until His purposes are complete.
    body: "Despite fierce opposition and a tribunal, Jesus keeps His promise: no weapon formed against Paul succeeds. Even Sosthenes, beaten for opposing Paul, later joins his ministry team—God's chain reactions turn every attack into eternal fruit."

keyIllustration: "A retired military officer gives advice to those battling depression: every morning when you get up, make your bed. If there's nothing else you can do that day, just make your bed. Every time you walk past it, you'll see you've done something—and that might give you momentum to do one more small thing. Paul, exhausted and afraid, picks up needle and thread to make tents—something concrete, something useful—while Jesus quietly rebuilds his strength."

application:
  - "When you're in the valley, don't isolate—find someone. The church exists to be Aquila and Priscilla for each other, offering shelter, work, meals, presence."
  - "Your suffering isn't wasted. God uses every hardship, every rejection, every moment of weakness to accomplish purposes you can't yet see—eternal fruit that glorifies Him."
  - "Submit your limits to Jesus. You don't need superpowers—Jesus is the superpower. Abiding in Him means trusting Him to carry what you can't, to provide what you lack, and to finish what He started."

toRemember: Apart from Christ, you can do nothing—but abiding in Him, you bear much fruit, even through weakness, fear, and suffering.
