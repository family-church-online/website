# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Faith is not measured by worldly success or failure, but by your response to God's faithfulness."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 11:39-40 ESV"
keyScriptureText: And all these, though commended through their faith, did not receive what was promised, since God had provided something better for us, that apart from us they should not be made perfect.

mainPoints:
  - title: Ordinary people made great by faith.
    body: Gideon hid from armies, Barak faced iron chariots, Samson struggled with faithfulness—but they conquered kingdoms, stopped lions, and escaped the sword because they trusted God.
  - title: Some heroes suffered instead of succeeding.
    body: The same chapter celebrates those who were stoned, sawn in two, and died in caves—not because their faith was weak, but because they refused to renounce God to escape torture.
  - title: "God doesn't see winners and losers the way we do."
    body: "Both groups are in the hall of faith because they responded to God's faithfulness, regardless of the outcome."
  - title: We are perfected together.
    body: "Old Testament saints and New Testament believers are one body, glorified together in Christ—God's plan includes all of us."

keyIllustration: "An uneducated shoemaker with a stutter was told by church elders to sit down when he shared his passion to take the gospel to India. His wife went insane. His son died. He labored seven years before seeing a single convert. By age seventy-three, William Carey had translated the Bible into thirty-four languages, founded India's first biblical college, and established forty-five teaching centers. Today, there are over twenty-five million Christians in India—five times the number in England. He persevered through faith, not because everything went well, but because he believed the gospel had power to transform lives."

application:
  - Stop measuring your faith by worldly outcomes—God measures it by your response to His faithfulness.
  - "If you're suffering for following Christ, you're not a failure—you're in the hall of faith with Zechariah, Isaiah, and countless others who refused to renounce God."
  - God is faithful. He will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear, and He will provide a way to endure.

toRemember: "The hall of fame is still open—your faith will be defined by how you respond to God's faithfulness, not by whether you conquer kingdoms or die in caves."
