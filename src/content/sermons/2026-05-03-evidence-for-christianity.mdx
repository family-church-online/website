mainPoints:
  - title: "Luke's historical accuracy is verifiable."
    body: "The depth soundings, the anchor field, the reef, the storm duration, the demographics, the governor's title, even the bacterial fever—every detail Luke recorded has been archaeologically or geographically confirmed."
  - title: Kindness without faith is not enough.
    body: The Maltese people were unusually kind, but they worshiped false gods and had no relationship with the living God—goodness does not equal salvation.
  - title: "Paul's grace toward his Roman captors reveals authentic faith."
    body: Paul stayed with and served Publius, a Roman official, even though Rome was persecuting him—this is what it looks like to love your enemies.
  - title: The gospel spreads like seed, not strategy.
    body: "Paul didn't plant a church on Malta or preach a crusade—he just lived like Jesus for three months, and that island became one of the earliest Christian strongholds in the Mediterranean."

keyIllustration: Three hundred square kilometres. Three hundred churches. Two thousand years later, Malta remains a Christian stronghold—not because Paul ran a campaign, but because he scattered seed.

application:
  - Stop picking and choosing what parts of Scripture to believe—if the mundane is true, the supernatural is too.
  - "Don't confuse niceness with salvation—relationship with God through Jesus is what saves, not morality."
  - Scatter the gospel wherever you go, by what you say and how you live, and trust Jesus to grow the seed.
