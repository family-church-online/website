# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Baptism does not save you — faith in Jesus Christ alone does. Baptism is the public declaration of what God has already done: forgiven your sins, made you His child, and filled you with His Spirit."
keyScriptureRef: "Matthew 28:19-20 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.

mainPoints:
  - title: Baptism is worship and obedience.
    body: It declares to the world what Christ has done for you. Jesus commanded it — not as an option, but as the first step of discipleship.
  - title: Baptism does not save you.
    body: "Cornelius received the Holy Spirit before he was baptised. The thief on the cross made no declaration except \"remember me\" — and Jesus said, \"Today you will be with me in paradise.\""
  - title: The four declarations you make before baptism are scriptural confessions of what you already believe.
    body: "You're not asking God to save you now — you're telling the world He already has."

keyIllustration: A man raised in a traditional Chinese home where the family burned incense to ancestors describes finding faith through a youth group in grade six. Years later, after straying and returning, he realises his first baptism was about curiosity, not transformation. Now married and walking with Christ, he stands to be baptised again — not to be saved, but to declare that grace has become the foundation of his life.

application:
  - "If you've been saved but never baptised as a believer, obedience is still waiting for you."
  - "Your story — however quiet, messy, or ordinary — is a declaration of God's faithfulness. Don't wait for a platform to share it."
  - "Baptism is not magic. It's a sign. The power is in the blood of Jesus and the work of the Spirit, not in the water."

toRemember: "When you've been married, you wear a ring to show the world. When you've been saved, you go under the water to declare it."
