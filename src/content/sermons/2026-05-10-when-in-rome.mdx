# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's faithfulness to His promises never changes, so neither should yours — even when the way you live them out has to."
keyScriptureRef: "Acts 28:30-31"
keyScriptureText: He lived there two whole years at his own expense, and welcomed all who came to him, proclaiming the kingdom of God and teaching about the Lord Jesus Christ with all boldness and without hindrance.

mainPoints:
  - title: Paul adapted his method, not his mission.
    body: "Chained in a house, he couldn't go to the synagogue — so he brought the synagogue to him."
  - title: "God's plan for Israel hasn't changed."
    body: The new covenant in Jeremiah 31 was always with the Jews first, and God remains faithful to that promise even through rejection.
  - title: "The \"hope of Israel\" is the Messiah."
    body: "Paul preached Jesus as the fulfillment of every Old Testament promise — Genesis 3:15, Genesis 49, Isaiah 9 — and some believed."
  - title: Circumstances test obedience, not cancel it.
    body: "Paul couldn't provide for himself, so the church did. He couldn't preach freely, so he asked for prayer — and God answered with boldness."

keyIllustration: "The phrase \"when in Rome, do as the Romans do\" comes from Ambrose writing to Augustine about adapting ministry methods across cultures — not about compromising convictions. Paul modeled this: locked in a house, he didn't stop testifying; he changed the venue but never the message."

application:
  - "Identify where you're boxed in — health, work, family — and ask God how to obey Him differently, not whether to obey Him at all."
  - Testify to Jesus from Scripture and from your life. No one can argue with your testimony.
  - Pray for boldness and clarity for yourself and others who are proclaiming the gospel in difficult places.

toRemember: No circumstance can take away your purpose if you remain faithful to what Jesus has called you to do.
