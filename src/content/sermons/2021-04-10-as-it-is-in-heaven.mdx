# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "Jesus is not in the tomb — He is enthroned in heaven as your High Priest, and you need to stop looking for comfort in the old system and follow Him into the new work He's already doing."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 8:1-2 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "Now the point in what we are saying is this: we have such a high priest, one who is seated at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven, a minister in the holy places, in the true tent that the Lord set up, not man."

mainPoints:
  - title: You have a heavenly High Priest, not an earthly one.
    body: Jesus is seated at the right hand of God — His sacrifice was once for all. The earthly priesthood was temporary, pointing to Him.
  - title: The earthly temple was only a copy.
    body: Moses was told to build everything according to the pattern shown on the mountain. The real tabernacle is in heaven, and Jesus ministers there now.
  - title: God has moved on — you need to move with Him.
    body: "The early Christians wanted to go back to the temple. But Jesus has introduced a new covenant. Stop looking for yesterday's answers and ask where He's calling you today."

keyIllustration: "The women came to the tomb on Easter morning to anoint Jesus' body — they needed closure, they needed to say goodbye. A stone blocked their way. But when they arrived, the stone was rolled away and Jesus was gone. The angel said, \"He is risen. He's not here. He's going ahead of you to Galilee — just like He told you.\" They were looking for a corpse when they could have been having breakfast with the risen Christ. That's what happens when we cling to the old system instead of following Jesus into what He's doing now."

application:
  - "Ask, seek, knock. Ask God what He's calling you to. Seek the opportunities around you — at school, at work, in your neighbourhood. Then knock: try it, step through the door, and see what He opens."
  - "Be with Jesus first. Before you do anything, spend time in prayer and Scripture. You can't be sent out to preach if you're not first with Him."
  - "Church happens where you are. Families are gathering to worship. People are praying with police officers, feeding the poor, packing care packages. The church isn't just a Sunday gathering — it's what you do in your home, your street, your workplace."

toRemember: "Jesus has gone ahead of you. He's not in the graveyard of yesterday. He's in Galilee, waiting for you to follow Him into the work He's already doing."
