# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: The elementary doctrines of the faith—repentance, faith, washing, consecration, resurrection, judgment—were never meant to be abandoned, but they were also never meant to be accomplished in your own strength. Christ fulfilled them all, and now He empowers you to live them by His Spirit.
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 6:1-3 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Therefore let us leave the elementary doctrine of Christ and go on to maturity, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith toward God, and of instruction about washings, the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment. And this we will do if God permits.

mainPoints:
  - title: The six doctrines are Old Testament foundations, not New Testament innovations.
    body: Repentance, faith, ceremonial washing, laying on of hands, resurrection, and judgment all appear in the Hebrew Scriptures—but under the old covenant, they relied on human effort and pointed forward to something greater.
  - title: "\"If God permits\" is the key to the whole book."
    body: "This phrase unlocks the difference between old and new: it's not your effort that saves or sanctifies you—it's God's sovereign grace. What He ordains, He enables. What He wills, He accomplishes."
  - title: Going back is heavier than going forward.
    body: "Under persecution, the Jewish Christians were tempted to return to the old system. But trying to live for God in your own strength is harder than any external trial. Christ didn't come to make the old way easier—He came to replace it with His power."

keyIllustration: "An electric bike looks like a normal bicycle, but inside is a motor and a battery. You can pedal it manually, but why would you, when you could flip the switch and receive 100–300% assistance? The old covenant is the unplugged bike—you know the route, you know the destination, but you're doing it in your own strength. The new covenant is the same bike, but now the power is on. You still pedal, but the Holy Spirit does the heavy lifting. Going back to the old way isn't humility—it's pride dressed as effort."

application:
  - Stop trying to do in your own strength what only the Holy Spirit can accomplish through you.
  - "If you feel stuck in sin, or weak in faith, don't go backward—ask God to give you what He commands. He will."
  - "Humility is not pretending you can do it. Humility is admitting you can't, and thanking Him that He already has."

toRemember: "When we trust in who He is and humble ourselves, He can push us up any mountain—not because we're strong, but because He is faithful."
