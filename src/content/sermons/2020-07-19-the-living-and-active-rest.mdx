# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "God's rest is entered not by hiding our sin but by letting His word expose it, so that we can look to Christ crucified and be healed."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 4:12-13 ESV"
keyScriptureText: For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and of spirit, of joints and of marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart. And no creature is hidden from His sight, but all are naked and exposed to the eyes of Him to whom we must give account.

mainPoints:
  - title: Three kinds of rest remain available.
    body: "Eternal rest in heaven, the rest of knowing we don't earn our salvation, and the daily rest that comes from trusting and obeying God."
  - title: The five-stage slide begins with drift.
    body: Drift from the word leads to doubt, then dullness, then deliberate disobedience, then outright defiance — each stage harder to reverse.
  - title: "Scripture is a surgeon's tool, not a weapon of shame."
    body: "God's word cuts through our self-deception to expose hidden sin — not to condemn us, but to remove what's killing us."
  - title: Look at the cross like Israel looked at the bronze serpent.
    body: Confess what you see in yourself, trust in what Christ has done, and the venom loses its power.

keyIllustration: "A diver encountered a juvenile black mamba on a rocky slope — the snake between him and safety, exuding a lethal threat impossible to ignore. That clarity of danger is what we should feel about sin. But often sin looks harmless — like a cone snail, beautiful and shiny, or a duck-billed platypus, soft and shy — yet both carry venom that can kill. The word of God reveals what we can't see on our own."

application:
  - Stop numbing or hiding the pain. Ask God to show you the real cause — unforgiveness, pride, ingratitude — and confess it.
  - Read Scripture to be changed, not just informed. Let the Holy Spirit convict you as you read about the sins of others.
  - "Confess daily. The serpent's cure wasn't gradual — it was immediate. Daily confession keeps sin from accumulating into shame."

toRemember: God already knows your worst, most disgusting secrets — and He loved you enough to die for you anyway.
