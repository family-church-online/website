# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: Baptism does not save you — it declares to the world that you have already died to sin and been raised to life by the same power that raised Jesus from the dead.
keyScriptureRef: "Romans 6:3-4 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Do you not know that all of us who have been baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into his death? We were buried therefore with him by baptism into death, in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, we too might walk in newness of life.

mainPoints:
  - title: Grace does not eliminate the need for daily repentance.
    body: All your sin is forgiven — past, present, future — but Scripture still commands daily confession because a changed heart grieves when it sins against the One who gave His Son.
  - title: Baptism is the sign of the new covenant, not the old.
    body: "Circumcision was the sign of being born into Abraham's covenant; baptism is the sign of being reborn through faith — a spiritual circumcision of the heart done by God, not human hands."
  - title: Burial certifies death; it does not cause it.
    body: "Baptism does not kill the old self — the old self must already be dead. Baptism is the public declaration: \"Look what God has done in me.\""
  - title: You are raised by the glory of the Father.
    body: "Scripture does not say raised by the power of the Father — it says by the glory. The miracle of new life is designed to make God's greatness visible to the watching world."

keyIllustration: "A fire alarm went off during the previous week's service. The congregation prayed for it to stop. Within minutes, someone who knew nothing about the building's fire systems felt prompted to act, got up, walked out, and found the shutoff within ten minutes. That is how miracles work: God invites ordinary believers to participate in His supernatural work."

application:
  - If you have trusted Christ but never been baptized, obey now — not to be saved, but to glorify God publicly for what He has already done in you.
  - If you have been baptized but are sitting on the sidelines, ask God where He wants to use you. The same power that raised Jesus lives in you.
  - If you are not sure you have truly died to sin and been raised with Christ, come forward today and settle it — salvation is not a life insurance policy; it is a complete transformation.

toRemember: The same power that raised Jesus off the stone slab lives and works in every Christian — and is calling you to serve so the world can see how awesome our God is.
