# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: You were created in the image of God to imitate Him—not to earn His approval, but because you are already His beloved child, empowered by the Holy Spirit to walk in the self-giving love of Christ.
keyScriptureRef: "Ephesians 5:1-2 ESV"
keyScriptureText: Therefore be imitators of God, as beloved children. And walk in love, as Christ loved us and gave himself up for us, a fragrant offering and sacrifice to God.

mainPoints:
  - title: We are hardwired to imitate.
    body: "From childhood we copy those around us—the question is not whether you'll imitate, but who you will imitate and why."
  - title: Imitate God as beloved children.
    body: "You don't mimic Christ to make God love you; you're able to walk like Jesus because God already adores you and has given you His Spirit."
  - title: Jesus walked in total self-giving love.
    body: "Even facing the agony of the cross, He never made it about Himself—He poured Himself out for others because that's what the Father does."
  - title: "The Holy Spirit empowers you to do what you can't."
    body: The same Spirit that filled Jesus at His baptism and raised Him from the dead is given to every believer who repents and trusts in Christ.

keyIllustration: A four-year-old boy watched his father take a Bible to the park every morning to spend time with God during a season of breakdown and anxiety. Without understanding why, the boy insisted on taking his own Bible to school every day—simply because he loved his dad and wanted to be like him. Imitation flows from love, not comprehension.

application:
  - Stop trying to manufacture Christlikeness in your own strength. Receive love, joy, and peace from God first—then patience, kindness, and goodness will flow.
  - "When life demands more than you have, lean harder into the Holy Spirit's power. The more pressure you're under, the more you need to reach out and love others—that's how Jesus walked."
  - "It's never too late to choose a different father to imitate. No matter your earthly father's legacy, you can walk like Jesus starting today."

toRemember: "You don't imitate God to please Him; you're empowered to imitate Him because He already loves you completely—and walking like Jesus is the very best you can be."
