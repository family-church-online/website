# ── SERMON NOTES ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
bigIdea: "The new covenant doesn't lower the bar — it changes who keeps it. God writes His law on our hearts, remembers our sin no more, and brings us into relationship by faith, not performance."
keyScriptureRef: "Hebrews 8:10 ESV"
keyScriptureText: "For this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days, declares the Lord: I will put my laws into their minds, and write them on their hearts, and I will be their God, and they shall be my people."

mainPoints:
  - title: The fault was not in the covenant but in the people.
    body: "The old covenant's terms were not wrong — Israel simply could not keep them. Breaking faith with God broke the covenant, leading to exile, loss, and separation."
  - title: "The new covenant rests on God's \"I will,\" not ours."
    body: "God no longer says, \"You must obey.\" He says, \"I will put My law in your heart. I will be your God. I will forgive and forget your sin.\" Six times in Jeremiah 31, God alone carries the covenant."
  - title: Everyone in the new covenant knows God personally.
    body: Under the old covenant, you could be circumcised or eat the Passover without faith. Under the new, only those born again by faith in Jesus are included — and all of them know Him.
  - title: Gentiles enter through the promise to Abraham, fulfilled in Christ.
    body: The new covenant was made with Israel, but God invites the nations in. Those who believe in Jesus are grafted into the promise by faith, not ethnicity or works.

keyIllustration: "A young girl with two broken arms faced surgery and had to go under anesthetic. She hated the loss of control. Sitting with her father, terrified, she made the decision to trust — not because it felt natural, but because she believed her father and the doctors would care for her. Surrender looked simple from the outside, but it required everything. The new covenant asks the same: trust God to do what you cannot."

application:
  - Stop trying to earn what God has already given. If you are in Christ, His law is already in your heart. Your obedience flows from love, not fear of failure.
  - "Let go of control and trust God's work in you. Salvation isn't about your ability to keep promises — it's about believing in His."
  - "If you've never surrendered to Christ, now is the time. You cannot clean yourself up first. Come as you are, believe in what He has done, and receive the covenant He keeps."

toRemember: God does not ask you to be strong enough. He asks you to trust Him to be strong for you.
