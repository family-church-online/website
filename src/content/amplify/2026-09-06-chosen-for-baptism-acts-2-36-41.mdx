summary: "Told bluntly that their own hands had crucified God's Son, the crowd at Pentecost could only respond two ways: rage, or a broken heart. Which would be yours?"
about: Baptism is our chosen act of obedience in response to a salvation the Father planned, the Son secured, and the Spirit applies.
takeaway:
  - You're measured against the sinless perfection of Christ, not against other people — which is exactly why no one comes out clean.
  - Owning your specific sin isn't shame or manipulation; it's the downward pull that makes God's lifting grace possible.
  - 'Salvation runs on six choices: you sinned, the Father chose to save you, the Spirit convicted you, the Son chose to die, you repent, and the Father gives His Spirit.'
  - Baptism is obedience you actively choose, yet never accomplish in your own strength.
  - Joy is not happiness; happiness drains away when you lose a match, get sick, or lose someone, but joy is an ember that cannot be put out.
forYou:
  - You compare yourself to other people and quietly conclude you're basically a good person.
  - You struggle to forgive someone who hurt you, and you carry anger you can't seem to put down.
  - You've done things you can never undo or make up for, and the guilt still follows you.
  - Your happiness keeps draining away with every hard circumstance, and you wonder whether anything lasts.
keyScripture: Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit. — Acts 2:38
bigIdea: Because God chose you, convicted you, and died for you before you ever chose Him, the only fitting response is to repent, be baptized, and receive His joy.
mainPoints:
  - heading: The sin we chose
    text: Though we weren't in the crowd, the sin each of us chooses is what put Jesus on the cross, so Peter's charge lands on us too.
  - heading: The salvation God chose
    text: Before we ever sinned the Father chose to save us, the Spirit chose to convict us, and the Son chose to lay down His life of His own accord.
  - heading: The response we make
    text: We actively choose to repent and be baptized in obedience, and God gives His Holy Spirit and an unquenchable joy in return.
keyIllustration: "A scuba diver hovers weightlessly over a coral reef in perfect neutral buoyancy — held by equal forces pulling down and lifting up — surrounded by colour-shifting cleaner shrimp, toothpaste-bright nudibranchs, and giant bass nudging in for a head-rub. To simply hover and refuse to swim on would be to miss all of it. That's how grace works: it carries us effortlessly, yet still calls us to choose to move forward in obedience."
meansForUs:
  - Stop measuring yourself against other people and honestly name the specific sins — the lies, the anger, the unforgiveness — you've been minimising.
  - If you've never obeyed Christ's call to repent and be baptized, make that active choice rather than waiting until you feel strong enough.
  - Anchor your joy in what God has chosen and done for you rather than in circumstances that come and go.
remember: Your joy is not fragile happiness that circumstances can steal, but an ember God lit before you were born that no loss can extinguish.
